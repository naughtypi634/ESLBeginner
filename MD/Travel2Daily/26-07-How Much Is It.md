# How Much Is It? · 多少钱

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 顾客 | How much is a double room for one night? | 双人间一晚多少钱？ |
| 前台 | Six hundred a night, taxes included. | 一晚六百，含税。 |
| 顾客 | Is breakfast included in the price? | 价格里含早餐吗？ |
| 前台 | No, breakfast is forty per person. | 不含，早餐每位四十。 |
| 顾客 | Is there a service charge if I pay by card? | 刷卡有手续费吗？ |
| 前台 | No, card is the same price. | 没有，刷卡价格一样。 |
| 顾客 | Can I get a discount for two nights? | 住两晚能便宜点吗？ |
| 前台 | Let me check. We have a two-night rate. | 我看看。我们有连住两晚的价格。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**How much is ___?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 问单价 | How much is a double room? | 双人间多少钱？ |
| 问总价 | How much is it for two nights? | 住两晚多少钱？ |
| 问含不含 | Is breakfast included? | 含早餐吗？ |
| 问额外费用 | Is there a service charge? | 有手续费吗？ |
| 问优惠 | Is there a discount for students? | 学生有优惠吗？ |

## 4. Answers 回答里会听到的

| 回答 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Six hundred a night, taxes included. | 一晚六百，含税。 |
| It's forty per person. | 每位四十。 |
| Breakfast is not included. | 不含早餐。 |
| No, there's no service charge. | 没有手续费。 |
| There's a ten percent discount. | 有九折优惠。 |

## 7. Practice 练习

### 填空

| # | 句子 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How ______ is a double room for one night? | much |
| 2 | Is breakfast ______ in the price? | included |
| 3 | Is there a service ______ if I pay by card? | charge |
| 4 | Is there a ______ for students? | discount |
| 5 | The ______ is two hundred. | deposit |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| a double room / one night | How much is a double room for one night? | 双人间一晚多少钱？ |
| the delivery fee / included | Is the delivery fee included? | 含配送费吗？ |
| a discount / a year | Is there a discount if I pay for a year? | 年付有优惠吗？ |
| the deposit / this apartment | How much is the deposit for this apartment? | 这房子押金多少？ |
| a service charge / the bill | Is there a service charge on the bill? | 账单里有服务费吗？ |
