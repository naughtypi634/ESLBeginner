# It Isn't Working. · 坏了 / 出问题了

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 客人 | Hello, the air conditioner isn't working. | 你好，空调坏了。 |
| 前台 | I'm sorry. What's your room number? | 抱歉。您的房间号是多少？ |
| 客人 | Room 812. The Wi-Fi isn't working either. | 812。Wi-Fi 也连不上。 |
| 前台 | Let me send someone up in ten minutes. | 我十分钟内派人上去。 |
| 客人 | Thanks. Also, there's water on the floor. | 谢谢。还有，地上有水。 |
| 前台 | Oh no. Would you like to change rooms? | 啊，要给您换个房间吗？ |
| 客人 | Yes, please. And the key card doesn't work. | 好，麻烦你。还有房卡刷不开。 |
| 前台 | I'll bring you a new one right away. | 我马上给您送一张新的。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**___ isn't working.**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 说故障 | The air conditioner isn't working. | 空调坏了。 |
| 说连不上 | The Wi-Fi isn't working. | Wi-Fi 连不上。 |
| 说打不开 | The key card doesn't work. | 房卡刷不开。 |
| 说没出来 | The hot water isn't coming out. | 热水出不来。 |
| 问怎么办 | Can you send someone up? | 能派人上来看一下吗？ |
