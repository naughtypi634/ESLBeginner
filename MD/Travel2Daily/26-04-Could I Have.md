# Could I Have ...? · 请给我

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | Could I have a glass of water, please? | 可以给我一杯水吗？ |
| 空乘 | Sure, here you go. | 好的，给您。 |
| 乘客 | Could I have a blanket, please? | 可以给我一条毯子吗？ |
| 空乘 | Of course. Anything else? | 当然。还要别的吗？ |
| 乘客 | Could I have the chicken, please? | 我要鸡肉那份，可以吗？ |
| 空乘 | Sorry, we're out of chicken. Beef? | 抱歉，鸡肉没有了，牛肉可以吗？ |
| 乘客 | That's fine. Could I have some hot tea too? | 可以。再给我一杯热茶行吗？ |
| 空乘 | Sure. I'll bring it in a minute. | 好的，我马上拿来。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**Could I have ___, please?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 要一样东西 | Could I have a blanket, please? | 可以给我一条毯子吗？ |
| 多要一点 | Could I have some extra napkins? | 可以多给我几张纸巾吗？ |
| 再加一样 | Could I have some hot water too? | 可以再给我一点热水吗？ |
| 换说法 | Can I get a glass of water, please? | 可以给我一杯水吗？ |
| 问有没有 | Do you have any decaf tea? | 你们有无咖啡因的茶吗？ |

| 回答 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Sure, here you go. | 好的，给您。 |
| Of course. Anything else? | 当然。还要别的吗？ |
| Sorry, we're out of chicken. | 抱歉，鸡肉没有了。 |
| I'll bring it in a minute. | 我马上拿来。 |
| Let me check if we have any left. | 我看看还有没有。 |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| What do you usually ask for on a flight? | 你在飞机上一般会要什么？ | blanket / water |
| Do you ask for less ice when you order drinks? | 点饮料你会要少冰吗？ | less ice / no sugar |
| How do you ask for the bill in a restaurant? | 在餐厅你怎么要账单？ | check / receipt |
| What do you do when they are out of something? | 想点的没有了你会怎么办？ | second choice |
| Do you ask for a receipt when you buy something? | 买东西你会要发票吗？ | receipt / app |
