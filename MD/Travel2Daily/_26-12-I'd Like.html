I'd Like ... / I'm Looking For ... · 我想要

1. Dialogue 对话

说话人English中文
顾客Hi, I'd like a room for two nights.你好，我想订一个房间，住两晚。
前台Sure. A single or a double?好的。单人间还是双人间？
顾客A double, please. I'd like a quiet room.双人间。我想要安静一点的房间。
前台Let me check. I have one on the eighth floor.我查一下。八楼有一间。
顾客Great. I'm also looking for a good noodle place.太好了。我还想找家好吃的面馆。
前台There's one across the street. It's open late.马路对面有一家，开到很晚。
顾客Perfect. I'd like a late check-out too, if possible.太好了。可以的话我想晚点退房。
前台No problem. We can do 2 p.m.没问题，可以到下午两点。

2. Pattern 句型公式

I'd like ___, please.

用法例句中文对照
想要一个I'd like a double room, please.我想要一个双人间。
加上要求I'd like a quiet room, please.我想要安静一点的房间。
正在找I'm looking for a noodle place.我在找一家面馆。
要带条件的I'd like a room with a view.我想要有景观的房间。
直接定下来I'll take the double room.我要那间双人间。

English中文

6. Discussion 讨论

Question中文对照Useful words

Do you prefer a quiet room or a room with a view?你喜欢安静的房间还是有景观的？quiet / view
How do you ask for a smaller size?要小一号你会怎么说？smaller size
Have you ever changed an appointment? Why?你改过预约吗？为什么？reschedule
What are you looking for right now?你现在正在找什么东西？apartment / gym
