How Long Does It Take ...? · 要多久

1. Dialogue 对话

说话人English中文
乘客How long does it take to get to the city center?去市中心要多久？
地勤About forty minutes by express train.坐快线大概四十分钟。
乘客How long is the layover in Seoul?在首尔转机要等多久？
地勤Three hours. You have time for lunch.三个小时。你有时间吃午饭。
乘客How long does the security check usually take?安检一般要多久？
地勤Twenty minutes now, but it gets busy at six.现在二十分钟，六点后会很挤。
乘客How far is the hotel from the airport?酒店离机场有多远？
地勤About fifty kilometers. A taxi takes an hour.大概五十公里。打车一小时。

2. Pattern 句型公式

How long does it take to ___?

用法例句中文对照
问时长How long does it take to get there?到那儿要多久？
具体回答It takes about forty minutes.大概四十分钟。
问距离How far is it from here?离这儿多远？
问走路How long does it take on foot?走路要多久？
问会不会更久Does it take longer at night?晚上会更久吗？

4. Answers 回答里会听到的

回答中文
About forty minutes by express train.坐快线大概四十分钟。
It takes about twenty minutes on foot.走路大概二十分钟。
Three hours. You have time for lunch.三个小时。你有时间吃午饭。
It depends on the traffic.要看路况。
Only ten minutes if you take the subway.坐地铁只要十分钟。
The line is short right now.现在排队的人不多。
It usually takes longer after six.六点以后一般更久。
