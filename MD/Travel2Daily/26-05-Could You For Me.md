# Could You ... for Me? · 能帮我一下吗

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | Could you help me put my bag up, please? | 能帮我把包放上去吗？ |
| 空乘 | Sure, let me do that. | 好的，我来。 |
| 乘客 | Could you wake me up before we land? | 落地前能叫醒我吗？ |
| 空乘 | Of course. What's your seat number? | 当然。您的座位号是多少？ |
| 乘客 | Could you call me a taxi, please? | 能帮我叫辆出租车吗？ |
| 前台 | Sure. Where are you going? | 好的。您去哪儿？ |
| 乘客 | Could you keep my luggage until four? | 能帮我把行李存到四点吗？ |
| 前台 | No problem. Here's your tag. | 没问题。这是您的号码牌。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**Could you ___ for me?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 请人帮忙 | Could you help me with this bag? | 能帮我搬一下这个包吗？ |
| 请人叫车 | Could you call me a taxi, please? | 能帮我叫辆车吗？ |
| 请人保管 | Could you keep my luggage until four? | 能帮我存到四点吗？ |
| 加上原因 | I can't reach it. Could you help? | 我够不着，能帮我吗？ |
| 换说法 | Would you mind helping me? | 能麻烦你帮我一下吗？ |

## 4. Answers 回答里会听到的

| 回答 | 中文 |
|---|---|

| Sorry, I'm busy right now. One moment. | 抱歉，我这会儿在忙，稍等。 |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| Who do you usually ask for help when you travel? | 出行时你一般找谁帮忙？ | staff / front desk |
| Have you ever helped a stranger? What happened? | 你帮过陌生人吗？怎么回事？ | luggage / photos |
| Do you find it easy to ask for help in English? | 用英语求助你觉得容易吗？ | nervous / practice |
| What do you ask the front desk to do for you? | 你一般让前台帮你做什么？ | luggage / taxi |
| Do you take photos for other people often? | 你常帮别人拍照吗？ | tourist / group |
