# Say That Again? · 没听清

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | Sorry, could you say that again, please? | 抱歉，能再说一遍吗？ |
| 地勤 | I said your gate has changed to 18. | 我说您的登机口改到 18 号了。 |
| 乘客 | Got it. Sorry, could you speak a little slower? | 明白了。抱歉，能说慢一点吗？ |
| 地勤 | Sure. Gate ... eighteen ... changed. | 好的。登机口……18 号……改了。 |
| 乘客 | What does "final call" mean? | "final call" 是什么意思？ |
| 地勤 | It means boarding is almost over. Hurry. | 意思是快要停止登机了，快点。 |
| 乘客 | Thanks. Could you write the gate number down? | 谢谢。能把登机口号写下来吗？ |
| 地勤 | No problem. Here you go. | 没问题。给您。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**Sorry, could you say that again?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 请重复 | Sorry, could you say that again? | 抱歉，能再说一遍吗？ |
| 请说慢 | Could you speak a little slower? | 能说慢一点吗？ |
| 问意思 | What does "final call" mean? | "final call" 是什么意思？ |
| 请写下来 | Could you write it down for me? | 能帮我写下来吗？ |
| 说没跟上 | Sorry, I don't follow. | 抱歉，我没听懂。 |

## 3. Slots 槽位

| 槽位 | 例句 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 再说一遍 | Sorry, could you say that again? | 抱歉，能再说一遍吗？ |
| 说慢一点 | Could you speak a little slower? | 能说慢一点吗？ |
| 说大声点 | Could you speak a little louder? | 能说大声一点吗？ |
| 问意思 | What does that mean? | 那是什么意思？ |
| 问怎么拼 | How do you spell that? | 那个怎么拼？ |
| 写下来 | Could you write it down for me? | 能帮我写下来吗？ |
| 确认号码 | Did you say gate 18 or gate 80? | 您是说 18 号还是 80 号？ |
| 指给我看 | Could you point to it on the map? | 能在地图上指给我看吗？ |

| Let me say it again more slowly. | 我再说慢一点。 |
| It means boarding is almost over. | 意思是快要停止登机了。 |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| Do you ask people to repeat, or just nod? | 你会让人重复，还是直接点头？ | repeat / nod |
| What is hard to understand on the phone? | 电话里最难听懂的是什么？ | accent / noise |
| How do you ask someone to slow down? | 请人说慢你会怎么说？ | slower / again |
| Have you ever missed an announcement? | 你漏听过广播吗？ | gate / platform |
| Do you use voice messages or text more? | 你更常用语音还是文字？ | voice / text |
