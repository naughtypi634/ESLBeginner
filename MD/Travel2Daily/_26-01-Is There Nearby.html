Is There ... Nearby? · 附近有没有

说话人English中文

地勤Yes, there's one next to Gate 12.有，12 号登机口旁边有一个。
乘客Are there any charging stations on this floor?这层有充电的地方吗？
地勤There are a few along the wall by the windows.靠窗那面墙边有几个。

地勤Sorry, I'm not sure. Let me ask my coworker.抱歉，我不太确定，我问一下同事。

2. Pattern 句型公式

Is there a ___ near here?

用法例句中文对照
提问 · 单数Is there a pharmacy near here?这附近有药店吗？
提问 · 复数Are there any lockers near here?这附近有存包柜吗？
回答 · 有Yes, there's one on the second floor.有，二楼有一个。
回答 · 没有No, there isn't one on this floor.没有，这层没有。
换成 haveDo you have a phone charger?你们有充电器吗？

4. Answers 回答里会听到的

回答中文
Yes, there's one on the second floor.有，二楼有一个。
It's next to Gate 12.在 12 号登机口旁边。
It's across from the coffee shop.在咖啡店对面。
It's at the end of the hall, on your right.走到走廊尽头，右手边。

Sorry, I'm not sure. Let me ask my coworker.抱歉，我不太确定，我问一下同事。
