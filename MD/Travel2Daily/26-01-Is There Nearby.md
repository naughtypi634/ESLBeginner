# Is There ... Nearby? · 附近有没有

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | Excuse me, is there a restroom nearby? | 请问，这附近有洗手间吗？ |
| 地勤 | Yes, there's one next to Gate 12. | 有，12 号登机口旁边有一个。 |
| 乘客 | Are there any charging stations on this floor? | 这层有充电的地方吗？ |
| 地勤 | There are a few along the wall by the windows. | 靠窗那面墙边有几个。 |
| 乘客 | Do you have a hot water machine here? | 你们这儿有饮水机吗？ |
| 地勤 | Sorry, I'm not sure. Let me ask my coworker. | 抱歉，我不太确定，我问一下同事。 |
| 乘客 | No problem. Is there a convenience store nearby? | 没事。这附近有便利店吗？ |
| 地勤 | Yes, on the second floor, next to the food court. | 有，二楼美食广场旁边。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**Is there a ___ near here?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 提问 · 单数 | Is there a pharmacy near here? | 这附近有药店吗？ |
| 提问 · 复数 | Are there any lockers near here? | 这附近有存包柜吗？ |
| 回答 · 有 | Yes, there's one on the second floor. | 有，二楼有一个。 |
| 回答 · 没有 | No, there isn't one on this floor. | 没有，这层没有。 |
| 换成 have | Do you have a phone charger? | 你们有充电器吗？ |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| What is near your home that you use every week? | 你家附近你每周都用的是什么？ | package locker / market |
| Which store do you wish was closer to you? | 你希望哪家店离你更近？ | pharmacy / gym |
| Tell us about a time you looked for something nearby. | 说说你一次在附近找东西的经历。 | printer / charger |
| Is it easy to find what you need near your office? | 你公司附近找东西方便吗？ | food court / ATM |
| Do you ask a person first or check your phone first? | 你先问人还是先查手机？ | map app / staff |

## 7. Practice 练习

### 填空

| # | 句子 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ______ a restroom on this floor? | Is there |
| 2 | ______ any lockers near the gate? | Are there |

| 5 | There ______ a few lockers by the elevator. | are |

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |

| print shop / the office | Is there a print shop near the office? | 公司附近有打印店吗？ |
| any power banks / this mall | Are there any power banks in this mall? | 这商场里有充电宝吗？ |
