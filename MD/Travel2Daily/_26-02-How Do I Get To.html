How Do I Get To ...? · 怎么去

1. Dialogue 对话

说话人English中文
乘客Excuse me, how do I get to Gate 32?请问，去 32 号登机口怎么走？
地勤Take the escalator up and turn right.坐扶梯上去，然后右转。
乘客How do I get to the baggage claim from here?从这里去行李提取处怎么走？
地勤Follow the signs, then take the train.跟着指示牌走，然后坐小火车。
乘客How do I get to the city center from the airport?从机场去市中心怎么走？
地勤Take the Airport Express. It's about 40 minutes.坐机场快线，大概四十分钟。
乘客How do I get to Terminal 2? I'm transferring.去 2 号航站楼怎么走？我要转机。
地勤There's a free shuttle outside Door 6.6 号门外有免费摆渡车。

4. Answers 回答里会听到的

回答中文
Go straight and turn right at the escalator.直走，在扶梯那里右转。
Take the escalator up to the third floor.坐扶梯上三楼。
Follow the signs for Terminal 2.跟着去 2 号航站楼的指示牌走。
Take the Airport Express, then Line 2.坐机场快线，然后换 2 号线。
It's about a ten-minute walk.走路大概十分钟。
You can't walk there. You'll need a taxi.走不过去，得打车。
Sorry, I'm not from here. Ask at the desk.抱歉，我不是本地的，去柜台问吧。
