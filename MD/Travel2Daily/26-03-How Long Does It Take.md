# How Long Does It Take ...? · 要多久

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | How long does it take to get to the city center? | 去市中心要多久？ |
| 地勤 | About forty minutes by express train. | 坐快线大概四十分钟。 |
| 乘客 | How long is the layover in Seoul? | 在首尔转机要等多久？ |
| 地勤 | Three hours. You have time for lunch. | 三个小时。你有时间吃午饭。 |
| 乘客 | How long does the security check usually take? | 安检一般要多久？ |
| 地勤 | Twenty minutes now, but it gets busy at six. | 现在二十分钟，六点后会很挤。 |
| 乘客 | How far is the hotel from the airport? | 酒店离机场有多远？ |
| 地勤 | About fifty kilometers. A taxi takes an hour. | 大概五十公里。打车一小时。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**How long does it take to ___?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 问时长 | How long does it take to get there? | 到那儿要多久？ |
| 具体回答 | It takes about forty minutes. | 大概四十分钟。 |
| 问距离 | How far is it from here? | 离这儿多远？ |
| 问走路 | How long does it take on foot? | 走路要多久？ |
| 问会不会更久 | Does it take longer at night? | 晚上会更久吗？ |

## 4. Answers 回答里会听到的

| 回答 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| About forty minutes by express train. | 坐快线大概四十分钟。 |
| It takes about twenty minutes on foot. | 走路大概二十分钟。 |
| Three hours. You have time for lunch. | 三个小时。你有时间吃午饭。 |
| It depends on the traffic. | 要看路况。 |
| Only ten minutes if you take the subway. | 坐地铁只要十分钟。 |
| The line is short right now. | 现在排队的人不多。 |
| It usually takes longer after six. | 六点以后一般更久。 |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| How long is your commute every day? | 你每天通勤要多久？ | forty minutes / two lines |
| Do you leave earlier than you need to? | 你会特意提前出门吗？ | rush hour / backup |
| How long do you usually wait for a delivery? | 你等快递一般多久？ | same day / next day |
| What takes the longest on a travel day for you? | 出行日你最花时间的是哪一步？ | security / baggage |
| Do you prefer a short layover or a long one? | 转机你喜欢短的还是长的？ | layover / risk |

## 7. Practice 练习

### 填空

| # | 句子 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How ______ does it take to get downtown? | long |
| 2 | It ______ about forty minutes. | takes |
| 3 | How ______ is the hotel from the airport? | far |
| 4 | How long does it take ______ foot? | on |
| 5 | It ______ on the traffic. | depends |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| the airport / by subway | How long does it take to the airport by subway? | 坐地铁去机场要多久？ |
| your office / on foot | How long does it take to your office on foot? | 走到你们公司要多久？ |
| far / the train station | How far is the train station from here? | 火车站离这儿多远？ |
| security / after six | How long does security take after six? | 六点后安检要多久？ |
| a refund | How long does a refund take? | 退款要多久？ |
