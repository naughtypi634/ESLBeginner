It Isn't Working. · 坏了 / 出问题了

1. Dialogue 对话

说话人English中文
客人Hello, the air conditioner isn't working.你好，空调坏了。
前台I'm sorry. What's your room number?抱歉。您的房间号是多少？
客人Room 812. The Wi-Fi isn't working either.812。Wi-Fi 也连不上。
前台Let me send someone up in ten minutes.我十分钟内派人上去。
客人Thanks. Also, there's water on the floor.谢谢。还有，地上有水。
前台Oh no. Would you like to change rooms?啊，要给您换个房间吗？
客人Yes, please. And the key card doesn't work.好，麻烦你。还有房卡刷不开。
前台I'll bring you a new one right away.我马上给您送一张新的。

2. Pattern 句型公式

___ isn't working.

用法例句中文对照
说故障The air conditioner isn't working.空调坏了。
说连不上The Wi-Fi isn't working.Wi-Fi 连不上。
说打不开The key card doesn't work.房卡刷不开。
说没出来The hot water isn't coming out.热水出不来。
问怎么办Can you send someone up?能派人上来看一下吗？
