# Do I Need To ...? · 我需要吗

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | Do I need to fill out a landing card? | 我需要填入境卡吗？ |
| 海关 | Yes, one per person. | 需要，一人一张。 |
| 乘客 | Do I need to declare my medicine? | 我的药需要申报吗？ |
| 海关 | Only if it's more than a month's supply. | 超过一个月用量才需要。 |
| 乘客 | Is it okay if I bring in some tea? | 我可以带点茶叶进来吗？ |
| 海关 | That's fine. No fresh fruit, though. | 可以。但不能带新鲜水果。 |
| 乘客 | Do I need to take my laptop out at security? | 过安检要把笔记本拿出来吗？ |
| 安检 | Yes, please. And your power bank too. | 需要，充电宝也要拿出来。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**Do I need to ___?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 问需要 | Do I need to fill out this form? | 我需要填这张表吗？ |
| 回答 · 不用 | No, you don't need to. | 不用。 |
| 问可不可以 | Is it okay if I bring some tea? | 我可以带茶叶吗？ |
| 问能带吗 | Can I take this on the plane? | 这个可以带上飞机吗？ |
| 问规定 | What's the rule on liquids? | 液体有什么规定？ |

## 4. Answers 回答里会听到的

| 回答 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Yes, you do. | 需要的。 |
| No, you don't need to. | 不用。 |
| Only if it's more than one bottle. | 只有超过一瓶才需要。 |
| You can do it online instead. | 可以在网上办。 |
| Please take it out and put it in the tray. | 请拿出来放在托盘里。 |
| Sorry, that's not allowed on the plane. | 抱歉，这个不能带上飞机。 |
| Yes, and please keep it with you. | 需要，请随身带着。 |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| Where do you check the rules before you travel? | 出行前你在哪查规定？ | app / website |
| Have you ever been stopped at security? Why? | 你过安检被拦过吗？为什么？ | liquids / power bank |
| Do you usually print your tickets or use your phone? | 你机票一般打印还是用手机？ | QR code / PDF |
| What do you always carry in your carry-on bag? | 随身包里你必带什么？ | power bank / medicine |
| Which rules in other countries surprise you? | 别国的哪些规定让你意外？ | tipping / shoes |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |

| a deposit / the apartment | Do I need to pay a deposit for the apartment? | 这房子要交押金吗？ |
| arrive / thirty minutes early | Do I need to arrive thirty minutes early? | 需要提前三十分钟到吗？ |
