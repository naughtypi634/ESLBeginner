# How Do I Get To ...? · 怎么去

## 1. Dialogue 对话

| 说话人 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 乘客 | Excuse me, how do I get to Gate 32? | 请问，去 32 号登机口怎么走？ |
| 地勤 | Take the escalator up and turn right. | 坐扶梯上去，然后右转。 |
| 乘客 | How do I get to the baggage claim from here? | 从这里去行李提取处怎么走？ |
| 地勤 | Follow the signs, then take the train. | 跟着指示牌走，然后坐小火车。 |
| 乘客 | How do I get to the city center from the airport? | 从机场去市中心怎么走？ |
| 地勤 | Take the Airport Express. It's about 40 minutes. | 坐机场快线，大概四十分钟。 |
| 乘客 | How do I get to Terminal 2? I'm transferring. | 去 2 号航站楼怎么走？我要转机。 |
| 地勤 | There's a free shuttle outside Door 6. | 6 号门外有免费摆渡车。 |

## 2. Pattern 句型公式

**How do I get to ___?**

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 提问 · 地点 | How do I get to the City Museum? | 去市博物馆怎么走？ |
| 提问 · 从这里 | How do I get to the subway from here? | 从这里去地铁站怎么走？ |
| 换说法 | What's the best way to get downtown? | 去市中心怎么走最好？ |
| 问距离 | How far is it from here? | 离这儿有多远？ |
| 问走路 | Can I walk there? | 能走过去吗？ |

## 4. Answers 回答里会听到的

| 回答 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Go straight and turn right at the escalator. | 直走，在扶梯那里右转。 |
| Take the escalator up to the third floor. | 坐扶梯上三楼。 |
| Follow the signs for Terminal 2. | 跟着去 2 号航站楼的指示牌走。 |
| Take the Airport Express, then Line 2. | 坐机场快线，然后换 2 号线。 |
| It's about a ten-minute walk. | 走路大概十分钟。 |
| You can't walk there. You'll need a taxi. | 走不过去，得打车。 |
| Sorry, I'm not from here. Ask at the desk. | 抱歉，我不是本地的，去柜台问吧。 |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| How do you usually get around your city? | 你在城里一般怎么出行？ | subway / shared bike |
| What is the fastest way to the airport for you? | 你去机场最快怎么走？ | express / taxi |
| Do you use a map app or ask people? | 你用地图软件还是问人？ | map app / sign |
| Tell us about a time you missed a train or flight. | 说说你一次误了车或飞机。 | gate / platform |
| Which exit do you use at your usual station? | 你常坐的地铁站走哪个口？ | Exit B / north |

## 7. Practice 练习

|---|---|---|
| 1 | ______ do I get to the train station? | How |

| 5 | Can I ______ there, or should I take a bus? | walk |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |

| the nearest pharmacy | How do I get to the nearest pharmacy? | 最近的药店怎么走？ |
| far / the train station | How far is the train station from here? | 火车站离这儿多远？ |
