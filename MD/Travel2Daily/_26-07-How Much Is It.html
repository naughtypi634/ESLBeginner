How Much Is It? · 多少钱

1. Dialogue 对话

说话人English中文
顾客How much is a double room for one night?双人间一晚多少钱？
前台Six hundred a night, taxes included.一晚六百，含税。
顾客Is breakfast included in the price?价格里含早餐吗？
前台No, breakfast is forty per person.不含，早餐每位四十。
顾客Is there a service charge if I pay by card?刷卡有手续费吗？
前台No, card is the same price.没有，刷卡价格一样。
顾客Can I get a discount for two nights?住两晚能便宜点吗？
前台Let me check. We have a two-night rate.我看看。我们有连住两晚的价格。

4. Answers 回答里会听到的

回答中文
Six hundred a night, taxes included.一晚六百，含税。
It's forty per person.每位四十。
Breakfast is not included.不含早餐。
No, there's no service charge.没有手续费。
There's a ten percent discount.有九折优惠。
Let me check. We have a two-night rate.我看看。我们有连住两晚的价格。
The deposit is two hundred.押金两百。
