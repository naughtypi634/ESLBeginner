## S01 Waking Up 起床

1. **wake up** 醒来 - *I wake up at 6:30 and make coffee first.*
2. **hit the snooze button** 按贪睡键 - *I hit the snooze button twice this morning.*
3. **get out of bed** 下床 - *It is hard to get out of bed on cold mornings.*
4. **sleep in** 睡懒觉 - *On Sundays we sleep in until ten.*
5. **stay in bed** 赖床 - *I stayed in bed until nine on Saturday.*
6. **sleep through the alarm** 没听见闹钟 - *I slept through the alarm and missed the bus.*
7. **turn off the alarm** 关掉闹钟 - *I turned off the alarm and went back to sleep.*
8. **make the bed** 整理床铺 - *She makes her bed before breakfast.*
9. **roll over** 翻个身 - *I rolled over and checked the time.*
10. **sit up** 坐起来 - *I sat up and looked at the clock.*
