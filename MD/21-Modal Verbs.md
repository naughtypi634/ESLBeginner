# Modal Verbs · 情态动词（初学者版 + 完整版）

情态动词两部分：**1. 最常见用法**（能力、许可、建议、义务、意愿）；**2. 可能性推测**（may / might / could / must / can't）。学完 Part 1 后，Part 2 是完整版总复习：补上所有核心情态动词、半情态动词、have done 结构和易混辨析。

## Part 1 · 初学者版 Beginner Edition

## 1. 最常见用法 Common Uses

### can 能 / 会（能力、许可）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **can** scan the code to pay. | 我能扫码付款。 |
| **Can** I sit here? | 我能坐这儿吗？ |
| I **can't** hear you — the metro is too loud. | 我听不清——地铁太吵了。 |
| I **can** ride a shared bike, but I can't drive. | 我会骑共享单车，但不会开车。 |

### could 可以（更礼貌；过去的能）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| **Could** you say that again? | 你能再说一遍吗？ |
| **Could** I use your charger? | 我能借你的充电器吗？ |
| When I was in school, I **could** run 5 km. | 上学时我能跑五公里。 |

### should 应该（建议、责任）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **should** bring an umbrella — it's going to rain. | 你应该带伞，要下雨了。 |
| I **should** reply to this message first. | 我应该先回这条消息。 |
| You **shouldn't** work overtime every day. | 你不该天天加班。 |
| We **should** split the bill evenly. | 我们应该 AA 平分账单。 |

### must / have to 必须（强义务）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **have to** scan the code before you get on. | 上车前必须扫码。 |
| I **have to** take the last metro home. | 我得赶末班地铁回家。 |
| I **must** pay the bill before 10 pm. | 我必须在十点前付款。 |
| You **don't have to** tip in China. | 在中国你不用给小费。 |
| You **mustn't** smoke here. | 这里禁止吸烟。 |

### will / would 将会 / 愿意

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **will** call you when I arrive. | 到了我给你打电话。 |
| **Would** you like some tea? | 要来点茶吗？ |
| **Would** you mind waiting a minute? | 你介意等一分钟吗？ |

## 2. 可能性推测 Possibility & Deduction

### may / might / could 可能（不确定）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| It **may** rain this afternoon. | 下午可能下雨。 |
| He **might** be stuck in traffic. | 他可能堵在路上了。 |
| The delivery **could** arrive late. | 外卖可能会晚到。 |
| She **might not** see the message. | 她可能没看到消息。 |
| It **may** be cheaper if you order from 美团. | 用美团点可能更便宜。 |

### must 一定（把握很大的肯定推测）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You've worked all day — you **must** be tired. | 你忙了一整天，一定很累。 |
| The line is so long — the food **must** be good. | 排这么长的队，这家一定好吃。 |
| Your phone is off — you **must** have run out of battery. | 你手机关机，一定是没电了。 |

### can't 不可能（把握很大的否定推测）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| That **can't** be true — I just saw him. | 那不可能是真的，我刚见过他。 |
| It **can't** be 9 pm already. | 不可能已经九点了。 |
| She **can't** be at home — the light is off. | 她不可能在家，灯都灭了。 |

| 推测词 | 确定程度（教学简化） | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **might** | 可能 50% | He **might** be stuck in traffic. 他可能堵在路上了。 |
| **could** | 也许 60% | The delivery **could** arrive late. 外卖可能会晚到。 |
| **may** | 可能 70% | It **may** rain this afternoon. 下午可能下雨。 |
| **must** | 一定 95% | You've worked all day — you **must** be tired. 你忙了一整天，一定很累。 |
| **can't** | 不可能 | That **can't** be true — I just saw him. 那不可能是真的，我刚见过他。 |

百分比只是教学上的直观简化；真实英语里确定程度会随语境变化，不用把它当精确数值记死。

## Part 2 · 完整版 Complete Edition

## 3. 情态动词的共同规则 Common Rules

| 规则 | 说明 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 没有人称和数的变化 | 主语是 he / she / it 也不加 -s | He **can** swim. 他会游泳。 |
| 后面接动词原形 | 情态动词 + do，不加 to | You **must** go now. 你现在必须走。 |
| 否定：直接加 not | 常见缩略见第 10 节 | You **shouldn't** work late. 你不该工作到很晚。 |
| 疑问：情态动词提前 | 不用 do / does / did 帮忙 | **Can** you help me? 你能帮我吗？ |
| 没有非谓语形式 | 不能写 to must / musting | 需要时用半情态动词：I'll **have to** leave. 我得走了。 |

## 4. 核心情态动词总览 All Nine Core Modals

| 情态动词 | 主要含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | 能力、许可、可能 | I **can** ride a shared bike. 我会骑共享单车。 |
| **could** | 过去能力、委婉、可能 | **Could** you say that again? 你能再说一遍吗？ |
| **may** | 许可、可能、祝愿 | **May** I come in? 我可以进来吗？ |
| **might** | 更不确定的可能、委婉 | He **might** be stuck in traffic. 他可能堵在路上了。 |
| **must** | 必须、肯定推测 | You **must** scan the code. 你必须扫码。 |
| **shall** | 提议、征询、正式规定 | **Shall** I open the window? 要我开窗吗？ |
| **should** | 应该、建议、预期 | You **should** bring an umbrella. 你应该带伞。 |
| **will** | 将来、意愿、习惯 | I **will** call you when I arrive. 到了我给你打电话。 |
| **would** | 委婉请求、过去习惯、虚拟 | **Would** you like some tea? 要来点茶吗？ |

## 5. 核心情态动词的更多用法 More Uses of Core Modals

| 情态动词 | 更多用法 | 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **can** | 一般性/理论上的可能 | It **can** be very cold here in winter. | 这里冬天可能很冷。 |
| **could** | 现在/将来的不确定推测 | It **could** rain later. | 晚些时候可能会下雨。 |
| **may** | 正式许可 | **May** I come in? | 我可以进来吗？ |
| **may** | 祝愿 | **May** you succeed! | 祝你成功！ |
| **might** | 更委婉的请求 | **Might** I ask a question? | 我能问个问题吗？ |
| **must** | 过去「必须」用 had to | I **had to** pay before 10 pm. | 我不得不十点前付款。 |
| **shall** | 提议 / 征询 | **Shall** we take the metro? | 我们坐地铁吧？ |
| **should** | 预期 / 推测 | The train **should** arrive soon. | 火车应该快到了。 |
| **will** | 习惯 / 客观规律 | Water **will** boil at 100°C. | 水在 100°C 会沸腾。 |
| **would** | 过去习惯动作 | We **would** play chess every evening. | 我们以前每晚都下棋。 |
| **would** | 虚拟语气 | If I were you, I **would** tell the truth. | 如果我是你，我会说实话。 |

### shall 提议、征询与正式规定

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| **Shall** I open the window? | 要我开窗吗？ |
| **Shall** we split the bill? | 我们 AA 平分账单吧？ |
| You **shall** have the money by Friday. | 你周五之前会拿到钱。（承诺，正式） |
| The tenant **shall** pay rent monthly. | 租户应按月支付租金。（条文） |

### ought to 应该（稍正式）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **ought to** be more careful. | 你应该更小心。 |
| The bus **ought to** come soon. | 公交应该很快就来。 |
| You **ought to** have apologized. | 你本应该道歉的。 |

## 6. 半情态动词 Semi-Modals

### have to / have got to 客观必须（外部规定）

have to 强调客观必须（规则、规定、现实），有各种时态；must 强调说话人主观认为必须。

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **had to** take the last metro home. | 我昨晚不得不赶末班地铁回家。 |
| You **will have to** pay before 10 pm. | 你十点前必须付款。 |
| I've **got to** leave now. | 我得走了。（口语） |
| You **don't have to** tip in China. | 在中国你不必给小费。（不必，允许不给） |
| You **mustn't** smoke here. | 这里禁止吸烟。（禁止，绝不能） |

### be able to 能够（补充 can 的时态）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| She **is able to** work from home. | 她能在家办公。 |
| I **was able to** fix my phone. | 我成功修好了手机。（一次成功） |
| I **will be able to** speak more English next year. | 明年我就能说更多英语了。 |
| I **have been able to** save money since March. | 从三月起我一直在省钱。 |

过去某一次成功做到某事用 was / were able to 或 managed to，不用 could。

### be going to 打算；有迹象的预测

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I'm **going to** visit my grandma. | 我打算去看奶奶。 |
| Look at the clouds! It's **going to** rain. | 看那些云！要下雨了。 |
| He is **going to** miss the metro. | 他要赶不上地铁了。 |

### used to 过去常常（现在不再这样）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **used to** take the bus to school. | 我以前坐公交上学。 |
| She **didn't use to** drink coffee. | 她以前不喝咖啡。 |
| **Did** you **use to** play the piano? | 你以前弹钢琴吗？ |
| I **used to** live near the metro. | 我以前住在地铁站附近。（状态） |

### had better 最好（强烈建议，常隐含警告）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You'd **better** bring an umbrella. | 你最好带把伞。 |
| You'd **better not** be late. | 你最好不要迟到。 |
| We'd **better** scan the code now. | 我们最好现在扫码。 |

### need 需要（情态用法，多用于否定和疑问）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **needn't** worry. | 你不必担心。 |
| **Need** I come? | 我需要来吗？ |
| You **needn't have** bought so much. | 你本不必买这么多。（但买了） |

### dare 敢于（多用于否定和疑问）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **daren't** ask. | 我不敢问。 |
| How **dare** you say that! | 你竟敢这么说！ |
| She doesn't **dare to** tell him. | 她不敢告诉他。 |

### would rather 宁愿

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I'd **rather** stay home than go out. | 我宁愿待在家也不愿出去。 |
| I'd **rather not** talk about it. | 我宁愿不谈这个。 |
| I'd **rather** you told me the truth. | 我更希望你对我说实话。 |

### be supposed to 应该（按规定、预期；据说）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You're **supposed to** wear a seatbelt. | 你应该系安全带。 |
| You're not **supposed to** smoke here. | 这里不允许抽烟。 |
| He is **supposed to** be very rich. | 据说他很有钱。 |

## 7. 情态动词 + have done（对过去的推测、遗憾与批评）

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **must have** done | 一定做过（肯定推测） | She **must have left**. | 她一定已经走了。 |
| **can't / couldn't have** done | 不可能做过 | He **can't have seen** us. | 他不可能看见我们。 |
| **may / might have** done | 可能做过 | They **may have forgotten**. | 他们可能忘了。 |
| **could have** done | 本可以做（但没做） | I **could have helped** you. | 我本可以帮你的。 |
| **should / ought to have** done | 本应该做（但没做） | You **should have called** me. | 你本应该给我打电话。 |
| **shouldn't / oughtn't to have** done | 本不该做（却做了） | You **shouldn't have said** that. | 你本不该说那种话。 |
| **needn't have** done | 本不必做（却做了） | We **needn't have booked**. | 我们本不必订位的。 |
| **would have** done | 虚拟，与过去事实相反 | I **would have come** if I'd known. | 如果我知道，我就会来。 |

## 8. 推测程度总结 Deduction Summary

| 确定程度 | 现在/将来 | 对过去 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 一定（95%） | **must** | **must have** done | You **must** be tired. 你一定很累。 |
| 应该（80%） | **should / ought to** | **should have** done | The train **should** arrive soon. 火车应该快到了。 |
| 可能（70%） | **may** | **may have** done | He **may** be at home. 他可能在家。 |
| 也许（50-60%） | **might / could** | **might / could have** done | It **might** rain. 可能会下雨。 |
| 不可能 | **can't / couldn't** | **can't / couldn't have** done | That **can't** be true. 那不可能是真的。 |

## 9. 易混辨析 Common Confusions

| 对比 | 区别 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| must vs have to | must 主观；have to 客观。mustn't 禁止；don't have to 不必 | I **must** go. 我必须走。 / I **have to** wear a uniform. 我得穿制服。 / You **mustn't** smoke. 禁止吸烟。 / You **don't have to** tip. 不必给小费。 |
| can vs may | 表许可 can 口语、may 正式；表能力只能用 can | **Can** I sit here? / **May** I come in? |
| could vs was able to | could 一般能力；was able to 具体一次成功 | I **could** run 5 km. / I **was able to** finish the report. 我成功写完了报告。 |
| used to vs would | 都表过去习惯；状态只能用 used to | I **used to** live near the metro. / We **would** play chess every evening. |
| should vs had better | should 建议；had better 更强烈、含警告 | You **should** see a doctor. / You'd **better** hurry. 你最好快点。 |
| will vs be going to | will 临时决定/单纯预测；be going to 事先计划/有迹象 | I **will** help you. 我来帮你。 / I'm **going to** visit my grandma. / It's **going to** rain. |
| may not vs mustn't | may not 可能不/不允许；mustn't 禁止 | He **may not** be home. 他可能不在家。 / You **mustn't** smoke here. 禁止吸烟。 |

## 10. 否定形式与疑问回答 Negative Forms & Short Answers

| 情态动词 | 否定 | 常见缩略 |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | cannot | **can't** |
| **could** | could not | **couldn't** |
| **may** | may not | （很少缩写） |
| **might** | might not | **mightn't** |
| **must** | must not | **mustn't** |
| **shall** | shall not | **shan't** |
| **should** | should not | **shouldn't** |
| **will** | will not | **won't** |
| **would** | would not | **wouldn't** |
| **ought to** | ought not to | **oughtn't to** |
| **need** | need not | **needn't** |
| **dare** | dare not | **daren't** |
| **had better** | had better not | （无常见缩略） |

| 问句 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|---|---|---|
| Can I sit here? | Yes, you **can**. | No, you **can't**. |
| May I come in? | Yes, you **may**. | No, you **may not**. |
| Must I finish it now? | Yes, you **must**. | No, you **needn't** / **don't have to**. |
| Need I pay now? | Yes, you **must**. | No, you **needn't**. |

## 11. Discussion 讨论（2026 · 中国 · 成人生活）

每个问题都围绕 2026 年中国成年人的真实生活场景，回答时尽量用情态动词；答案 tags 只是提示，说出自己的真实想法更好。

### 基础题 Basic

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| You scan a QR code to pay, but the payment fails. What **should** you do? | 扫码付款失败了，你该怎么办？ | **Should** check the network / **Can** try Alipay or WeChat Pay / **Had better** ask the shop |
| Your boss asks you to use AI for a report. What **can** you do with DeepSeek or 豆包? | 老板让你用 AI 写报告，你能用 DeepSeek 或豆包做什么？ | **Can** ask it to draft / **Must** check the facts / **Shouldn't** copy it blindly |
| Self-driving taxis (萝卜快跑) now run in some Chinese cities. **Would** you take one alone at night? | 萝卜快跑已经出现在一些中国城市，你会在深夜一个人坐吗？ | Yes, I **would** / I **might**, but only with a friend / No, I **wouldn't** |
| The retirement age is rising. What **must** people in their 30s do to prepare? | 退休年龄在推迟，三十多岁的人必须做什么准备？ | **Must** save money / **Should** stay healthy / **Had better** learn new skills |
| Delivery drones **may** arrive in your neighborhood this year. What **could** go wrong? | 今年无人机送外卖可能到你家附近，可能出什么问题？ | They **might** be noisy / They **could** drop packages / They **must** be safe first |
| A concert ticket goes on sale at 10 am. What **had you better** do first? | 演唱会门票上午十点开抢，你最好先做什么？ | **Had better** test the app / **Should** prepare my ID / **Can** join the presale |
| Your friend always buys the newest phone. What **should** you say to them? | 你朋友总买最新款手机，你应该说什么？ | You **don't have to** upgrade / You **should** compare prices / It **may** drop in price later |
| Your electric car is low on battery during a holiday road trip. What **mustn't** you do? | 假期自驾电动车快没电了，什么绝对不能做？ | **Mustn't** wait until it dies / **Should** plan charging stops / **Had better** use a charging app |
| The 2026 World Cup games are in North America. **Would** you stay up to watch? | 2026 世界杯在北美举行，你会熬夜看球吗？ | I **might** watch the final / I **shouldn't** stay up too late / I **would** if my team plays |
| Your parents live alone and are getting older. What **should** you do for them? | 父母年纪大了独自居住，你应该为他们做什么？ | **Should** call them more / **Could** visit every month / **Had better** check their health app |
| Hospitals now use 电子医保. What **don't you have to** do anymore? | 医院现在用电子医保，哪些事你不再需要做了？ | **Don't have to** carry the card / **Don't have to** queue for paper forms / **Can** use my phone |
| Your colleague works overtime every night. What **would you rather** say to them? | 同事天天加班，你宁愿对他说什么？ | I'd **rather** say: rest first / You **shouldn't** work every night / You **mustn't** forget your health |
| House prices are uncertain in your city. **Would** you rent or buy? | 你所在城市房价还不确定，你会租房还是买房？ | I'd **rather** rent / I **would** buy if I **could** / I **might** wait a year |

### 进阶题 Advanced

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| You arrive at the station, but the train has already left. What **should you have** done? | 你到车站时火车已经开走了，你本应该怎么做？ | **Should have** booked an earlier ticket / **Shouldn't have** slept in / **Could have** left home earlier |
