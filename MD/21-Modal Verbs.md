# Modal Verbs · 情态动词

## 1. 最常见用法 Common Uses

### can 能 / 会（能力、许可）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **can** scan the code to pay. | 我能扫码付款。 |
| **Can** I sit here? | 我能坐这儿吗？ |
| I **can't** hear you — the subway is too loud. | 我听不清——地铁太吵了。 |
| I **can** ride a shared bike, but I can't drive. | 我会骑共享单车，不会开车。 |

### could 可以（更礼貌；过去的能力）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| **Could** you say that again? | 你能再说一遍吗？ |
| **Could** I use your charger? | 我能借你的充电器吗？ |
| When I was in school, I **could** run 5 km. | 上学时我能跑五公里。 |

### should 应该（建议、责任）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **should** bring an umbrella — it's going to rain. | 你应该带把伞，要下雨了。 |
| I **should** reply to this message first. | 我应该先回这条消息。 |
| You **shouldn't** work overtime every day. | 你不该天天加班。 |
| We **should** split the bill evenly. | 我们应该 AA 平分账单。 |

### must / have to 必须（强义务）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **have to** scan the code before you get on. | 上车前必须扫码。 |
| I **have to** take the last subway home. | 我得赶末班地铁回家。 |
| I **must** pay the bill before 10 p.m. | 我必须在晚上十点前付款。 |
| You **don't have to** tip in China. | 在中国你不用给小费。 |
| You **mustn't** smoke here. | 这里禁止吸烟。 |

### will / would 将会 / 愿意

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **will** call you when I arrive. | 到了我给你打电话。 |
| **Would** you like some tea? | 要来点茶吗？ |
| **Would** you mind waiting a minute? | 你介意等一分钟吗？ |

## 2. 可能性推测 Possibility & Deduction

### may / might / could 可能（不确定）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| It **may** rain this afternoon. | 下午可能下雨。 |
| He **might** be stuck in traffic. | 他可能堵在路上了。 |
| The delivery **could** arrive late. | 外卖可能会晚到。 |
| She **might not** see the message. | 她可能没看到消息。 |
| Ordering takeout on 美团 **may** be cheaper. | 美团点外卖可能更便宜。 |

### must 一定（把握很大的肯定推测）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You've worked all day — you **must** be tired. | 你忙了一整天，一定很累。 |
| The line is so long — the food **must** be good. | 排这么长的队，一定好吃。 |
| Your phone is off — you **must** be out of battery. | 你关机了，一定没电了。 |

### can't 不可能（把握很大的否定推测）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| That **can't** be true — I just saw him. | 那不可能是真的，我刚见过他。 |
| It **can't** be 9 p.m. already. | 不可能已经晚上九点了。 |
| She **can't** be at home — the light is off. | 她不可能在家，灯都灭了。 |

| 推测词 | 确定程度 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **might** | 可能 50% | He **might** be stuck in traffic. 他可能堵在路上了。 |
| **could** | 也许 60% | The delivery **could** arrive late. 外卖可能会晚到。 |
| **may** | 可能 70% | It **may** rain this afternoon. 下午可能下雨。 |
| **must** | 一定 95% | You **must** be tired. 你一定很累。 |
| **can't** | 不可能 | That **can't** be true. 那不可能是真的。 |

## 3. Discussion 讨论

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| Your QR payment fails. What **should** you do? | 扫码付款失败怎么办？ | Try WeChat Pay / Check the Wi-Fi / Ask the staff |
| Your boss wants an AI report. What **can** AI do? | 老板要 AI 写报告，AI 能做什么？ | Draft it / Check the facts / Don't copy it blindly |
| **Would** you take a 萝卜快跑 taxi alone at night? | 你敢深夜独自坐萝卜快跑吗？ | Yes, I **would** / I **might** with a friend |
| The retirement age is rising. What **must** you do now? | 退休年龄推迟，你必须做什么？ | Save money / Stay healthy / Learn new skills |
| Delivery drones **may** come soon. What **could** go wrong? | 无人机送外卖，可能出什么问题？ | They **might** be noisy / They **could** drop it |
| Tickets go on sale at 10 a.m. What **had you better** do? | 门票十点开抢，你最好先做什么？ | **Had better** test the app / **Should** add my ID |
| Your friend buys every new phone. What **should** you say? | 朋友总买最新款手机，你该说什么？ | You **don't have to** upgrade / Compare prices |
| Your EV is low on battery. What **mustn't** you do? | 电动车快没电了，绝对不能做什么？ | **Mustn't** wait / Plan charging stops / Use an app |
| The 2026 World Cup is in North America. **Would** you? | 世界杯在北美，你会熬夜看吗？ | I **might** watch the final / Not every game |
| Your parents live alone. What **should** you do for them? | 父母独自居住，你该为他们做什么？ | Call more / Visit monthly / Check their health app |
| Hospitals now take 电子医保. What **don't you have to** do? | 用电子医保后，哪些事不用做了？ | Carry the card / Fill out paper forms |
| Your colleague works every night. What **would you rather** say? | 同事天天加班，你宁愿说什么？ | Rest first / **Shouldn't** work every night |
| House prices are uncertain. **Would** you rent or buy? | 房价不确定，你会租房还是买房？ | I'd **rather** rent / I **would** buy if I **could** |
