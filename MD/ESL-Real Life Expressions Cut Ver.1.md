# Part 1 问候与寒暄

> 标注说明：〔口语〕朋友间非正式说法；〔正式〕工作或正式场合；〔慎用〕语气容易伤人或引起误会，注意使用场合。

1.  初次见面

    1.  Nice to meet you!

    2.  It's a pleasure to meet you!

    3.  I'm pleased to finally meet you in person.

    4.  Good to meet you.

    5.  It's an honor to meet you.

2.  你来啦

    1.  There you are!（原来你在这/可算找到你了）

    2.  I'm glad you came.

    3.  We've been waiting for you.

    4.  We've been expecting you.

    5.  You came!

3.  好久不见

    1.  It's been a long time.

    2.  It's been a while.

    3.  Long time no see.

4.  询问近况

    1.  What's up?

    2.  How's it going?

    3.  How are you doing?

    4.  What's new?

    5.  What have you been up to?

5.  我很好

    1.  I'm fine.

    2.  I'm okay.

    3.  I'm great.

    4.  I'm good.（最常用）

    5.  Not bad.

6.  一般，不太好

    1.  Could be better.（一般般，还能更好）

    2.  Not so great.（不太好）

    3.  Nothing special.（没什么特别的）

    4.  Just getting by.（勉强过得去）

    5.  Can't complain.（还行，没什么可抱怨的）

7.  你看起来不错

    1.  You look great.

    2.  You look pretty good.

    3.  You look just fine.

    4.  You look well.

8.  道别

    1.  Bye

    2.  See you later.

    3.  I'll miss you.

    4.  Take care.

    5.  See you around.

# Part 2 礼貌用语

1.  感谢

    1.  Thank you for coming.

    2.  Thanks for your advice.

    3.  I appreciate your cooperation.〔正式〕

    4.  I owe you one.（我欠你个人情）

2.  不客气

    1.  You're welcome.

    2.  No problem.

    3.  My pleasure.

    4.  Don't mention it.

    5.  It's okay.

3.  对不起

    1.  I'm sorry I'm late.

    2.  It's my fault.

    3.  I apologize for the inconvenience.〔正式〕

    4.  I was so stupid.（自我责怪时用）

    5.  I was out of my mind.（意思是"我当时昏了头"，解释自己失控时用）

    6.  I didn't mean to.（我不是故意的）

    7.  My apologies.

4.  没关系

    1.  Forget it.（非正式；也可表示"算了"）

    2.  It's okay.

    3.  Never mind.

    4.  That's all right.

    5.  Don't worry about it.

    6.  Apology accepted.

5.  不好意思

    1.  Excuse me,

    2.  May I?

    3.  Sorry to bother you.

# Part 3 表达感受与状态

1.  高兴

    1.  **I'm** so/really/very **glad** you came.

    2.  **I'm happy to** hear that.

    3.  I feel great.

    4.  **I'm** very **pleased** to be working with you.

    5.  You made my day.

2.  兴奋

    1.  I'm so excited.

    2.  **I can't wait to** see you.

    3.  **I'm looking forward to** working with you.

    4.  **I'm** very **excited to** hear about your trip.

3.  生气

    1.  **I'm mad at** my boyfriend.

    2.  **I'm** a little bit **upset with** him.

    3.  **I'm angry at** him for breaking his promise.

4.  厌恶不耐烦

    1.  That's it.（够了！也可以表示"就这些/说完了"，见 Part 7）

    2.  That's enough.

    3.  Give me a break.

5.  苦恼怎么办

    1.  What should I do?

    2.  I don't know what to do.

    3.  What am I supposed to do?

6.  惊呆

    1.  **I can't believe** you did that.

    2.  **I'm shocked that** you don't eat spicy food.

7.  羡慕

    1.  That's so nice.

    2.  Lucky you.

    3.  You're so lucky.

    4.  I envy you.

8.  厌倦

    1.  **I'm tired of** staying at home all day.

    2.  **I'm sick of** the traffic every morning.

    3.  **I don't want to** hear your excuses anymore.

    4.  **I'm bored with** staying in the office every day.（口语里也有人用 bored of，教学中建议用 bored with）

9.  紧张、担心等情绪

    1.  I'm scared of the interview tomorrow.

    2.  I'm worried about the flu.

    3.  I'm nervous about speaking in front of everyone.

    4.  I'm anxious about the results of my checkup.

10. 繁忙

    1.  I'm busy.

    2.  I'm occupied with...（〔正式〕）

    3.  **I'm tied up with** work.（被工作缠住，脱不开身）

    4.  **I'm in the middle of** something.

# Part 4 表达想法与偏好

1.  吃的好吃

    1.  It's good.

    2.  It's delicious.

    3.  It's tasty.

    4.  It tastes good.

2.  我认为

    1.  I think...

    2.  I guess...（没什么自信的猜想）

    3.  I'm afraid...（恐怕，后接否定居多，如 I'm afraid I can't.）

    4.  In my opinion, ...

3.  喜欢热爱

    1.  I really like...

    2.  I like ... a lot. / I like ... very much.

    3.  I'm crazy about...（对…着迷）

    4.  I can't live without...（离不开…，夸张说法）

    5.  I'm into...（对…感兴趣，〔口语〕）

4.  不喜欢不擅长

    1.  **I don't like** the weather.

    2.  **I really don't like ...**

    3.  **I don't really like ...**

    4.  **I can't handle** alcohol.（handle = 受得了/应付得了）

    5.  I'm not good at ...（如 I'm not good at math.）

    6.  Basketball **is not my thing**.

    7.  I'm bad at ...（如 I'm bad at cooking.）

5.  打算

    1.  **I want to** try that.

    2.  **I'm going to** study abroad.

    3.  **I will** go to Tianjin this Saturday.

6.  做选择

    1.  **I'd rather** eat at home.

    2.  **I prefer** the window seat.

    3.  Taking a bus **is better than** taking a taxi.

    4.  I like tea **better than** coffee.

7.  不知为何

    1.  **Somehow**, I just know it.

    2.  **I don't know why, but** I feel so sleepy today.

    3.  **For some reason**, I'm a good judge of people.

    4.  **I just feel like** going out today.

    5.  Just because.（不为什么）

# Part 5 请求、建议与关心（对别人说）

1.  表达请求

    1.  **May I** have some privacy, **please**?

    2.  **Can you** give that to me?

    3.  **Would you mind if** I sit here?（更正式：Would you mind if I sat here?）

    4.  I'd like to open the window if that's OK with you.

    5.  Would it be OK if ...?

2.  请求许可

    1.  **Can/Could/May I** try this on?

    2.  Do/Would you mind if ...?（注意：回答 No 表示"不介意，请便"）

3.  表示建议

    1.  How about...? / What about...?（How about + 名词/动名词）

    2.  **Why not** stay here?

    3.  I think maybe you should leave him.

4.  提醒别人不要做...

    1.  You **can't** take pictures here.

    2.  You shouldn't stay up late.

    3.  **You'd better not** eat too much.

5.  询问计划

    1.  **What are you doing** this coming holiday?

    2.  **Are you free** for lunch tomorrow?

    3.  Are you busy?

    4.  Do you have any plans for ...?（问长假等较长的时间）

6.  邀约

    1.  **Let's** go to that new restaurant.

    2.  **Do you want to** go swimming?

    3.  **We should** totally go there.（totally 是口语加强词，很随意）

    4.  Would you like to...（较正式、客气）

7.  表示关心

    1.  What's wrong?

    2.  What's wrong with you?〔慎用〕（常带指责语气；关心别人用 What's wrong? / Are you OK?）

    3.  Are you OK?

    4.  What's the matter?

    5.  Is everything OK?

    6.  What's with him?（他怎么回事？）

8.  加油打气

    1.  **Good luck with** your exam!

    2.  You can do it!

    3.  Keep it up!

    4.  Keep up the good work!

9.  别紧张

    1.  Relax!

    2.  Calm down!

    3.  Take it easy!

    4.  Don't be nervous!

# Part 6 回应与互动（对别人的话作出反应）

1.  赞成

    1.  I agree.

    2.  I'm with you.

    3.  I couldn't agree more.（再同意不过了）

    4.  I think so, too.

    5.  I'm all for it.（我完全赞成）

2.  反对

    1.  I don't think so.

    2.  I disagree.

    3.  **I'm not so sure about** that.

3.  也许

    1.  Maybe/Perhaps.（50%）

    2.  Could be.（觉得有可能但不大）

    3.  Who knows?（谁知道呢）

4.  拒绝

    1.  Sorry, I can't.

    2.  Maybe next time.

    3.  I think I'll pass.（我就不去了）

    4.  **I don't think** it's a good idea.

5.  表示惊讶（真的吗？）

    1.  Really?

    2.  Are you kidding me?

    3.  What?

    4.  Are you sure?

    5.  Are you serious?

    6.  No way!〔口语〕

    7.  That's impossible!

6.  不出所料

    1.  I told you so.（我早说过了）

    2.  I knew it!（我就知道！）

    3.  I thought so, too.（我也这么觉得）

7.  表示同情遗憾

    1.  I'm sorry about that.

    2.  I don't know what to say.

    3.  That's too bad.

    4.  I know how you feel.

8.  祝贺好消息

    1.  That's great!

    2.  That sounds great/awesome!

    3.  You did it.

    4.  You made it.

    5.  You nailed it.〔口语〕（干得漂亮）

    6.  Good/Great job!

    7.  Well done!

    8.  I'm so happy for you!

9.  你说得对

    1.  You're right.

    2.  I get it.

    3.  I see.

    4.  I understand.

    5.  That makes sense.

10. 表示感同身受

    1.  I know.

    2.  I know what you mean.

    3.  Same here.

    4.  That happened to me, too.

    5.  I was there.（我当时也在场/我也经历过）

11. 然后呢？

    1.  Oh yeah?

    2.  So what?〔慎用〕（带挑衅/不耐烦的语气）

    3.  What else?

    4.  Then what?

    5.  And?（口语，催促对方继续说）

12. 确认？

    1.  The store opens at 10, right?

    2.  Don't forget to bring the key, okay?

    3.  You work here, don't you?

    4.  We met last week, remember?

13. 不想被干涉

    1.  Leave me alone!

    2.  Go away.

    3.  None of your business.〔慎用〕（比较冲）

    4.  Stay away from me!

    5.  I don't wanna hear it.（wanna = want to，〔口语〕）

# Part 7 对话衔接与实用短句

1.  嗯...（思考/拖延）

    1.  Well...I'll try my best.

    2.  Um...What do you mean?

    3.  Let me see...

2.  过渡语（不知道如何回应的时候）

    - Well...

    - The thing is...

    - Let's see

    - Let me see/think

    - Like...〔口语〕

    - It's a kind of...

    - I mean

    - You know

3.  常用短句

    1.  Do you have a minute?

    2.  I need your help.

    3.  Who is this?（电话问是谁）

    4.  That's it.（说完了/就这些；注意也可表示"够了"，见 Part 3）

    5.  There you go.（给你 / 这就对了，看语境）

        - Here's your coffee. There you go!（给你）

        - There you go! You did it!（这就对了）

    6.  What do you recommend?

    7.  Got it?

    8.  How was your weekend/holiday/exam/interview?

    9.  How long is this going to take?

    10. How do I get to ...?（如 How do I get to the station?）

    11. Not much. / Nothing much.
