# Part 1 问候与寒暄

1.  初次见面

    1.  Nice to meet you!
        例：Nice to meet you! I'm Lin, the new designer.（很高兴认识你！我是新来的设计师小琳。）

    2.  It's a pleasure to meet you!

    3.  I'm pleased to finally meet you in person.
        例：I've heard so much about you — I'm pleased to finally meet you in person.（久仰大名，终于见到本人了。）

    4.  Good to meet you.

    5.  It's an honor to meet you.

2.  你来啦

    1.  There you are!（原来你在这/可算找到你了）
        例：There you are! We've been waiting for 20 minutes.（可算来了！我们等了 20 分钟了。）

    2.  I'm glad you came.
        例：I'm glad you came — the hotpot's already ordered.（你能来太好了，火锅都点好了。）

    3.  We've been waiting for you.

    4.  We've been expecting you.

    5.  You came!

3.  好久不见

    1.  It's been a long time.

    2.  It's been a while.

    3.  Long time no see.
        例：Hey, long time no see! How's work going?（嘿，好久不见！工作怎么样？）

4.  询问近况

    1.  What's up?
        例：What's up?——通常回 Not much. 或 What's up?，不需要真的汇报近况。

    2.  How's it going?

    3.  How are you doing?

    4.  What's new?

    5.  What have you been up to?
        例：What have you been up to? I haven't seen you since last month.（最近在忙什么？上个月以后就没见过你。）

5.  我很好

    1.  I'm fine.

    2.  I'm okay.

    3.  I'm great.

    4.  I'm good.（最常用）
        例：I'm good — just finished a big project.（挺好的，刚完成一个大项目。）

    5.  Not bad.

6.  一般，不太好

    1.  Could be better.（一般般，还能更好）
        例：Could be better — my laptop broke this morning.（一般般吧，今早电脑坏了。）

    2.  Not so great.（不太好）

    3.  Nothing special.（没什么特别的）

    4.  Just getting by.（勉强过得去）
        例：Just getting by, you know, rent is high.（勉强撑着，你知道的，房租不低。）

    5.  Can't complain.（还行，没什么可抱怨的）

7.  你看起来不错

    1.  You look great.
        例：You look great! New haircut?（你气色真好！剪头发了？）

    2.  You look pretty good.

    3.  You look just fine.

    4.  You look well.

8.  道别

    1.  Bye

    2.  See you later.

    3.  I'll miss you.

    4.  Take care.
        例：Take care! Text me when you get home.（路上小心！到家给我发消息。）

    5.  See you around.

9.  情景对话

    1.  A: Hey! Long time no see!（嘿！好久不见！）

    2.  B: Yeah! How's it going?（是啊！最近怎么样？）

    3.  A: Pretty good. You look great!（挺好的，你气色真好！）

    4.  B: Thanks! I just started a new job.（谢谢！我刚换了个新工作。）

    5.  A: Nice! We should grab a coffee sometime.（不错呀！改天一起喝杯咖啡。）

    6.  B: Sure! See you around!（好呀！回头见！）

> 误区：问"在吗？"不要直译 Are you there?——英语里直接说 Hey, are you free? 或 Hello。
> 提醒：What's up? 只是打招呼，不是真问"怎么了"，回 Not much. 或 What's up? 都可以。

# Part 2 礼貌用语

1.  感谢

    1.  Thank you for coming.
        例：Thank you for coming to my birthday party.（谢谢你赶来参加我的生日聚会。）

    2.  Thanks for your advice.

    3.  I appreciate your cooperation.〔正式〕

    4.  I owe you one.（我欠你个人情）
        例：I owe you one — thanks for covering my shift yesterday.（我欠你个人情，谢谢你昨天帮我顶班。）

2.  不客气

    1.  You're welcome.

    2.  No problem.
        例：A: Thanks for the ride! B: No problem, see you tomorrow.（谢谢你捎我一程！——不客气，明天见。）

    3.  My pleasure.
        例：My pleasure — happy to help with your resume.（不客气，帮你改简历我很乐意。）

    4.  Don't mention it.

    5.  It's okay.

3.  对不起

    1.  I'm sorry I'm late.
        例：I'm sorry I'm late — the subway was delayed again.（对不起我迟到了，地铁又晚点了。）

    2.  It's my fault.

    3.  I apologize for the inconvenience.〔正式〕

    4.  I was so stupid.（自我责怪时用）

    5.  I was out of my mind.（意思是"我当时昏了头"，解释自己失控时用）

    6.  I didn't mean to.（我不是故意的）
        例：I didn't mean to ignore your message — my phone died.（我不是故意不回你消息，手机没电了。）

    7.  My apologies.

4.  没关系

    1.  Forget it.（非正式；也可表示"算了"）

    2.  It's okay.

    3.  Never mind.

    4.  That's all right.

    5.  Don't worry about it.
        例：A: Sorry about the noise last night. B: Don't worry about it.（昨晚有点吵，抱歉。——没事。）

    6.  Apology accepted.
        例：Apology accepted. Let's just move on.（我接受道歉，翻篇吧。）

5.  不好意思

    1.  Excuse me,

    2.  May I?

    3.  Sorry to bother you.
        例：Sorry to bother you, but could I borrow your charger?（不好意思打扰你，能借下充电器吗？）

6.  情景对话

    1.  A: Sorry I'm late — traffic was terrible.（对不起我迟到了，路上堵得厉害。）

    2.  B: Don't worry about it. I just got here too.（没事，我也刚到。）

    3.  A: Thanks for being understanding. I owe you one.（谢谢你这么通情达理，我欠你个人情。）

    4.  B: No problem. Let's order!（不客气，点菜吧！）

> 误区："不用谢"别推辞——英语里直接说 No problem. / My pleasure. 就好，不需要说 It's nothing。
> 提醒：I appreciate your cooperation. 适合正式场合（邮件、会议），朋友之间用会显得生硬。

# Part 3 表达感受与状态

1.  高兴

    1.  **I'm** so/really/very **glad** you came.
        例：I'm so glad you came with me to the interview — I got the offer!（幸好你陪我去面试——我拿到 offer 了！）

    2.  **I'm happy to** hear that.

    3.  I feel great.

    4.  **I'm** very **pleased** to be working with you.

    5.  You made my day.

2.  兴奋

    1.  I'm so excited.

    2.  **I can't wait to** see you.
        例：I can't wait to see the new apartment tomorrow!（我等不及明天去看新房了！）

    3.  **I'm looking forward to** working with you.

    4.  **I'm** very **excited to** hear about your trip.

3.  生气

    1.  **I'm mad at** my boyfriend.
        例：I'm mad at my roommate for eating my takeout again.（室友又吃我外卖，我气死了。）

    2.  **I'm** a little bit **upset with** him.

    3.  **I'm angry at** him for breaking his promise.

4.  厌恶不耐烦

    1.  That's it.（够了！也可以表示"就这些/说完了"，见 Part 7）
        例：That's it! I'm not waiting for the elevator — I'll take the stairs.（够了！我不等电梯了，走楼梯。）

    2.  That's enough.

    3.  Give me a break.

5.  苦恼怎么办

    1.  What should I do?
        例：What should I do? I locked myself out again.（怎么办？我又把自己锁门外了。）

    2.  I don't know what to do.

    3.  What am I supposed to do?

6.  惊呆

    1.  **I can't believe** you did that.
        例：I can't believe you got the last ticket to the concert!（我不敢相信你抢到了演唱会最后一张票！）

    2.  **I'm shocked that** you don't eat spicy food.

7.  羡慕

    1.  That's so nice.

    2.  Lucky you.

    3.  You're so lucky.
        例：You're so lucky — your company has Friday off!（你太幸运了，你们公司周五放假！）

    4.  I envy you.

8.  厌倦

    1.  **I'm tired of** staying at home all day.

    2.  **I'm sick of** the traffic every morning.
        例：I'm sick of eating the same delivery food every day.（我受够天天吃同一家外卖了。）

    3.  **I don't want to** hear your excuses anymore.

    4.  **I'm bored with** staying in the office every day.（口语里也有人用 bored of，教学中建议用 bored with）

9.  紧张、担心等情绪

    1.  I'm scared of the interview tomorrow.

    2.  I'm worried about the flu.

    3.  I'm nervous about speaking in front of everyone.
        例：I'm nervous about the interview tomorrow — I'll practice my answers tonight.（明天面试我好紧张，今晚练练回答。）

    4.  I'm anxious about the results of my checkup.

10. 繁忙

    1.  I'm busy.

    2.  I'm occupied with...（〔正式〕）

    3.  **I'm tied up with** work.（被工作缠住，脱不开身）

    4.  **I'm in the middle of** something.
        例：I'm in the middle of something — can I call you back in 10 minutes?（我正忙着，10 分钟后回你电话行吗？）

11. 情景对话

    1.  A: You look tired. What's up?（你看起来很累，怎么了？）

    2.  B: I'm sick of working overtime every day.（我受够天天加班了。）

    3.  A: That's rough. You should take a break this weekend.（那挺辛苦的，这周末该歇歇。）

    4.  B: I know — I can't wait for Saturday!（我知道，我已经等不及周六了！）

> 提醒：I'm bored 是"我觉得无聊"，I'm boring 是"我这人很无趣"，别用错。
> 提醒：担心或紧张时，用 I'm worried about / nervous about + 具体的事，
> 比只说 I'm nervous 更自然。

# Part 4 表达想法与偏好

1.  吃的好吃

    1.  It's good.

    2.  It's delicious.
        例：This is delicious! What's in the sauce?（太好吃了！酱里放了什么？）

    3.  It's tasty.

    4.  It tastes good.

2.  我认为

    1.  I think...
        例：I think we should take the metro — it's faster than driving.（我觉得坐地铁，比开车快。）

    2.  I guess...（没什么自信的猜想）
        例：I guess it'll be fine, but let's check the weather first.（我觉得应该没事，不过先查下天气。）

    3.  I'm afraid...（恐怕，后接否定居多，如 I'm afraid I can't.）

    4.  In my opinion, ...

3.  喜欢热爱

    1.  I really like...

    2.  I like ... a lot. / I like ... very much.

    3.  I'm crazy about...（对…着迷）
        例：I'm crazy about the new noodle shop near our office.（我超爱公司附近那家新面馆。）

    4.  I can't live without...（离不开…，夸张说法）

    5.  I'm into...（对…感兴趣，〔口语〕）

4.  不喜欢不擅长

    1.  **I don't like** the weather.

    2.  **I really don't like ...**

    3.  **I don't really like ...**

    4.  **I can't handle** alcohol.（handle = 受得了/应付得了）
        例：I can't handle spicy food — my mouth is on fire.（我吃不了辣，嘴都快冒火了。）

    5.  I'm not good at ...（如 I'm not good at math.）
        例：I'm not good at math, so I always use an app to split the bill.（我数学不好，所以分摊账单老用 App。）

    6.  Basketball **is not my thing**.

    7.  I'm bad at ...（如 I'm bad at cooking.）

5.  打算

    1.  **I want to** try that.

    2.  **I'm going to** study abroad.
        例：I'm going to save up for a trip to Yunnan this winter.（我打算今年冬天攒钱去云南玩。）

    3.  **I will** go to Tianjin this Saturday.

6.  做选择

    1.  **I'd rather** eat at home.
        例：I'd rather eat at home than order takeout — it's cheaper.（我宁愿在家吃也不点外卖，更省钱。）

    2.  **I prefer** the window seat.

    3.  Taking a bus **is better than** taking a taxi.

    4.  I like tea **better than** coffee.

7.  不知为何

    1.  **Somehow**, I just know it.

    2.  **I don't know why, but** I feel so sleepy today.
        例：I don't know why, but I feel so sleepy after lunch every day.（不知道为什么，我每天午饭后都好困。）

    3.  **For some reason**, I'm a good judge of people.

    4.  **I just feel like** going out today.

    5.  Just because.（不为什么）

8.  情景对话

    1.  A: What do you want for dinner?（晚饭想吃什么？）

    2.  B: I'd rather cook at home. I'm tired of delivery.（我宁愿在家做，外卖吃腻了。）

    3.  A: Good point. Let's make hotpot!（有道理，那做火锅吧！）

    4.  B: Sounds great! I can't live without hotpot.（好呀！我可离不开火锅。）

> 误区：别每句话都用 I think——英语里 I think 后面常跟具体理由，只说 I think it's good. 会显得敷衍。
> 提醒：I guess 表示"不太确定"，正式场合写邮件用 I believe 或 In my opinion 更稳妥。

# Part 5 请求、建议与关心（对别人说）

1.  表达请求

    1.  **May I** have some privacy, **please**?

    2.  **Can you** give that to me?
        例：Can you pass me the soy sauce?（能递我一下酱油吗？）

    3.  **Would you mind if** I sit here?（更正式：Would you mind if I sat here?）
        例：Would you mind if I sit here? The other tables are full.（你介意我坐这吗？其他桌子都满了。）

    4.  I'd like to open the window if that's OK with you.

    5.  Would it be OK if ...?

2.  请求许可

    1.  **Can/Could/May I** try this on?
        例：May I try this on? I'll be quick.（我可以试穿这件吗？很快就好。）

    2.  Do/Would you mind if ...?（注意：回答 No 表示"不介意，请便"）

3.  表示建议

    1.  How about...? / What about...?（How about + 名词/动名词）

    2.  **Why not** stay here?
        例：Why not stay here tonight? It's too late to drive back.（今晚就住下吧，开回去太晚了。）

    3.  I think maybe you should leave him.
        例：I think maybe you should talk to your landlord about the noise.（我觉得你应该跟房东说说噪音的事。）

4.  提醒别人不要做...

    1.  You **can't** take pictures here.

    2.  You shouldn't stay up late.

    3.  **You'd better not** eat too much.
        例：You'd better not eat too much before the run.（跑步前最好别吃太多。）

5.  询问计划

    1.  **What are you doing** this coming holiday?

    2.  **Are you free** for lunch tomorrow?
        例：Are you free for lunch tomorrow? There's a new place near the office.（明天有空一起吃午饭吗？公司附近有家新店。）

    3.  Are you busy?

    4.  Do you have any plans for ...?（问长假等较长的时间）

6.  邀约

    1.  **Let's** go to that new restaurant.

    2.  **Do you want to** go swimming?
        例：Do you want to go swimming this weekend? The pool is open again.（这周末去游泳吗？泳池又开了。）

    3.  **We should** totally go there.（totally 是口语加强词，很随意）

    4.  Would you like to...（较正式、客气）

7.  表示关心

    1.  What's wrong?
        例：What's wrong? You look upset.（怎么了？你看起来心情不好。）

    2.  What's wrong with you?〔慎用〕（常带指责语气；关心别人用 What's wrong? / Are you OK?）

    3.  Are you OK?

    4.  What's the matter?

    5.  Is everything OK?

    6.  What's with him?（他怎么回事？）

8.  加油打气

    1.  **Good luck with** your exam!
        例：Good luck with your exam tomorrow!（祝你明天考试顺利！）

    2.  You can do it!

    3.  Keep it up!

    4.  Keep up the good work!

9.  别紧张

    1.  Relax!

    2.  Calm down!

    3.  Take it easy!
        例：Take it easy — it's just an interview, you'll be fine.（放轻松，就是场面试，你没问题。）

    4.  Don't be nervous!

10. 情景对话

    1.  A: Are you free this weekend?（这周末有空吗？）

    2.  B: I think so. Why?（应该有空，怎么了？）

    3.  A: Do you want to help me move? I'll buy dinner.（能来帮我搬家吗？我请你吃晚饭。）

    4.  B: Sure! What time should I come?（好呀！我几点过来？）

> 提醒：Calm down! 语气容易像在指责对方，安慰人用 Take it easy. 或 Don't worry. 更温和。
> 误区：What's wrong with you? 常带指责语气，关心别人用 What's wrong? 或 Are you OK?。

# Part 6 回应与互动（对别人的话作出反应）

1.  赞成

    1.  I agree.

    2.  I'm with you.

    3.  I couldn't agree more.（再同意不过了）

    4.  I think so, too.

    5.  I'm all for it.（我完全赞成）
        例：I'm all for it — let's start next week.（我完全赞成，下周就开始吧。）

2.  反对

    1.  I don't think so.
        例：I don't think so — the metro is faster.（我觉得不是，地铁更快。）

    2.  I disagree.

    3.  **I'm not so sure about** that.

3.  也许

    1.  Maybe/Perhaps.（50%）
        例：Maybe. Let me check my schedule first.（也许吧，我先看下日程。）

    2.  Could be.（觉得有可能但不大）

    3.  Who knows?（谁知道呢）

4.  拒绝

    1.  Sorry, I can't.
        例：Sorry, I can't — I have a family dinner that day.（抱歉我去不了，那天有家庭聚餐。）

    2.  Maybe next time.

    3.  I think I'll pass.（我就不去了）

    4.  **I don't think** it's a good idea.

5.  表示惊讶（真的吗？）

    1.  Really?

    2.  Are you kidding me?

    3.  What?

    4.  Are you sure?

    5.  Are you serious?

    6.  No way!〔口语〕
        例：No way! You got the job?（不会吧！你拿到那份工作了？）

    7.  That's impossible!

6.  不出所料

    1.  I told you so.（我早说过了）
        例：I told you so — the train was late again.（我早说了，火车又晚点。）

    2.  I knew it!（我就知道！）

    3.  I thought so, too.（我也这么觉得）

7.  表示同情遗憾

    1.  I'm sorry about that.

    2.  I don't know what to say.

    3.  That's too bad.
        例：That's too bad — did you try calling the helpline?（太可惜了，你打客服电话了吗？）

    4.  I know how you feel.

8.  祝贺好消息

    1.  That's great!

    2.  That sounds great/awesome!

    3.  You did it.

    4.  You made it.

    5.  You nailed it.〔口语〕（干得漂亮）
        例：You nailed it! Your presentation was amazing.（干得漂亮！你汇报太棒了。）

    6.  Good/Great job!

    7.  Well done!

    8.  I'm so happy for you!

9.  你说得对

    1.  You're right.
        例：You're right — I should have checked the price first.（你说得对，我该先查价格的。）

    2.  I get it.

    3.  I see.

    4.  I understand.

    5.  That makes sense.

10. 表示感同身受

    1.  I know.

    2.  I know what you mean.

    3.  Same here.
        例：Same here — my phone bill was crazy this month too.（我也是，这个月话费也贵得离谱。）

    4.  That happened to me, too.

    5.  I was there.（我当时也在场/我也经历过）

11. 然后呢？

    1.  Oh yeah?

    2.  So what?〔慎用〕（带挑衅/不耐烦的语气）

    3.  What else?

    4.  Then what?
        例：Then what? Did she reply?（然后呢？她回了没？）

    5.  And?（口语，催促对方继续说）

12. 确认？

    1.  The store opens at 10, right?
        例：We're meeting at 7, right?（我们 7 点见，对吧？）

    2.  Don't forget to bring the key, okay?

    3.  You work here, don't you?

    4.  We met last week, remember?

13. 不想被干涉

    1.  Leave me alone!

    2.  Go away.

    3.  None of your business.〔慎用〕（比较冲）
        例：None of your business — I'll handle it myself.（不关你的事，我自己处理。）

    4.  Stay away from me!

    5.  I don't wanna hear it.（wanna = want to，〔口语〕）

14. 情景对话

    1.  A: Guess what? I got a raise!（你猜怎么着？我涨工资了！）

    2.  B: No way! That's great! Congratulations!（不会吧！太好了！恭喜！）

    3.  A: Thanks! I'm buying coffee tomorrow.（谢谢！明天咖啡我请。）

    4.  B: I'll take that — I told you so, you deserved it.（那我可不客气——我早说了，你应得的。）

> 提醒：Really? 的语调和重音不同，可以是真惊讶，也可以是敷衍回应；想追问细节用 Oh yeah? 或 Then what?。
> 提醒：回应对方难处时说 I know. 可能显得冷淡，用 I know what you mean. 或 Same here. 更有人情味。

# Part 7 对话衔接与实用短句

1.  嗯...（思考/拖延）

    1.  Well...I'll try my best.
        例：Well... let me think about it and get back to you.（嗯……我想想再回复你。）

    2.  Um...What do you mean?

    3.  Let me see...
        例：Let me see... I think it's on the third floor.（我看看……应该在 3 楼。）

2.  过渡语（不知道如何回应的时候）

    - Well...

    - The thing is...
        例：The thing is, I can't afford a new phone right now.（问题是，我现在买不起新手机。）

    - Let's see

    - Let me see/think

    - Like...〔口语〕

    - It's a kind of...

    - I mean

    - You know
        例：You know, it's like a fast-food version of hotpot.（怎么说呢，有点像快餐版火锅。）

3.  常用短句

    1.  Do you have a minute?
        例：Do you have a minute? I need your help with the report.（有空吗？帮我看看这份报告。）

    2.  I need your help.

    3.  Who is this?（电话问是谁）

    4.  That's it.（说完了/就这些；注意也可表示"够了"，见 Part 3）

    5.  There you go.（给你 / 这就对了，看语境）

        - Here's your coffee. There you go!（给你）

        - There you go! You did it!（这就对了）

    6.  What do you recommend?
        例：What do you recommend here? It's my first time.（这家店你推荐什么？我第一次来。）

    7.  Got it?

    8.  How was your weekend/holiday/exam/interview?

    9.  How long is this going to take?
        例：How long is this going to take? I have a meeting at 3.（这要多久？我 3 点有会。）

    10. How do I get to ...?（如 How do I get to the station?）

    11. Not much. / Nothing much.

4.  情景对话

    1.  A: Do you have a minute?（你有空吗？）

    2.  B: Sure, what's up?（有啊，怎么了？）

    3.  A: The thing is, I need your help moving this weekend.（是这样，这周末搬家想请你帮忙。）

    4.  B: No problem. What time?（没问题，几点？）

> 提醒：Well... / You know 是口语填充词，书面写作和正式邮件里不要用。
> 提醒：Got it? 有时显得不耐烦，想确认对方是否明白，用 Does that make sense? 更客气。
