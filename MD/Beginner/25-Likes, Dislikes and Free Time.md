# Likes, Dislikes and Free Time 喜好与空闲时间

## 1. 喜欢和不喜欢的说法

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I **like** + 动名词 | I **like** cooking at home after work. | 我下班喜欢在家做饭。 |
| I **love** + 动名词 | I **love** sleeping in on weekends. | 我周末特别爱睡懒觉。 |
| I **enjoy** + 动名词 | I **enjoy** hiking with my coworkers. | 我喜欢和同事一起爬山。 |
| I **don't mind** + 动名词 | I **don't mind** doing dishes after dinner. | 晚饭后洗碗我不介意。 |
| I **can't stand** + 动名词 | I **can't stand** waiting in line at the bank. | 我最受不了在银行排队。 |
| I **hate** + 动名词 | I **hate** going to the dentist. | 我特别讨厌去看牙医。 |
| I **don't really like** + 动名词 | I **don't really like** eating alone at noon. | 我不太喜欢中午一个人吃饭。 |
| I'm **crazy about** + 动名词 | My roommate is **crazy about** baking cakes. | 室友特别迷做蛋糕。 |
| I **prefer** + 动名词 | I **prefer** taking the subway to work. | 我上班更愿意坐地铁。 |

| 动词 | 后面接 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| like ／ love ／ enjoy | 动名词 -ing | I enjoy **hiking** on weekends. |
| don't mind | 动名词 -ing | I don't mind **doing** dishes. |
| can't stand ／ hate | 动名词 -ing | I can't stand **waiting** in line. |
| be into ／ be crazy about | 动名词 -ing | She is into **making** short videos. |
| look forward to | 动名词 -ing | I look forward to **seeing** you Friday. |

## 2. prefer 与 would rather

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I **prefer** A to B | I **prefer** tea to coffee in the morning. | 早上比起咖啡我更爱喝茶。 |
| I **would rather** + 动词原形 | I **would rather** stay home tonight. | 今晚我宁愿待在家。 |
| I'd **rather** A than B | I'd **rather** walk than take a taxi. | 我宁愿走路也不打车。 |
| **Which do you prefer**? | **Which do you prefer** — hotpot or barbecue? | 你更喜欢哪个，火锅还是烧烤？ |
| I **prefer** + 动词原形 | I **prefer** to cook at home on weekdays. | 工作日我更愿意在家做饭。 |
| I'd **rather not** + 动词原形 | I'd **rather not** eat out tonight. | 今晚我宁愿不出去吃。 |
| **Would you rather** A or B? | **Would you rather** hike or camp? | 你宁愿爬山还是露营？ |

## 5. 程度和口头说法

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm really **into** … | I'm really **into** baking sourdough lately. | 我最近迷上做酸面包了。 |
| I'm **not a big fan of** … | My brother is **not a big fan of** horror movies. | 我哥不太喜欢恐怖片。 |
| **It's not really my thing** | **It's not really my thing** — I prefer tea. | 这不是我的菜，我更爱喝茶。 |
| I'm **obsessed with** … | I'm **obsessed with** that crime show right now. | 我最近迷上那部犯罪剧了。 |
| I **used to** … but now … | I **used to** hate running, but now I run 5K. | 我以前讨厌跑步，现在天天跑。 |
| I'm **not into** … | I'm **not into** horror games at all. | 我一点也不喜欢恐怖游戏。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I like play games. | I like **playing** games. | like 后面接动名词 -ing。 |
| I don't mind to wait. | I don't mind **waiting**. | mind 后面接动名词。 |
| I would rather to stay home. | I'd rather **stay** home. | would rather 后接动词原形。 |
| I'm boring in this movie. | I'm **bored** by this movie. | 人用 bored，物用 boring。 |
| I very like hiking. | I **really like** hiking. | 不能用 very 直接修饰动词。 |
| I prefer tea than coffee. | I prefer tea **to** coffee. | prefer A to B，不用 than。 |

## 7. 约人一起玩

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **Do you want to** + 动词原形? | **Do you want to** play badminton Saturday? | 周六想打羽毛球吗？ |
| **How about** + 动名词? | **How about** grabbing hotpot tonight? | 今晚去吃火锅怎么样？ |
| **Let's** + 动词原形 | **Let's** go camping next month. | 我们下个月去露营吧。 |
