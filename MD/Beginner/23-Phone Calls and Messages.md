# Phone Calls and Messages 电话与消息

## 1. 打电话和接电话

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Hello, this is Ming speaking. | **Hello, this is Ming speaking.** I'm on my way. | 你好，我是小明。我在路上。 |
| Is this a good time? | **Is this a good time** — or should I call later? | 现在方便说话吗？晚点再打？ |
| Can I call you back later? | **Can I call you back in ten minutes?** At work. | 十分钟后我再打给你好吗？我在上班。 |
| You've got the wrong number. | **You've got the wrong number** — no Li Na here. | 你打错了，这儿没有李娜。 |
| Hold on a second, please. | **Hold on a second, please** — let me find a pen. | 请稍等一下，我找支笔。 |
| Who's calling, please? | **Who's calling, please?** I'm driving. | 请问您是哪位？我在开车。 |
| Sorry, who is this? | **Sorry, who is this?** I missed your call. | 不好意思你是哪位？刚没接到。 |
| Can I call you right back? | **Can I call you right back?** I'm at dinner. | 我马上回你好吗？我在吃饭。 |
| Let me put you on speaker. | **Let me put you on speaker** — I'm cooking. | 我开免提，我在做饭。 |
| I dialed the wrong number. | **Sorry, I dialed the wrong number.** | 抱歉，我打错号码了。 |

## 2. 听不清的时候

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sorry, I can't hear you. | **Sorry, I can't hear you** — it's loud here. | 抱歉，我听不清，这儿太吵。 |
| You're breaking up. | **You're breaking up** — I only got half. | 你那边信号断断续续的。 |
| The subway has no signal. | **The subway has no signal** — I'll text you. | 地铁里没信号，我发消息给你。 |
| Could you say that again? | **Could you say that again** — a little slower? | 你能再说一遍吗？慢一点。 |
| Let me call you from outside. | **Let me call you from outside** — one sec. | 我出去外面再打给你。 |
| I'm losing you. | **I'm losing you** — say that again? | 信号快断了，你再说一遍？ |
| Can you hear me now? | **Can you hear me now?** I moved to the window. | 现在能听见吗？我挪到窗边了。 |
| My signal is bad here. | **My signal is bad here** — in the elevator. | 我这儿信号差，在电梯里。 |
| Let's switch to WeChat. | **Let's switch to WeChat** — it keeps dropping. | 咱们换微信吧，电话老断。 |
| It's noisy on my end. | **It's noisy on my end** — I'm outside. | 我这边太吵，我在外面。 |

## 3. 留言和转达

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can I leave a message? | **Can I leave a message?** He's not picking up. | 我能留个言吗？他没接。 |
| Could you tell her I called? | **Could you tell her I called?** It's urgent. | 你能告诉她我打过吗？我有急事。 |
| I'll let him know. | **I'll let him know** as soon as he's back. | 他一回来我就告诉他。 |
| I'll check and call you back. | **I'll check and call you back** in an hour. | 我查一下，一小时内回你电话。 |
| She's in a meeting right now. | **She's in a meeting right now** — try later. | 她正在开会，你晚点再打吧。 |
| Can I try again later? | **Can I try again later?** She may be busy. | 我晚点再打行吗？她可能在忙。 |
| Tell him it's about the order. | **Tell him it's about the order** — thanks! | 跟他说是订单的事，谢谢！ |

## 8. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| My phone has no electricity. | My phone is about to die. | 手机快没电，用 die 不用 electricity。 |
| I will reply you later. | I'll call you back later. | 回电话是 call back，reply 不加人。 |
| Hello, I am Li Ming. | Hello, this is Li Ming. | 电话里说名字用 this is，不用 I am。 |
| Can you speak loudly? | Could you speak up? | 让对方大声说用 speak up。 |
| You didn't answer me. | You didn't pick up. | 接电话用 pick up，不说 answer me。 |
