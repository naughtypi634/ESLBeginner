# Describing Your Home and Neighborhood 描述你的住处和小区

## 1. have / has 现在时

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：I have / He has | I **have** a one-bedroom apartment near Line 2. | 我在2号线附近有一套一居室。 |
| 第三人称单数用 has | My roommate **has** a cat that sleeps on the washer. | 我室友有只猫，天天睡在洗衣机上。 |
| 否定：don't have / doesn't have | I **don't have** a dryer — everything goes on the balcony. | 我没有烘干机，衣服全晾阳台。 |
| 疑问：Do you have…? / Does she have…? | **Do you have** air conditioning in your bedroom? | 你卧室有空调吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. | 有。/ 没有。 |

## 6. 描述住处的句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| My neighborhood has + 名词 | My neighborhood **has** a 24-hour pharmacy downstairs. | 我们小区楼下就有24小时药店。 |
| There is a + 单数名词 | **There is** a package pickup station right downstairs. | 楼下就有个快递驿站。 |
| There are + 复数名词 | **There are** two subway lines within a ten-minute walk. | 走路十分钟内能到两条地铁线。 |
| My apartment is + 形容词 | My apartment **is** small but sunny. | 我房子小，但采光好。 |
| I can + 动词 + 地点 | I **can** buy vegetables at the market and walk home. | 我可以在菜市场买菜，再走回家。 |
| My neighborhood is + 形容词 | My neighborhood **is** quiet on weekdays and loud on weekends. | 我们小区平时安静，周末很吵。 |

## 7. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Do you rent or own your place? | 你是租房还是自己的房子？ |
| How long is your commute? | 你通勤要多久？ |
| What's the best thing about your neighborhood? | 你们小区最好的一点是什么？ |
| Is there anything you'd change about your apartment? | 你的房子有什么你想改的？ |
| Do you have a balcony? What do you use it for? | 你家有阳台吗？用来做什么？ |
| How much do you pay for rent? | 你房租多少？ |
| What's within a five-minute walk of your home? | 你家五分钟路程内有什么？ |
| Would you rather live downtown or in the suburbs? | 你愿意住市中心还是郊区？ |
| What do you look for when you rent? | 你租房最看重什么？ |

## 8. Practice 练习

### 填空：have / has

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | My apartment ______ a small balcony, so I dry my clothes there. |
| 2 | ______ you ______ a washing machine, or do you use a laundromat? |
| 3 | I ______ (not) a dryer, so towels take two days to dry in winter. |
| 4 | ______ your roommate ______ his own bathroom? |
| 5 | We ______ two air conditioners — one in the bedroom, one in the living room. |

### 填空：there is / there are

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | ______ a 24-hour convenience store right under my building. |
| 2 | ______ three buses that go straight to my office. |
| 3 | ______ any good noodle places near your apartment? |
| 4 | ______ a gym in my neighborhood, but it's always packed after 7 p.m. |
| 5 | ______ two subway lines and a farmers market within ten minutes of my place. |

### 改错

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | My neighborhood have many restaurants. |
| 2 | There is many cafes near my home. |
| 3 | My apartment is have a big kitchen. |
| 4 | Do your roommate has a car? |
| 5 | It have a park across the street. |
