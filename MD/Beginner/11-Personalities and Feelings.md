# Personalities and Feelings 性格与感受

## 1. 询问感受

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| How are you **doing**? | 你最近怎么样？ |
| How's it **going**? | 怎么样啊？ |
| How are you **feeling** today? | 你今天感觉怎么样？ |
| You **okay**? | 你没事吧？ |
| What's **up**? | 怎么了？ |

## 2. 表达感受

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I am + 形容词 | **I'm wiped out** — three meetings back to back and no lunch. | 我累瘫了，连着三个会，午饭都没吃。 |
| I feel + 形容词 | I **feel** relieved — the deadline moved to next Friday. | 我松了口气，截止日期推到下周五了。 |
| I'm feeling + 形容词 | **I'm feeling** a lot better after that nap. | 睡了那一觉之后我好多了。 |
| I don't feel + 形容词 | I **don't feel** great today — think I'm coming down with something. | 我今天不太舒服，感觉要感冒。 |
| I feel + 形容词 + when… | I **feel** happy **when** my package shows up a day early. | 快递提前一天到我就特别开心。 |

## 5. 描述性格的句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| He / She **is** + 形容词 | My roommate **is** the most laid-back person I know. | 我室友是我认识的最随性的人。 |
| He / She **has** a + 形容词 + personality | He **has** a laid-back **personality**. | 他性格很随性。 |
| He / She **looks** + 形容词 | He **looks** serious, but he's actually funny. | 他看着严肃，其实很好笑。 |
| He / She **seems like** + 句子 | She **seems like** she's always in a hurry. | 她看起来永远在赶时间。 |
| He / She **is** a + 名词 | My cousin **is** a total **homebody**. | 我表弟是个彻底的宅男。 |

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| My manager **is** straight-talking, which I like. | 我领导说话很直，我挺喜欢。 |
| My aunt **is** incredibly generous — she feeds anyone who walks in the door. | 我阿姨特别大方，谁进门都给做饭。 |
| My uncle **is** so gullible that he forwards every scam link he gets. | 我叔叔太容易上当了，收到诈骗链接都转发。 |
| My best friend **is** reliable — I call her first. | 我最好的朋友特别靠得住，有事我先找她。 |

## 6. makes me… 句型

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 名词 + makes me + 形容词 | My cat meeting me at the door **makes me** happy. | 我的猫在门口等我，让我特别开心。 |
| 动名词 + makes me + 形容词 | Rush hour **makes me** annoyed. | 早高峰让我很烦。 |
| 人 + makes me + 形容词 | My mom **makes me** crazy on every call. | 我妈每次通话都让我头疼。 |
| 动名词 + makes me + 动词 | Working overtime **makes me** hungry by 9 p.m. | 加班到九点我就饿。 |

## 8. Practice 练习

### 填空：is / has / looks / seems like

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | My roommate ______ the most laid-back person I know. |
| 2 | He ______ a laid-back personality — nothing gets to him. |
| 3 | She ______ serious, but she's actually really funny. |
| 4 | He ______ he's always in a hurry. |

### 填空：makes me

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rush hour ______ (make) me annoyed before 8 a.m. |
| 2 | My cat meeting me at the door ______ (make) me happy. |
| 3 | Working overtime ______ (make) me hungry. |
| 4 | Sitting in traffic ______ (make) me tired. |

### 改错

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | My roommate is a person very laid-back. |
| 2 | He have a generous personality. |
| 3 | Working overtime make me tired. |
| 4 | My aunt is very much generous. |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| my manager / straight-talking | My manager is straight-talking, which I appreciate. | 我领导说话很直，我挺喜欢这点。 |
| my best friend / reliable | My best friend is reliable. | 我最好的朋友特别靠得住。 |
| rush hour / makes me / annoyed | Rush hour makes me annoyed before 8 a.m. even starts. | 早高峰让我八点还没到就烦了。 |
| I / feel / relieved / deadline moved | I feel relieved — the deadline moved. | 我松了口气，截止日期推迟了。 |
