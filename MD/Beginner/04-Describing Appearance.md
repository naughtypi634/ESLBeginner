# Describing Appearance 描述外貌

## 1. 三种描述外貌的句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| He / She **is** + 形容词 | My cousin **is** tall — he ducks in our elevator. | 我表弟很高，进电梯得低头。 |
| She **has** + 名词 | The new hire **has** long hair and a round face. | 新来的同事留长发，脸圆圆的。 |
| He / She **looks** + 形容词 | He **looks** tired — he worked all night. | 他看起来很累，通宵干活了。 |
| He / She **is wearing** + 名词 | **He is wearing** a black cap at the gym. | 他在健身房戴着黑帽子。 |

| 区别 | 说明 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| is + 形容词 | 天生的、比较固定的特征 | He **is** tall. ／ She **is** short. |
| has + 名词 | 头发、脸、配饰 | She **has** long hair. ／ He **has** a beard. |
| looks + 形容词 | 此刻的感觉，也许只是暂时 | You **look** tired. ／ She **looks** happy today. |

## 3. look like 句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What does he **look like**? | **What does he look like?** — Tall, with glasses. | 他长什么样？——高个，戴眼镜。 |
| He **looks like** his dad. | The new guy **looks like** his dad — same smile. | 新同事跟他爸很像，笑容一样。 |
| She **looks like** a movie star. | With sunglasses on, she **looks like** a movie star. | 戴着墨镜，她像个电影明星。 |
| What is she **like**? | **What is she like?** — She's warm and funny. | 问性格，不是问长相。 |
## 4. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| He has tall. | He **is** tall. | 身高体型用 is。 |
| She is long hair. | She **has** long hair. | 头发、身体部位用 has。 |
| He looks like tired. | He **looks** tired. | looks 后直接跟形容词。 |
| I am 30 years old age. | I **am** 30. | years old 后不加 age。 |
