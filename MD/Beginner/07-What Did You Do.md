# What Did You Do? 谈过去做过的事

## 1. 一般过去时形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：动词 + -ed | I **ordered** takeout at 11 p.m. and **regretted** it at 6 a.m. | 我晚上11点点了外卖，早上6点就后悔了。 |
| 否定：didn't + 动词原形 | I **didn't sleep** well — my neighbor was loud. | 我没睡好，邻居太吵了。 |
| 疑问：Did + 主语 + 动词原形 | **Did** you **get** my WeChat message about the meeting? | 你收到我关于开会的微信了吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**. | 收到了。/ 没有。 |
| 特殊疑问：What did you…? | **What did you** have for lunch? | 你午饭吃了什么？ |

### -ed 拼写规则

| 规则 | 动词 → 过去式 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般加 -ed | work → worked | I **worked** from home all week. |
| 以 e 结尾加 -d | live → lived | We **lived** in a 12-square-meter room that year. |
| 辅音字母 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -ed | study → studied | I **studied** English every night in college. |
| 重读闭音节双写末尾字母 | stop → stopped | I **stopped** at the convenience store for water. |

### 最容易犯的三个错

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Did you went to the gym? | Did you **go** to the gym? | did 后面用动词原形 |
| I didn't ordered takeout. | I didn't **order** takeout. | didn't 后面也用原形 |
| I was order takeout. | I **ordered** takeout. | 过去时不需要再加 was |

## 3. 过去时间表达

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| yesterday | 昨天 | the day before yesterday | 前天 |
| last night | 昨晚 | last weekend | 上个周末 |
| last week | 上周 | last month | 上个月 |
| last year | 去年 | last fall | 去年秋天 |
| two days ago | 两天前 | a few minutes ago | 几分钟前 |
| this morning | 今天早上 | in 2024 | 2024年 |
| when I was in college | 我上大学的时候 | when I was a kid | 我小时候 |

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| I **took** the subway to the interview, but a signal delay **made** me late. | 我坐地铁去面试，信号故障让我迟到了。 |
| We **went** to a hotpot place for the team dinner and **stayed** until 10. | 团建去了火锅店，待到十点才走。 |
| I **forgot** my umbrella, so I **bought** one from the convenience store for 30 yuan. | 我忘带伞了，就在便利店花30块买了一把。 |
| My roommate **slept** through his alarm. | 我室友睡过头了。 |
| I **sent** the wrong file to the whole group chat and **had** to apologize. | 我把文件发错群了，只能道歉。 |
| She **quit** her job in March and **started** her own studio. | 她三月辞职，自己开了个工作室。 |

### 填空：用过去式

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | I ______ (order) takeout at 11 p.m. and regretted it at 6 a.m. |
| 2 | My roommate ______ (sleep) through his alarm and missed the train. |
| 3 | We ______ (go) to a hotpot place for the team dinner. |
| 4 | I ______ (not / get) the package — the station was closed. |
| 5 | She ______ (quit) her job in March and started her own studio. |

### 改错

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Did you went to the gym? |
| 2 | I didn't ordered takeout. |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I / forget / umbrella / so / buy / one | I forgot my umbrella, so I bought one. | 我忘带伞了，就买了一把。 |
| we / go / hotpot / stay / until 10 | We went to a hotpot place and stayed until 10. | 我们去了火锅店，待到十点。 |
| what / you / do / last weekend | What did you do last weekend? — I just stayed in. | 你上个周末做了什么？——就在家待着。 |
