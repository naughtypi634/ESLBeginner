# At the Airport and Train Station 机场与车站

## 1. 值机和托运

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to check in. | **I'd like to check in** — my flight leaves at nine. | 我想办值机，航班九点起飞。 |
| Can I have an aisle seat? | **Can I have an aisle seat** — I get up a lot. | 能给个靠过道的座位吗？我常起身。 |
| I have one bag to check. | **I have one bag to check** and one carry-on. | 我要托运一件，随身带一件。 |
| Is this bag too heavy? | **Is this bag too heavy** for a carry-on? | 这个包当随身行李超重吗？ |
| Where's the check-in counter? | **Where's the check-in counter** for China Eastern? | 东航的值机柜台在哪？ |
| Can I have a window seat? | **Can I have a window seat** for my mom? | 能给我妈一个靠窗座位吗？ |
| How many bags can I check? | **How many bags can I check** on this ticket? | 这张票能免费托运几件？ |
| Is the flight full tonight? | **Is the flight full tonight** or can I switch? | 今晚航班满了吗，能换一班吗？ |
| Where do I drop my bag? | **Where do I drop my bag** for the belt? | 行李在哪儿放上输送带？ |

## 4. 晚点和改签

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| The flight is delayed by two hours. | **The flight is delayed by two hours** — now gate C12. | 航班晚点两小时，改到C12登机口。 |
| Is there an earlier train? | **Is there an earlier train** to Nanjing tonight? | 今晚去南京有更早的一班吗？ |
| Can I change my ticket? | **Can I change my ticket** to the next one? | 我能改签到下一班吗？ |
| I missed my train. | **I missed my train** — the subway was too slow. | 我误了高铁，地铁太慢了。 |
| Where do I go for a refund? | **Where do I go for a refund** on this ticket? | 这张票退票去哪儿办？ |
| The train is 20 minutes late. | **The train is 20 minutes late**, platform 8 now. | 高铁晚点20分钟，改8站台。 |
| Can I change it on 12306? | **Can I change it on 12306** right now? | 能在12306上改签吗？ |

## 7. 行李问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| My bag is over the limit. | **My bag is over the limit** by two kilos. | 我的行李超重两公斤。 |
| How much is the extra fee? | **How much is the extra fee** for five kilos? | 超五公斤要加多少钱？ |
| My bag didn't come out. | **My bag didn't come out** at carousel 9. | 我的行李没从9号转盘出来。 |

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I want to check my luggages. | I want to check my luggage. | luggage 不可数，没有复数。 |
| Where is the bag claim? | Where is the baggage claim? | 固定说法是 baggage claim。 |
| The flight is late. | The flight is delayed. | 航班晚点用 delayed。 |
| I go to pick my ticket. | I go to pick up my ticket. | 取票要用 pick up。 |
