# Getting Help and Emergencies 求助与紧急情况

## 1. 开口请人帮忙

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Could you help me for a sec? | **Could you help me for a sec?** My phone won't scan. | 能帮我一下吗？我手机扫不了码。 |
| Could you take a photo for us? | **Could you take a photo for us?** Just press here. | 能帮我们拍张照吗？按这里就行。 |
| Would you mind holding my bag? | **Would you mind holding my bag** for a minute? | 帮我拿一下包好吗？就一分钟。 |
| Can I borrow your charger? | **Can I borrow your charger?** My phone is at 2%. | 能借下充电器吗？我手机只剩2%。 |
| Where can I find a taxi? | **Where can I find a taxi?** I'm not from here. | 在哪能打到车？我不是本地人。 |
| Could you show me the way? | **Could you show me the way** to the service desk? | 能告诉我服务台怎么走吗？ |
| Can I use your hotspot? | **Can I use your hotspot?** I ran out of data. | 能借下热点吗？我流量用完了。 |
| Would you mind helping me? | **Would you mind helping me** with this suitcase? | 能帮我搬下箱子吗？ |
| Do you have a power bank? | **Do you have a power bank?** Mine is empty. | 你有充电宝吗？我的没电了。 |

## 2. 丢东西和找东西

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I lost my phone. | **I lost my phone** on the subway this morning. | 我手机今早丢在地铁上了。 |
| I left my bag on the subway. | **I left my bag on the subway** — probably gone. | 我把包落在地铁上了，估计找不回来。 |
| Did anyone turn in a wallet? | **Did anyone turn in a wallet?** It's black leather. | 有人交来钱包吗？我的是黑皮的。 |
| Where's the lost and found? | **Where's the lost and found** at this station? | 这个站的失物招领在哪？ |
| I left it in the taxi. | **I left it in the taxi** — the blue one. | 我落在出租车上了，蓝色的那辆。 |
| I left my umbrella on the bus. | **I left my umbrella** on the number 7 bus. | 我把伞落在7路公交上了。 |
| Someone took my bag. | **Someone took my bag** at security, by mistake. | 有人安检处错拿了我的包。 |
| Can you check the cameras? | **Can you check the cameras** near gate 3? | 能查下3号口监控吗？ |
| Here's my receipt number. | **Here's my receipt number** for the lost item. | 这是我失物的回执单号。 |

## 4. 紧急情况

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Call an ambulance. | **Call an ambulance** — 120, right now. | 叫救护车，打120，快点。 |
| I need a doctor. | **I need a doctor** — I feel really dizzy. | 我需要看医生，我头晕得厉害。 |
| Where's the police station? | **Where's the police station?** I lost my wallet. | 警察局在哪？我钱包丢了。 |

| There's been an accident. | **There's been an accident** at the crosswalk. | 人行横道那儿出事故了。 |

## 5. 说不清时的救命句

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I don't speak much English. | **I don't speak much English** — sorry about that. | 我英语说得不多，不好意思。 |
| Could you say that again? | **Could you say that again** a little slower? | 你能再说一遍吗？慢一点。 |
| Could you write it down? | **Could you write it down** for me, please? | 能帮我写下来吗？ |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I lost my phone yesterday ago. | I lost my phone **yesterday**. | 只说 yesterday 就够了。 |
| Can you help me? I want borrow charger. | **Could you lend me** a charger? | 借给别人用 lend。 |
| My phone has no electricity. | My phone **is dead**. | 没电了说 is dead。 |
| Please give me help. | **Could you help me?** | 请人帮忙用 Could you。 |
| My wallet is stolen! | My wallet **was stolen** just now. | 已经丢了的用过去式 was。 |
