# Would You Like To…? 邀请与改约

## 1. 发出邀请

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Would you like to + 动词原形? | **Would you like to** grab hotpot on Friday? | 周五想一起吃火锅吗？ |
| Do you want to + 动词原形? | **Do you want to** join us for a movie? | 想跟我们一起看电影吗？ |
| How about + 时间? | **How about** Saturday afternoon? | 周六下午怎么样？ |
| Are you free + 时间? | **Are you free** this weekend? | 这周末你有空吗？ |
| We're + 动词-ing | We're getting together — **come** with us. | 我们聚一聚，一起来吧。 |
| Want to + 动词原形? | **Want to** get takeout together tonight? | 今晚想一起点外卖吗？ |
| Are you up for + 名词? | **Are you up for** a hike on Sunday? | 周日想去爬山吗？ |
| Would you be free to + 动词? | **Would you be free to** join our group order? | 你有空一起拼单吗？ |

## 2. 接受邀请

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sure, that sounds fun. | **Sure, that sounds fun** — I'm free Friday. | 好啊，我周五有空。 |
| I'd love to. | **I'd love to** — see you there at seven. | 我很愿意，七点见。 |
| Count me in. | **Count me in** — it's been ages. | 算我一个，好久没见了。 |
| What time should I be there? | **What time should I be there?** Around seven? | 我该几点到？七点左右？ |
| I'll bring something. | **I'll bring something** — drinks and fruit? | 我带点东西，饮料和水果？ |
| That works for me. | **That works for me** — I'll be there at seven. | 我可以，我七点到。 |
| I'm down. | **I'm down** — camping sounds fun. | 我去，露营听着不错。 |
| Sounds good, I'm in. | **Sounds good, I'm in** — should I bring snacks? | 好啊，算我，要带零食吗？ |

## 3. 婉拒邀请

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd love to, but… | **I'd love to, but** I'm working late tonight. | 我很想去，但今晚要加班。 |
| Maybe next time. | **Maybe next time** — I'm slammed this week. | 下次吧，这周忙疯了。 |
| I can't make it this week. | **I can't make it** this week — sorry. | 这周我去不了，抱歉。 |
| Thanks for asking, though. | **Thanks for asking, though** — have fun! | 不过谢谢你叫我，玩得开心！ |
| Rain check? | Can I **take a rain check**? | 能改天吗？ |
| I'm swamped this week. | **I'm swamped** this week — can we do next week? | 这周太忙，下周行吗？ |
| Sorry, I already have plans. | **Sorry, I already have plans** that night. | 抱歉，那晚我有安排了。 |
| I'm on a deadline this week. | **I'm on a deadline** — it's due Friday. | 我这周赶项目，周五交。 |

## 4. 改约

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can we push it to Sunday? | **Can we push it to Sunday?** Same place? | 能推到周日吗？还是老地方？ |
| Something came up. | **Something came up** — I'm stuck at work. | 临时有事，我走不开。 |
| Can we do it another day? | **Can we do it another day?** | 能换个日子吗？ |
| Let's aim for next weekend. | **Let's aim for next weekend** — I'll text you. | 就定下周末吧，我再发你。 |
| I'll let you know by Friday. | **I'll let you know by Friday** — work is a mess. | 我周五前给你答复。 |

## 6. 确认细节

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What time works for you? | **What time works for you?** I'm free after six. | 你几点方便？我六点后有空。 |
| Where should we meet? | **Where should we meet?** By the subway exit? | 在哪碰头？地铁口吗？ |
| How are you getting there? | **How are you getting there?** Subway or a ride? | 你怎么过去？地铁还是打车？ |

## 7. 说得更随意或更正式

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Wanna + 动词原形? | **Wanna grab lunch** after the meeting? | 开完会一起吃饭？ |
| You in? | **You in?** We're ordering milk tea. | 你来吗？我们在拼单奶茶。 |
| Would you care to + 动词? | **Would you care to join us** for dinner? | 您愿意和我们一起吃饭吗？ |

## 8. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Who do you usually invite out on the weekend? | 周末你一般约谁出去？ |
| What was the last invitation you accepted? | 你最近答应了谁的邀请？ |
| How do you say no without hurting a friend? | 怎么拒绝才不伤朋友？ |
| Have you ever rescheduled plans at the last minute? | 你有过临时改约吗？ |
| What would you like to do with friends next month? | 下个月想和朋友做什么？ |
