# What Did You Do? 谈过去做过的事

## 1. 一般过去时形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：动词 + -ed | I **ordered** takeout at 11 p.m. and **regretted** it at 6 a.m. | 我晚上11点点了外卖，早上6点就后悔了。 |
| 否定：didn't + 动词原形 | I **didn't sleep** well — my neighbor was loud. | 我没睡好，邻居太吵了。 |
| 疑问：Did + 主语 + 动词原形 | **Did** you **get** my WeChat message about the meeting? | 你收到我关于开会的微信了吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**. | 收到了。/ 没有。 |
| 特殊疑问：What did you…? | **What did you** have for lunch? | 你午饭吃了什么？ |
| 特殊疑问：Where did you…? | **Where did you** go after the meeting? | 你开完会去哪了？ |
| 特殊疑问：How did…? | **How did** the interview **go**? | 面试进行得怎么样？ |

### -ed 拼写规则

| 规则 | 动词 → 过去式 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般加 -ed | work → worked | I **worked** from home all week. |
| 以 e 结尾加 -d | live → lived | We **lived** in a 12-square-meter room that year. |
| 辅音字母 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -ed | study → studied | I **studied** English every night in college. |
| 重读闭音节双写末尾字母 | stop → stopped | I **stopped** at the convenience store for water. |

### 最容易犯的三个错

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Did you went to the gym? | Did you **go** to the gym? | did 后面用动词原形 |
| I didn't ordered takeout. | I didn't **order** takeout. | didn't 后面也用原形 |
| I was order takeout. | I **ordered** takeout. | 过去时不需要再加 was |

## 3. 过去时间表达

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| yesterday | 昨天 | the day before yesterday | 前天 |
| last night | 昨晚 | last weekend | 上个周末 |
| last week | 上周 | last month | 上个月 |
| this morning | 今天早上 | two days ago | 两天前 |
| last Friday | 上周五 | an hour ago | 一小时前 |
| last year | 去年 | last summer | 去年夏天 |
| this afternoon | 今天下午 | last Tuesday | 上周二 |

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| I **took** the subway to the interview, but a signal delay **made** me late. | 我坐地铁去面试，信号故障让我迟到了。 |
| We **went** to a hotpot place for the team dinner and **stayed** until 10. | 团建去了火锅店，待到十点才走。 |
| I **forgot** my umbrella, so I **bought** one at the convenience store for 30 yuan. | 我忘带伞了，就在便利店花30块买了一把。 |

## 4. 讲经历用的连接词

| 连接词 | 作用 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| first | 先说第一件事 | **First** I **checked** my emails and **made** a to-do list. | 我先看了邮件列了个待办清单。 |
| then | 接着说下一件 | **Then** I **took** the subway to the interview across town. | 然后我坐地铁去城那头面试。 |
| after that | 再往后 | **After that** we **grabbed** hotpot with the new team. | 那之后我们和新团队一起吃了火锅。 |

## 5. 问别人过去做了什么

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Did you…? | **Did you** watch the game last night? | 你昨晚看比赛了吗？ |
| What did you…? | **What did you** do after work yesterday? | 你昨天下班后做了什么？ |
| How was…? | **How was** your first day at the new job? | 你新工作第一天怎么样？ |

## 6. 过去时间的其它说法

| 提示 | 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 指不久前的一天 | the other day | **The other day** I ran into my old manager. | 前几天我碰到了以前的主管。 |
| 指一段时间前 | a while ago | **A while ago** I started learning to swim. | 前段时间我开始学游泳。 |
| 指某一年的往事 | back in 2020 | **Back in 2020** I moved to Shenzhen for work. | 2020年我为了工作搬到深圳。 |
