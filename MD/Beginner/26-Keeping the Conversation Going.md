# Keeping the Conversation Going 把对话聊下去

## 1. 反义疑问句怎么构成

| 规则 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| 前面肯定，后面否定 | You like hotpot, **don't you**? |
| 前面否定，后面肯定 | You're not a morning person, **are you**? |
| be 动词用 be 动词呼应 | She's your manager, **isn't she**? |
| 情态动词用情态动词呼应 | You can't drive, **can you**? |
| 现在完成时用 have／has | You've tried that milk tea place, **haven't you**? |
| 后面用代词，不用名词 | Your brother plays guitar, **doesn't he**? |

## 3. 怎么回答

| 问句 | 事实 | 回答 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| You're coming tonight, **aren't you**? | 来 | **Yes, I am.** | 对，我去。 |
| You're coming tonight, **aren't you**? | 不来 | **No, I'm not.** | 不，我不去。 |
| You're **not** coming, are you? | 来 | **Yes, I am.** | 会，我去。 |
| You're **not** coming, are you? | 不来 | **No, I'm not.** | 对，我不去。 |
| You **didn't** take the subway, did you? | 坐了 | **Yes, I did.** | 坐了，我坐地铁来的。 |
| You **didn't** take the subway, did you? | 没坐 | **No, I didn't.** | 没，我打车来的。 |
| She's in your parent group, **isn't she**? | 是 | **Yes, she is.** | 是，她在群里。 |

## 4. 反义疑问句常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| You like tea, isn't it? | You like tea, **don't you**? | 实义动词要用 do／does 呼应 |
| She is busy, doesn't she? | She is busy, **isn't she**? | be 动词只能和 be 动词呼应 |
| You can't swim, can't you? | You can't swim, **can you**? | 前面否定，后面要用肯定 |

## 5. 追问和接话

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Really? | **Really**? You moved to a new apartment? | 真的？你搬家了？ |
| How come? | **How come**? I thought the rent was paid. | 怎么会？我以为租金付过了。 |
| What happened next? | **What happened next**? Did HR say anything? | 后来呢？HR说什么了吗？ |
| Me too. | **Me too** — I've been ordering takeout all week. | 我也是，我这一周都在点外卖。 |
| Same here. | **Same here** — my commute is over an hour too. | 我也一样，我通勤也一个多小时。 |
| That makes sense. | **That makes sense** — no wonder you're so tired. | 有道理，难怪你这么累。 |
| And you? | **And you**? Did you book the trip too? | 你呢？你也订了行程吗？ |
| Tell me more. | **Tell me more** — what did your manager say? | 细说细说，领导怎么说的？ |
| That sounds tough. | **That sounds tough** — working every weekend. | 听起来挺难的，每个周末都加班。 |
