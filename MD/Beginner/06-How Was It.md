# How Was It? 谈过去的状态和经历

## 1. was / were 形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：I / He / She / It + was | My first job interview **was** a disaster. | 我的第一次面试简直是灾难。 |
| 肯定：You / We / They + were | We **were** broke our first year in Beijing. | 在北京的第一年我们特别穷。 |
| 否定：wasn't / weren't | I **wasn't** ready for that exam, and it showed. | 那次考试我没准备好，分数也说明了。 |
| 疑问：Was / Were + 主语 | **Were** you a good student in high school? | 你高中是好学生吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **was**. / No, I **wasn't**. | 是。/ 不是。 |
| 特殊疑问 | **How** was your first week at the new job? | 新工作第一周怎么样？ |

## 2. 谈过去的身份、状态和感受

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I was a/an + 身份 | I **was** a shy kid — I ate lunch alone all through middle school. | 我小时候很内向，初中一直一个人吃午饭。 |
| I was + 形容词 | The subway **was** packed this morning, so I walked one stop. | 今早地铁太挤了，我提前一站走过去的。 |
| It was + 形容词 | My first paycheck **was** 2,800 yuan, and I thought I was rich. | 我第一份工资2800，当时觉得自己挺有钱。 |
| We were + 形容词 | **How** was the trip? — Great, but the hotel **was** tiny. | 旅行怎么样？——挺好，就是酒店特别小。 |

## 3. 连接词 because / so / and / or / but

| 连接词 | 作用 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **because** | 说原因 | I was tired **because** I stayed up watching a drama until 2 a.m. | 我累是因为追剧追到凌晨两点。 |
| **so** | 说结果 | I was tired, **so** I ordered takeout and went straight to bed. | 我太累了，所以点了外卖就直接睡了。 |
| **and** | 补充 | The trip was great, **and** the weather was perfect. | 旅行很棒，天气也很好。 |
| **or** | 给选择 | Was it your first job **or** your second? | 那是你第一份工作还是第二份？ |
| **but** | 说转折 | The test was difficult, **but** I passed. | 考试很难，但我过了。 |

## 4. makes me… 句型

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 名词 + makes me + 形容词 | My cat sleeping next to me **makes me** calm. | 我的猫睡在旁边让我很平静。 |
| 动名词 + makes me + 形容词 | Studying English **makes me** nervous on video calls. | 英语视频通话让我紧张。 |
| 动名词 + makes me + 动词 | Working overtime **makes me** hungry by 9 p.m. | 加班到九点我就饿。 |
| 人 + makes me + 形容词 | My mom **makes me** crazy on every call. | 我妈每次通话都让我头疼。 |

## 5. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Were you a good student in middle school? | 你初中是好学生吗？ |
| Do you have a driver's license? When did you get it? | 你有驾照吗？什么时候考的？ |
| Do you remember your first English class? | 你记得第一节英语课吗？ |
| How was your first job interview? | 你第一次面试怎么样？ |
| Do you remember your first paycheck? What did you buy? | 你记得第一份工资吗？买了什么？ |
| How was your first trip without your parents? | 你第一次自己去旅行怎么样？ |
| Were you shy as a kid? | 你小时候内向吗？ |
| What was the best day of your last year? | 去年最棒的一天是哪天？ |
| What makes you happy at work? | 工作中什么让你开心？ |
| What makes you tired these days? | 你最近为什么感到累？ |

## 6. Practice 练习

### 填空：was / were

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | My first job interview ______ a disaster — I forgot the company's name. |
| 2 | ______ you nervous before your first class? |
| 3 | We ______ broke in our first year, so we shared a 12-square-meter room. |
| 4 | The meeting ______ only ten minutes, which was a nice surprise. |
| 5 | My parents ______ both teachers before they retired. |
| 6 | It ______ (not) my first time in Shanghai, but I still got lost. |

### 填空：because / so / and / or / but

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | I was tired, ______ I ordered takeout and went straight to bed. |
| 2 | I was tired ______ I stayed up watching a drama until 2 a.m. |
| 3 | The trip was great, ______ the weather was perfect. |
| 4 | The test was hard, ______ I passed on the first try. |
| 5 | Was that your first job ______ your second? |

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | Studying English ______ (make) me nervous, especially on calls. |
| 2 | Sitting in traffic ______ (make) me tired before work even starts. |
| 3 | My cat sleeping next to me ______ (make) me calm. |
| 4 | My mom asking about marriage ______ (make) me crazy. |
| 5 | Working overtime ______ (make) me hungry. |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| my first job interview / disaster | My first job interview was a disaster. | 我第一次面试是场灾难。 |
| I / tired / so / ordered takeout | I was tired, so I ordered takeout and went to bed. | 我太累了，点外卖后直接睡了。 |
| the subway / packed / so / walked | The subway was packed, so I walked one stop. | 地铁太挤，我提前一站走过去。 |
| overtime / makes me / hungry | Working overtime makes me hungry. | 加班让我很饿。 |
