# Shopping and Returns 购物与退换

## 1. 进店和试穿

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm just looking, thanks. | **I'm just looking, thanks** — the clerk steps back. | 我只是随便看看，谢谢。 |
| Could I try this on? | **Could I try this on** — I'm a medium. | 我穿中码，能试穿吗？ |
| Where's the fitting room? | **Where's the fitting room** — there are five people. | 试衣间在哪？排了五个人。 |
| Do you have this in a medium? | **Do you have this in a medium** — this one's too big. | 这件有中码吗？太大了。 |
| Do you have a smaller size? | **Do you have a smaller size** in the blue one? | 有小一号的吗？蓝色的。 |
| Is this one on sale too? | **Is this one on sale too** — it's 30% off. | 这件也打折吗？ |
| Can I take two sizes in? | **Can I take two sizes in** and pick one? | 我能拿两个码进去挑吗？ |

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|

| It's a bit over my budget. | **It's a bit over my budget** — I'll wait for the sale. | 有点超预算了。 |
| I'll take it. | **I'll take it** — the medium fits perfectly. | 我要了，中码正好。 |
| Let me think about it. | **Let me think about it** on the way to lunch. | 让我再想想。 |

## 7. 面料、洗护和售后

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Is this cotton or polyester? | **Is this cotton or polyester** — it feels warm. | 这是棉的还是涤纶的？ |
| Can I machine wash it? | **Can I machine wash it** or dry-clean only? | 能机洗还是只能干洗？ |
| It shrank after one wash. | **It shrank after one wash** — down a size. | 洗一次就缩水，小了一码。 |

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Can I try on it? | Can I try it on? | 代词要放在 try 和 on 中间。 |
| How much is the price? | How much is it? | 问价格直接说 How much is it。 |
| This one is more cheap. | This one is cheaper. | 短单词比较级加 -er，不用 more。 |
| I want buy this. | I want to buy this. | want 后面接 to 加动词原形。 |
