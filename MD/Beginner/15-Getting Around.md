# Getting Around 交通出行

## 1. 问交通方式和回答

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How do you get to work? | **How do you get to work?** I never see you on Line 2. | 你怎么上班？我天天挤2号线。 |
| I take the subway. | **I take the subway** every morning at seven thirty. | 我每天早上七点半坐地铁。 |
| I ride a shared bike. | **I ride a shared bike** from the gate to the station. | 我从小区门口骑共享单车到地铁站。 |
| I drive. | **I drive** on rainy days — the subway is too packed. | 下雨天我开车，地铁太挤了。 |
| I walk. | **I walk** — my office is only fifteen minutes away. | 我走路，公司就十五分钟。 |
| It's a 20-minute ride. | **It's a 20-minute ride** if there's no traffic. | 不堵车的话二十分钟车程。 |
| How do you get to the airport? | **How do you get to the airport?** Subway, then express. | 你怎么去机场？地铁转快线。 |
| I take the bus. | **I take the bus** when the subway is packed at eight. | 八点地铁太挤我就坐公交。 |
| I carpool with a coworker. | **I carpool with a coworker** — we take turns driving. | 我和同事拼车，轮流开。 |

## 2. 坐地铁和公交

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Which line goes to the train station? | **Which line goes to the train station?** I'm new here. | 哪条线到火车站？我刚来这边。 |
| Where do I transfer? | **Where do I transfer?** I'm going to People's Square. | 我在哪儿换乘？我要去人民广场。 |
| How many stops is it? | **How many stops is it?** I don't want to miss mine. | 有几站？我不想坐过站。 |
| Does this bus go downtown? | **Does this bus go downtown?** I need the mall. | 这趟公交到市中心吗？我要去商场。 |
| Which exit should I take? | **Which exit should I take** for the night market? | 去夜市我该走哪个出口？ |
| Is this the right platform? | **Is this the right platform** for Line 10? | 这是10号线的站台吗？ |
| Do I need to tap out? | **Do I need to tap out** when I leave the station? | 出站还要再刷一次吗？ |
| The next train is in two minutes. | **The next train is in two minutes** — check the board. | 下一班还有两分钟，看屏幕。 |

| The driver is three minutes away. | **The driver is three minutes away** — I'm at the gate. | 司机还有三分钟到，我在门口。 |

| I'll pay by phone. | **I'll pay by phone** — the app already has my card. | 我用手机付，App里绑了卡。 |

| Can I use my phone to pay? | **Can I use my phone to pay** at the gate? | 闸机能用手机支付吗？ |

## 5. 路上状况和要多久

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How long does it take to get there? | **How long does it take to get there** by subway? | 坐地铁到那儿要多久？ |
| It takes about 40 minutes. | **It takes about 40 minutes** in the morning rush. | 早高峰大概要四十分钟。 |
| I'm stuck in traffic. | **I'm stuck in traffic** on the expressway again. | 我又堵在高架上了。 |
| The subway is packed at 8. | **The subway is packed at 8** — I can't even move. | 八点地铁挤爆了，动都动不了。 |
| I missed my stop. | **I missed my stop** — I was on my phone. | 我坐过站了，刚在看手机。 |
| I'll be there in ten minutes. | **I'll be there in ten minutes** — just parking. | 我十分钟到，正在停车。 |
| Is the road closed today? | **Is the road closed today?** There's a marathon. | 今天封路吗？有马拉松。 |
| The ring road is backed up. | **The ring road is backed up** — nothing is moving. | 环路堵死了，车都不动。 |
| The subway is running late. | **The subway is running late** — ten more minutes. | 地铁晚点了，还得十分钟。 |

## 7. 交通词汇和常见错误

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| subway | 地铁 | bus | 公交车 |
| high-speed train | 高铁 | train | 火车 |
| platform | 站台 | exit | 出口 |
| transfer | 换乘 | line | 线路 |

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I go to work by foot. | I go to work **on foot**. | 步行用 on foot。 |
| I wait the bus at the gate. | I **wait for** the bus at the gate. | wait 后面要加 for。 |
| The subway is too crowd. | The subway is too **crowded**. | 说拥挤用 crowded。 |
| Get off the taxi here. | Get **out of** the taxi here. | 出租车用 out of。 |
| I go to home by subway. | I take the subway **home**. | home 前不加 to。 |
