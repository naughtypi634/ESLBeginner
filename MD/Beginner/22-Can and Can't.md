# Can and Can't 会什么与能不能

## 1. can ／ can't 形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：can + 动词原形 | I **can** cook noodles, but that's about it. | 我会煮面，也就这样了。 |
| 否定：can't + 动词原形 | I **can't** fix my laptop, so I'm taking it in. | 我不会修电脑，只能拿去修。 |
| 疑问：Can + 主语 + 动词原形 | **Can** you help me carry this upstairs? | 你能帮我搬上楼吗？ |
| 简答：Yes ／ No | Yes, I **can**. ／ No, I **can't**. | 能。／不能。 |
| 特殊疑问 | **What** can you cook without a recipe? | 你不用菜谱会做什么菜？ |
| 能力对比：can ／ can't | I **can** cook rice, but I **can't** make soup. | 我会煮饭，但不会煲汤。 |
| 否定疑问：Can't + 主语 + 动词？ | **Can't** you just text him instead? | 你就不能发消息给他吗？ |
### can 后面用动词原形

| 正确说法 | 常见错误 |
|---|---|
| I can **swim**. | I can **to swim**. |
| He can **cook**. | He can **cooks**. |
| **Can** you drive? | **Do** you can drive? |
| I **can't** drive yet. | I **don't can** drive yet. |
## 2. 能力：会做什么

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| swim | 游泳 | drive | 开车 |
| cook | 做饭 | ride a bike | 骑自行车 |
| use Excel | 会用 Excel | speak a little Japanese | 会说一点日语 |
| fix a bike | 修自行车 | sing | 唱歌 |
| play badminton | 打羽毛球 | change a tire | 换轮胎 |
| take good photos | 会拍照 | use chopsticks | 会用筷子 |
| order takeout in English | 会用英语点外卖 | book a ticket online | 会在网上订票 |
| take the subway alone | 会自己坐地铁 | use self-checkout | 会用自助结账 |
| fix a printer jam | 会修打印机卡纸 | change a light bulb | 会换灯泡 |
| give a short talk | 会做简短汇报 | cook three dishes | 会做三个菜 |
| answer work calls | 能接工作电话 | unlock a shared bike | 会扫共享单车 |
| make dumplings | 会包饺子 | parallel park | 会侧方停车 |

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I can ... 会做 | I **can** change a tire in ten minutes. | 我十分钟能换个轮胎。 |
| I can't ... 不会 | I **can't** sing, I just mouth the words. | 我不会唱歌，只能对嘴型。 |
| Can you ...? 问对方 | **Can** you use Excel for this report? | 这份报表你会用 Excel 做吗？ |
| I can't ... at all 完全不会 | I **can't** drive at all, I take the subway. | 我完全不会开车，都坐地铁。 |
| Can he ／ she ...? 问第三人 | **Can she** drive to the airport herself? | 她能自己开车去机场吗？ |
## 3. 请求和许可

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you help me with this? | **Can you help me** with this? The lid is stuck. | 你能帮我弄一下吗？盖子拧不开。 |
| Can I borrow your charger? | **Can I borrow** your charger for ten minutes? | 我借你充电器用十分钟行吗？ |
| Can I sit here? | **Can I sit** here? Every other seat is taken. | 我能坐这儿吗？别的位子都有人了。 |
| Can I pay by phone? | **Can I pay** by phone? I forgot my wallet. | 我手机付行吗？钱包忘带了。 |
| Can I bring a friend? | **Can I bring** a friend to the party? | 我能带个朋友来聚会吗？ |
## 4. can 表示可能

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 可能：can + 动词原形 | It **can** get cold at night, bring a jacket. | 晚上可能会冷，带件外套。 |
| 不可能：can't | That **can't** be right, check the numbers again. | 那不可能，数字再核对一下。 |
| 不允许：can't | You **can't** park here after 7 a.m., they tow. | 早上七点后这儿不能停车，会被拖走。 |
### can't 的两种意思

| 意思 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 不可能（推测） | He **can't** be at the gym, he just texted me. | 他不可能在健身房，刚给我发消息。 |
| 不能、不允许 | You **can't** take photos in here, museum rule. | 这儿不能拍照，这是规定。 |
## 5. What can you do in + 地点

| 提问 | 回答 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What can you do in Chengdu? | You **can** eat hotpot and see pandas. | 在成都你能吃火锅、看熊猫。 |
| What can you do in 30 minutes? | I **can** cook noodles and shower. | 三十分钟我能煮面、冲个澡。 |
| What can you do on a rainy weekend? | I **can** stay in and finish that show. | 下雨的周末我能宅家追完剧。 |
## 6. 客气地请人帮忙：Could you ／ Would you mind

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Could you ...? | **Could you** send me the file before lunch? | 你能午饭前把文件发我吗？ |
| Would you mind ...? | **Would you mind** grabbing my package downstairs? | 你方便下楼帮我拿个快递吗？ |
| Do you think you could ...? | **Do you think you could** cover my shift Friday? | 周五你能替我上个班吗？ |
