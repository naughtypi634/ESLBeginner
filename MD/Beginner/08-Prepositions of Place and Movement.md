# Prepositions of Place and Movement 位置与方向介词

## 1. in / on / at 表地点

| 规则 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **in** + 空间、容器 | My keys are **in** the bag with the charger. | 钥匙在包里，和充电器放一起。 |
| **in** + 大地点 | My cousin lives **in** Chengdu now. | 我表妹现在住成都。 |
| **on** + 表面 | Your takeout is **on** the table by the door. | 你的外卖在靠门的桌上。 |
| **on** + 楼层 | We live **on** the 12th floor, facing south. | 我们住12楼，朝南。 |
| **on** + 墙面 | There's a calendar **on** the wall next to the AC. | 空调旁边的墙上挂着日历。 |
| **at** + 具体地点 | I'll wait **at** the door of the gym. | 我在健身房门口等你。 |
| **at** + 点位 | She's **at** the bus stop in front of the school. | 她在学校前面的公交站。 |
| **at** + 角落点 | Let's meet **at** the corner of the street. | 我们在街角碰头吧。 |

## 2. 方位说法

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| next to | 在……旁边 | beside | 在……旁边 |
| between | 在……之间 | across from | 在……对面 |
| behind | 在……后面 | in front of | 在……前面 |
| near | 在……附近 | far from | 离……远 |
| on the left | 在左边 | on the right | 在右边 |
| at the corner of | 在……拐角 | on the corner | 在街角 |
| inside | 在里面 | outside | 在外面 |
| upstairs | 在楼上 | downstairs | 在楼下 |
| on the top floor | 在顶楼 | at the back | 在后面 |
| in the middle | 在中间 | around the corner | 在拐角附近 |
| at the end of the street | 在街道尽头 | opposite | 在……对面 |
| along | 沿着 | past | 经过 |

## 3. 描述东西在哪

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| The + 东西 + is on the desk | My charger is **on** the desk next to my laptop. | 我的充电器在桌上，电脑旁边。 |
| The + 地点 + is across from + 地点 | The pharmacy is **across from** the bank, by the gate. | 药店在银行对面，在小区门口。 |
| There's a + 东西 + on the corner | **There's a** coffee shop **on the corner**. | 拐角有家咖啡店。 |
| My apartment is on the + 楼层 | My apartment is **on** the 6th floor, at the end. | 我家在6楼，走到头就是。 |
| The + 东西 + is in + 容器 | The keys are **in** my bag, under the umbrella. | 钥匙在包里，雨伞下面。 |
| The + 东西 + is behind + 地点 | The package locker is **behind** the gate, on the right. | 快递柜在大门后面，靠右。 |
| The + 地点 + is between A and B | The noodle place is **between** the bank and the pharmacy. | 面馆在银行和药店之间。 |
| The + 地点 + is upstairs | The gym is **upstairs**, next to the food court. | 健身房在楼上，美食广场旁边。 |

## 4. 移动方向介词

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| into | 进入 | out of | 出来 |
| onto | 到……上面 | off | 从……下来 |
| across | 穿过 | along | 沿着 |
| through | 穿过（内部） | up | 向上 |
| down | 向下 | past | 经过 |
| toward | 朝…… | around | 绕过 |
| over | 越过 | under | 从……下面 |
| back to | 回到 | up to | 走到…… |
| to | 到 | from | 从 |
| by | 经由 | down the street | 沿街 |

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I walk through + 地点 | I walk **through** the park to the subway. | 我穿过公园去地铁站。 |
| get into + 车 | She got **into** a taxi and left. | 她上了出租车走了。 |
| drive past + 地点 | We drove **past** your building on the way home. | 回家路上我们开过你那栋楼。 |
| run across + 地点 | He ran **across** the street to catch the bus. | 他跑过马路去赶公交。 |

## 5. 交通和日常搭配

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **on** the subway | I scroll WeChat **on the subway** every morning. | 我每天早上在地铁上刷微信。 |
| **on** the bus | He ate his baozi **on the bus**, standing. | 他站着在公交上吃包子。 |
| **in** a taxi | My boss took a work call **in a taxi**. | 老板在出租车上接了个工作电话。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I live in the 12th floor. | I live **on** the 12th floor. | 楼层用 on。 |
| I go to home after work. | I go **home** after work. | home 前不加 to。 |
| I arrived to Shanghai last night. | I arrived **in** Shanghai last night. | 大城市用 in。 |
| The bank is near of my home. | The bank is **near** my home. | near 后面不加 of。 |
| I'm on the way to home. | I'm **on the way home**. | way 后面直接跟 home。 |
| I got on the taxi at the gate. | I got **in** the taxi at the gate. | 出租车用 in，不用 on。 |

## 7. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| What's on the wall next to your desk? | 你桌子旁边的墙上有什么？ |
| What's right across from your building? | 你家楼正对面是什么？ |
| How do you get to work — subway or bus? | 你怎么去上班，坐地铁还是公交？ |
| What do you usually do on the subway? | 你在地铁上一般干什么？ |
| Where do you put your phone at night? | 你晚上把手机放哪儿？ |
