# Family and Relationships 家人与亲属
## 1. 介绍家人
| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 主语 + am / is / are | I**'m an only child**, so my mom calls me every night. | 我是独生子女，我妈每天打电话。 |
| 第三人称单数用 is | My sister **is** a nurse, but she wants to quit. | 我姐是护士，不过她打算辞职。 |
| 复数主语用 are | We **are** a family of four in three different cities. | 我们家四口人，分散在三个城市。 |
| 表语用形容词 | My parents **are** both retired and travel a lot now. | 我爸妈都退休了，现在常出去玩。 |
| 否定用 aren't | My parents **aren't** retired, so they both work. | 我爸妈没退休，还在上班。 |

## 3. 代词与物主代词
| 主格 | 形容词性物主 | 宾格 | 名词性物主 |
|---|---|---|---|
| I 我 | my 我的 | me 我 | mine 我的 |
| you 你 | your 你的 | you 你 | yours 你的 |
| he 他 | his 他的 | him 他 | his 他的 |
| she 她 | her 她的 | her 她 | hers 她的 |
| it 它 | its 它的 | it 它 | — |
| we 我们 | our 我们的 | us 我们 | ours 我们的 |
| they 他们 | their 他们的 | them 他们 | theirs 他们的 |

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| This apartment is **ours** — we bought it last year. | 这套房是我们的，去年买的。 |
| That blue umbrella by the door is **yours**, right? | 门口那把蓝伞是你的吧？ |
| The last piece of cake is **hers**, not mine. | 最后一块蛋糕是她的，不是我的。 |
| The cat is **theirs**, but the scooter is **his**. | 猫是他们的，但电动车是他的。 |

## 4. 谈家里的事
| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I have + 数量 + 亲属 | I **have** one brother, and he's still in college. | 我有一个弟弟，还在上大学。 |
| I'm the + 最高级 | I **am the oldest**, so everyone asks me for money. | 家里我最大，谁缺钱都找我要。 |
| live with + 人 | We **live with** my parents and split the rent. | 我们和爸妈一起住，房租平摊。 |
| live in + 地点 | My parents **live in** a small town near the highway. | 我爸妈住在高速边上的小镇。 |
| call + 人 + 时间 | I **call** my mom **every Sunday** night. | 我每周日晚上都给妈妈打电话。 |
| help take care of + 人 | I **help take care of** my grandma on weekends. | 周末我帮忙照顾奶奶。 |
| video call + 人 | I **video call** my parents on the way home. | 我下班路上跟爸妈视频。 |

## 5. 亲戚问话怎么答
| 提问 | 回答 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Are you married? | Not yet — **I'm still looking**. | 还没，还在找。 |
| Do you have kids? | One — **a three-year-old boy**. | 有一个，三岁的男孩。 |
| How old is your son? | He **just turned eight** last month. | 他上个月刚满八岁。 |
| What does your sister do? | She **works at a bank** downtown. | 她在市中心一家银行上班。 |
| When are you having a second one? | **That's a long story.** | 这事说来话长。 |

## 6. 常见错误
| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I have a older brother. | I have **an** older brother. | older 前用 an |
| He is my brother older. | He is my **older** brother. | 年龄词放在名词前 |
| This is me mother. | This is **my** mother. | 名词前用 my |
| I am only child. | I am **an** only child. | only child 前加 an |

## 7. 聊家人常用的小说法
| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| like my aunt | **Like my aunt**, I talk really fast. | 像我姑姑，我说话特别快。 |
| an only child | **As an only child**, I get all the attention. | 作为独生子女，全家都疼我一个。 |
| live with my parents | I still **live with my parents** to save money. | 为了省钱，我还跟爸妈住。 |
| have a second kid | We might **have a second kid** next year. | 我们可能明年要二胎。 |
