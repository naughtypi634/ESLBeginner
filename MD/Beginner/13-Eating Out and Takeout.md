# Eating Out and Takeout 点餐与外卖

## 1. 进门和订位

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you have a table for two? | **Do you have a table for two?** We just got off work. | 有两人桌吗？我们刚下班。 |
| I have a reservation under Wang. | **I have a reservation under Wang**, for six thirty. | 我订了位，姓王，六点半。 |
| How long is the wait? | **How long is the wait?** This place is packed. | 要等多久？这家店爆满。 |
| Can we sit outside? | **Can we sit outside?** It's nice out tonight. | 能坐外面吗？今晚天气挺好。 |
| Just one, please. | **Just one, please.** I'm eating alone tonight. | 就一位，我今晚一个人吃。 |
| Do you take walk-ins? | **Do you take walk-ins?** We didn't reserve. | 没订位能进吗？我们没预约。 |
| Can we get a booth? | **Can we get a booth?** We want to talk. | 能给个卡座吗？我们想聊天。 |
| Two adults and one kid. | **Two adults and one kid**, please. | 两个大人一个小孩，谢谢。 |

## 2. 点菜

| Could we get one more bowl of rice? | **Could we get one more bowl of rice?** | 能再来一碗米饭吗？ |
| What's your signature dish? | **What's your signature dish?** I'll try that. | 招牌菜是什么？我试试那个。 |

| Less oil, please. | **Less oil, please** — I'm on a diet. | 少放点油，我在控制饮食。 |
| Can you make it mild? | **Can you make it mild?** My kid is eating too. | 能做微辣吗？我孩子也吃。 |
| No ice, please. | **No ice, please** — I have a sore throat. | 去冰，我嗓子疼。 |
| No MSG, please. | **No MSG, please** — I get thirsty. | 不要放味精，我吃了口渴。 |

| Can I get it without garlic? | **Can I get it without garlic?** I have a meeting. | 能不放蒜吗？我一会儿要开会。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I want eat noodles. | I **want to eat** noodles. | want 后面接 to do。 |
| Can I have a rice? | Can I have **a bowl of rice**? | 米饭论碗，不说 a rice。 |
| I am allergic of peanuts. | I am allergic **to** peanuts. | 固定说法 be allergic to。 |
| Please no put cilantro. | **No cilantro, please.** | 忌口用 No…, please. |
| This is too spicy for me eat. | This is too spicy **for me to eat**. | for sb to do 结构。 |
