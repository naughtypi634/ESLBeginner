# Weather and Small Talk 天气与寒暄

## 1. 问天气

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What's the weather like today? | **What's the weather like** today? Coat or not? | 今天天气怎么样？要穿外套吗？ |
| Is it supposed to rain? | **Is it supposed to rain** after lunch? | 午饭后会下雨吗？ |
| How's the weather in Shanghai? | **How's the weather in Shanghai** this week? | 上海这周天气怎么样？ |
| What's the temperature? | **What's the temperature** outside right now? | 现在外面多少度？ |
| Is it always this hot in July? | **Is it always this hot in July** in Wuhan? | 武汉七月总这么热吗？ |
| Is it going to snow tonight? | **Is it going to snow** tonight — flight at six? | 今晚会下雪吗？我六点的航班。 |
| Should I bring a jacket? | **Should I bring a jacket** for the subway? | 坐地铁要带外套吗？ |
| How cold is it outside? | **How cold is it outside** in Harbin? | 哈尔滨外面多冷？ |
| What's the high today? | **What's the high today** — 38 degrees? | 今天最高多少度？ |

## 3. 说天气

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It's freezing today. | It's **freezing** today — my phone died outside. | 今天冻死了，手机在外面关机。 |
| It's pouring outside. | It's **pouring** — the delivery rider is soaked. | 下暴雨了，外卖骑手全淋湿了。 |
| It's supposed to clear up. | It's **supposed to clear up** after lunch. | 午后天应该会放晴。 |
| It's so humid today. | It's so **humid** my shirt sticks to me. | 太潮了，衬衫都黏在身上。 |
| The temperature dropped. | The **temperature dropped** ten degrees overnight. | 一夜之间降了十度。 |
| It's drizzling. | It's **drizzling** — grab a light jacket. | 在下毛毛雨，带件薄外套。 |
| The wind is picking up. | The **wind is picking up** — typhoon tonight. | 风越来越大了，今晚台风。 |
| It feels colder than it looks. | It **feels colder** than it looks — 2 degrees. | 体感比看起来冷，才2度。 |

## 4. 拿天气开头聊天

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Nice weather today, huh? | **Nice weather today, huh**? Let's eat outside. | 今天天气不错啊，在外面吃吧。 |
| It finally cooled down. | **It finally cooled down** — no AC tonight. | 终于凉快了，今晚不用开空调。 |
| Can you believe this rain? | **Can you believe this rain** — all week long. | 这雨没完没了，下了一整周。 |
| This heat is unreal. | **This heat is unreal** — 39 degrees again. | 热得离谱，又是39度。 |
| At least the smog cleared up. | **At least the smog cleared up** — blue sky! | 还好雾霾散了，天蓝了。 |
| Finally some sun. | **Finally some sun** — let's take a walk. | 总算出太阳了，出去走走。 |
| Winter came early this year. | **Winter came early** this year — it's snowing. | 今年入冬早，都下雪了。 |
| Supposed to snow tomorrow. | **Supposed to snow** tomorrow — saw it online. | 明天说要下雪，网上看到的。 |

## 5. 天气和安排

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Take an umbrella with you. | **Take an umbrella with you** — typhoon tonight. | 带把伞，今晚有台风。 |
| The flight might be delayed. | **The flight might be delayed** — heavy fog. | 航班可能晚点，雾太大了。 |
| Let's stay in today. | **Let's stay in today** — order some hot pot. | 今天不出门了，点个火锅吧。 |
| I got soaked on the way here. | **I got soaked on the way here** — no umbrella. | 来的路上淋透了，没带伞。 |
| It's too hot to eat outside. | **It's too hot to eat outside** — order in? | 太热了，别在外面吃，点外卖。 |

| Hang the laundry inside. | **Hang the laundry inside** — it's going to rain. | 衣服晾屋里，要下雨了。 |

## 6. 天气预报的说法

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| a 60% chance of rain | There's a **60% chance of rain** after six. | 傍晚起降水概率六成。 |
| turning sunny tomorrow | Tomorrow is **turning sunny** by noon. | 明天中午转晴。 |
| a drop of 8 degrees | We're getting **a drop of 8 degrees** tonight. | 今晚降温8度。 |
| it feels like 30 | It says **it feels like 30** outside. | 体感温度说是30度。 |
