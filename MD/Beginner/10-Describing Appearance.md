# Describing Appearance 描述外貌

## 1. 三种描述外貌的句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| He / She **is** + 形容词 | My cousin **is** really tall — he has to duck in our elevator. | 我表弟特别高，进我们家电梯得低头。 |
| He / She **has** + 名词 | The new hire **has** round glasses and a green jacket. | 新来的同事戴圆框眼镜，穿绿外套。 |
| He / She **looks** + 形容词 | You **look** exhausted — did you sleep at all? | 你看起来很累，到底睡了没？ |

| 区别 | 说明 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| is + 形容词 | 天生的、稳定的特征 | She **is** tall. / He **is** slim. |
| has + 名词 | 身体部位、配饰 | She **has** long hair. / He **has** a beard. |
| looks + 形容词 | 此刻给人的感觉，可能只是暂时的 | You **look** tired. / She **looks** happy today. |

## 3. look like 句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| look like + 名词 | My mom **looks like** her sister. | 我妈和她姐姐长得很像。 |
| look like + 名词（人/物） | He **looks like** a young version of his dad. | 他就像他爸的年轻版。 |
| look like + 句子 | He **looks like** he's about to fall asleep in this meeting. | 他看起来下一秒就要在会里睡着了。 |
| look like + 句子 | She **looks like** she just came from the gym. | 她看起来刚从健身房出来。 |

## 4. 表达偏好

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I prefer A to B | I **prefer** coffee **to** tea in the morning. | 早上我更喜欢咖啡。 |
| I prefer + 动名词 | I **prefer** texting to calling. | 我更喜欢发消息而不是打电话。 |
| I prefer + 动名词 + to + 动名词 | I **prefer** eating out **to** cooking after a long day. | 累了一天，我更愿意出去吃而不是做饭。 |
| I like A more than B | I **like** short hair **more than** long hair — it's easier to manage. | 短发长发我更喜欢短发，好打理。 |
| I'd rather A than B | **I'd rather** walk **than** squeeze onto a packed bus. | 我宁愿走路，也不愿挤公交。 |
| Would you rather…? | **Would you rather** take the subway **or** ride a shared bike? | 你更愿意坐地铁还是骑共享单车？ |

## 5. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Do you look more like your mom or your dad? | 你长得更像妈妈还是爸爸？ |
| Who's the tallest person you know? | 你认识的人里谁最高？ |
| Do you wear glasses or contacts? | 你戴眼镜还是隐形？ |
| What do you notice first about a person? | 你最先注意到一个人的什么？ |
| Has your look changed in the last five years? | 你最近五年形象变了吗？ |
| Do people say you look younger or older than you are? | 别人说你显年轻还是显老？ |
| Do you prefer coffee or tea in the morning? | 早上你更喜欢咖啡还是茶？ |
| Would you rather be tall or have great hair? | 你更想要高个子还是好头发？ |
| Do you care about clothes and style? | 你在意穿着打扮吗？ |
| Do you prefer eating out or cooking at home? | 你更喜欢下馆子还是在家做？ |
