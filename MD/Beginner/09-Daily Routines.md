# Daily Routines 日常作息

## 1. 一般现在时形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：动词原形 | I **get up** at 7 and I'm out the door by 8. | 我七点起床，八点出门。 |
| 第三人称加 -s | My roommate **works** from home on Fridays. | 我室友周五在家办公。 |
| 否定：don't ／ doesn't | I **don't drink** coffee after 3 p.m. | 我下午三点后不喝咖啡。 |
| 疑问：Do ／ Does | **Do you nap** on the weekend? | 你周末午睡吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **do**. ／ No, I **don't**. | 午睡。／不午睡。 |
| 第三人称否定：doesn't | My husband **doesn't eat** breakfast on workdays. | 我老公工作日不吃早饭。 |
| 第三人称疑问：Does | **Does your mom call** you every Sunday? | 你妈每周日都打给你吗？ |
| 特殊疑问 | **What time do you clock in** at your office? | 你们几点打卡上班？ |

### 第三人称单数变化

| 规则 | 动词 → 第三人称 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般加 -s | check → checks | Mom **checks** the family group chat after dinner. |
| 以 s／x／ch／sh 结尾加 -es | watch → watches | My dad **watches** the news while he cooks. |
| 辅音 + y 变 i 加 -es | study → studies | My sister **studies** for her CPA exam nightly. |
| have → has | have → has | My brother **has** two kids and no free time. |
| go → goes | go → goes | My dad **goes** to the market before 7 a.m. |
| do → does | do → does | My coworker **does** yoga in the office at noon. |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| She work from home. | She **works** from home. | 第三人称单数加 -s。 |
| He don't like coffee. | He **doesn't** like coffee. | 第三人称用 doesn't。 |
| My mom watch the news at night. | My mom **watches** the news at night. | ch 结尾加 -es。 |
| She go to bed at 11. | She **goes** to bed at 11. | go 变 goes。 |
| I eat breakfast at the morning. | I eat breakfast **in the morning**. | 早上用 in，不用 at。 |
| I do a shower before bed. | I **take a shower** before bed. | 洗澡用 take a shower。 |
| I go to gym every day. | I go to **the** gym every day. | 健身房前加 the。 |
