# Time, Days and Dates 时间、星期与日期

## 1. 问时间和说时间

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What time is it? | **What time is it?** — I think I'm late. | 几点了？——我可能迟到了。 |
| Do you have the time? | **Do you have the time?** My phone just died. | 你知道现在几点吗？我手机没电了。 |
| It's 7:30. / It's 7:15. | **It's 7:15** — I have to clock in at 8. | 七点一刻了，我八点要打卡。 |
| It's half past seven. | **It's half past seven** — the subway is packed. | 七点半了，地铁挤满了。 |
| It's a quarter to nine. | **It's a quarter to nine** — the meeting started. | 八点四十五了，会已经开始了。 |
| Sharp at 8. | My interview is **at 8 sharp**, so don't be late. | 面试是八点整，别迟到。 |

### 说时间的规则

| 写法 | 读法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00 | seven o'clock | My alarm goes off **at seven o'clock** on weekdays. |
| 7:30 | half past seven | I get home **at half past seven** on a good day. |
| 7:15 | a quarter past seven | The delivery came **at a quarter past seven**. |
| 7:45 | a quarter to eight | The train leaves **at a quarter to eight**. |

## 2. 一天里的时间段

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| in the morning | 早上 | in the afternoon | 下午 |
| in the evening | 傍晚、晚上 | at night | 夜里 |
| at noon | 中午 | at midnight | 半夜 |
| on time | 准时 | running late | 要迟到了 |

| 搭配 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| in + the morning | I catch up on WeChat **in the morning** on the subway. | 我早上在地铁上刷微信。 |
| at + noon / night | **At night** I finally get time to study English. | 到了晚上我才有点时间学英语。 |
| at + 7:30 | My shift starts **at 7:30**, and I clock in at 7:25. | 我七点半上班，七点二十五打卡。 |
| on + Wednesday | We have a team sync **on Wednesday** at 10. | 我们周三十点开团队同步会。 |

## 3. 星期

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 周一 | Tuesday | 周二 |
| Wednesday | 周三 | Thursday | 周四 |
| Friday | 周五 | Saturday | 周六 |

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| on + 星期几 | We have a stand-up **on Monday** at 10. | 我们周一十点开站会。 |
| on the weekend | I sleep in and order takeout **on the weekend**. | 周末我睡到自然醒，然后点外卖。 |
| every + 星期几 | I go to the gym **every Wednesday** after work. | 我每周三下班后去健身房。 |

## 5. 约时间

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What time works for you? | **What time works for you?** — I'm free after 6. | 你几点方便？——我六点后都行。 |
| Are you free on Friday? | **Are you free on Friday?** Let's grab hotpot. | 你周五有空吗？去吃火锅。 |
| Let's meet at 6. | **Let's meet at 6** by the subway exit. | 咱们六点在地铁口见。 |

## 6. 说时间段的常用说法

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| in 20 minutes | The high-speed train leaves **in 20 minutes**. | 高铁二十分钟后开。 |
| half an hour | I'll be there **in half an hour**, after this call. | 我半小时后到，这个电话打完就走。 |
| all day | My phone was on silent **all day**, so I missed the group chat. | 我一整天手机静音，错过了群消息。 |
