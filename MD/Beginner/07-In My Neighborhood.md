# In My Neighborhood 附近有什么

## 1. there is / there are

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| There's a + 单数 | **There's a** convenience store downstairs. | 楼下就有家便利店。 |
| There are + 复数 | **There are** two subway lines near my place. | 我家附近有两条地铁线。 |
| 否定：There isn't a | **There isn't a** gym nearby. | 附近没有健身房。 |
| 疑问：Is there a…? | **Is there a** pharmacy around here? | 这附近有药店吗？ |
| 简答 | **Yes, there is.** / **No, there isn't.** | 有。／没有。 |
| 复数否定：There aren't any | **There aren't any** bus stops near my apartment. | 新公寓附近没有公交站。 |
| There's no + 名词 | **There's no** parking lot by my building. | 我这栋楼旁边没有停车场。 |
| How many … are there? | **How many** coffee shops **are there** on this street? | 这条街上有多少家咖啡店？ |
| Are there any…? | **Are there any** noodle places around here? | 附近有面馆吗？ |
| There's … across from … | **There's a** gym **across from** the clinic. | 诊所对面有家健身房。 |

## 3. 描述附近有什么

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It's right downstairs. | **It's right downstairs** — I go down in slippers. | 就在楼下，趿着拖鞋就去。 |
| It's a five-minute walk. | **It's a five-minute walk** to the wet market. | 走到菜市场五分钟。 |
| It's across from … | **It's across from the bank** — you can't miss it. | 就在银行对面，很好找。 |
| It's next to … | **It's next to the noodle place**, by the gate. | 就在面馆旁边，小区门口。 |
| There's one around the corner. | **There's one around the corner** — two minutes away. | 拐角就有一个，两分钟。 |
| It's on the first floor of the mall. | **It's on the first floor of the mall**, by the elevator. | 在商场一楼，电梯旁边。 |
| It's between … and … | **It's between the bakery and the bank** on the corner. | 就在面包店和银行中间。 |
| It's right by the subway exit. | **It's right by the subway exit** — takes one minute. | 就在地铁口旁边，一分钟就到。 |
| It's a ten-minute bike ride. | **It's a ten-minute bike ride** to the big supermarket. | 骑车十分钟到大超市。 |
| It's just past the … | **It's just past the** print shop. | 就在打印店过去一点。 |

## 5. 问路前先问有没有

| 提问 | 回答 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Is there a subway station near here? | Yes — Line 4, one block away. | 这附近有地铁站吗？ |
| Where's the closest pharmacy? | There's one across from the bank. | 最近的药店在哪？ |
| Is it far? | No, about five minutes on foot. | 远吗？ |
| Is there a phone repair shop around here? | Yes, right by the compound gate. | 这附近有修手机的吗？ |
| Is there a 24-hour convenience store here? | Yes, at the corner past the bank. | 这儿有24小时便利店吗？ |
