# Frequency 频率副词与频率表达

## 1. 频率副词从高到低

| 频率 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **always** 100% | I **always** put my phone on the charger before bed. | 我睡前总把手机放上充电座。 |
| **almost always** 95% | My mom **almost always** calls me on Saturday morning. | 我妈周六早上几乎总会打来。 |
| **usually** 90% | I **usually** order groceries on the app at night. | 我一般晚上在App上买菜。 |
| **often** 70% | She **often** works late before a product release. | 上新前她经常加班。 |
| **sometimes** 50% | I **sometimes** take the subway instead of biking. | 我有时坐地铁，不骑车。 |
| **occasionally** 30% | We **occasionally** grab hot pot after Friday standup. | 周五例会后我们偶尔吃火锅。 |
| **rarely ／ hardly ever** 10% | I **rarely** use cash — I pay by phone now. | 我很少用现金，都手机付。 |
| **never** 0% | I **never** check work messages on weekends. | 我周末从不看工作消息。 |

## 2. 频率副词放哪里

| 位置 | 规则 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be 动词之后 | 主语 + be + 副词 | My roommate **is always** up before 6 a.m. |
| 实义动词之前 | 主语 + 副词 + 动词 | She **often** grabs noodles near the office. |
| 助动词和实义动词之间 | 助动词 + 副词 + 实义动词 | I **don't usually** eat breakfast on workdays. |
| 疑问句里 | 助动词 + 主语 + 副词 + 动词 | **Do you often** work on the weekend? |
| 简答和短句里 | 副词可放句首或句尾 | **Sometimes** I walk. ／ I walk **sometimes**. |

## 3. 频率短语

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| every day | 每天 | every other day | 隔天 |
| once a day | 一天一次 | twice a day | 一天两次 |
| once a week | 一周一次 | twice a week | 一周两次 |
| once a month | 一月一次 | three times a month | 一月三次 |
| every Monday | 每周一 | every weekend | 每个周末 |
| every other weekend | 隔周周末 | a few times a week | 一周几次 |
| most days | 大多数日子 | most weekends | 大多数周末 |
| every couple of weeks | 每两周左右 | a couple of times a year | 一年两三次 |
| now and then | 偶尔 | every now and then | 时不时 |
| from time to time | 时不时 | once in a while | 偶尔 |
| all the time | 一直 | on and off | 断断续续 |
| almost never | 几乎从不 | twice as often | 两倍频繁 |

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| once a + 时间单位 | I check the group chat **once a day**. | 我一天看一次群消息。 |
| twice a + 时间单位 | We order takeout **twice a week**, usually Friday. | 我们一周两次外卖，一般周五。 |
| three times a + 时间单位 | I call my parents **three times a week**. | 我一周给爸妈打三次电话。 |
| 数字 + times a + 时间单位 | She jogs **four times a week** before work. | 她一周跑四次，都在上班前。 |
| every + 时间单位 | The gym is packed **every Monday** night. | 健身房每周一晚上都爆满。 |
| a 可以换成 per | Take this **twice per day** after meals. | 这药一天吃两次，饭后。 |

## 4. How often 句型

| 提问 | 回答 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **How often do you** work out? | **Three times a week** after work. | 你多久健身一次？——一周三次。 |
| **How often does she** visit her parents? | **About once a month**, on a weekend. | 她多久看一次父母？——大约一月一次。 |
| **How often do you** eat out? | **Twice a week**, with coworkers. | 你多久下馆子一次？——一周两次。 |
| **How often do you** order takeout? | **Maybe three times a week**. | 你多久点一次外卖？——一周三次左右。 |
| **How often do you** go back home? | **Twice a year**, usually for Spring Festival. | 你多久回一次家？——一年两次。 |
| **How often do you** check your phone? | **Every five minutes**, honestly. | 你多久看一次手机？——五分钟一次。 |
| **How often do you** call your mom? | **Almost every day**, on the way home. | 你多久给妈妈打一次电话？——几乎每天。 |
| **How often do you** eat breakfast? | **Never** — I'm out the door by 7:30. | 你多久吃一次早饭？——从不。 |

## 5. 程度和口头说法

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| on and off | I go to the gym **on and off** — never for long. | 我健身断断续续，坚持不了几天。 |
| barely ever | I **barely ever** order takeout now — I cook. | 我现在几乎不点外卖了，都自己做。 |
| every five minutes | He checks his phone **every five minutes**. | 他五分钟就看一次手机。 |
| maybe twice a month | I eat out **maybe twice a month**, that's it. | 我一个月大概下两次馆子。 |
| hardly ever … anymore | I **hardly ever** take the bus anymore. | 我现在几乎不坐公交了。 |
| always the last one | She **is always the last one** to leave the office. | 她总是最后一个离开办公室的。 |
| all the time | He's on his phone **all the time** at meals. | 他吃饭时一直盯着手机。 |

## 6. 和频率有关的时态

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 | 讲习惯和固定安排 | I **usually** get to the office before 9. |
| every day ／ every Monday | 频率短语放句尾 | I drink coffee **every day** before work. |
| used to | 以前常做，现在不做了 | I **used to** stay up late on weekends. |

## 7. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I always am late. | I **am always** late. | be 动词放在频率副词前。 |
| I go always to the gym. | I **always go** to the gym. | 实义动词前放频率副词。 |
| I go to gym twice a week. | I go to **the** gym twice a week. | 健身房前要加 the。 |
| I every day drink coffee. | I drink coffee **every day**. | 频率短语放句尾。 |
| I used to go there, but now I don't go always. | …but now I **hardly ever** go there. | 讲现在的频率用 hardly ever。 |
| She don't always cook. | She **doesn't always** cook. | 第三人称否定用 doesn't。 |
