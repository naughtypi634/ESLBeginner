# At the Doctor's and Pharmacy 看病买药

## 1. 说症状

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I **have** a + 症状 | I **have** a headache — it started after lunch. | 我头疼，午饭后开始的。 |
| My + 部位 + **hurts** | My throat **hurts** and I can't swallow. | 我嗓子疼，咽东西都疼。 |
| I've **had** a fever since… | I've **had** a fever since last night. | 我从昨晚就开始发烧了。 |
| I **feel** + 形容词 | I **feel** dizzy when I stand up too fast. | 我起猛了就头晕。 |
| It **hurts** right here. | It **hurts** right here, under my ribs. | 就这儿疼，肋骨下面。 |
| I've **been** + 动名词 | I've **been** coughing all week, at night. | 我咳了一整周，都是晚上。 |
| I'm **allergic** to + 东西 | I'm **allergic** to seafood — my lips swell up. | 我对海鲜过敏，嘴唇会肿。 |
| I **sprained** my + 部位 | I **sprained** my ankle running for the bus. | 我赶公交把脚崴了。 |
| I **can't** sleep at night | I **can't** sleep at night — I keep scrolling. | 我晚上睡不着，一直刷手机。 |
| I **think** I'm getting sick | I **think** I'm getting sick after the gym. | 我健身回来感觉要病了。 |

## 3. 挂号看病

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to see a doctor. | **I'd like to see a doctor** — I've felt bad for days. | 我想看医生，难受好几天了。 |
| Do I need an appointment? | **Do I need an appointment**, or can I walk in? | 需要预约吗，还是直接来？ |
| Where's the registration desk? | **Where's the registration desk?** I need to sign in. | 挂号处在哪？我得先登记。 |
| I have insurance. | **I have insurance** — here's my card and ID. | 我有医保，这是我的卡和身份证。 |
| How long is the wait? | **How long is the wait?** I have to get back to work. | 要等多久？我还得回去上班。 |
| Can I see a specialist? | Can I **see a specialist** about my knee? | 我能看专科吗？膝盖的毛病。 |
| What time do you open? | What time do you **open**? I want to come early. | 你们几点开门？我想早点来。 |

## 4. 买药

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you have anything for a cough? | **Do you have anything for a cough?** I can't sleep. | 有治咳嗽的药吗？我睡不着。 |
| I need some painkillers. | **I need some painkillers** for my back. | 我要点止痛药，治背疼。 |
| How often should I take it? | **How often should I take it** — before meals? | 多久吃一次？饭前吃吗？ |
| Is this over the counter? | **Is this over the counter**, or by prescription? | 这是非处方药还是要处方？ |
| Do I need a prescription for this? | **Do I need a prescription for this** medicine? | 这个药需要处方吗？ |
| Do you have a cheaper one? | Do you have a **cheaper one**? This is pricey. | 有便宜点的吗？这个太贵。 |
| Can I use my insurance card? | **Can I use my insurance card** for this? | 这个能刷医保卡吗？ |

## 5. 医生和药师怎么说

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Take it twice a day after meals. | **Take it twice a day after meals** — morning and night. | 一天两次，饭后吃，早晚各一次。 |
| Drink more water and get some rest. | **Drink more water and get some rest** for two days. | 多喝水，休息两天。 |
| Come back if it doesn't get better. | **Come back if it doesn't get better** in three days. | 三天还不好就再来。 |
| You need a blood test. | **You need a blood test** — go to the second floor. | 你需要验血，去二楼。 |
| Avoid spicy food for a few days. | **Avoid spicy food for a few days** — no hotpot. | 这几天别吃辣，别吃火锅。 |
| I'll write you a prescription. | **I'll write you a prescription** for it. | 我给你开个处方。 |
| Your results are normal. | **Your results are normal** — no treatment needed. | 结果正常，不用治。 |

## 6. 疼痛的程度和时间

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It's not that bad. | **It's not that bad** — I can still finish work. | 不太严重，我还能干完活。 |
| It comes and goes. | The pain **comes and goes** — worse at night. | 一阵一阵的，晚上更疼。 |
| It started last night. | **It started last night** after hotpot. | 从昨天晚上开始的，吃完火锅。 |
| It's getting worse. | **It's getting worse** — I can't eat anything. | 越来越疼，什么都吃不下。 |
| It hurts a lot. | **It hurts a lot** when I take a deep breath. | 一深呼吸就特别疼。 |
