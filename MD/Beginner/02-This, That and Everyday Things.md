# This, That and Everyday Things 指认身边的东西

## 1. this ／ that ／ these ／ those

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| this 近处一个 | **This is** my phone — the one in my hand. | 这是我手机，手里这个。 |
| that 远处一个 | **That's** the elevator, next to the coffee stand. | 那是电梯，咖啡摊旁边。 |
| these 近处多个 | **These are** my keys, right here in my pocket. | 这些是我的钥匙，就在口袋里。 |
| those 远处多个 | **Those are** my coworkers, waiting by the gate. | 那些是我同事，在门口等着。 |
| 打电话用 this | **Hi, this is** Ming — can you hear me now? | 喂，我是Ming，能听见吗？ |
## 2. a ／ an ／ 复数

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| a + 辅音音开头 | I need **a** charger for my phone. | 我需要一个手机充电器。 |
| an + 元音音开头 | She left **an** umbrella in the office. | 她把伞落在办公室了。 |
| 复数加 -s ／ -es | She packed **three boxes** and **two power banks**. | 她装了三箱、两个充电宝。 |
| 不规则复数 | **Two knives** are missing from the kitchen. | 厨房少了两把刀。 |
| 看读音定 a ／ an | It took **an hour** to get to **a university** downtown. | 去市中心的大学花了一小时。 |

## 5. 指认和找东西

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Which one? | **Which one** is yours — the black or the gray? | 哪个是你的，黑的还是灰的？ |
| The one on the table. | **The one on the table** is mine. | 桌上那个是我的。 |
| Is this yours? | **Is this yours?** It was on my desk. | 这是你的吗？在我桌上。 |
## 6. 买东西和过安检时指东西

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| This one, please. | **This one, please** — the blue one. | 要这个，蓝色的。 |
| Can I see that one? | **Can I see that one** on the shelf? | 那个能给我看看吗？架子上那个。 |
| Not this one. | **Not this one — that one**, the bigger one. | 不是这个，是那个大点的。 |
## 7. 数量与量词搭配

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| a bottle of | I grabbed **a bottle of** water at the corner store. | 我在街角商店拿了瓶水。 |
| two cups of | She picked up **two cups of** coffee for the team. | 她给团队买了两杯咖啡。 |
| three orders of | We ordered **three orders of** fries to share. | 我们点了三份薯条分着吃。 |
