# Can and Can't 会做什么和能不能做

## 1. can / can't 形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：can + 动词原形 | I **can** drive, but I've never owned a car. | 我会开车，但从没买过车。 |
| 否定：can't + 动词原形 | I **can't** parallel park, so I park three blocks away. | 我不会侧方停车，只能停三个路口外。 |
| 疑问：Can + 主语 + 动词原形 | **Can** you cook anything besides instant noodles? | 除了泡面你还会做别的吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **can**. / No, I **can't**. | 会。/ 不会。 |
| 特殊疑问 | **What** can you cook? | 你会做什么菜？ |

| 正确说法 | 常见错误 |
|---|---|
| I can **swim**. | I can **to swim**. |
| He can **cook**. | He can **cooks**. |
| **Can** you drive? | **Do** you can drive? |
| I **can't** drive. | I **don't can** drive. |

## 2. 能力：会做什么

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| drive | 开车 | swim | 游泳 |
| cook | 做饭 | ride a bike | 骑自行车 |
| play the guitar | 弹吉他 | play the piano | 弹钢琴 |
| sing | 唱歌 | dance | 跳舞 |
| draw | 画画 | take good photos | 拍照 |
| make dumplings | 包饺子 | use a wok | 用炒锅 |
| fix a bike | 修自行车 | change a tire | 换轮胎 |
| code | 写代码 | give a speech | 演讲 |

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| I **can** make dumplings, but only the ugly ones — mine always fall apart. | 我会包饺子，但只会包丑的，我的总是破。 |
| My grandpa **can** still ride a bike at 78, and he beats me up the hill. | 我爷爷78岁还能骑车，上坡比我还快。 |
| I **can't** swim, so I sit by the pool with my phone when the team goes. | 我不会游泳，所以同事去泳池我都坐在边上玩手机。 |
| My roommate **can** fall asleep in ten seconds — I've timed him. | 我室友十秒就能睡着，我掐过表。 |
| **Can** you take good photos? Mine always come out blurry. | 你会拍照吗？我拍的总是糊的。 |

## 3. 请求与许可

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you + 动词? 请求帮忙 | **Can you** pick up my package on your way home? | 你回家路上能帮我取个快递吗？ |
| Can you + 动词? 改约 | **Can you** meet at 6 at the subway exit instead of the office? | 我们能改到6点在地铁口见吗？ |
| Can you + 动词? 换班 | **Can you** cover my shift on Saturday? I'll take yours next week. | 周六你能帮我顶个班吗？下周我替你。 |
| Can I + 动词? 征求许可 | **Can I** borrow your charger for ten minutes? | 我借用一下充电器，十分钟。 |
| Can I + 动词? 付款 | **Can I** pay by phone here? | 这儿能用手机支付吗？ |

## 4. can 表示可能

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| **Can** you come to the team dinner on Friday? | 周五的团建聚餐你能来吗？ |
| **Can** we do it next week instead? This week is packed. | 能改到下周吗？这周排满了。 |
| Sorry, I **can't** make it tonight — I'm on deadline. | 不好意思今晚去不了，我在赶截止日期。 |
| I **can** drop it off on my way to work tomorrow. | 我明天上班路上可以顺路送过去。 |

### can't 的两种意思

| 意思 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 做不到 | I **can't** lift that box alone — it's full of books. | 那个箱子我一个人搬不动，全是书。 |
| 不允许 / 不可能 | You **can't** park here after 7 a.m. — they tow. | 早上七点后这儿不能停车，会被拖走。 |

## 5. What can you do in + 地点

| 提问 | 回答 |
|---|---|
| What can you do in Chengdu? | You **can** eat hotpot every night and still spend under 50 yuan. |
| What can you do on a rainy weekend? | I **can** stay in, order in, and finally finish that drama. |
| What can you do with 100 yuan? | You **can** get two meals, a milk tea, and a taxi home. |
| What can you do in your neighborhood at 11 p.m.? | Not much — I **can** only get snacks at the convenience store. |
| What can you do in 30 minutes? | I **can** cook noodles, shower, and answer two WeChat messages. |

## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Can you drive? | 你会开车吗？ |
| Can you cook? What can you make? | 你会做饭吗？会做什么？ |
| Can you swim? | 你会游泳吗？ |
| Can you play any instrument? | 你会什么乐器吗？ |
| Can you sing well? | 你唱歌好听吗？ |
| What's something you can't do that everyone else can? | 有什么别人都会就你不会的？ |
| What can you do that surprises people? | 你有什么让别人意外的本事？ |
| Could you do your job in English? | 你工作能用英语做吗？ |
| Can you cook better than your parents? | 你做饭比你父母强吗？ |
| What can you do to improve your English this month? | 这个月你能做什么来提高英语？ |

## 7. Practice 练习

### 填空：can / can't

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | I ______ speak a little English, but I get nervous on calls. |
| 2 | My grandpa ______ use a smartphone better than my dad. |
| 3 | ______ you help me carry this box up to the sixth floor? |
| 4 | He ______ (not) come tonight — he's on deadline. |
| 5 | ______ I pay by phone here? |

### 改错

| 序号 | 句子 |
|---|---|
| 1 | He can cooks very well. |
| 2 | I can to swim. |
| 3 | Do you can drive? |
| 4 | I don't can come to the meeting. |
| 5 | She cans play the piano. |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I / can / cook / noodles | I can cook noodles, but that's about it. | 我会煮面，也就这样了。 |
| I / can't / swim | I can't swim, so I skip the pool trips. | 我不会游泳，所以泳池活动我都不去。 |
| can you / pick up / package | Can you pick up my package on your way home? | 你回家路上能帮我取快递吗？ |
| can / we / next week / instead | Can we do it next week instead? This week is packed. | 能改到下周吗？这周排满了。 |
