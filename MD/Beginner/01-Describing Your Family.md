# Describing Your Family 介绍你自己和家人

## 1. be 动词现在时

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：I am / You are / He is | I **am** a programmer at a small app company in Hangzhou. | 我是杭州一家小App公司的程序员。 |
| 第三人称单数用 is | My sister **is** a nurse at the city hospital. | 我妹妹是市医院的护士。 |
| 复数用 are | We **are** a family of four, but we only meet up at Spring Festival. | 我们家四口人，但只有春节才聚齐。 |
| 否定：am not / isn't / aren't | I **am not** married yet. My parents ask on every call. | 我还没结婚，爸妈每次通话都要问。 |
| 疑问：Am / Are / Is + 主语 | **Are** you the oldest in your family? | 你是家里老大吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **am**. / No, I'm **not**. | 是。/ 不是。 |

## 4. 代词与物主代词

| 主格 | 形容词性物主 | 宾格 | 名词性物主 |
|---|---|---|---|
| I 我 | my 我的 | me 我 | mine 我的（东西） |
| you 你 | your 你的 | you 你 | yours 你的（东西） |
| he 他 | his 他的 | him 他 | his 他的（东西） |
| she 她 | her 她的 | her 她 | hers 她的（东西） |
| it 它 | its 它的 | it 它 | — |
| we 我们 | our 我们的 | us 我们 | ours 我们的（东西） |
| they 他们 | their 他们的 | them 他们 | theirs 他们的（东西） |

| 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| **My** brother sends **me** money every month, and I pay **him** back after my bonus. | 我哥每月给我打钱，我发了年终奖再还他。 |
| This bike is **mine** — that one with the red basket is **hers**. | 这辆车是我的，那辆带红筐的是她的。 |
| The last slice of cake is **yours**. I already had two. | 最后一块蛋糕归你，我已经吃了两块。 |
| **Our** landlord raised the rent, so **we** are looking for a new place. | 房东涨租了，所以我们正在找新房子。 |

## 5. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| He are my brother. | He **is** my brother. | he / she / it 用 is |
| I no am married. | I **am not** married. | 否定词放在 be 后面 |
| Are you have a sister? | **Do** you have a sister? | have 的疑问句用 do，不用 are |
| My father is teacher. | My father is **a** teacher. | 单数职业前要加 a/an |
| This is mine car. | This is **my** car. | 名词前用 my，不用 mine |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I / work as / designer | I work as a designer at a furniture company. | 我在一家家具公司做设计师。 |
| my dad / delivery driver | My dad is a delivery driver. He works every day except Monday. | 我爸是外卖骑手，一周只休周一。 |
| we / family of three | We are a family of three, and we eat dinner together on Sundays. | 我们家三口人，周日一起吃晚饭。 |
| this bike / mine | This bike is mine. My brother's is the one with the flat tire. | 这辆车是我的，我哥那辆爆胎了。 |
