# At the Hotel 住酒店

## 1. 入住

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I have a reservation under Wang. | **I have a reservation under Wang** — two nights. | 我用王的名字订了两晚。 |
| I'd like to check in. | **I'd like to check in** — the room is for one. | 我想办入住，一人住。 |
| Is breakfast included? | **Is breakfast included** in the room rate? | 房费含早餐吗？ |
| Could I have a room higher up? | **Could I have a room higher up** — I sleep better there. | 能给我高一点的房间吗？ |
| Here's my passport. | **Here's my passport** — and my phone for the QR code. | 这是我的护照，还有手机扫码。 |
| Could I check in early? | **Could I check in early** — my train came at noon. | 我能提前入住吗？我中午就到了。 |
| I booked a room on Meituan. | **I booked a room on Meituan** — here's my order. | 我在美团订的房，这是订单号。 |
| Is there a security deposit? | **Is there a security deposit** on this room? | 这间房要交押金吗？ |
| Could I have a quieter room? | **Could I have a quieter room** away from the street? | 能给我间安静点的房吗？ |

## 3. 提要求和反映问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| The air conditioning isn't working. | **The air conditioning isn't working** in my room. | 我房间的空调坏了。 |
| Could I get an extra towel? | **Could I get an extra towel** for room 1208? | 1208房能再给条毛巾吗？ |
| The Wi-Fi isn't connecting. | **The Wi-Fi isn't connecting** — can you help? | 无线网连不上，能帮忙吗？ |
| Could you keep my luggage? | **Could you keep my luggage** after checkout for an hour? | 退房后能帮我存一小时吗？ |
| Can I have a late checkout? | **Can I have a late checkout** until two? | 能让我两点再退房吗？ |
| Could you send someone up? | **Could you send someone up** to fix the shower? | 能派人上来修淋浴吗？ |
| The room smells like smoke. | **The room smells like smoke** — I booked nonsmoking. | 房间有烟味，我订的是无烟房。 |

## 5. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I want check in. | I want **to check in**. | want 后面要加 to。 |
| I have a reservation of Wang. | I have a reservation **under** Wang. | 按名字订房用 under。 |
| The air conditioner is not work. | The air conditioner **isn't working**. | 坏了用现在进行时。 |
| Can I have a towel more? | Can I have **one more towel**? | 再来一条用 one more。 |
