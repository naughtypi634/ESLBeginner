## 3. 问价钱与付款

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How much is it? | **How much is** this jacket? | 这件外套多少钱？ |
| How much does it cost? | **How much does** the set meal **cost**? | 套餐多少钱？ |
| How much for two? | **How much for two** tickets? | 两张票多少钱？ |
| What's the total? | **What's the total** with delivery? | 加配送一共多少？ |
| Is there a discount? | **Is there a discount** if I buy three? | 买三个有折扣吗？ |
| pay by WeChat Pay | **Can I pay by WeChat Pay** here? | 这儿能用微信支付吗？ |
| Do you take Alipay? | **Do you take Alipay** at this stand? | 这个摊位收支付宝吗？ |
| Card or cash? | **Card or cash?** — Cash, please. | 刷卡还是现金？——现金。 |
| Keep the change. | **Keep the change** — thanks! | 不用找零了，谢谢！ |
| Can I get a receipt? | **Can I get a receipt** for the company? | 能开报销发票吗？ |
| Do you take cash? | **Do you take cash** at this noodle stand? | 这个面摊收现金吗？ |

## 5. 讲价和砍价

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you make it cheaper? | **Can you make it cheaper** if I take two? | 买两件能便宜点吗？ |
| What's your best price? | **What's your best price** for the whole box? | 整箱最低多少？ |
| Round it down. | **Round it down** to 30 and I pay cash. | 抹个零到30，我付现金。 |
| Throw in the shipping. | **Throw in the shipping** and I'll buy two. | 包邮我就买两件。 |
| I'll take it. | 85 is fine, **I'll take it**. | 85我买了。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| How much is cost? | How much **does it** cost? | cost 是动词，要加 does it |
| It's 30 yuans. | It's 30 **yuan**. | yuan 不加 s |
| I want pay by cash. | I want **to pay in** cash. | want 后加 to，介词用 in |
