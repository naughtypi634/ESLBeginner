# How Was It? 谈过去的状态和感受

## 1. was / were 形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：I ／ He ／ She + was | I **was** at the office until nine. | 我在办公室待到九点。 |
| 肯定：You ／ We ／ They + were | You **were** right about the rent. | 房租那件事你说对了。 |
| 过去的人 | He **was** my roommate for two years. | 他当过我两年室友。 |
| 否定：wasn't ／ weren't | We **weren't** ready for the move. | 搬家我们没准备好。 |
| 疑问：Was ／ Were + 主语 | **Was** the interview hard? | 面试难吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, it **was**. ／ No, it **wasn't**. | 是。／ 不是。 |
| 特殊疑问：How was…? | **How** was your first week at the new job? | 新工作第一周怎么样？ |
| 肯定：It + was | The checkup report **was** fine this time. | 这次体检报告没问题。 |
| 过去的地点 | My parents **were** at my apartment last week. | 上周我爸妈在我家。 |
| 否定疑问：Weren't…? | **Weren't** you at the team building last Friday? | 上周五团建你没去吗？ |

## 2. 谈过去的身份、状态和感受

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I was a/an + 身份 | I **was** a student back then. | 那时候我还是学生。 |
| It was my first + 名词 | It **was** my first time in Shanghai. | 那是我第一次来上海。 |
| I was + 形容词 | I **was** so nervous before the presentation. | 汇报前我特别紧张。 |
| It was + 形容词 + but worth it | The trip **was** tiring but worth it. | 旅行很累但值得。 |
| It wasn't as + 形容词 + as… | It **wasn't** as bad as I thought. | 没我想的那么糟。 |
| We were + 形容词 | We **were** so lost in the new neighborhood. | 在新小区我们完全迷路。 |
| It was such a/an + 名词 | It **was** such a busy week at work. | 那周工作特别忙。 |
| I was + 形容词 + that 从句 | I **was** surprised that the tickets sold out. | 票卖光了我很意外。 |
| We were + 形容词 + about | We **were** worried about the rent at first. | 一开始我们担心房租。 |

## 3. 连接词 because / so / and / or / but

| 连接词 | 作用 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **because** | 说原因 | I **was** tired **because** I worked until ten. | 我累是因为加班到十点。 |
| **so** | 说结果 | It went well, **so** I treated myself to hotpot. | 面试顺利，我吃了火锅犒劳自己。 |
| **and** | 补充并列 | The hotel **was** clean, **and** breakfast was free. | 酒店很干净，早餐还免费。 |
| **or** | 给出选择 | **Was** it your first job **or** your second? | 那是你第一份还是第二份工作？ |
| **but** | 说转折 | The test **was** hard, **but** I passed. | 考试很难，但我过了。 |
| **but** | 说转折 | The flight **was** delayed, **but** we made it. | 航班延误了，但我们赶上了。 |
| **so** | 说结果 | I **was** nervous, **so** I practiced for an hour. | 我紧张，就练了一小时。 |

## 4. makes me 句型

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 现在：动名词 + makes me + 形容词 | Waiting **makes me** nervous. | 等待让我紧张。 |
| 现在：名词 + makes me + 形容词 | Hotpot after work **makes me** happy. | 下班吃火锅让我开心。 |
| 过去：名词 + made me + 形容词 | The checkup report **made me** worried. | 体检报告让我很担心。 |
| 过去：名词 + made me + 动词 | That movie **made me** cry. | 那部电影让我哭了。 |
| 过去：名词 + made me + 动词 | My first paycheck **made me** feel grown up. | 第一份工资让我觉得长大了。 |
| 现在：动名词 + makes me + 形容词 | The morning commute **makes me** sleepy. | 早高峰通勤让我犯困。 |

## 5. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I were tired. | I **was** tired. | I 后面用 was |
| It wasn't bad, because I was nervous. | It wasn't bad, **but** I was nervous. | 转折用 but |
| He was go to Beijing. | He **went** to Beijing. | 有动词就不用 was |
| The movie makes me cried. | The movie **made me** cry. | 过去用 made + 原形 |
| There was so many people. | There **were** so many people. | people 是复数用 were |
| I was boring at the meeting. | I **was** bored at the meeting. | 人用 bored，物用 boring |

## 6. 说过去的感受

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| nervous 紧张 | I was **nervous** before my first interview. | 第一次面试前我很紧张。 |
| excited 兴奋 | She was **excited** about her new apartment. | 她对新公寓很兴奋。 |
| relieved 松了口气 | We were **relieved** when the report was normal. | 报告正常，我们松了口气。 |
