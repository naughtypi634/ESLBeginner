# Asking for Directions 问路与方向

## 1. 开口问路

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How do I get to the station? | **Excuse me, how do I get to** the subway station? | 地铁站怎么走？ |
| Where's the nearest pharmacy? | **Where's the nearest** pharmacy around here? | 最近的药店在哪？ |
| Am I going the right way? | **Am I going the right way** to the mall? | 我走的方向对吗？ |
| Is it far from here? | **Is it far from here?** I'm on foot. | 离这儿远吗？ |
| Can you show me on the map? | **Can you show me on the map** on your phone? | 能在你手机上指一下吗？ |
| Where's the nearest ATM? | **Where's the nearest ATM** from this gate? | 最近的取款机在哪儿？ |
| Which exit is for the mall? | **Which exit is for the mall?** I just got here. | 去商场走哪个出口？ |
| Could you point me to the park? | **Could you point me to the park** at the corner? | 能指一下公园在哪儿吗？ |

## 2. 方向说法

| English | 中文 | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| turn left | 左转 | turn right | 右转 |
| go straight | 一直走 | go past the bank | 走过银行 |
| cross the street | 过马路 | at the corner | 在拐角 |
| on your left | 在你左边 | on your right | 在你右边 |
| go up the escalator | 上扶梯 | go down the stairs | 下楼梯 |
| take the second exit | 走第二个出口 | follow this road | 沿这条路走 |
| go back | 往回走 | turn around | 掉头 |
| at the traffic light | 在红绿灯处 | at the crosswalk | 在人行横道 |
| cross at the light | 在红绿灯过马路 | the next intersection | 下一个路口 |
| the first turn | 第一个转弯 | the second block | 第二个路口 |
| around the corner | 拐角就是 | on the corner | 在街角 |
| take Exit B | 走B出口 | by the subway gate | 在地铁口 |

## 3. 位置介词

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It's next to … | **It's next to the bank** — the green sign. | 就在银行旁边，绿色招牌。 |
| It's across from … | **It's across from the mall**, by the exit. | 在商场对面，出口旁边。 |
| It's between … and … | **It's between the bakery and the pharmacy.** | 在面包店和药店之间。 |
| It's behind … | **It's behind the bus stop**, past the gate. | 在公交站后面，过了大门。 |
| It's in front of … | **It's in front of the park**, near the gate. | 在公园前面，靠近大门。 |
| It's near … | **It's near subway Exit B** — the white building. | 在地铁B口附近，白楼。 |
| It's at the end of … | **It's at the end of this street**, past the bank. | 在这条街的尽头，过了银行。 |
| It's inside … | **It's inside the mall** on the first floor. | 在商场里面，一楼。 |

## 4. 没听懂怎么办

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Could you say that again? | **Sorry, could you say that again?** I missed that. | 不好意思，能再说一遍吗？ |
| Could you speak slower? | **Could you speak a little slower?** I'm lost. | 你能说慢一点吗？我没跟上。 |
| Do you mean left or right? | **Do you mean left or right?** I'm not sure. | 你是说左还是右？ |
| I'm not from here. | **I'm not from here** — I just got off the subway. | 我不是本地人，刚下地铁。 |
| Let me check the map. | **Let me check the map** on my phone first. | 我先看下手机地图。 |
| Sorry, I didn't catch that. | **Sorry, I didn't catch that** — the street name. | 不好意思，没听清街名。 |
| Which way did you say? | **Which way did you say** — left or right? | 你说的是哪边？ |
| Can you send it on WeChat? | **Can you send it on WeChat?** I'll follow it. | 能用微信发给我吗？ |

## 5. 给别人指路

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Go straight for two blocks. | **Go straight for two blocks** and turn right. | 直走两个路口，然后右转。 |
| Turn right at the light. | **Turn right at the traffic light** by the mall. | 在商场那个红绿灯右转。 |
| It's on your left. | **It's on your left**, next to the bank. | 在你左边，银行旁边。 |
| You can't miss it. | **You can't miss it** — it's right by the exit. | 很好找，就在出口旁边。 |
| It's a ten-minute walk. | **It's about a ten-minute walk** from the mall. | 从商场走大概十分钟。 |
| It's right past the crosswalk. | **It's right past the crosswalk** — the red sign. | 过了人行横道就是，红招牌。 |

## 6. 用地标说路

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It's the glass building. | **It's the glass building** next to the bank. | 就是银行旁边那栋玻璃楼。 |
| Go past the traffic light. | **Go past the traffic light** — on your right. | 过了红绿灯，就在你右边。 |
| It's above the McDonald's. | **It's above the McDonald's** — take the elevator. | 在麦当劳楼上，坐电梯上去。 |

## 7. 追问、确认和导航

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Is it walking distance? | **Is it walking distance** from this exit? | 从这个口走过去远吗？ |
| How long does it take? | **How long does it take** to walk there? | 走过去要多久？ |
| Is there a closer one? | **Is there a closer one** — I'm in a hurry. | 有没有更近的？我赶时间。 |

## 8. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Have you ever asked a delivery rider for directions? | 你有过向外卖骑手问路吗？ |
| Which exit do you usually take at your subway station? | 你平时在地铁站走哪个出口？ |
| Do you look at your phone map first, or just ask someone? | 你会先看手机地图，还是直接问人？ |
| Is your neighborhood easy to find for visitors? | 你家小区对访客来说好找吗？ |
| Have you ever given directions to a tourist? | 你有过给游客指路吗？ |
