# Right Now 现在进行时

## 1. 现在进行时形式

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：be + -ing | I **'m waiting** for the elevator on the 18th floor. | 我在18楼等电梯。 |
| 第三人称：is + -ing | She **'s working** from home today. | 她今天在家办公。 |
| 复数：are + -ing | We **'re ordering** takeout on the way home. | 我们在回家路上点外卖。 |
| 否定：be + not + -ing | They **aren't answering** my WeChat calls. | 他们不接我微信电话。 |
| 疑问：Be + 主语 + -ing? | **Are** you **listening** to my voice message? | 你在听我的语音吗？ |
| 否定疑问：Aren't + 主语 | **Aren't** you **coming** to the team dinner? | 你不来部门聚餐吗？ |
| 简答：Yes ／ No | Yes, I **am**. ／ No, I**'m not**. | 在听。／没在听。 |
| 特殊疑问 | **What are you doing** after work tonight? | 你今晚下班后干嘛？ |
| 特殊疑问 + is | **What is she doing** in the meeting room? | 她在会议室里干嘛？ |

### -ing 拼写规则

| 规则 | 动词 → -ing | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般直接加 -ing | work → working | I**'m working** late again tonight. |
| 以 e 结尾，去 e 加 -ing | write → writing | He**'s writing** the report on his phone. |
| 重读闭音节，双写末尾字母 | sit → sitting | We**'re sitting** in traffic on the ring road. |
| 以 ie 结尾，变 y 加 -ing | lie → lying | My phone **is lying** on the sofa somewhere. |
| 以 -y 结尾，直接加 -ing | study → studying | She**'s studying** English in the library. |
| 以 -ee 结尾，直接加 -ing | see → seeing | He**'s seeing** a dentist this afternoon. |

## 2. 现在进行时的四种用法

| 用法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 此刻正在发生 | I can't talk, I**'m carrying** two bags of groceries. | 说不了话，我拎着两袋菜。 |
| 此刻正在发生 | Hold on, I**'m paying** with my phone at the store. | 等一下，我在扫码付款。 |
| 这段时间一直在做 | I**'m staying** at my parents' place this week. | 我这周住我爸妈那儿。 |
| 这段时间一直在做 | I**'m taking** a Python class this fall. | 我这学期在上 Python 课。 |
| 已安排好的近期计划 | I**'m meeting** a client at 10 tomorrow morning. | 我明早十点见客户。 |
| 已安排好的近期计划 | We**'re having** hot pot with my team on Friday. | 周五我和团队吃火锅。 |
| 反复让人烦：always + -ing | He**'s always losing** his keys before we leave. | 他总在出门前找不到钥匙。 |

## 4. 一般现在时 vs 现在进行时

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 | 经常做的事 | I **take** the subway to work every day. |
| 现在进行时 | 此刻正在做的事 | **I'm taking** a taxi today, the subway is packed. |
| 一般现在时 | 一直如此的事实 | I **work** at a tech company in Hangzhou. |

## 5. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I am work now. | I **am working** now. | 进行时是 be + -ing。 |
| She is watch TV. | She is **watching** TV. | 动词必须加 -ing。 |
| I am knowing him. | I **know** him. | 状态动词不用进行时。 |
| He is having a car. | He **has** a car. | 拥有用 have，不用进行时。 |
| He is liking the coffee. | He **likes** the coffee. | like 是状态动词，不用进行时。 |

## 6. 打电话和发消息时说“正在做”

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 正在开会 | Sorry, I**'m sitting** in a meeting right now. | 不好意思，我正在开会。 |
| 正在开车 | I**'m driving**, can I call you back? | 我在开车，等下回你。 |
| 在路上 | I**'m heading** to your office now. | 我正在去你公司。 |
