# My Home 我的住处

## 1. have / has 现在时

| 结构 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：have | I **have** a small apartment near the subway. | 我在地铁附近有套小房子。 |
| 第三人称：has | My roommate **has** a cat that sleeps on the sofa. | 我室友有只猫，总睡在沙发上。 |
| 否定：don't have | We **don't have** a dryer in our apartment. | 我们公寓没有烘干机。 |
| 疑问：Do you have…? | **Do you have** a washing machine in the kitchen? | 你厨房里有洗衣机吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. | 有。／没有。 |
| 疑问：Does he have…? | **Does your roommate have** a desk in his room? | 你室友房间里有书桌吗？ |

## 4. 描述住处的句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It's a one-bedroom. | **It's a one-bedroom** with a balcony. | 一居室，带个阳台。 |
| It's a two-bedroom. | My friend and I share **a two-bedroom** near Line 2. | 我和朋友合租一个两居室。 |
| on the 12th floor | **I live on the 12th floor** — the view is great. | 我住12楼，视野很好。 |
| small but cozy | **My place is small but cozy** after I added a rug. | 添了块地毯，小家很温馨。 |
| a 20-minute walk | **It's a 20-minute walk** from my door to the subway. | 从我家到地铁走20分钟。 |
| I share it with… | **I share it with a roommate** to save on rent. | 我跟人合租，省点房租。 |
| It's a walk-up. | **It's a walk-up** — I climb six floors every day. | 没电梯的老楼，我每天爬六层。 |
## 5. 租房看房

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How much is the rent? | **How much is the rent** for this one-bedroom? | 这个一居室房租多少？ |
| Is it furnished? | **Is it furnished**, or should I buy my own bed? | 带家具吗，还是自己买床？ |
| Utilities included? | **Are utilities included** in the rent? | 房租含水电费吗？ |
| When can I move in? | **When can I move in** if I sign the contract today? | 今天签约，什么时候能入住？ |
| Can I take a look? | **Can I take a look** at the bedroom before I decide? | 决定前我能看看卧室吗？ |
| Deposit negotiable? | **Is the deposit negotiable** if I pay three months? | 一次付三个月押金能商量吗？ |
| Agent fee? | **Do I pay an agent fee** on top of the deposit? | 押金之外还要中介费吗？ |
## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I have a apartment. | I have **an** apartment. | apartment 前用 an。 |
| He have a car. | He **has** a car. | 第三人称单数用 has。 |
| I live in 12th floor. | I live **on** the 12th floor. | 楼层用 on the … floor。 |
| My home is very small and cozy. | My **place** is small but cozy. | home 指家，住处用 place。 |
## 7. 家务和打扫

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| do the laundry | I **do the laundry** every Sunday morning. | 我每周日早上洗衣服。 |
| take out the trash | Could you **take out the trash** before work? | 你上班前能顺手扔垃圾吗？ |
| mop the floor | I **mop the floor** right after my roommate cooks. | 室友做完饭我就拖地。 |
| air the room | I open the window to **air the room** after dinner. | 晚饭后我开窗通风。 |
| hang the clothes out | I **hang the clothes out** on the balcony to dry. | 我把衣服晾在阳台。 |
