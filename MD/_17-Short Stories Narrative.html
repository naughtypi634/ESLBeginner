Short Stories & Narrative · 讲故事

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
You tell a friend about your morning. What do you say first?跟朋友讲早上发生的事，你先说什么？
→ I wake up / I drink tea / I go out我起床 / 我喝茶 / 我出门
Do you enjoy telling stories about your day?你喜欢讲一天的事吗？
→ Yes, fun / No, boring / Maybe sometimes喜欢，好玩 / 不，无聊 / 偶尔

2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

Start a Story 开头

EnglishChineseExample
First / One day首先 / 有一天One day, I missed the bus.
This morning今天早上This morning, I overslept.
There is / I see有 / 我看到I see a long line outside.

Add More 推进

EnglishChineseExample
Then / Next然后 / 接着Then I scanned the bike code.
After / And之后 / 而且After that, I got coffee.
So / But所以 / 但是But the app was down.

End a Story 收尾

EnglishChineseExample
Finally / In the end最后 / 到头来Finally, I made it on time.
That's all / The end就这些 / 完That's all for today.
I feel我觉得I feel lucky it worked out.

Feelings in a Story 故事里的情绪

EnglishChineseExample
happy / excited开心 / 兴奋I was excited to get home.
tired / hungry累 / 饿I was tired after work.
scared / sad害怕 / 难过I was scared of missing the train.

3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

EnglishChineseKeywords
The story ______ about my morning coffee.这个故事是关于我早上咖啡的。is / are / am
I ______ not tell long stories.我不讲长故事。do / does / am
______, I opened the delivery app.……，我打开了外卖 App。First / Finally / Next
