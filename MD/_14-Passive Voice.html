Passive Voice · 被动语态

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
Your food is delivered. Where is it left?外卖到了，放在哪里？
→ At the door / In the locker / At the desk门口 / 快递柜 / 桌上
In your home, what is often cleaned?你家经常打扫什么？
→ The floor / The table / The window地板 / 桌子 / 窗户

2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

Ordering Food 点餐

EnglishChineseExample
is ordered / is delivered被下单 / 被配送The food is delivered in 30 minutes.
is packed / is cooked被打包 / 被做熟The meal is cooked fresh.
is paid / is served被支付 / 被端上The bill is paid online.

Work Tasks 工作任务

EnglishChineseExample
is assigned / is sent被分配 / 被发送The report is sent every Friday.
is scheduled / is checked被安排 / 被检查Emails are checked in the morning.
is read / is answered被阅读 / 被回复The message is answered quickly.

Daily Chores 家务

EnglishChineseExample
is washed / is dried被洗 / 被晾The dishes are washed after dinner.
is swept / is taken被扫 / 被带走The floor is swept on weekends.
is boiled / is made被煮 / 被做The bed is made every morning.

Online Content 网络内容

EnglishChineseExample
is liked / is shared被点赞 / 被转发The video is shared everywhere.
is uploaded / is saved被上传 / 被保存The photo is uploaded to 小红书.
is scanned / is blocked被扫码 / 被屏蔽The QR code is scanned at the door.

3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

EnglishChineseKeywords
The dishes are ______ after dinner.晚饭后碗被……washed / dried / put away
My phone is ______ for work every day.我的手机每天被……用于工作。used / charged / fixed
The package ______ left at the locker.包裹被放在快递柜。is / was / will be
