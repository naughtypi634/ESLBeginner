B1/B2 旅游签证
B1/B2 旅游签证01
Q

What's the purpose of your trip?

A

I go to the US for tourism. I want to see New York and Los Angeles. After my trip, I will go back to China.

策略要点明确旅游目的，并立即强调“会回来”。
Q

How long will you stay?

A

I will stay for 14 days. I have only 2 weeks of holiday from my job. Then I must go back to work.

策略要点用“假期只有两周”强调必须回国上班。
