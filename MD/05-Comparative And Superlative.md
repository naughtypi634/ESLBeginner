# Comparative And Superlative

—  1  — 
COMPARATIVE & SUPERLATIVE 
  Rule 1  Short adj: add -er / -est 
tall → taller → tallest 
short → shorter → shortest 
fast → faster → fastest 
slow → slower → slowest 
long → longer → longest 
small → smaller → smallest 
  Rule 2  Ending in -e: add -r / -st 
nice → nicer → nicest 
large → larger → largest 
safe → safer → safest 
close → closer → closest 
wise → wiser → wisest 
brave → braver → bravest 
  Rule 3  CVC: double consonant + -er/-est 
big → bigger → biggest 
hot → hotter → hottest 
thin → thinner → thinnest 
fat → fatter → fattest 
wet → wetter → wettest 
sad → sadder → saddest 
  Rule 4  Ending in -y: y→i + -er/-est 
happy → happier → happiest 
easy → easier → easiest 
busy → busier → busiest 
lazy → lazier → laziest 
heavy → heavier → heaviest 
angry → angrier → angriest 
  Rule 5  Long adj (≥2 syll): more / most 
careful → more ~ → most ~ 
useful → more ~ → most ~ 
expensive → more ~ → most ~ 
interesting → more ~ → most ~ 
difficult → more ~ → most ~ 
comfortable → more ~ → most ~

COMMON COLLOCATIONS 
  one of the/ my/ other determiner + superlative   
—  This is one of the most packed subway lines during rush hour. 
—  It's one of the most watched shows on my feed right now. 
—  That was one of the most boring meetings I've had this week. 
  the + comparative 1, the + comparative 2   
—  The earlier I wake up, the heavier the traffic gets. 
—  The more I work overtime, the less I feel like cooking. 
—  The older I get, the more I hate small talk. 
  way/ much / far / a lot + comparative   
—  After a few weeks here, I became a lot more confident. 
—  Scrolling TikTok is way more addictive than I thought.. 
—  This project is way more lucrative than the old one. 
  comparative + comparative 
—  It is getting hotter and hotter. 
—  As he grows up, he becomes more and more independent. 
—  Ever since McDonald’s became Jingongmen, the burgers have been getting smaller and smaller! 
the + ordinal number + superlative 
—  Beijing is the second largest city in China by population 
—  This is the second most watched show on Bilibili. 
—  He/ She is probably my second favorite actor.

SPEAKING PRACTICE 
 
  COMPARE   
1.  Which drains you more, back-to-back meetings or just staring at screens all day? 
  
2.  Which helps you zone out better, scrolling shorts or binging a series? 
  
3.  Which is more exhausting, pretending to focus in a workshop or actually working overtime? 
  
4.  Which stresses you out more, your annual performance review or a blind date setup? 
  
5.  Which is more annoying, running out of phone storage or losing your Wi-Fi signal? 
  
 
  SUPERLATIVE   
1.  What's your absolute favorite way to shut off your brain after work? 
  
2.  What's the most useless thing you've ever sat through at work? 
  
3.  What's the most ridiculous excuse you have ever heard? 
  
4.  What's the most overpriced thing you bought? 
  
5.  What's the worst food you've eaten this week?
