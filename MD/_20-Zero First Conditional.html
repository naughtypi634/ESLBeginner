Zero & First Conditional · 条件句

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
If you scan a QR code, what happens?扫码之后会怎样？
→ Get menu / Pay bill / Find shop出现菜单 / 付款 / 找到店铺
If you forget your phone, what do you do?忘带手机你会怎么办？
→ Feel lost / Ask a friend / Go home觉得慌 / 问朋友 / 回家拿

2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

Weather Actions 天气应对

EnglishChineseExample
take an umbrella带伞If it rains, I take an umbrella.
wear a coat穿外套If it's cold, I wear a coat.
stay inside待在家里If it storms, I stay inside.
turn on the AC开空调If it's hot, I turn on the AC.

Work Routines 工作习惯

EnglishChineseExample
call the boss给老板打电话If I'm late, I call my boss.
send a message发消息If the meeting changes, I send a message.
join the meeting加入会议If I'm free, I join the meeting.
check DingTalk看钉钉If I'm off work, I still check DingTalk.

Eating Habits 吃饭习惯

EnglishChineseExample
order food点外卖If I'm lazy, I order food.
cook noodles煮面If I'm hungry, I cook noodles.
buy fruit买水果If I pass a shop, I buy fruit.
use 美团用美团If I want takeout, I use 美团.

Travel Tips 出行

EnglishChineseExample
take a shared bike骑共享单车If it's near, I take a shared bike.
take the subway坐地铁If it's far, I take the subway.
open the map打开地图If I'm lost, I open the map.
check the weather查天气If I go out, I check the weather.

3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

EnglishChineseKeywords
If it rains, I ______ an umbrella.下雨的话我带伞。bring / use / need
If I am hungry, I ______ a snack.饿的话我吃点零食。eat / buy / make
If you scan the code, the menu ______ appear.扫码菜单就会出现。will / would / did

4. Discussion 讨论

EnglishChineseAnswer tags
If it rains, do you stay home?下雨你待在家吗？Stay inside / Go out / Take the bus
If it is hot, what do you do first?天热你先做什么？Drink water / Turn on AC / Eat ice cream
If your boss calls, do you answer?老板打电话你接吗？Answer fast / Send a text / Call back
If you are late, what do you say?迟到你会说什么？Say sorry / Tell the reason / Send a message
If you get a package, where do you go?有包裹你去哪取？Express locker / Front desk / Ask a neighbor
