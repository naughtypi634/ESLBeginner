# Present Perfect · Experiences 现在完成时（经历）

## 1. Form 结构

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定：have / has + 过去分词 | I **have tried** the noodle place downstairs. | 我试过楼下那家面馆。 |
| 第三人称单数用 has | My roommate **has been** to that gym twice. | 我室友去过那家健身房两次。 |
| 否定：haven't / hasn't | I **haven't tried** camping yet. | 我还没试过露营。 |
| 疑问：Have you ever…? | **Have you ever** ordered groceries online? | 你在网上买过菜吗？ |
| 简答 | Yes, I **have**. / No, I **haven't**. / Not yet. | 有。/ 没有。/ 还没。 |
| 次数 | I **have taken** the high-speed train four times. | 我坐过四次高铁。 |

## 2. Past Participles 常用过去分词

| 原形 | 过去分词 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| eat | eaten | I **have eaten** at that place twice. | 那家店我吃过两次。 |
| be | been | She **has been** to Hangzhou three times. | 她去过杭州三次。 |
| try | tried | I **have tried** rock climbing once. | 我试过一次攀岩。 |
| see | seen | I **have seen** that movie twice. | 那部电影我看过两遍。 |
| take | taken | I **have taken** the subway at 6 a.m. | 我早上六点坐过地铁。 |
| ride | ridden | I **have ridden** a shared bike by the river. | 我在江边骑过共享单车。 |
| make | made | I **have made** dumplings with my team. | 我和同事一起包过饺子。 |
| write | written | I **have written** three reports this week. | 我这周写了三份报告。 |
| meet | met | I **have met** her once at a team dinner. | 我在团建饭局上见过她一次。 |
| do | done | I **have done** karaoke with my team. | 我和同事一起唱过K。 |
| get | gotten | I **have gotten** someone else's package. | 我拿过别人的快递。 |
| speak | spoken | I **have spoken** English on a work call. | 我在工作电话里说过英语。 |

## 3. ever / never / already / yet

| 副词 | 位置 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ever | 疑问句，过去分词前 | **Have you ever** tried hot pot alone? | 你一个人吃过火锅吗？ |
| never | 过去分词前 | I **have never** sung in front of people. | 我从没在人前唱过歌。 |
| already | have / has 之后 | I **have already** tried that milk tea shop. | 那家奶茶店我已经试过了。 |
| yet | 否定句、疑问句句末 | I **haven't tried** rock climbing **yet**. | 我还没试过攀岩。 |
| before | 句末 | I **have been** to Beijing **before**. | 我以前去过北京。 |
| twice / a few times | 句末 | I **have tried** camping **twice**. | 露营我试过两次。 |

## 4. Sentence Patterns 常用句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Have you ever + 过去分词? | **Have you ever** ordered takeout after midnight? | 你半夜点过外卖吗？ |
| I've been to + 地点 | I **have been to** Dali twice. | 我去过大理两次。 |
| I've never + 过去分词 | I **have never** lived alone. | 我从没一个人住过。 |
| I've already + 过去分词 | I **have already** finished today's report. | 今天的报告我写完了。 |
| I haven't + 过去分词 + yet | I **haven't booked** the train ticket yet. | 我还没订高铁票。 |
| It's the first time I've + 过去分词 | **It's the first time** I **have spoken** English at work. | 这是我第一次在工作中说英语。 |
| I've been meaning to + 动词原形 | I **have been meaning to** try that gym. | 我一直想试那家健身房。 |
| How many times have you + 过去分词? | **How many times have you** moved since college? | 毕业后你搬过几次家？ |

## 5. Present Perfect or Past Simple 该用哪个时态

| 用法 | 现在完成时 | 一般过去时 |
|---|---|---|
| 说经历，不点时间 | I **have been** to Chengdu. | — |
| 点明时间 | — | I **went** to Chengdu in 2024. |
| 问经历 | **Have you ever** flown first class? | — |
| 问某次具体经过 | — | **When did** you fly first class? |
| 常见信号词 | ever / never / already / yet / before | yesterday / last week / in 2024 / ago |
| 结果留到现在 | I **have lost** my key. | I **lost** my key yesterday. |

### 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I have went to Shanghai. | I have **been** to Shanghai. | 用过去分词 been |
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. | 有具体时间用过去时 |
| Have you ever gone to Chengdu? | Have you ever **been** to Chengdu? | 问"去过"用 been to |
| I am in Shanghai for three years. | I **have been** in Shanghai for three years. | 持续到现在用现在完成时 |

## 6. Real-Life Expressions 日常表达

| 表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| cross it off my bucket list | 完成心愿清单上的一项 | I **have finally crossed** bungee jumping **off** my bucket list. |
| give it a shot | 试一试 | I **have never** tried rock climbing, but I'll **give it a shot**. |
| out of my comfort zone | 走出舒适区 | I got **out of my comfort zone** and did karaoke last week. |
| not my thing | 不是我的菜 | I **have tried** camping twice; it's just **not my thing**. |
| been there, done that | 这我早经历过了 | Staying up till 2 a.m. for a deadline? **Been there, done that.** |
| I've been meaning to… | 我一直想…… | I **have been meaning to** try that hot pot place. |

## 7. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| Have you ever ordered takeout after midnight? | 你半夜点过外卖吗？ | Yes, more than once / Only once / Never |
| Have you ever slept through your subway stop? | 你坐地铁睡过站吗？ | Yes, twice / Almost / Never |
| Have you ever lived in another city? | 你在别的城市住过吗？ | Yes, for two years / Only for work / Never |
| Have you ever tried camping? | 你试过露营吗？ | Yes, many times / Once / Not yet |
| Have you ever spoken English on a work call? | 你在工作电话里说过英语吗？ | Yes, often / A few times / Never |
| Have you ever sent a message to the wrong group? | 你把消息发错过群吗？ | Yes, and deleted it / Yes, too late / Never |

### 选择：现在完成时还是过去时

| # | 句子 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I ______ (visit) Shanghai last fall. | visited |
| 2 | I ______ (visit) Shanghai three times. | have visited |
| 3 | ______ you ______ (see) that movie last weekend? | Did / see |
| 4 | ______ you ever ______ (see) that movie? | Have / seen |
| 5 | She ______ (finish) the report an hour ago. | finished |
| 6 | She ______ (already / finish) the report. | has already finished |

### 造句

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |

| been / Dali / twice | I have been to Dali twice. | 我去过大理两次。 |
| already / today's report | I have already finished today's report. | 今天的报告我写完了。 |
| never / live alone | I have never lived alone. | 我从没一个人住过。 |
