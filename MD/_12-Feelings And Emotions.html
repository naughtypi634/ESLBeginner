Feelings & Emotions · 感受与情绪

Positive 积极

words

happy 开心 I'm happy — my takeout arrived early.

excited 兴奋 I'm excited about the Chengdu trip this weekend.

proud 自豪 I'm proud of our project launch.

relaxed 放松 I feel relaxed walking along the river at sunset.

phrases

be in a good mood 心情好 I'm in a good mood — my coffee was free today.

feel great 感觉很好 I feel great after a morning run in the park.

look on the bright side 往好处想 I missed the bus, but I look on the bright side — extra time for breakfast.

idioms

be on a high 兴奋不已 I'm on a high after my first raise.

be in my element 如鱼得水 I'm in my element cooking hotpot with friends.

be living my best life 活得美滋滋 I'm living my best life with a beach trip this summer.

Negative 消极

words

sad 难过 I feel sad saying goodbye at the airport.

angry 生气 I'm angry about the 40-minute-late delivery.

worried 担心 I'm worried about tomorrow's deadline.

stressed 有压力 I feel stressed before the quarterly review.

phrases

be in a bad mood 心情不好 I'm in a bad mood because I lost my earbuds on the metro.

feel down 情绪低落 I've been feeling down since my best friend moved away.

get on my nerves 惹我烦 The construction noise gets on my nerves every morning.

idioms

be in a funk 蔫了 / 提不起劲 I've been in a funk because it rained all weekend.

be in a rut 陷入一成不变 I'm in a rut at work — same tasks every day.

be at my breaking point 快撑不住了 I'm at my breaking point with back-to-back meetings.

Neutral 中性

words

tired 累 I'm tired after the night shift.

nervous 紧张 I'm nervous about the interview at 10 am.

surprised 惊讶 I'm surprised — my boss gave me Friday off.

bored 无聊 I'm bored on the 40-minute metro ride.

phrases

be on my mind 放在心上 My apartment search has been on my mind all day.

have mixed feelings 百感交集 I have mixed feelings about leaving my old job.

be in two minds 拿不定主意 I'm in two minds about renewing my gym card.

idioms

be in limbo 悬着 / 没着落 My promotion is in limbo — waiting for approval.

be up in the air 悬而未决 My weekend plans are up in the air — it depends on the weather.
