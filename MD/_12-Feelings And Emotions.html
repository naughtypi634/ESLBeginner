Feelings & Emotions · 感受与情绪

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
Your friend sends a long voice message. How do you feel?朋友发来超长语音，你什么感觉？
→ Annoyed / Curious / Tired烦 / 好奇 / 累
When do you feel most relaxed?你什么时候最放松？
→ After work / In bed / Walking outside下班后 / 床上 / 在外面散步

2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

Happy Words 开心词

EnglishChineseExample
happy / excited开心 / 兴奋I'm excited about the trip.
proud / thankful自豪 / 感激I'm thankful for my family.
lucky / surprised幸运 / 惊讶I feel lucky today.

Sad Words 低落词

EnglishChineseExample
sad / angry难过 / 生气I'm angry about the late delivery.
worried / stressed担心 / 有压力I feel stressed before deadlines.
tired / bored累 / 无聊I'm tired after work.

Body Words 身体感受

EnglishChineseExample
hungry / thirsty饿 / 渴I'm hungry — let's order food.
sleepy / sick困 / 不舒服I feel sleepy at 3 pm.
hot / cold热 / 冷It's too hot today.

3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

EnglishChineseKeywords

My ______ makes me feel happy.我的……让我开心。friend / food / music
I'm ______ about the meeting tomorrow.我明天开会前很……worried / excited / nervous
