Present Continuous · 现在进行时

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
You see a friend looking at her phone and smiling. What is she doing?朋友看着手机笑，她在干什么？
→ Watching a funny clip / Reading a long message / Looking at photos看搞笑视频 / 读长消息 / 看照片
Think of a busy street in your city. What are people doing right now?想想城里热闹的街，人们现在在干什么？
→ Riding shared bikes / Scanning codes / Walking dogs骑共享单车 / 扫码 / 遛狗
