# How to describe a person

HOW TO DESCRIBE A PERSON
APPE ARANCE   外貌
He/She is + adj (build & height)  ·  He/She has + noun (hair & face)  ·  He/She wears + noun (clothes)
WORD / PHRASE
中文
EXAMPLE
tall
高的
He is tall.
not very tall
不太高
She is not very tall.
slim / thin
苗条 / 瘦
She is slim. He is thin.
strong / fit
强壮 / 健壮
He is strong and fit.
medium height
中等身高
She is medium height.
long / short hair
长发 / 短发
She has long black hair.
curly / straight hair
卷发 / 直发
He has short curly hair.
blonde / gray hair
金发 / 灰白发
She has blonde hair.
bald
秃头
He is bald.
a beard
胡子
He has a beard.
big eyes
大眼睛
She has big eyes.
a nice smile
好看的笑容
He has a nice smile.
glasses
眼镜
He wears glasses.
a suit / a dress
西装 / 连衣裙
She wears a suit to work.
casual / smart clothes
休闲装 / 精致着装
He usually wears casual clothes.
PERSONALIT Y   性格
He/She is (very / quite / a bit / sometimes) + adj
POSITIVE 正面
中文
EXAMPLE
kind
善良
She is very kind.
funny
有趣
He is quite funny.
friendly
友好
She is very friendly.
honest
诚实
She is honest.
helpful
乐于助人
He is helpful.
hard-working
勤奋
She is hard-working.
confident
自信
She is confident.
outgoing
外向
He is outgoing.

PERSONALIT Y   性格（负面）
He/She is (very / quite / a bit / sometimes) + adj
NEGATIVE 负面
中文
EXAMPLE
rude
粗鲁
He is rude sometimes.
lazy
懒
She is a bit lazy.
selfish
自私
He is selfish.
careless
粗心
She is a bit careless.
impatient
没耐心
He is impatient.
bossy
爱指挥人
She is a bit bossy.
shy
害羞
He is quite shy.
HOBBIES & INTERESTS   爱好
He/She likes / enjoys / loves + noun / verb-ing  ·  His/Her hobby is + noun / verb-ing
WORD / PHRASE
中文
EXAMPLE
reading
阅读
She loves reading novels.
cooking
做饭
He enjoys cooking at home.
swimming
游泳
She likes swimming every weekend.
running / jogging
跑步 / 慢跑
He goes running in the morning.
hiking / camping
徒步 / 露营
She enjoys hiking with friends.
cycling
骑行
His hobby is cycling.
singing / dancing
唱歌 / 跳舞
She loves singing and dancing.
drawing / painting
画画 / 绘画
He is good at drawing.
watching movies / TV
看电影 / 电视
She likes watching movies at home.
playing games / sports
玩游戏 / 运动
He enjoys playing games online.
JOBS & WORK   工作
He/She is a/an + job  ·  He/She works as a/an + job  ·  He/She works in/at + place
WORD / PHRASE
中文
EXAMPLE
teacher
老师
She is a teacher at a primary school.
doctor / nurse
医生 / 护士
He works as a doctor in a hospital.
driver
司机
He is a driver for a delivery company.
cook / chef
厨师 / 主厨
She works as a cook in a restaurant.
waiter / waitress
服务员
He is a waiter at a café.
engineer
工程师
He is an engineer at a tech company.
programmer
程序员
She works as a programmer.
designer
设计师
He is a designer for an app.
manager
经理
He is a manager at a small company.
student
学生
She is a student at university.
