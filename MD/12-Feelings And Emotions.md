# Feelings & Emotions · 感受与情绪

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习情绪表达，了解 feel / be + 情绪词的用法，学会说出自己的感受，并应用到和同事、朋友聊天中。

## 1. Warm-up 热身

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| Your friend sends a long voice message. How do you feel? | 朋友发来超长语音，你什么感觉？ |
| → Annoyed / Curious / Tired | 烦 / 好奇 / 累 |
| When do you feel most relaxed? | 你什么时候最放松？ |
| → After work / In bed / Walking outside | 下班后 / 床上 / 在外面散步 |

## 2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

### Happy Words 开心词

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| happy / excited | 开心 / 兴奋 | I'm **excited** about the trip. |
| proud / thankful | 自豪 / 感激 | I'm **thankful** for my family. |
| lucky / surprised | 幸运 / 惊讶 | I feel **lucky** today. |

### Sad Words 低落词

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| sad / angry | 难过 / 生气 | I'm **angry** about the late delivery. |
| worried / stressed | 担心 / 有压力 | I feel **stressed** before deadlines. |
| tired / bored | 累 / 无聊 | I'm **tired** after work. |

### Body Words 身体感受

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hungry / thirsty | 饿 / 渴 | I'm **hungry** — let's order food. |
| sleepy / sick | 困 / 不舒服 | I feel **sleepy** at 3 pm. |
| hot / cold | 热 / 冷 | It's too **hot** today. |

## 3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

| English | Chinese | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| I feel **______** today because the metro is too crowded. | 我今天很……，因为地铁太挤了。 | tired / annoyed / stressed |
| My **______** makes me feel happy. | 我的……让我开心。 | friend / food / music |
| I'm **______** about the meeting tomorrow. | 我明天开会前很…… | worried / excited / nervous |

## 4. Discussion 讨论

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| Your boss sends a long voice message. How do you feel? | 老板发来长语音，你什么感觉？ | Worried / Bored / Curious |
| What makes you feel happy at work? | 什么让你上班开心？ | Good coworkers / Free lunch / Easy tasks |
| You get home after a long day. How do you feel? | 累了一天回到家，你什么感觉？ | Tired / Hungry / Happy |
| What helps you relax in the evening? | 晚上什么能让你放松？ | TV show / Phone call / Hot bath |
