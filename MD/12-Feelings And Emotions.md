## Discussion 讨论

**Your boss sends a long voice message. How do you feel?**

> worried / bored / curious / nervous / excited

**You get home after a long day. How do you feel?**

> tired / hungry / happy / sleepy / relaxed

**Your takeout is 40 minutes late. How do you feel?**

> angry / hungry / annoyed / stressed / sad

**You have a deadline tomorrow and it's already 10 pm. How do you feel?**

> stressed / tired / worried / nervous / sleepy

**A friend cancels your weekend plan at the last minute. How do you feel?**

> sad / bored / disappointed / surprised / angry

**What makes you feel happy at work?**

> good coworkers / free lunch / easy tasks / praise from my boss / finishing on time

**What helps you relax in the evening?**

> tv show / phone call / hot bath / music / a walk

**When do you feel most proud of yourself?**

> at work / after paying bills / after exercising / after helping a friend / after learning something new

**What makes you feel nervous?**

> interviews / talking to my boss / meeting new people / giving a speech / being late

**What always cheers you up when you're down?**

> music / food / calling a friend / funny videos / a walk outside
