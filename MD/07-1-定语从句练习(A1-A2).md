# 定语从句练习 A1-A2

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习定语从句，了解 who / which / that 的简单用法，学会用定语从句描述人和物，并应用到幼儿园和家庭的日常场景中。

第一阶梯
基础句式
不修饰，只陈述。
【幼儿园日常】
1. 我送孩子去幼儿园。
(I take the kids to kindergarten.)
2. 幼儿园八点开门。
(The kindergarten opens at eight.)
3. Leo 在幼儿园吃早饭。
(Leo eats breakfast at kindergarten.)
4. 我下午四点半接孩子。
(I pick up the kids at half past four.)
5. 今天幼儿园有亲子活动。
(There is a parent-child activity at kindergarten today.)
【家里的节奏】
6. 晚饭做好了。
(Dinner is ready.)
7. 孩子们在午睡。
(The kids are taking a nap.)
8. 洗衣机在转。
(The washing machine is running.)
9. 我把菜放进了冰箱。
(I put the vegetables in the fridge.)
10. 睡前要刷牙。
(We brush our teeth before bed.)
【北京的生活】
11. 我们住在北京。
(We live in Beijing.)
12. 小区门口有公交站。
(There is a bus stop at our gate.)
13. 今天地铁很挤。
(The subway is very crowded today.)
14. 菜市场的菜很新鲜。
(The vegetables at the market are fresh.)
15. 明天要降温。
(It will get colder tomorrow.)
【孩子们】
16. Leo 喜欢拼积木。
(Leo likes building blocks.)
17. Abby 在学画画。
(Abby is learning to draw.)
18. 孩子们在抢玩具。
(The kids are fighting over a toy.)
19. 哥哥在给妹妹讲故事。
(The big brother is telling his little sister a story.)
20. 孩子们都睡着了。
(The kids are all asleep.)

第二阶梯
加形容词
名词前面加一个形容词。
【早餐与厨房】
1. 我做了顿简单的晚饭。
(I made a **simple** dinner.)
2. 我买了条新鲜的鱼。
(I bought **fresh** fish.)
3. 我泡了杯热牛奶。
(I made a cup of **hot** milk.)
4. 冰箱里有新鲜的草莓。
(There are **fresh** strawberries in the fridge.)
5. 我用了便宜的鸡蛋。
(I used **cheap** eggs.)
【玩具与绘本】
6. Leo 想要个蓝色的玩具车。
(Leo wants a **blue** toy car.)
7. Abby 在看一本有趣的绘本。
(Abby is reading an **interesting** picture book.)
8. 我给孩子买了套新积木。
(I bought the kids a **new** set of blocks.)
9. 这是个很吵的玩具。
(This is a **noisy** toy.)
10. 那本旧绘本破了一页。
(The **old** picture book has a torn page.)
【小区与公园】
11. 小区里有个新滑梯。
(There's a **new** slide in our neighborhood.)
12. 我们常去附近的小公园。
(We often go to a **nearby** park.)
13. 公园里有干净的厕所。
(There are **clean** restrooms in the park.)
14. 小区门口有家好吃的包子铺。
(There's a **delicious** baozi shop at our gate.)
15. 快递柜旁边有棵大树。
(There's a **big** tree next to the parcel lockers.)
【天气与季节】
16. 今天是个好天气。
(It's a **nice** day today.)
17. 这是北京最冷的一个月。
(This is the **coldest** month in Beijing.)
18. 我买了件厚外套。
(I bought a **thick** coat.)
19. 孩子们穿了新雨鞋。
(The kids wore their **new** rain boots.)
20. 我们度过了一个开心的下午。
(We had a **happy** afternoon.)

第三阶梯
定语从句 — 作主语（who / that + 动词）
把形容词换成句子。who / that 后面直接跟动词。
【孩子】
1. 我有个每天要听故事的儿子。
(I have a son **who wants a story every day**.)
2. 我有个爱穿裙子的女儿。
(I have a daughter **who loves to wear dresses**.)
3. 我认识一个会自己穿鞋的孩子。
(I know a child **who can put on his shoes by himself**.)
4. 有个小朋友把积木推倒了。
(There's a kid **who knocked down the blocks**.)
5. 爱哭的那个孩子现在睡着了。
(The child **who cries a lot** is asleep now.)
【老师与邻居】
6. 我认识一位在北京教英语的老师。
(I know a teacher **who teaches English in Beijing**.)
7. 幼儿园有位会讲故事的老师。
(There's a teacher at kindergarten **who tells great stories**.)
8. 隔壁住着一位会做包子的阿姨。
(There's an auntie next door **who makes great baozi**.)
9. 小区里有个能和 Leo 玩的外国小朋友。
(There's a foreign child in our neighborhood **who can play with Leo**.)
10. 我认识一位会修电器的师傅。
(I know a repairman **who can fix appliances**.)
【东西与购物】
11. 这是一本能教孩子认字的书。
(This is a book **that teaches kids to read**.)
12. 我买了辆能带两个孩子的电动车。
(I bought an e-bike **that can carry two kids**.)
13. 我买了件耐脏的围裙。
(I bought an apron **that doesn't show dirt**.)
14. 我买了个会自己关的灯。
(I bought a lamp **that turns off by itself**.)
15. 我找了辆能折叠的婴儿车。
(I found a stroller **that folds up**.)
【地点与活动】
16. 我们去了一个有很多游乐设施的小区公园。
(We went to a park **that has lots of playground equipment**.)
17. 这是一家卖手工玩具的小店。
(This is a shop **that sells handmade toys**.)
18. 我报了一个教亲子英语的课。
(I joined a class **that teaches parent-child English**.)
19. 我们常去一家能让孩子玩沙子的餐厅。
(We often go to a restaurant **that lets kids play with sand**.)
20. 我下了一个能省钱的买菜软件。
(I downloaded an app **that helps me save money on groceries**.)

第四阶梯
定语从句 — 作主语 · 加细节
从句写长一点，加入副词或并列结构。
【孩子的小习惯】
1. 我有个早上总赖床、起床很慢的儿子。
(I have a son **who stays in bed and gets up very slowly**.)
2. 我有个吃完饭就跑、不擦嘴的女儿。
(I have a daughter **who runs away after eating and doesn't wipe her mouth**.)
3. 我有个爱问问题、问个不停的孩子。
(I have a child **who asks questions all the time**.)
4. Leo 是个会照顾妹妹的好哥哥。
(Leo is a big brother **who takes care of his little sister**.)
5. 我有个一坐车就睡着的孩子。
(I have a child **who falls asleep on the bus every time**.)
【北京出行】
6. 我住在一个离菜场和幼儿园都近的小区。
(I live in a neighborhood **that is close to the market and the kindergarten**.)
7. 我买了辆满电能跑一天的电动车。
(I bought an e-bike **that can run all day on one charge**.)
8. 这是辆不用钥匙就能开的电动车。
(This is an e-bike **that starts without a key**.)
9. 我们常坐一辆不太挤的公交。
(We often take a bus **that is not too crowded**.)
10. 我找了个离地铁站很近的幼儿园。
(I found a kindergarten **that is very close to the subway**.)
【做饭与采购】
11. 我买了个早上能自己煮粥的电饭煲。
(I bought a rice cooker **that can cook porridge by itself in the morning**.)
12. 我买了包煮起来很快的面条。
(I bought noodles **that cook very fast**.)
13. 我买了个装得多、推着也轻的购物车。
(I bought a cart **that holds a lot and is easy to push**.)
14. 我买了个能保鲜很久的饭盒。
(I bought a lunch box **that keeps food fresh for a long time**.)
15. 我下载了一个能帮孩子学英语的软件。
(I downloaded an app **that helps the kids learn English**.)
【周末与娱乐】
16. 我们常去一个能滑滑梯、还能玩沙子的公园。
(We often go to a park **that has a slide and a sandbox**.)
17. 我给孩子买了套能拼房子、也能拼车的积木。
(I bought the kids blocks **that can make houses and cars**.)
18. 我选了本图画多、字很少的绘本。
(I chose a picture book **that has many pictures and few words**.)
19. 我们看了一部孩子喜欢、大人也喜欢的动画片。
(We watched a cartoon **that kids love and adults like too**.)
20. 我认识一位会烤蛋糕、还会说中文的外教。
(I know a teacher **who can bake cakes and speak Chinese**.)

第五阶梯
定语从句 — 作宾语（that / whom 可省略）
从句修饰宾语。that / whom 可以省略。
【早餐与做饭】
1. 我早上煮的那碗粥 Leo 全喝完了。
(The porridge **(that) I made this morning** is all gone.)
2. 我买的那些鸡蛋很便宜。
(The eggs **(that) I bought** were cheap.)
3. 我做的红烧肉是孩子们的最爱。
(The braised pork **(that) I cooked** is the kids' favorite.)
4. 我热的那杯牛奶凉了。
(The milk **(that) I warmed up** is cold now.)
5. 我今天买的鱼晚上做。
(I'll cook the fish **(that) I bought today** tonight.)
【网购与快递】
6. 我在网上买的那双鞋有点小。
(The shoes **(that) I bought online** are a little small.)
7. 我订的那箱牛奶下午到了。
(The milk **(that) I ordered** arrived in the afternoon.)
8. 我买的那个拼图玩具到了。
(The puzzle **(that) I bought** has arrived.)
9. 我退的那件衣服退款了。
(I got my money back for the dress **(that) I returned**.)
10. 我买的那袋米太重了。
(The rice **(that) I bought** is too heavy.)
【幼儿园与手工】
11. 孩子们在幼儿园做的手工很可爱。
(The craft **(that) the kids made in kindergarten** is very cute.)
12. Leo 画的那幅画贴在冰箱上。
(The picture **(that) Leo drew** is on the fridge.)
13. 老师发的通知我看完了。
(I read the notice **(that) the teacher sent**.)
14. 幼儿园发的食谱不错。
(The menu **(that) the kindergarten gave us** looks good.)
15. 孩子们昨天学的歌很好听。
(The song **(that) the kids learned yesterday** is lovely.)
【日常小事】
16. 你昨天讲的那个故事是孩子们的最爱。
(The story **(that) you told yesterday** is the kids' favorite.)
17. 我每天骑的那辆电动车没电了。
(The e-bike **(that) I ride every day** has no power.)
18. 这家店卖的包子很好吃。
(The buns **(that) this shop sells** are delicious.)
19. 我洗的那些衣服已经干了。
(The clothes **(that) I washed** are dry now.)
20. 我们上周拍的照片洗好了。
(The photos **(that) we took last week** are printed.)

第六阶梯
定语从句 — 作宾语 · 加细节
从句写长一点，开头句式变一变。
【采购与做饭】
1. 我昨天在菜市场买的那条鱼很新鲜。
(The fish **(that) I bought at the market yesterday** is very fresh.)
2. 我用优惠券买的那箱牛奶很划算。
(The milk **(that) I bought with a coupon** was a good deal.)
3. 我早上给孩子热的那杯奶没喝完。
(The milk **(that) I warmed for the kids this morning** is not finished.)
4. 我在楼下小店买的那包盐用完了。
(The salt **(that) I bought at the corner shop** is all gone.)
5. 我上周腌的那些萝卜可以吃了。
(The radish **(that) I pickled last week** is ready to eat.)
【网购与家电】
6. 我在网上买的那个扫地机器人很好用。
(The robot vacuum **(that) I bought online** works very well.)
7. 我在网上退的那双凉鞋退款了。
(I got my money back for the sandals **(that) I returned online**.)
8. 我给孩子买的那本点读书到了。
(The talking book **(that) I bought for the kids** has arrived.)
9. 我去年买的那辆婴儿车送给朋友了。
(The stroller **(that) I bought last year** is now with a friend.)
10. 我在二手平台挂的那个玩具卖出去了。
(The toy **(that) I listed on the second-hand app** is sold.)
【孩子的东西】
11. Leo 在幼儿园画的那幅画贴在冰箱上。
(The picture **(that) Leo drew in kindergarten** is on the fridge.)
12. 孩子们昨天看的那个动画片里有只猫。
(The cartoon **(that) the kids watched yesterday** has a cat in it.)
13. 我给孩子买的那件雨衣有点大。
(The raincoat **(that) I bought for the kids** is a little big.)
14. 老师上周表扬的那幅画是 Abby 画的。
(The picture **(that) the teacher praised last week** is Abby's.)
15. 我们上周玩过的那个沙坑现在还在。
(The sandbox **(that) we played in last week** is still there.)
【邻里与小区】
16. 我今天在楼下捡到的那串钥匙是谁的？
(Whose are the keys **(that) I found downstairs today**?)
17. 邻居送的那筐草莓很甜。
(The strawberries **(that) the neighbor gave us** are very sweet.)
18. 我订的那个快递放错柜子了。
(The parcel **(that) I ordered** went to the wrong locker.)
19. 物业贴的那张通知说明天停水。
(The notice **(that) the property office put up** says the water will stop tomorrow.)
20. 我早上买的那杯豆浆已经凉了。
(The soy milk **(that) I bought this morning** is already cold.)

第七阶梯
关系副词 — WHERE 地点
修饰地点。where = 在那里。
【小区与周边】
1. 我们住的小区楼下就有快递柜。
(The neighborhood **where we live** has parcel lockers downstairs.)
2. 这是孩子们天天玩滑梯的公园。
(This is the park **where the kids play on the slide every day**.)
3. 孩子们午睡的那个房间朝南。
(The room **where the kids take naps** faces south.)
4. 我们放儿童车的那间储藏室很小。
(The storage room **where we keep the stroller** is small.)
5. 我晾衣服的那个阳台朝西。
(The balcony **where I hang the clothes** faces west.)
【买菜与购物】
6. 我常去买菜的那家超市今天打折。
(The supermarket **where I usually shop** has a sale today.)
7. 我买鸡蛋的那家店在菜市场里面。
(The shop **where I buy eggs** is inside the market.)
8. 那家卖手擀面的店生意很好。
(The shop **where they make noodles by hand** is always busy.)
9. 这是我拿快递的那个驿站。
(This is the pickup station **where I get my packages**.)
10. 我买豆浆的那家早餐店早上排长队。
(The breakfast shop **where I buy soy milk** has a long line in the morning.)
【公园与玩耍】
11. 我们周末去野餐的那个公园人很多。
(The park **where we picnic on weekends** is crowded.)
12. 这是孩子们可以跑着玩的广场。
(This is the square **where the kids can run and play**.)
13. 我常带孩子看鱼的那个水池在公园东边。
(The pond **where I take the kids to see fish** is on the east side of the park.)
14. 我们放风筝的那块草地很大。
(The lawn **where we fly kites** is very big.)
15. 孩子们学轮滑的那个广场晚上很热闹。
(The square **where the kids learn skating** is lively at night.)
【学校与接送】
16. 孩子们上幼儿园的那栋楼离家很近。
(The building **where the kids go to kindergarten** is close to home.)
17. 我每天等孩子放学的那个门口有棵大树。
(The gate **where I wait for the kids every day** has a big tree.)
18. 这是家长接送孩子的那条路。
(This is the road **where parents pick up and drop off their kids**.)
19. 我小时候常去的那家书店还在。
(The bookstore **where I went as a child** is still there.)
20. 我学游泳的那个游泳馆拆了。
(The swimming pool **where I learned to swim** is gone.)

第八阶梯
关系副词 — WHEN 时间 & WHY 原因
上 10 句修饰时间，下 10 句修饰原因。
【WHEN · 时间】
1. 我还记得第一次送 Leo 上幼儿园的那天。
(I still remember the day **when I first took Leo to kindergarten**.)
2. 孩子们睡着后的那一小时是我自己的时间。
(The hour **when the kids are asleep** is my own time.)
3. 老公加班回家的那个晚上孩子们已经睡了。
(The night **when my husband came home late**, the kids were already asleep.)
4. 接孩子放学的那一刻是我一天最开心的时候。
(The moment **when I pick up the kids** is my favorite time of the day.)
5. 早上叫孩子起床的那十分钟最累。
(The ten minutes **when I wake the kids up** are the hardest.)
6. 周末早上是我能多睡一会儿的时候。
(Weekend mornings are the time **when I can sleep a little longer**.)
7. 秋天是北京最美的时候。
(Autumn is the season **when Beijing is most beautiful**.)
8. 我还记得孩子们第一次自己穿鞋的那天。
(I still remember the day **when the kids put on their shoes by themselves for the first time**.)
9. 周三下午是幼儿园上亲子课的时候。
(Wednesday afternoon is the time **when the kindergarten has the parent-child class**.)
10. 晚上六点以后是小区广场最热闹的时候。
(After six in the evening is the time **when our neighborhood square is most lively**.)
【WHY · 原因】
11. 这就是我每天带娃去公园的原因。
(This is the reason **why I take the kids to the park every day**.)
12. 你知道 Leo 不想睡午觉的原因吗？
(Do you know the reason **why Leo doesn't want to take a nap**?)
13. 孩子们午睡长是我能和朋友喝下午茶的原因。
(The kids' long nap is the reason **why I can have afternoon tea with friends**.)
14. 这就是我不给孩子买太多零食的原因。
(This is the reason **why I don't buy too many snacks for the kids**.)
15. 这就是我每天学英语的原因。
(This is the reason **why I study English every day**.)
16. 你知道我选这家幼儿园的原因吗？
(Do you know the reason **why I chose this kindergarten**?)
17. 这就是我总在同一个菜摊买菜的原因。
(This is the reason **why I always buy vegetables from the same stall**.)
18. 天气不好是我带孩子坐地铁的原因。
(Bad weather is the reason **why I take the kids on the subway**.)
19. 这就是我不让孩子看太多动画片的原因。
(This is the reason **why I don't let the kids watch too many cartoons**.)
20. 这就是我每天六点起床的原因。
(This is the reason **why I get up at six every day**.)
