# 100 Common English Phrases and Sentence Patterns

> 来源：https://basicenglishspeaking.com/100-common-phrases-and-sentence-patterns/

## 01 日常插话与转场  SMALL TALK & FILLERS

### 007. By the way…

**Examples**

- By the way, dinner is at seven.
  - 对了，晚饭七点开饭。
- By the way, the store is closed today.
  - 对了，今天商店关门。
- By the way, I have your umbrella.
  - 对了，你的伞在我这儿。

**Dialogue**

> A: Let’s meet at the park.
> 我们在公园见面吧。
> B: OK. By the way, what time?
> 好。对了，几点？
> A: Six. By the way, bring water.
> 六点。对了，带点水。
> B: Sure.
> 好。

### 025. How come…?

**Examples**

- How come you are so late?
  - 你怎么这么晚？
- How come the store is closed?
  - 商店怎么关门了？
- How come you don’t like coffee?
  - 你怎么不喜欢咖啡？

**Dialogue**

> A: How come you are so late?
> 你怎么这么晚？
> B: The bus didn’t come.
> 公交没来。
> A: How come?
> 怎么回事？
> B: I don’t know. Maybe traffic.
> 不知道，可能堵车。

### 055. It’s on the tip of my tongue.

**Examples**

- His name is on the tip of my tongue.
  - 他的名字就在嘴边。
- The word is on the tip of my tongue.
  - 那个词就在嘴边。
- The song name is on the tip of my tongue.
  - 那首歌的名字就在嘴边。

**Dialogue**

> A: What’s the name of that song?
> 那首歌叫什么？
> B: It’s on the tip of my tongue.
> 就在嘴边。
> A: Take your time.
> 慢慢想。
> B: Sorry, it’s still on the tip of my tongue.
> 抱歉，还是想不起来。

### 058. It’s your turn…

**Examples**

- It’s your turn to order.
  - 轮到你点菜了。
- It’s your turn to pay.
  - 轮到你付钱了。
- It’s your turn to sing.
  - 轮到你唱歌了。

**Dialogue**

> A: This song is nice.
> 这首歌真好听。
> B: It’s your turn to sing.
> 轮到你唱了。
> A: No, it’s your turn!
> 不，该你了！
> B: OK, I’ll sing first.
> 好，我先唱。

### 071. Speaking of…

**Examples**

- Speaking of food, I’m hungry.
  - 说到吃的，我饿了。
- Speaking of the movie, let’s watch it.
  - 说到那部电影，我们看吧。
- Speaking of Tom, he called you.
  - 说到汤姆，他给你打过电话。

**Dialogue**

> A: I want to eat.
> 我想吃东西。
> B: Speaking of food, there’s a new shop.
> 说到吃的，有家新店。
> A: Speaking of new shops, let’s try it.
> 说到新店，我们去试试。
> B: OK.
> 好。

### 072. Thanks to…

**Examples**

- Thanks to you, I’m on time.
  - 多亏了你，我准时到了。
- Thanks to the map, I found the place.
  - 多亏了地图，我找到了地方。
- Thanks to the rain, it’s cool now.
  - 多亏了这场雨，现在凉快了。

**Dialogue**

> A: You are early today!
> 你今天来早了！
> B: Thanks to you, I woke up on time.
> 多亏了你，我按时起床了。
> A: Good. Thanks to the new bus, I’m early too.
> 好。多亏了新公交，我也早到了。
> B: Great.
> 太好了。

### 084. …what-do-you-call-it (what·cha·ma·call·it)

**Examples**

- Pass me the what-do-you-call-it… the thing for the bottle.
  - 把那个什么递给我……开瓶子的那个。
- I put it in the whatchamacallit… the blue box.
  - 我把它放在那个叫什么来着……蓝盒子里。
- Where is the what-do-you-call-it? The remote.
  - 那个什么在哪儿？遥控器。

**Dialogue**

> A: Where is the what-do-you-call-it? The remote.
> 那个什么在哪儿？遥控器。
> B: It’s on the table.
> 在桌上。
> A: And the whatchamacallit? The charger.
> 还有那个什么？充电器。
> B: Next to the bed.
> 在床边。


## 02 确认与询问  CONFIRMING & ASKING

### 001. Are you sure…?

**Examples**

- Are you sure the store opens at nine?
  - 你确定商店九点开门吗？
- Are you sure we have milk at home?
  - 你确定家里还有牛奶吗？
- Are you sure you locked the door?
  - 你确定锁门了吗？

**Dialogue**

> A: I’m hungry. Let’s eat.
> 我饿了，我们吃饭吧。
> B: Are you sure? The noodles are still hot.
> 你确定？面条还热着呢。
> A: Yes, I want some.
> 是的，我想吃点。
> B: OK, let’s eat.
> 好，那吃吧。

### 011. Do you agree…?

**Examples**

- Do you agree that the movie is good?
  - 你同意这部电影好看吗？
- Do you agree with me?
  - 你同意我的看法吗？
- Do you agree that we go by subway?
  - 你同意我们坐地铁去吗？

**Dialogue**

> A: The movie was so good.
> 这部电影太好看了。
> B: Do you agree that we watch it again?
> 你同意我们再看一遍吗？
> A: Yes! Do you agree to go tomorrow?
> 同意！你同意明天去吗？
> B: Sure.
> 当然。

### 017. Have you ever…?

**Examples**

- Have you ever been to Beijing?
  - 你去过北京吗？
- Have you ever tried this cake?
  - 你吃过这个蛋糕吗？
- Have you ever lost your keys?
  - 你丢过钥匙吗？

**Dialogue**

> A: Have you ever been to the new park?
> 你去过那个新公园吗？
> B: No. Is it nice?
> 没有。那里好吗？
> A: Yes. Have you ever walked there?
> 好。你走过那边吗？
> B: Not yet. Let’s go.
> 还没，我们去吧。

### 027. How do you like…?

**Examples**

- How do you like the food?
  - 你觉得这里的饭菜怎么样？
- How do you like your new home?
  - 你觉得你的新家怎么样？
- How do you like the weather here?
  - 你觉得这里的天气怎么样？

**Dialogue**

> A: How do you like the food here?
> 你觉得这里的饭菜怎么样？
> B: I like it.
> 我喜欢。
> A: How do you like the soup?
> 你觉得汤怎么样？
> B: It’s a bit salty, but good.
> 有点咸，但还行。

### 028. How long does it take…?

**Examples**

- How long does it take to get there?
  - 到那儿要多久？
- How long does it take to cook rice?
  - 煮米饭要多久？
- How long does it take to walk to the station?
  - 走到车站要多久？

**Dialogue**

> A: How long does it take to walk to the station?
> 走到车站要多久？
> B: About ten minutes.
> 大概十分钟。
> A: How long does it take by bus?
> 坐公交要多久？
> B: Five minutes, but the bus is slow today.
> 五分钟，但今天公交很慢。

### 029. How often…?

**Examples**

- How often do you cook?
  - 你多久做一次饭？
- How often do you call your mom?
  - 你多久给妈妈打一次电话？
- How often does the bus come?
  - 公交多久来一趟？

**Dialogue**

> A: How often do you cook?
> 你多久做一次饭？
> B: Every day.
> 每天都做。
> A: How often do you eat out?
> 你多久出去吃一次？
> B: On weekends.
> 周末出去吃。

### 062. I wonder if…？

**Examples**

- I wonder if he is home.
  - 我不知道他在不在家。
- I wonder if it will rain.
  - 我不知道会不会下雨。
- I wonder if the store is open.
  - 我不知道商店开不开门。

**Dialogue**

> A: I wonder if the bus will come soon.
> 我不知道公交会不会很快来。
> B: Me too. I wonder if it’s late.
> 我也是。我不知道是不是晚点了。
> A: Let’s wait five more minutes.
> 我们再等五分钟。
> B: OK.
> 好。

### 081. What becomes of…？

**Examples**

- What becomes of the food after the party?
  - 聚会后那些吃的会怎样？
- What becomes of the old clothes?
  - 那些旧衣服会怎样？
- What becomes of the empty boxes?
  - 那些空箱子会怎样？

**Dialogue**

> A: What becomes of the food after dinner?
> 晚饭剩下的饭菜会怎样？
> B: We put it in the fridge.
> 我们放进冰箱。
> A: What becomes of the rest?
> 剩下的呢？
> B: We eat it tomorrow.
> 明天吃掉。

### 092. What’s your favorite…?

**Examples**

- What’s your favorite food?
  - 你最喜欢吃什么？
- What’s your favorite song?
  - 你最喜欢哪首歌？
- What’s your favorite place?
  - 你最喜欢什么地方？

**Dialogue**

> A: What’s your favorite food?
> 你最喜欢吃什么？
> B: Noodles.
> 面条。
> A: What’s your favorite place to eat them?
> 你最喜欢去哪儿吃？
> B: The shop near my home.
> 我家附近那家店。

### 093. Where can I…?

**Examples**

- Where can I buy milk?
  - 我在哪儿能买到牛奶？
- Where can I sit?
  - 我坐哪儿？
- Where can I find the bus stop?
  - 我在哪儿能找到公交站？

**Dialogue**

> A: Where can I buy water?
> 我在哪儿能买到水？
> B: At the shop over there.
> 那边那家店。
> A: Where can I find the bus stop?
> 我在哪儿能找到公交站？
> B: Next to the shop.
> 就在商店旁边。


## 03 习惯与适应  HABITS & ADJUSTMENT

### 002. Are you used to?

**Examples**

- Are you used to the cold here?
  - 你习惯这里的冷天气了吗？
- Are you used to getting up early?
  - 你习惯早起吗？
- Are you used to eating spicy food?
  - 你习惯吃辣吗？

**Dialogue**

> A: You moved here last year, right?
> 你去年搬来的，对吧？
> B: Yes. Are you used to the cold here?
> 对。你习惯这里的冷天气了吗？
> A: Almost. It’s getting better.
> 差不多，越来越习惯了。
> B: Good. The winter is long.
> 那就好，这里的冬天很长。

### 009. Did you use to…

**Examples**

- Did you use to live here?
  - 你以前住这儿吗？
- Did you use to run in the morning?
  - 你以前早上跑步吗？
- Did you use to cook at home?
  - 你以前在家做饭吗？

**Dialogue**

> A: Did you use to live near this park?
> 你以前住在这个公园附近吗？
> B: Yes, ten years ago.
> 对，十年前。
> A: Did you use to come here often?
> 你以前常来这儿吗？
> B: Every weekend.
> 每个周末都来。


## 04 观点与判断  OPINIONS & JUDGMENT

### 003. As far as…

**Examples**

- As far as I know, the shop is open.
  - 据我所知，那家店开着。
- As far as I can see, this bag is too big.
  - 依我看，这个包太大了。
- As far as I remember, the store is on this street.
  - 我记得，那家店就在这条街上。

**Dialogue**

> A: Is the bank open today?
> 今天银行开门吗？
> B: As far as I know, yes.
> 据我所知，开。
> A: Good. As far as I can see, it’s open until six.
> 好，我看它开到六点。
> B: Let’s go now.
> 那我们走吧。

### 004. As far as… (is) (am) (are) concerned,…

**Examples**

- As far as I’m concerned, this food is good.
  - 在我看来，这饭挺好吃的。
- As far as the price is concerned, it’s OK.
  - 就价格来说，还可以。
- As far as the plan is concerned, we are fine.
  - 就计划来说，我们没问题。

**Dialogue**

> A: Do you like this café?
> 你喜欢这家咖啡店吗？
> B: As far as I’m concerned, it’s great.
> 在我看来，很好。
> A: The coffee is a bit sweet.
> 咖啡有点甜。
> B: As far as the price is concerned, it’s fair.
> 就价格来说，还算公道。

### 033. I can’t say…

**Examples**

- I can’t say I like it.
  - 我不能说我喜欢它。
- I can’t say the movie is good.
  - 我不能说这电影好看。
- I can’t say I’m happy about it.
  - 我不能说我对此满意。

**Dialogue**

> A: How is the new café?
> 新咖啡店怎么样？
> B: I can’t say I like it.
> 我不能说我喜欢。
> A: Why?
> 为什么？
> B: The coffee is too sweet.
> 咖啡太甜了。

### 035. I dare say…

**Examples**

- I dare say it will rain.
  - 我敢说会下雨。
- I dare say she is right.
  - 我敢说她说得对。
- I dare say he will be late.
  - 我敢说他会迟到。

**Dialogue**

> A: Is it cold outside?
> 外面冷吗？
> B: I dare say it is. Take a coat.
> 我敢说冷。带件外套。
> A: OK. I dare say it will rain too.
> 好。我敢说还会下雨。
> B: Take an umbrella.
> 带把伞。

### 040. I have no idea…

**Examples**

- I have no idea where my keys are.
  - 我不知道我的钥匙在哪儿。
- I have no idea what to eat.
  - 我不知道吃什么。
- I have no idea when the bus comes.
  - 我不知道公交什么时候来。

**Dialogue**

> A: Where is my phone?
> 我的手机在哪儿？
> B: I have no idea. Did you check the kitchen?
> 我不知道。你查过厨房吗？
> A: Yes. I have no idea where I put it.
> 查了。我不知道放哪儿了。
> B: Call it with my phone.
> 用我的手机打一下。
### 051. It is… that…

**Examples**

- It is my mom who cooks dinner.
  - 是我妈妈做的晚饭。
- It was the rain that made me late.
  - 是下雨让我迟到了。
- It is in the morning that I run.
  - 我是早上跑步的。

**Dialogue**

> A: Who cleaned the room?
> 谁打扫的房间？
> B: It was my sister who cleaned it.
> 是我姐姐打扫的。
> A: Nice. It was the cake that I ate most.
> 真好。蛋糕是我吃得最多的。
> B: Haha, I know.
> 哈哈，我知道。

### 056. It’s said that…

**Examples**

- It’s said that the food here is good.
  - 据说这里的饭菜好吃。
- It’s said that it will rain tomorrow.
  - 据说明天会下雨。
- It’s said that this shop is old.
  - 据说这家店很老。

**Dialogue**

> A: Why is there a long line?
> 为什么排这么长的队？
> B: It’s said that the bread here is good.
> 据说这里的面包好吃。
> A: It’s said that they sell out fast.
> 据说很快就卖完了。
> B: Then let’s hurry.
> 那我们赶紧去。

### 059. It may surprise you, but…

**Examples**

- It may surprise you, but I can cook.
  - 你可能想不到，我会做饭。
- It may surprise you, but I like mornings.
  - 你可能想不到，我喜欢早上。
- It may surprise you, but I don’t like milk tea.
  - 你可能想不到，我不喜欢奶茶。

**Dialogue**

> A: You look so happy.
> 你看起来很开心。
> B: It may surprise you, but I like Mondays.
> 你可能想不到，我喜欢周一。
> A: Really? It may surprise you, but I do too.
> 真的吗？你可能也想不到，我也喜欢。
> B: Ha, then we are the same.
> 哈哈，那我们一样。

### 065. No wonder…

**Examples**

- You ate a lot; no wonder you’re full.
  - 你吃了很多，难怪你饱了。
- You ran; no wonder you’re tired.
  - 你跑了步，难怪你累了。
- No wonder you’re cold. The window is open.
  - 难怪你冷，窗户开着呢。

**Dialogue**

> A: I’m so tired.
> 我好累。
> B: No wonder. You woke up at 5.
> 难怪。你五点就起了。
> A: Yes. No wonder I can’t keep my eyes open.
> 是啊，难怪我睁不开眼。
> B: Sleep early tonight.
> 今晚早点睡。


## 05 比较与选择  COMPARISONS & CHOICES

### 008. Compared to…

**Examples**

- Compared to yesterday, it’s cold today.
  - 跟昨天比，今天很冷。
- Compared to the bus, the subway is fast.
  - 跟公交比，地铁快多了。
- This phone is cheap compared to that one.
  - 这个手机比那个便宜。

**Dialogue**

> A: How is the new bike?
> 新自行车怎么样？
> B: Compared to my old one, it’s much better.
> 跟我旧的那辆比，好太多了。
> A: Is it fast?
> 快吗？
> B: Yes. Compared to walking, it’s very fast.
> 快。跟走路比，特别快。

### 019. He is as… as…

**Examples**

- He is as tall as me.
  - 他和我一样高。
- The water is as cold as ice.
  - 水跟冰一样凉。
- This bag is as light as paper.
  - 这个包和纸一样轻。

**Dialogue**

> A: Who is the tall man?
> 那个高个子是谁？
> B: That’s Tom. He is as tall as my brother.
> 是汤姆。他和我哥一样高。
> A: Oh. Is he as young as us?
> 哦。他跟我们一样年轻吗？
> B: Yes, same age.
> 对，同龄。

### 020. He is either…or…

**Examples**

- He is either at home or at work.
  - 他不是在家就是在上班。
- She is either sleeping or watching TV.
  - 她不是在睡觉就是在看电视。
- I’m either too early or too late.
  - 我不是太早就是太晚。

**Dialogue**

> A: Where is Dad?
> 爸爸在哪儿？
> B: He is either in the kitchen or in the yard.
> 他不是在厨房就是在院子里。
> A: I checked. He’s not there.
> 我都看了，不在。
> B: Then he is either outside or at the store.
> 那他要么出去了要么在商店。
### 021. He is so… that…

**Examples**

- The soup is so hot that I can’t drink it.
  - 汤太烫了，我没法喝。
- The movie is so good that I want to see it again.
  - 这电影太好看了，我想再看一遍。
- The bag is so heavy that I can’t carry it.
  - 这个包太重了，我拎不动。

**Dialogue**

> A: How is the soup?
> 汤怎么样？
> B: It’s so hot that I can’t drink it.
> 太烫了，我没法喝。
> A: Wait a minute.
> 等一会儿。
> B: OK. It’s so good that I don’t want to stop.
> 好。太好喝了，我停不下来。

### 022. He is not only… but also…

**Examples**

- She is not only kind but also smart.
  - 她不仅善良，而且聪明。
- This place is not only cheap but also clean.
  - 这地方不仅便宜，而且干净。
- He is not only my friend but also my neighbor.
  - 他不仅是我的朋友，还是我的邻居。

**Dialogue**

> A: Who is Lily?
> 莉莉是谁？
> B: She is not only my colleague but also my neighbor.
> 她不仅是我的同事，还是我的邻居。
> A: Nice. Is she kind?
> 那挺好的。她善良吗？
> B: Yes, she is not only kind but also funny.
> 善良，她不仅善良，而且很有趣。

### 042…. as… as possible

**Examples**

- Please come as soon as possible.
  - 请尽快来。
- Please speak as slowly as possible.
  - 请尽量说慢一点。

  - 让包尽量轻一点。

> 你什么时候能来？
> B: As soon as possible. I’m leaving now.
> 尽快。我现在就出发。
> A: OK. Come as quickly as possible.
> 好，尽快来。
> B: I will.
> 我会的。

### 050. I really go for…

**Examples**

- I really go for sweet food.
  - 我特别爱吃甜的。
- I really go for this song.
  - 我特别喜欢这首歌。
- I really go for the blue one.
  - 我特别喜欢蓝色的那个。

**Dialogue**

> 你喜欢哪个包？
> B: I really go for the blue one.
> 我特别喜欢蓝色的。
> A: Me too. I really go for the small one.
> 我也是。我特别喜欢小的那个。
> B: Let’s buy the blue one.
> 那就买蓝色的吧。

### 057. It’s up to…

**Examples**

- It’s up to you.
  - 你说了算。
- It’s up to him to decide.
  - 由他来决定。
- It’s up to the weather whether we go out.
  - 出不出门取决于天气。

**Dialogue**

**Examples**

- I bet he is late again.
  - 我打赌他又要迟到。
- I bet the food is good.
  - 我打赌这饭很好吃。
- I bet you like this song.
  - 我打赌你喜欢这首歌。

**Dialogue**

> A: Where is Tom?
> 汤姆在哪儿？
> B: I bet he is still sleeping.
> 我打赌他还在睡觉。
> A: I bet he forgot the time.
> 我打赌他忘了时间。
> B: Call him.
> 给他打电话。

### 031. I can hardly believe that…

**Examples**

- I can hardly believe that you are here!
  - 我真不敢相信你在这儿！
- I can hardly believe that this cake is so cheap.
  - 我真不敢相信这蛋糕这么便宜。
- I can hardly believe that he said that.
  - 我真不敢相信他说了那种话。

**Dialogue**

> A: I got a new job!
> 我找到新工作了！
> B: I can hardly believe that! That’s great!
> 我真不敢相信！太棒了！
> A: I can hardly believe that I start on Monday.
> 我真不敢相信我周一开始上班。
> B: Let’s eat out to celebrate.
> 我们去吃饭庆祝一下。

### 032. I can’t help…

**Examples**

- I can’t help laughing.
  - 我忍不住笑。
- I can’t help looking at my phone.
  - 我忍不住看手机。
- I can’t help eating more cake.
  - 我忍不住多吃蛋糕。

**Dialogue**

> A: Why are you laughing?
> 你笑什么？
> B: I can’t help laughing at this video.
> 我忍不住笑这个视频。
> A: Show me.
> 给我看看。
> B: You’ll laugh too. You can’t help it.
> 你也会笑的，你忍不住的。

### 037. I’d hate for you to…

**Examples**

- I’d hate for you to be late.
  - 我不想让你迟到。
- I’d hate for you to get wet.
  - 我不想让你淋湿。
- I’d hate for you to miss the bus.
  - 我不想让你错过公交。

**Dialogue**

> A: I’m going out now.
> 我现在要出去了。
> B: I’d hate for you to get wet. Take an umbrella.
> 我不想让你淋湿，带把伞。
> A: OK. I’d hate for you to wait for me.
> 好。我不想让你等我。
> B: I won’t. Go.
> 我不等，你走吧。

### 039. If there is one thing that… me, it’s…

**Examples**

- If there is one thing that makes me happy, it’s good food.
  - 如果说有什么让我开心，那就是好吃的。
- If there is one thing that bothers me, it’s loud noise.
  - 如果说有什么让我烦，那就是吵闹声。
- If there is one thing that worries me, it’s losing my keys.
  - 如果说有什么让我担心，那就是丢钥匙。

**Dialogue**

> A: What do you hate about the bus?
> 你讨厌公交的什么？
> B: If there is one thing that bothers me, it’s the noise.
> 如果说有什么让我烦，那就是噪音。
> A: Me too. If there is one thing that helps me, it’s my earphones.
> 我也是。如果说有什么能帮我，那就是耳机。
> B: Good idea.
> 好主意。

## 11 假设与条件  HYPOTHETICALS & CONDITIONS

### 038. If it hadn’t been for…

**Examples**

- If it hadn’t been for your help, I would have been late.
  - 要不是你帮忙，我就迟到了。
- If it hadn’t been for the rain, we would have gone out.
  - 要不是下雨，我们就出去了。
- If it hadn’t been for the map, I would have gotten lost.
  - 要不是有地图，我就迷路了。

**Dialogue**

> A: You got here on time!
> 你准时到了！
> B: If it hadn’t been for you, I would have been late.
> 要不是你，我就迟到了。
> A: Me?
> 我？
> B: Yes, you woke me up. If it hadn’t been for your call, I would have slept in.
> 对，你叫醒了我。要不是你打电话，我就睡过头了。

### 087. What if…?

**Examples**

- What if it rains?
  - 下雨怎么办？
- What if he doesn’t come?
  - 他不来怎么办？
- What if we are late?
  - 我们迟到了怎么办？

**Dialogue**

> A: Let’s go to the park.
> 我们去公园吧。
> B: What if it rains?
> 下雨怎么办？
> A: Then we stay home.
> 那就待在家。
> B: What if the park is closed?
> 公园关门怎么办？
> A: We’ll see.
> 到时候再说。

### 090. What would you do if…?

**Examples**

- What would you do if you lost your phone?
  - 如果你手机丢了怎么办？
- What would you do if it rained?
  - 如果下雨了怎么办？
- What would you do if he said no?
  - 如果他拒绝怎么办？

**Dialogue**

> A: What would you do if you lost your keys?
> 如果你钥匙丢了怎么办？
> B: I would call you.
> 我会给你打电话。
> A: What would you do if I wasn’t there?
> 如果我不在怎么办？
> B: I would wait outside.
> 我就在外面等着。

### 094. Where there is… there is…

**Examples**

- Where there is a line, there is good food.
  - 哪里有排队，哪里就有好吃的。
- Where there is music, there is fun.
  - 哪里有音乐，哪里就有欢乐。
- Where there is Wi-Fi, there is me.
  - 哪里有 Wi-Fi，哪里就有我。

> 有道理。
> B: Where there is good food, there are people.
> 哪里有好吃的，哪里就有人。

### 095. Whether or not…

**Examples**

- Whether or not it rains, I’m going.

- Whether or not you like it, eat some.

## 12 解释与澄清  EXPLAINING & CLARIFYING

### 006. But this doesn’t mean that…

**Examples**

- I’m tired, but this doesn’t mean that I’m sad.
  - 我很累，但这不代表我不开心。
- The food is cheap, but this doesn’t mean that it’s bad.
  - 食物便宜，但不代表不好吃。
- He is quiet, but this doesn’t mean that he is shy.
  - 他话少，但不代表他害羞。

**Dialogue**

> A: You look quiet today.
> 你今天话不多。
> B: I’m tired, but this doesn’t mean that I’m unhappy.
> 我很累，但这不代表我不开心。
> A: Oh, good.
> 哦，那就好。
> B: I just need some sleep.
> 我只是需要睡一觉。

### 054. It’s not that… but…

**Examples**

- It’s not that I don’t like tea, but I want coffee.
  - 不是我不喜欢茶，而是我想喝咖啡。
- It’s not that I’m tired, but I want to rest.
  - 不是我不累，而是我想休息。
- It’s not that the food is bad, but I’m full.
  - 不是饭菜不好，而是我吃饱了。

**Dialogue**

> A: Do you want some tea?
> 你想喝点茶吗？
> B: It’s not that I don’t like tea, but I want water.
> 不是我不喜欢茶，而是我想喝水。
> A: OK. It’s not that I’m busy, but I’m tired too.
> 好吧。不是我不忙，而是我也累了。
> B: Let’s rest.
> 那休息一下。
