Model Story

Back then, I used to be really shy, and I never spoke in meetings.

那时候我特别害羞，开会从不发言。

Then my manager asked me to present on a Friday call.

后来经理让我在周五的电话会上做汇报。

At first, my hands shook.

一开始我手都在抖。

Little by little, it got easier, and after that I started volunteering for small talks.

慢慢地就变简单了，之后我开始主动认领小的发言。

These days, I speak my mind at work, and nobody thinks it's strange.

现在我在工作上有话直说，也没人觉得奇怪。

Looking back, that Friday call changed me.

回头看，是那次周五的电话会改变了我。
