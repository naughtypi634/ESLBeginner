# it-句型

It 常用句型

It is/was + adj. (+ for + someone【宾格】) + to do It is useful (for travelers) to know a few basic words.
评价 Evaluation
有用的
useful
It is useful to learn some English for travel.
容易的
easy
It is easy to pay with your phone in China.
安全的
safe
It is safe to walk here at night.
合适的
appropriate
It is appropriate to dress well for an interview.
明智的
wise
It is wise to save money for the future.
合理的
reasonable
It is reasonable to spend 30% of your pay on rent.
自然的
natural
It is natural for young people to use mobile payment.
正常的
normal
It is normal for office workers to feel tired on Monday.
普遍的
common
It is common for students to take the postgraduate exam.
方便的
convenient
It is convenient for me to take the subway to work.
必要性 / 重要性 Necessity
必要的
necessary
It is necessary to have a driver's license for this job.
重要的
important
It is important to protect your personal information.
至关重要的
vital
It is vital for a new business to have good cash flow.
负面 Negative
没用的
useless
It is useless to learn words but never speak.
危险的
dangerous
It is dangerous to use your phone while walking.
困难的
difficult
It is difficult to buy a house in a big city.
不可能的
impossible
It is impossible to lose 10kg in one month.
荒谬的
absurd
It is absurd to learn everything from short videos.
不寻常的
unusual
It is unusual for her to be late for class.

It is/was + adj. + of + someone【宾格】 + to do It was kind of you to help me when I was sick.
正面品质 Positive Qualities
善良的
kind
It was kind of you to help the old woman.
好的
nice
It was nice of you to buy me coffee.
贴心的
thoughtful
It was thoughtful of you to remember my birthday.
体谅的
considerate
It was considerate of you to wait for everyone.
慷慨的
generous
It was generous of you to donate money.
负面品质 Negative Qualities
粗暴的
rude
It was rude of you to shout at the waiter.
愚蠢的
stupid
It was stupid of me to lose my ID card.
调皮的
naughty
It was naughty of the kid to draw on the wall.
粗心的
careless
It was careless of me to leave my phone on the bus.
自私的
selfish
It is selfish of you to keep the AC all to yourself.
残忍的
cruel
It was cruel of him to laugh at her accent.

It 常用句型

It takes/took someone (宾格) + some time + to do It takes me 20 minutes to walk to the subway station.
时间相关 Time
花时间
takes time
It takes time to learn a new skill well.
花多久
takes (time)
It took me three months to pass the driving test.
花⼀上午
takes all morning
It took us all morning to clean the house.
花多年
takes years
It took her years to pay off the mortgage.
花⼀会⼉
takes a while
It takes a while to get used to the new job.
花整整⼀天
takes a whole day
It took them a whole day to finish the report.

It costs/cost someone (宾格) + 成本 + to do It cost me 200 yuan to get my bike repaired.
⾦钱成本 Money
花多少钱
costs money
It costs a lot of money to raise a child in a big city.
花具体⾦额
costs (amount)
It cost us 5,000 yuan to fix the air conditioner.
花⼤价钱
costs a fortune
It costs a fortune to eat at that restaurant.
花很少钱
costs little
It costs very little to take the bus in this city.
⾮⾦钱成本 Non-Monetary Cost
付出代价
costs (effort)
It cost him his health to work 80 hours a week.
花精⼒
costs energy
It costs a lot of energy to take care of a baby.

It is + adj./n. + doing It is useless complaining. / It is no use complaining.
值得 / 有用 Worthwhile & Useful
有用的
useful
It is useful learning how to use Excel at work.
值得的
worth
It is worth spending 200 yuan on a good book.
有意义的
worthwhile
It is worthwhile going to a TEDx talk.
有意义的
meaningful
It is meaningful teaching kids in poor areas.
愉快的
nice
It is nice having a picnic in the park on weekends.
有趣的
fun
It is fun playing board games with friends.
不值得 / 没用 Not Worth & Useless
没用的
useless
It is useless worrying all day but doing nothing.
毫无意义的
meaningless
It is meaningless buying luxury bags just to show off.
白费力气的
pointless
It is pointless arguing with people online.
无聊的
boring
It is boring listening to a long speech.
累人的
tiring
It is tiring commuting three hours every day.
令人挫败的
frustrating
It is frustrating waiting for a reply that never comes.

It 常用句型

It + be + 动词过去分词 + that 从句 It is believed that smoking is bad for our health.
客观表述 Objective Statements
公认
accepted
It is accepted that too much short video is bad for focus.
据报道
reported
It is reported that more students will graduate this year.
据说
said
It is said that the new cafe has really good coffee.
被认为
thought
It is thought that black coffee is good for your health.
人们相信
believed
It is believed that AI will change many office jobs.
已宣布
announced
It is announced that a new high-speed train will open soon.
一致同意
agreed
It is agreed that less overtime is a good thing.
已决定
decided
It is decided that everyone will get a pay raise this year.
主观推测 Subjective Speculation
希望
hoped
It is hoped that house prices will not go up anymore.
预计
expected
It is expected that more than half of new cars will be EVs.
预测
predicted
It is predicted that China will go carbon-neutral by 2030.
估计
estimated
It is estimated that over 200 million people are single.
声称
claimed
It is claimed that this health product can cure everything.
表明
suggested
It is suggested that exercise three times a week helps you live longer.
建议
recommended
It is recommended that adults get a health check every year.
要求
required
It is required that you have a visa to travel abroad.

It + 特定动词 + that 从句 It seems that no one knows what happened in the park.
推测 / 结果 Speculation & Result
似乎
seems
It seems that this year's exam will be harder.
看起来
appears
It appears that the hotpot restaurant has fake queues.
碰巧
happens
It happens that my friend works at that big company.
结果
turns out
It turns out that the popular restaurant's reviews were fake.
由此得出
follows
It follows that more work doesn't always mean better results.
后来得知
transpired
It transpired that the layoff list was decided long ago.
最新消息
emerged
It emerged that house prices in small towns are also falling.
突然想到
occurs
It occurs to me that I can use AI for my weekly report.
心理活动 / 想法 Mental Activities
突然意识到
strikes me
It strikes me that the 35-year-old worry is just fear created by others.
想到
occurs to me
It occurs to me that I should start a side business with AI.
想起来
comes to me
It comes to me that today is the deadline for the report.
重要的是
matters
It matters that we stay healthy more than making a lot of money.
说得通
makes sense
It makes sense to travel the world instead of buying a house.
偶尔想
crosses my mind
It crosses my mind that I could have chosen a different path.
