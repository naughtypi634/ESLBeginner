# it-句型

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习 it 句型，了解 It is + 形容词 + to do 的用法，学会用 it 来表达评价和感受，并应用到工作与生活的对话中。

It 常用句型

It is/was + adj. (+ for + someone【宾格】) + to do **It is useful** to learn some English for travel.

正面评价 Positive Evaluation
有用的
useful
**It is useful** to learn AI skills for your career.
容易的
easy
**It is easy** to pay with your phone in China.
安全的
safe
**It is safe** to walk here at night.
合适的
appropriate
**It is appropriate** to dress well for an interview.
明智的
wise
**It is wise** to save money for the future.
方便的
convenient
**It is convenient** for me to take the subway to work.
轻松的
relaxing
**It is relaxing** to take a walk by the river after dinner.
令人愉快的
pleasant
**It is pleasant** to have tea with old friends on weekends.
令人兴奋的
exciting
**It is exciting** to try a new hobby after retirement.

中性评价 Neutral Evaluation
合理的
reasonable
**It is reasonable** to spend 30% of your pay on rent.
自然的
natural
**It is natural** for young people to use mobile payment.

normal
**It is normal** for office workers to feel tired on Monday.

common
**It is common** for adults to keep learning after work.
典型的
typical
**It is typical** for a new store to offer big discounts at first.
少见的
rare
**It is rare** to see people pay with paper money in big cities.

负面评价 Negative Evaluation
没用的
useless
**It is useless** to learn words but never speak.
危险的
dangerous
**It is dangerous** to use your phone while walking.
困难的
difficult

荒谬的
absurd
**It is absurd** to learn everything from short videos.
不寻常的

**It is unusual** for him to miss a deadline.
尴尬的

**It is awkward** to eat alone in a fancy restaurant.
浪费的
wasteful
**It is wasteful** to order more food than you can eat.
不公平的
unfair
**It is unfair** to expect everyone to work overtime.
不健康的
unhealthy
**It is unhealthy** to stay up late scrolling your phone.

必要性 / 重要性 Necessity & Importance
必要的
necessary
**It is necessary** to have a driver's license for this job.
重要的
important

至关重要的

关键的

强制的
mandatory

可取的
