Modal Verbs · 情态动词

1. 最常见用法 Common Uses

can 能 / 会（能力、许可）

EnglishChinese
I can scan the code to pay.我能扫码付款。
Can I sit here?我能坐这儿吗？
I can't hear you — the subway is too loud.我听不清——地铁太吵了。
I can ride a shared bike, but I can't drive.我会骑共享单车，不会开车。

could 可以（更礼貌；过去的能力）

EnglishChinese
Could you say that again?你能再说一遍吗？
Could I use your charger?我能借你的充电器吗？
When I was in school, I could run 5 km.上学时我能跑五公里。

should 应该（建议、责任）

EnglishChinese
You should bring an umbrella — it's going to rain.你应该带把伞，要下雨了。
I should reply to this message first.我应该先回这条消息。
You shouldn't work overtime every day.你不该天天加班。
We should split the bill evenly.我们应该 AA 平分账单。

must / have to 必须（强义务）

EnglishChinese
You have to scan the code before you get on.上车前必须扫码。
I have to take the last subway home.我得赶末班地铁回家。
I must pay the bill before 10 p.m.我必须在晚上十点前付款。
You don't have to tip in China.在中国你不用给小费。
You mustn't smoke here.这里禁止吸烟。

will / would 将会 / 愿意

EnglishChinese
I will call you when I arrive.到了我给你打电话。
Would you like some tea?要来点茶吗？
Would you mind waiting a minute?你介意等一分钟吗？

2. 可能性推测 Possibility & Deduction

may / might / could 可能（不确定）

EnglishChinese
It may rain this afternoon.下午可能下雨。
He might be stuck in traffic.他可能堵在路上了。
The delivery could arrive late.外卖可能会晚到。
She might not see the message.她可能没看到消息。
Ordering takeout on 美团 may be cheaper.美团点外卖可能更便宜。

must 一定（把握很大的肯定推测）

EnglishChinese
You've worked all day — you must be tired.你忙了一整天，一定很累。
The line is so long — the food must be good.排这么长的队，一定好吃。
Your phone is off — you must be out of battery.你关机了，一定没电了。

can't 不可能（把握很大的否定推测）

EnglishChinese
That can't be true — I just saw him.那不可能是真的，我刚见过他。
It can't be 9 p.m. already.不可能已经晚上九点了。
She can't be at home — the light is off.她不可能在家，灯都灭了。
推测词确定程度例句
might可能 50%He might be stuck in traffic. 他可能堵在路上了。
could也许 60%The delivery could arrive late. 外卖可能会晚到。
may可能 70%It may rain this afternoon. 下午可能下雨。
must一定 95%You must be tired. 你一定很累。
can't不可能That can't be true. 那不可能是真的。
