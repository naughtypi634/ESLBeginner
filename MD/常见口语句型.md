# 常见口语句型

## 01 意愿与打算  WILLINGNESS & PLANS

- I'm willing to help you.  我愿意帮你。
- I want to go abroad.  我想出国。
- I'm ready to go.  我准备好出发了。
- I'm about to leave.  我正要出门。
- I can't wait to see you.  我等不及要见你。
- I was going to say that.  我刚才正要说这个。
- I wanted to call you.  我刚才想给你打电话。
- I wish I could take a day off.  真希望我能休一天假。
- It's time to go.  该走了。
- I plan to learn English this year.  今年我打算学英语。
- I'm thinking about moving to a new apartment.  我在考虑搬新房子。
- I hope to get a promotion this year.  我希望今年能升职。
- I'm going to order takeout tonight.  今晚我打算点外卖。
- I decided to join a gym.  我决定去办健身卡。
- I'm saving up for a new phone.  我在攒钱买新手机。
- I'm looking forward to the weekend.  我盼着周末快点到。
- I want to cook dinner for my family.  我想给家人做顿晚饭。
- I'm going to take the subway home.  我打算坐地铁回家。
- I'd like to book a table for two.  我想订一个两人位。
- I plan to visit my parents this weekend.  这周末我打算去看父母。

## 02 感觉与情绪  FEELINGS & EMOTIONS

- I feel like watching a movie.  我想看场电影。
- I'm really into movies.  我很喜欢看电影。
- I can't help crying.  我忍不住想哭。
- It is worth trying.  值得一试。
- It's no use crying.  哭也没用。
- I feel bad about the incident.  对这件事我很过意不去。
- I'm getting better at cooking.  我做饭越来越好了。
- It seems that they are not so happy.  他们看起来不太开心。
- I would give anything for it.  我愿意为它付出一切。
- I feel so tired after work.  下班后我觉得很累。
- I'm so happy to see you.  见到你我太开心了。
- I feel nervous before the interview.  面试前我很紧张。
- I'm worried about the exam.  我很担心考试。
- I feel proud of my kids.  我为我的孩子感到骄傲。
- I get angry when I'm stuck in traffic.  堵车的时候我会很烦躁。
- I'm excited about the trip.  我对这次旅行很兴奋。
- I feel relaxed after a hot shower.  冲个热水澡后我觉得很放松。
- I miss my hometown.  我想念我的家乡。
- I'm grateful for your help.  我很感谢你的帮助。
- I feel lonely in this new city.  在这个新城市我觉得很孤单。

## 03 请求与提议  REQUESTS & OFFERS

- Don't forget to call me.  别忘了给我打电话。
- Remember to call me.  记得给我打电话。
- I'm here to help you.  我是来帮你的。
- I need you to do this.  我需要你做这件事。
- I told you to bring the key.  我告诉过你带钥匙。
- Do you want me to leave?  要我离开吗？
- Do you want something to drink?  你想喝点什么吗？
- Would you mind if I sit here?  我坐这里你介意吗？
- Help yourself to some water.  你自己倒水喝。
- I was wondering if you could help me carry these bags.  我在想你能不能帮我拎一下这些袋子。
- Can you pass me the salt, please?  请把盐递给我好吗？
- Could you turn down the music?  你能把音乐调小点吗？
- Please give me a hand with this box.  请帮我搬一下这个箱子。
- Would you like a cup of coffee?  你想喝杯咖啡吗？
- Can I borrow your charger?  我能借你的充电器吗？
- Let me help you with that.  让我帮你吧。
- Could you send me the address?  能把地址发给我吗？
- Please wait for me at the gate.  请在门口等我。
- Can you call me when you arrive?  到了能给我打个电话吗？
- Would you like to join us for dinner?  你愿意和我们一起吃晚饭吗？

## 04 建议与选择  SUGGESTIONS & CHOICES

- It's better to take the subway.  坐地铁更好。
- You might as well take the subway.  你还不如坐地铁。
- You might want to sit down.  你也许想坐下来。
- All you have to do is listen.  你只要听着就行。
- What do you say we eat out today?  我们今天出去吃怎么样？
- Is it okay if I open the window?  我开窗可以吗？
- What if the meeting runs late?  万一会议开晚了怎么办？
- You don't have to say that.  你不必那么说。
- It's just that I don't really like that color.  只是我不太喜欢那个颜色。
- The thing is, I need some time.  问题是，我需要点时间。
- How about we grab a coffee?  我们去喝杯咖啡怎么样？
- Why don't we order takeout?  我们点外卖怎么样？
- You should get some rest.  你应该休息一下。
- Maybe we can meet on Friday.  也许我们周五见面。
- Let's go to the park instead.  我们改去公园吧。
- It's a good idea to save money.  存钱是个好主意。
- Try the green one, it's cheaper.  试试绿色的，更便宜。
- You'd better bring an umbrella.  你最好带把伞。
- Shall we leave now?  我们现在走好吗？
- Let's decide after dinner.  我们晚饭后再决定。

## 05 责任与必须  OBLIGATION

- I have to do it. I don't have a choice.  我必须做，我别无选择。
- I must do it.  我必须做。
- I have to get up early tomorrow.  明天我得早起。
- You have to be there on time.  你必须准时到。
- I have to finish this report tonight.  今晚我必须写完这份报告。
- I have to take the kids to school.  我得送孩子上学。
- You have to pay before you leave.  离开前你必须付款。
- I must call my mom today.  今天我必须给妈妈打电话。
- I have to clean the kitchen.  我得打扫厨房。
- We have to be quiet in the library.  在图书馆我们必须安静。
- You have to wear a helmet on the e-bike.  骑电动车必须戴头盔。
- I have to pick up my package today.  今天我得去取快递。
- You have to bring your ID card.  你必须带上身份证。
- I have to go to the bank.  我得去趟银行。
- We have to wait for the green light.  我们必须等绿灯。
- You have to wash your hands before dinner.  吃饭前你必须洗手。
- I have to charge my phone now.  我现在必须给手机充电。
- She has to work overtime tonight.  她今晚得加班。
- We have to buy tickets in advance.  我们必须提前买票。
- You have to keep your promise.  你必须守信用。

## 06 习惯与适应  HABITS & ADJUSTMENT

- I used to like swimming.  我以前喜欢游泳。
- I am used to my work.  我习惯了我的工作。
- I am getting used to the new environment.  我正慢慢适应新环境。
- I used to take the bus to work.  我以前坐公交上班。
- I used to play basketball in college.  我大学时经常打篮球。
- I am used to getting up early.  我习惯早起了。
- I used to live in Beijing.  我以前住在北京。
- I'm getting used to the new city.  我正慢慢适应这座新城市。
- I used to drink coffee every day.  我以前每天喝咖啡。
- She is used to working at night.  她习惯上夜班。
- I used to play games a lot.  我以前经常打游戏。
- I am used to taking the subway.  我习惯坐地铁。
- I'm getting used to working from home.  我正慢慢适应在家办公。
- He used to smoke, but not now.  他以前抽烟，现在不了。
- I am used to eating spicy food.  我习惯吃辣。
- I used to keep a diary.  我以前写日记。
- We are used to ordering food online.  我们习惯在网上点餐。
- I'm getting used to the new boss.  我正慢慢适应新老板。
- I used to walk to school.  我以前走路去上学。
- I am used to checking my phone first.  我习惯先看手机。

## 07 责备与抱怨  BLAME & COMPLAINTS

- How could you do this to me?  你怎么能这样对我？
- Why did you do that?  你为什么要那样做？
- Why are you so mean?  你为什么这么刻薄？
- You can't be serious.  你不是认真的吧。
- What's with him today?  他今天怎么了？
- What on earth are you doing?  你到底在干什么？
- Why didn't you tell me earlier?  你为什么不早点告诉我？
- You should have asked me first.  你应该先问我的。
- Don't blame me for everything.  别什么事都怪我。
- I'm tired of waiting for you.  我等得不耐烦了。
- The food here is too salty.  这里的菜太咸了。
- The train is late again.  火车又晚点了。
- My phone battery dies so fast.  我手机电池掉得太快了。
- The office is too hot today.  今天办公室太热了。
- This app keeps crashing.  这个 App 老是闪退。
- The traffic is terrible this morning.  今天早上交通太堵了。
- Why is the internet so slow?  网怎么这么慢？
- You're always late for work.  你上班老迟到。
- I've told you a hundred times.  我跟你说过一百遍了。
- That's not what I asked for.  我要的不是这个。

## 08 遗憾与假设  REGRETS & HYPOTHETICALS

- I should have told you.  我本该告诉你的。
- I shouldn't have done that.  我本不该那么做。
- I would have taken that offer.  我当时就会接受那个机会。
- I wouldn't have booked that hotel.  我就不会订那家酒店。
- I could have done more.  我本可以做得更多。
- I couldn't have saved more money.  我本来也存不下更多钱。
- I might have met you before.  我以前可能见过你。
- In case it rains, bring an umbrella.  以防下雨，带把伞。
- Let's say we leave at 8.  比如说我们 8 点出发。
- I should have gotten up earlier.  我本该早点起床的。
- I shouldn't have eaten so much.  我不该吃那么多的。
- If I had more time, I'd travel more.  如果我有更多时间，我会多去旅行。
- If I were you, I'd say yes.  如果我是你，我会答应。
- I would be happier if I lived closer.  如果我住得近一点，我会更开心。
- What if we miss the last train?  万一我们错过末班车怎么办？
- What if the shop is closed?  万一店关门了怎么办？
- I wish I had studied harder.  真希望我以前更努力学。
- I wish it weren't so expensive.  真希望它没那么贵。
- Even if it rains, we'll still go.  即使下雨，我们也会去。
- If I had known, I would have come.  如果我知道，我就来了。

## 09 想法与观点  OPINIONS

- I think we should order takeout tonight.  我觉得今晚我们点外卖吧。
- I can tell you're tired.  我看得出来你累了。
- It is like talking to an old friend.  就像和老朋友聊天一样。
- That's what I mean.  我就是这个意思。
- What I'm saying is I don't care.  我的意思是我无所谓。
- No offense, but you are not what we're looking for.  无意冒犯，但你不是我们要找的人。
- It depends on the weather.  那要看天气。
- I didn't mean to hurt you.  我不是故意伤害你的。
- That makes sense.  有道理。
- You are not that different from me.  你和我没那么不一样。
- There is nothing like my daughter.  没什么比得上我女儿。
- In my opinion, this plan is good.  在我看来，这个计划不错。
- I believe we can do it.  我相信我们能做到。
- I'm not sure about this one.  这一个我不太确定。
- To be honest, I don't like it.  说实话，我不太喜欢。
- I don't think that's a good idea.  我觉得那不是个好主意。
- From my point of view, it's fine.  在我看来，这样挺好。
- I agree with you.  我同意你的看法。
- I'm afraid I can't agree.  恐怕我不能同意。
- The way I see it, we should wait.  依我看，我们应该等等。

## 10 不确定与发现  UNCERTAINTY & DISCOVERIES

- I don't know where he is.  我不知道他在哪儿。
- I don't know why he is late.  我不知道他为什么迟到。
- I don't know if they will come.  我不知道他们会不会来。
- You never know what will happen.  你永远不知道会发生什么。
- You have no idea how happy I am.  你不知道我有多开心。
- It turns out that we live in the same building.  原来我们住同一栋楼。
- I thought it was you.  我还以为是你。
- I have nothing to hide.  我没什么好隐瞒的。
- I'm not sure if I locked the door.  我不确定自己锁门了没有。
- I wonder where my keys are.  我在想我的钥匙放哪儿了。
- I can't find my phone anywhere.  我到处找不到手机。
- Maybe he missed the bus.  也许他没赶上公交。
- Perhaps we should ask someone.  也许我们应该问问别人。
- It looks like it's going to rain.  看起来要下雨了。
- It sounds like a good plan.  听起来是个好计划。
- I figured out the problem.  我弄明白问题出在哪儿了。
- It turned out to be a mistake.  结果那是个误会。
- It happened that we met again.  碰巧我们又见面了。
- I'm not sure about the price.  价格我不太确定。
- I'll find out tomorrow.  我明天查一下。

## 11 提问与回应  QUESTIONS & RESPONSES

- What is this for?  这是干什么用的？
- What is it like to travel abroad?  出国旅游是什么感觉？
- Have you been to Japan?  你去过日本吗？
- How did you like your trip?  你这次旅行感觉怎么样？
- Do you have any seats available?  还有空位吗？
- Why are you so happy today?  你今天怎么这么开心？
- What do you want for dinner?  晚饭想吃什么？
- I forgot to bring my notebook.  我忘带笔记本了。
- There is a convenience store downstairs.  楼下有家便利店。
- There are two elevators in our building.  我们楼有两部电梯。
- I've been thinking about studying abroad.  我一直在考虑出国留学。
- I am in the middle of fixing the toilet.  我正在修马桶。
- What time does the store close?  商店几点关门？
- How long does it take to get there?  到那里要多久？
- Which bus goes to the airport?  哪路公交到机场？
- Where can I charge my phone?  我在哪儿能给手机充电？
- How much is this bag?  这个包多少钱？
- Can you speak a little slower?  你能说慢一点吗？
- What do you do for a living?  你是做什么工作的？
- What's the weather like today?  今天天气怎么样？

## 12 过渡与连接  FILLERS & CONNECTORS

- You know what?  你知道吗？
- I think so.  我也这么觉得。
- Just in case.  以防万一。
- Let me think about it.  让我想想。
- It's up to you.  你决定吧。
- Let's keep in touch.  我们保持联系。
- I'll let you know if I have time.  有时间我会告诉你。
- By the way, have you eaten?  顺便问一下，你吃饭了吗？
- Anyway, let's get started.  总之，我们开始吧。
- To be honest, I'm a bit busy.  说实话，我有点忙。
- As I said before, it's fine.  就像我之前说的，没关系。
- In other words, we need more time.  换句话说，我们需要更多时间。
- For example, take the subway.  比如说，坐地铁。
- First of all, let me introduce myself.  首先，让我自我介绍一下。
- In short, it was a good day.  总之，这是美好的一天。
- That reminds me, I have a meeting.  这提醒了我，我还有个会。
- Speaking of food, let's eat out.  说到吃的，我们出去吃吧。
- Believe it or not, I was late.  信不信由你，我迟到了。
- As a matter of fact, I know him.  事实上，我认识他。
- After all, family comes first.  毕竟，家人最重要。
