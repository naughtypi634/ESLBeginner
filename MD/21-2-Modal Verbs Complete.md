# Modal Verbs Complete · 情态动词完整版

## 1. 情态动词的共同规则 Common Rules

| 规则 | 说明 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 没有人称和数的变化 | 主语是 he / she / it 也不加 -s | He **can** swim. 他会游泳。 |
| 后面接动词原形 | 情态动词 + do，不加 to | You **must** go now. 你现在必须走。 |
| 否定：直接加 not | 常见缩略见第 8 节 | You **shouldn't** work late. 你不该工作到很晚。 |
| 疑问：情态动词提前 | 不用 do / does / did 帮忙 | **Can** you help me? 你能帮我吗？ |
| 没有非谓语形式 | 不能写 to must / musting | 需要时用 have to：I'll **have to** leave. 我得走了。 |

## 2. 核心情态动词总览 All Nine Core Modals

| 情态动词 | 主要含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | 能力、许可、可能 | I **can** ride a shared bike. 我会骑共享单车。 |
| **could** | 过去能力、委婉、可能 | **Could** you say that again? 你能再说一遍吗？ |
| **may** | 许可、可能、祝愿 | **May** I come in? 我可以进来吗？ |
| **might** | 更不确定的可能、委婉 | He **might** be stuck in traffic. 他可能堵在路上了。 |
| **must** | 必须、肯定推测 | You **must** scan the code. 你必须扫码。 |
| **shall** | 提议、征询、正式规定 | **Shall** I open the window? 要我开窗吗？ |
| **should** | 应该、建议、预期 | You **should** bring an umbrella. 你应该带把伞。 |
| **will** | 将来、意愿、习惯 | I **will** call you when I arrive. 到了我给你打电话。 |
| **would** | 委婉请求、过去习惯、虚拟 | **Would** you like some tea? 要来点茶吗？ |

## 3. 核心情态动词的更多用法 More Uses of Core Modals

| 情态动词 | 更多用法 | 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **can** | 一般性 / 理论上的可能 | It **can** be very cold in Beijing. | 北京可能很冷。 |
| **could** | 现在 / 将来的不确定推测 | It **could** rain later. | 晚些时候可能会下雨。 |
| **may** | 正式许可 | **May** I come in? | 我可以进来吗？ |
| **may** | 祝愿 | **May** you succeed! | 祝你成功！ |
| **might** | 更委婉的请求 | **Might** I ask a question? | 我能问个问题吗？ |
| **must** | 过去「必须」用 had to | I **had to** pay before 10 p.m. | 我不得不十点前付款。 |
| **shall** | 提议 / 征询 | **Shall** we take the subway? | 我们坐地铁吧？ |
| **should** | 预期 / 推测 | The train **should** arrive soon. | 火车应该快到了。 |
| **will** | 习惯 / 客观规律 | Water **will** boil at 100°C. | 水在 100°C 会沸腾。 |
| **would** | 过去习惯动作 | My grandpa **would** walk me to school. | 爷爷以前常送我上学。 |
| **would** | 虚拟语气 | If I were you, I **would** tell the truth. | 如果我是你，我会说实话。 |

### shall 提议、征询与正式规定

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| **Shall** I open the window? | 要我开窗吗？ |
| **Shall** we split the bill? | 我们 AA 平分账单吧？ |
| You **shall** have the money by Friday. | 你周五之前会拿到钱。（承诺，正式） |
| The tenant **shall** pay rent monthly. | 租户应按月支付租金。（条文） |

### ought to 应该（稍正式）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **ought to** apologize first. | 你应该先道歉。 |
| The bus **ought to** come soon. | 公交应该很快就来。 |
| You **ought to** have apologized. | 你本应该道歉的。 |

## 4. 半情态动词 Semi-Modals

### have to 客观必须（规则、现实）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **had to** take the last subway home. | 我昨晚不得不赶末班地铁回家。 |
| You **will have to** pay before 10 p.m. | 你十点前必须付款。 |
| I **have to** leave — my meeting starts at 2 p.m. | 我得走了，两点有会。 |
| You **don't have to** tip in China. | 在中国你不必给小费。（不必，允许不给） |
| You **mustn't** smoke here. | 这里禁止吸烟。（禁止，绝不能） |

### be able to 能够（补充 can 的时态）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| She **is able to** work from home. | 她能在家办公。 |
| I **was able to** fix my phone. | 我成功修好了手机。（一次成功） |
| I **will be able to** speak more English next year. | 明年我就能多说英语了。 |
| I **have been able to** save money since March. | 从三月起我一直在省钱。 |

### be going to 打算；有迹象的预测

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I'm **going to** visit my grandma. | 我打算去看奶奶。 |
| Look at the clouds! It's **going to** rain. | 看那些云！要下雨了。 |
| He is **going to** miss the subway. | 他要赶不上地铁了。 |

### used to 过去常常（现在不再这样）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **used to** take the subway to work. | 我以前坐地铁上班。 |
| She **didn't use to** drink coffee. | 她以前不喝咖啡。 |
| **Did** you **use to** play the piano? | 你以前弹钢琴吗？ |
| I **used to** live near the subway station. | 我以前住在地铁站附近。（状态） |

### had better 最好（强烈建议，常隐含警告）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You'd **better** bring an umbrella. | 你最好带把伞。 |
| You'd **better not** be late. | 你最好不要迟到。 |
| We'd **better** scan the code now. | 我们最好现在扫码。 |

### need 需要（美式常用 need to）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You **don't need to** bring your ID. | 你不用带身份证。 |
| **Do** I **need to** book a table? | 我需要订位吗？ |
| You **didn't need to** buy so much. | 你本不必买这么多。（但买了） |

### dare 敢于（多用于否定和疑问）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I **don't dare** ask my boss for a day off. | 我不敢跟老板请一天假。 |
| **How dare** you say that! | 你竟敢这么说！ |
| She **didn't dare to** tell him. | 她不敢告诉他。 |

### would rather 宁愿

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I'd **rather** stay home than go out. | 我宁愿待在家也不愿出去。 |
| I'd **rather not** talk about it. | 我宁愿不谈这个。 |
| I'd **rather** you told me the truth. | 我更希望你对我说实话。 |

### be supposed to 应该（按规定、预期；据说）

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You're **supposed to** wear a seatbelt. | 你应该系安全带。 |
| You're not **supposed to** smoke here. | 这里不允许抽烟。 |
| He is **supposed to** be very rich. | 据说他很有钱。 |

## 5. 情态动词 + have done（对过去的推测、遗憾与批评）

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **must have** done | 一定做过（肯定推测） | She **must have left**. | 她一定已经走了。 |
| **can't have** done | 不可能做过 | He **can't have seen** us. | 他不可能看见我们。 |
| **may have** done | 可能做过 | They **may have forgotten**. | 他们可能忘了。 |
| **could have** done | 本可以做（但没做） | I **could have helped** you. | 我本可以帮你的。 |
| **should have** done | 本应该做（但没做） | You **should have called** me. | 你本应该给我打电话。 |
| **shouldn't have** done | 本不该做（却做了） | You **shouldn't have said** that. | 你本不该说那种话。 |
| **would have** done | 与过去事实相反 | I **would have come** if I'd known. | 如果我知道，我就会来。 |

## 6. 推测程度总结 Deduction Summary

| 确定程度 | 现在 / 将来 | 对过去 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 一定（95%） | **must** | **must have** done | You **must** be tired. 你一定很累。 |
| 应该（80%） | **should / ought to** | **should have** done | The train **should** arrive. 火车快到了。 |
| 可能（70%） | **may** | **may have** done | He **may** be home. 他可能在家。 |
| 也许（50-60%） | **might / could** | **might / could have** done | It **might** rain. 可能下雨。 |
| 不可能 | **can't / couldn't** | **can't / couldn't have** done | That **can't** be right. 那不可能。 |

## 7. 易混辨析 Common Confusions

| 对比 | 区别 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| must vs have to | must 主观；have to 客观 | I **must** go — the client is waiting. 我必须走了。 |
| ^ | mustn't 禁止 | You **mustn't** smoke here. 这里禁止吸烟。 |
| ^ | don't have to 不必 | You **don't have to** tip in China. 在中国不用给小费。 |
| can vs may | 表许可：can 口语 | **Can** I sit here? 我能坐这儿吗？ |
| ^ | 表许可：may 正式 | **May** I come in? 我可以进来吗？ |
| ^ | 表能力只能用 can | I **can** drive. 我会开车。 |
| could vs was able to | could 表一般能力 | I **could** run 5 km when I was 20. 我二十岁时能跑五公里。 |
| ^ | was able to 表具体一次做到 | I **was able to** finish the report. 我成功写完了报告。 |
| used to vs would | used to 表过去习惯 | I **used to** take the subway to work. 我以前坐地铁上班。 |
| ^ | used to 也表过去的状态 | I **used to** live near the subway station. 我以前住地铁站附近。 |
| ^ | would 强调反复的动作 | My grandpa **would** walk me to school. 爷爷以前常送我上学。 |
| should vs had better | should 表建议 | You **should** see a doctor. 你应该去看医生。 |
| ^ | had better 更强烈，含警告 | You'd **better** hurry. 你最好快点。 |
| will vs be going to | will 表临时决定 | I **will** help you. 我来帮你。 |
| ^ | be going to 表事先计划 | I'm **going to** visit my grandma. 我打算去看奶奶。 |
| ^ | be going to 表有迹象的预测 | Look at the clouds! It's **going to** rain. 要下雨了。 |
| may not vs mustn't | may not 表可能不 | He **may not** be home. 他可能不在家。 |
| ^ | may not 也表不允许 | You **may not** park here. 这里不允许停车。 |
| ^ | mustn't 表禁止 | You **mustn't** smoke here. 这里禁止吸烟。 |

## 8. 否定形式与疑问回答 Negative Forms & Short Answers

| 情态动词 | 否定 | 常见缩略 |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | cannot | **can't** |
| **could** | could not | **couldn't** |
| **may** | may not | （少缩写） |
| **might** | might not | （少缩写） |
| **must** | must not | **mustn't** |
| **shall** | shall not | （少缩写） |
| **should** | should not | **shouldn't** |
| **will** | will not | **won't** |
| **would** | would not | **wouldn't** |
| **ought to** | ought not to | （少缩写） |
| **have to** | do not have to | **don't have to** |
| **had better** | had better not | （少缩写） |

| 问句 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|---|---|---|
| Can I sit here? | Yes, you **can**. | No, you **can't**. |
| May I come in? | Yes, you **may**. | No, you **may not**. |
| Should I call him back? | Yes, you **should**. | No, you **shouldn't**. |
| Do I have to pay now? | Yes, you **do**. | No, you **don't have to**. |
