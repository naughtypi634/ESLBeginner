# Daily Routine — Adverbs & Phrases · 日常作息

## 1. Morning / Workday / Evening / Weekend 作息表达

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| I usually **wake up** at 7 and **scan the QR code** on the bus. | 我通常 7 点起床，在公交上扫码。 |
| I **check my phone** before I get out of bed. | 我起床前先看手机。 |
| I **eat a quick breakfast** — 豆浆 and a bun. | 我快速吃个早饭——豆浆加包子。 |
| At work I **send emails** and **use DingTalk** all day. | 上班我整天发邮件、用钉钉。 |
| I **order takeout** for lunch when the canteen line is long. | 食堂排队太长时我点外卖。 |
| After dinner I **watch Douyin** for a while. | 晚饭后我会刷一会儿抖音。 |
| I **send voice messages** to my family instead of calling. | 我给家人发语音消息，而不是打电话。 |
| On weekends I **sleep late** and **eat out** with friends. | 周末我睡懒觉，和朋友出去吃。 |
| I **walk the dog** in the park every evening. | 我每天晚上都带狗去公园散步。 |
| I **shop online** twice a month, mostly on Pinduoduo. | 我每月网购两次，主要用拼多多。 |
