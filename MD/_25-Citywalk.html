Citywalk · Places, Prepositions & Directions

1. Prepositions of Place 位置介词

介词例句中文对照
in 空间里面My power bank is in my backpack.充电宝在背包里。
on 表面、屏幕、地图The meeting point is on the map.集合点在地图上。
at 具体点位、门口Let's meet at Exit B of the subway station.我们在地铁站 B 口见。
in front of / behindThe taxi stand is in front of the mall; the parking lot is behind it.出租车候车点在商场前面，停车场在后面。
next to / besideThe ATM is next to the pharmacy door.取款机在药店门口旁边。
between / amongThe bank is between the bakery and the movie theater.银行在面包店和电影院之间。
opposite / across fromThe gym is opposite the park; the bus stop is across from my building.健身房在公园对面；公交站就在我楼下对面。
under / overWe waited under the pedestrian bridge.我们在人行天桥下等。
above / belowThe cafe is above the bookstore; the supermarket is one floor below.咖啡店在书店上面；超市在下面一层。
near / far fromMy place is near the subway station but far from the office.我家离地铁站近，但离公司远。

3. There be 句型

句型例句中文对照
There is + 单数There is a supermarket near my home.我家附近有一家超市。
There are + 复数There are two subway stations near the office.公司附近有两个地铁站。
否定There isn't a bank on this street.这条街上没有银行。
疑问Is there a movie theater near here?这附近有电影院吗？

4. Prepositions of Movement 方向介词

介词例句中文对照
into / out ofShe walked into the pharmacy and came out of it with a cold drink.她走进药店，拿着一瓶冷饮出来。
on / offGet on Line 2 at People's Square and get off at Nanjing Road.在人民广场上 2 号线，南京东路下。
up / downWalk up this street, then down the stairs.沿这条街往上走，然后下楼梯。
介词例句中文对照
across / alongWe walked across the square and then along the river.我们横穿广场，然后沿江走。
over / underThe bike lane goes over the pedestrian bridge; cars go under it.自行车道走天桥上面，汽车走下面。
towards / away fromThe delivery rider is riding towards our building.外卖骑手正朝我们楼骑过来。
throughWalk through the underpass and take Exit 3.走地下通道，从 3 号口出来。
past / aroundWalk past the pharmacy, then around the fountain.走过药店，然后绕过喷泉。
acrossWalk across the road at the crosswalk.走斑马线过马路。

5. Directions 问路与指路

场合例句中文对照
问附近有没有Excuse me, is there a subway station near here?请问，这附近有地铁站吗？
问怎么走Excuse me, how do I get to the City Museum?请问，去市博物馆怎么走？
问时间、出口How long does it take on foot? Which exit should I take?走路要多久？我该走哪个出口？
直走Go straight along this street for about 300 meters.沿这条街直走约三百米。
转弯Turn left at the second traffic light.在第二个红绿灯左转。
经过地标Walk past the pharmacy; it's right after the bakery.走过药店，就在面包店再过去一点。
过马路、穿通道Cross the road at the crosswalk, then go through the underpass.走斑马线过马路，然后走地下通道。
在左右手边It's on your left, opposite the post office.在你左手边，邮局对面。
拐个弯、距离It's just around the corner — you can't miss it. About a five-minute walk.拐个弯就是——不会找不到的。走路大约五分钟。
在楼上Go up to the fifth floor; the gym is next to the elevator.上五楼，健身房在电梯旁边。
建议You can also ride a shared bike — it's faster.也可以骑共享单车，更快。
