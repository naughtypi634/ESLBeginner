# Passive Voice · 被动语态

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习被动语态，了解 be + 过去分词的用法，学会用被动语态描述事情，并应用到工作汇报和生活场景中。

## 1. Warm-up 热身

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| Your food is delivered. Where is it left? | 外卖到了，放在哪里？ |
| → At the door / In the locker / At the desk | 门口 / 快递柜 / 桌上 |
| In your home, what is often cleaned? | 你家经常打扫什么？ |
| → The floor / The table / The window | 地板 / 桌子 / 窗户 |

## 2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

### Ordering Food 点餐

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| is ordered / is delivered | 被下单 / 被配送 | The food **is delivered** in 30 minutes. |
| is packed / is cooked | 被打包 / 被做熟 | The meal **is cooked** fresh. |
| is paid / is served | 被支付 / 被端上 | The bill **is paid** online. |

### Work Tasks 工作任务

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| is assigned / is sent | 被分配 / 被发送 | The report **is sent** every Friday. |
| is scheduled / is checked | 被安排 / 被检查 | Emails **are checked** in the morning. |
| is read / is answered | 被阅读 / 被回复 | The message **is answered** quickly. |

### Daily Chores 家务

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| is washed / is dried | 被洗 / 被晾 | The dishes **are washed** after dinner. |
| is swept / is taken | 被扫 / 被带走 | The floor **is swept** on weekends. |
| is boiled / is made | 被煮 / 被做 | The bed **is made** every morning. |

### Online Content 网络内容

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| is liked / is shared | 被点赞 / 被转发 | The video **is shared** everywhere. |
| is uploaded / is saved | 被上传 / 被保存 | The photo **is uploaded** to 小红书. |
| is scanned / is blocked | 被扫码 / 被屏蔽 | The QR code **is scanned** at the door. |

## 3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

| English | Chinese | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| The dishes are **______** after dinner. | 晚饭后碗被…… | washed / dried / put away |
| My phone is **______** for work every day. | 我的手机每天被……用于工作。 | used / charged / fixed |
| The package **______** left at the locker. | 包裹被放在快递柜。 | is / was / will be |

## 4. Discussion 讨论

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| What is often ordered online in your home? | 你家经常网购什么？ | Snacks / Clothes / Electronics |
| Why is a product returned? | 商品为什么被退货？ | Wrong size / Damaged box / Not needed |
| When are tasks assigned to you? | 你什么时候被分配任务？ | Every morning / After lunch / Before weekend |
| What is cleaned every day in your home? | 你家每天打扫什么？ | The floor / The dishes / The table |
