# Passive Voice · 被动语态

## 1. 构成 Structure

### 常规时态 Regular Tenses (be + done)

| 时态与结构 | 英文例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 (is / are + done) | The office floor **is cleaned** every night. | 办公室地板每晚都会被打扫。 |
| 一般现在时 (is / are + done) | Takeout meals **are delivered** to the front desk. | 外卖餐品会被送到前台。 |
| 一般过去时 (was / were + done) | My flight **was canceled** due to the thunderstorm. | 我的航班因雷暴天气被取消了。 |
| 一般过去时 (was / were + done) | The weekly report **was submitted** before 6 PM. | 周报在下午 6 点前提交了。 |
| 一般将来时 (will be + done) | Your iced latte **will be ready** in three minutes. | 您的冰拿铁三分钟内做好。 |
| 一般将来时 (will be + done) | The new metro line **will be opened** next month. | 新地铁线路将于下月开通。 |
| 现在进行时 (is / are being + done) | The elevator **is being repaired** right now. | 电梯现在正在维修中。 |
| 现在进行时 (is / are being + done) | The meeting room **is being used** for an interview. | 会议室正用于一场面试。 |
| 现在完成时 (have / has been + done) | The rent **has already been paid** on WeChat. | 房租已经在微信上付清了。 |
| 现在完成时 (have / has been + done) | The shared bike **has been unlocked** with the app. | 共享单车已通过软件解锁。 |

### 特殊构成 Special Structures

| 类别与结构 | 英文例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| It is said that... (据说) | **It is said that** the new cafe downstairs offers free tea. | 据说楼下新咖啡店送免费茶。 |
| It is reported that... (据报道) | **It is reported that** the subway line 8 opened today. | 据报道地铁 8 号线今日开通。 |
| It is believed that... (普遍认为) | **It is believed that** a short nap boosts work efficiency. | 人们普遍认为午睡能提效。 |
| It is expected that... (预计) | **It is expected that** the heavy rain will stop soon. | 预计大雨很快就会停下来。 |
| can be done (可以被做) | This PDF file **can be downloaded** to your phone. | 这个 PDF 文件可下载到手机。 |
| must be done (必须被做) | The QR code **must be scanned** at the entrance. | 进门处必须扫描场所码。 |
| should be done (应该被做) | The trash **should be sorted** into kitchen bins. | 垃圾应该分类投入厨余桶。 |
| sb + be given + sth (双宾语) | The intern **was given** a new laptop on day one. | 实习生首日分到一台新电脑。 |
| sth + be sent to + sb (双宾语) | The invite **was sent to** all team members on WeChat. | 邀请函已在微信发给全员。 |
| get + done (口语被动) | I **got caught** in the rain near the subway exit. | 我在地铁口附近淋了雨。 |
| get + paid (发薪/收费) | We **get paid** on the 10th day of every month. | 我们每个月 10 号发工资。 |
| 疑问句 (Be + 主语 + done?) | **Was** your parcel **left** in the smart locker? | 你的快递放在智能柜了吗？ |
| 否定句 (be + not + done) | The file **was not sent** to my personal email. | 文件没有发到我的个人邮箱。 |

## 2. 作用 Function

| 核心作用 | 英文例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 不知动作执行者 (Unknown Agent) | My electric scooter **was stolen** near the station. | 我的电动车在车站附近被偷了。 |
| 不知动作执行者 (Unknown Agent) | A window in the hallway **was broken** last night. | 昨晚走廊的一扇窗户被打破了。 |
| 执行者不言自明 (Obvious Agent) | Milk tea **is delivered** to my office desk every day. | 奶茶每天都被送到我的办公桌。 |
| 执行者不言自明 (Obvious Agent) | Street trees **are watered** early in the morning. | 街道树木清晨都会被浇水。 |
| 强调受体或结果 (Focus on Result) | Our proposal **was approved** by the manager today. | 我们的方案今天被经理批准了。 |
| 强调受体或结果 (Focus on Result) | Five hundred meals **are cooked** here every noon. | 这里每天中午烹制五百份餐品。 |
| 客观陈述与委婉 (Objective & Polite) | A typo **was found** on page three of the report. | 报告第三页发现了一处打字错误。 |
| 规则通知与禁令 (Official Rules) | Smoking **is not permitted** inside the building. | 大楼内部任何区域均严禁吸烟。 |

## 3. 实际例子 Real-World Examples

### 外卖与快递 Delivery & Packages

| 英文例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| The spicy hotpot **is packed** in an insulated bag. | 麻辣烫装在保温袋里以保持温度。 |
| My parcel **was left** in the smart locker by the gate. | 我的快递被放在大门旁的智能柜里。 |
| The bubble tea **was picked up** by the rider just now. | 奶茶刚才已经被外卖骑手取走了。 |
| The wrong dish **was returned** through the app quickly. | 送错的餐品很快通过软件退回了。 |

## 4. Discussion 讨论

| 讨论问题 | 中文对照 | 答题关键词 |
|---|---|---|
| What is usually delivered to your home every week? | 每周通常有什么东西送到你家？ | takeout / fresh fruit / parcel boxes / milk tea |
| What tasks are assigned to you at work on Mondays? | 周一工作中通常会分配给你什么任务？ | weekly reports / client emails / team meetings |
| What was recently repaired or fixed in your home? | 你家里最近有什么东西被维修或修好了？ | air conditioner / water pipe / door lock / light |
| How are important notices shared in your company? | 你们公司的重要通知通常是如何发布的？ | WeChat group / work email / bulletin board |
| When you shop online, why is an item usually returned? | 网购时，一件商品通常因什么原因退货？ | wrong size / damaged box / color difference |
