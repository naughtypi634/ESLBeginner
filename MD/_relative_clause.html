Relative Clause
定语从句 · 从零开始练习（Practice from Zero）
Relative words at a glance（关系词速查）who 人·主语　 whom 人·宾语　 whose 谁的·表所属which / that 物　 where 地点　 when 时间　 why 原因
PART 1Join the Sentences · 合并句子
Turn the second sentence into a relative clause. 把第二句变成定语从句，合并成一个句子。
1. I have a neighbor.She always waters my plants when I travel.
2. I bought a milk tea.It has pearls and extra cheese foam.
3. We use a parcel locker.It is at the gate of our community.
4. I found a charger.Its cable was broken.
5. The taxi driver was very kind.He took me to the train station.
6. The concert was amazing.I bought tickets for it.
7. This is the wet market.My mom buys vegetables here.
8. I still remember the day.I got my first job offer on that day.

PART 2Choose the Correct Word · 选词填空
Options: who / whom / whose / which / that / where / when / why. Some blanks have more than one correct answer.
1. The man ______ is standing at the door is my uncle.
2. The book ______ I borrowed from you is on my desk.
3. This is the hotel ______ we stayed last summer.
4. The girl ______ phone was stolen looks very upset.
5. Do you know the reason ______ he was late this morning?
6. Winter is the season ______ vegetable prices usually go up.
7. The woman ______ you talked to just now is our new manager.
8. I want a phone ______ battery can last two days.
9. That is the park ______ we fly kites every weekend.
10. The cake ______ my mom made tasted really good.
11. This is the moment ______ everything changed.
12. The shoes ______ I bought online are a little too small.

PART 4Correct the Mistakes · 改错
Each sentence has one mistake. 每句只有一处错误，请改正。
1. The man which lives next door is a doctor.
2. This is the book who I told you about.
3. The teacher, that I met yesterday, is very friendly.
4. I know the girl whose her mother is a singer.
5. This is the house where I lived in.
6. The movie that we saw it last night was great.

Answer Key · 参考答案
PART 1 · Join the Sentences
1.I have a neighbor who always waters my plants when I travel.
2.I bought a milk tea that has pearls and extra cheese foam.
3.We use a parcel locker that is at the gate of our community.
4.I found a charger whose cable was broken.
5.The taxi driver who took me to the train station was very kind.
6.The concert that I bought tickets for was amazing.
7.This is the wet market where my mom buys vegetables.
8.I still remember the day when I got my first job offer.
PART 2 · Choose the Correct Word
1. who / that　2. that / which　3. where　4. whose　5. why　6. when　7. whom / that　8. whose　9. where　10. that / which　11. when　12. that / which
PART 3 · Translate into English
1.I like trainers who don't push courses.
2.This is the park where I run every day.
3.I lost the key that opens the office door.
4.Do you remember the restaurant where we first met?
5.This is the reason why I bring lunch every day.
6.The girl who helped me carry my luggage yesterday is my classmate.
PART 4 · Correct the Mistakes
1.The man who lives next door is a doctor.which → who：先行词是人。
2.This is the book that / which I told you about.who → that / which：先行词是物。
3.The teacher, whom I met yesterday, is very friendly.非限定性定语从句不用 that。
4.I know the girl whose mother is a singer.whose 后面不再加 her。
5.This is the house where I lived. / This is the house that I lived in.where 和 in 不能重复使用。
6.The movie that we saw last night was great.从句里去掉多余的 it。
