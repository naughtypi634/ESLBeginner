# There be 句型

There be 句型
01 肯定句
There is/are + 名词 + 地点/时间
Location 地点存在
**There's a KFC** right outside the metro station.
地铁口就有一家肯德基。
**There's a convenience store** at the corner of my street.
我家街口有一家便利店。
**There are three delivery guys** waiting outside the gate.
大门外有三个外卖小哥在等。
**There's a new self-service laundry** in this building.
这栋楼新开了自助洗衣房。
**There's a shared office space** on the third floor.
三楼有一个共享办公空间。
Time & Event 时间事件
**There's a team standup** every morning at 9:30.
每天早上九点半有团队站会。
**There's a live concert** at the stadium this Saturday.
这周六体育场有一场演唱会。
**There's a performance review** next quarter.
下季度有绩效评估。
**There's an online course** on AI tools this weekend.
这周末有个AI工具的在线课程。
**There's a team dinner** after the project launches.
项目上线后有团队聚餐。

02 否定句
There is/are + not / no + 名词
Missing Items 物品缺失
**There isn't any hot water** — did someone use it all?
没有热水了——谁用完了？
**There aren't enough sockets** in this meeting room.
这间会议室插座不够。
**There's no signal** in the elevator at all.
电梯里完全没信号。
**There's no elevator** in this old walk-up building.
这栋老式公寓没有电梯。
**There aren't any vegetarian options** in the canteen today.
今天食堂没有素菜选项。
Situation Negation 情况否定
**There are no vegetarian options** on this menu.
这个菜单上没有素食选择。
**There's no point** in waiting — the store's closed.
没必要等了——店已经关门了。
**There's no way** I'm waking up at 5 am for that.
我绝不可能五点起床去干那个。
**There's no mention** of a bonus in the contract.
合同里没有提到奖金。
**There aren't any direct flights** from here to that city.
从这里到那个城市没有直飞航班。

03 一般疑问句
Is/Are there + 名词 + 地点？
Asking about Items 询问物品
**Is there a water dispenser** on this floor?
这层有饮水机吗？
**Is there a USB port** next to this desk?
这张桌子旁边有USB接口吗？
**Are there any seats** left in the back?
后面还有空位吗？
**Is there a printer** I can use nearby?
附近有我能用的打印机吗？
Asking about Places 询问地点
**Is there a KFC** near the metro station?
地铁站附近有肯德基吗？
**Are there any good restaurants** in this area?
这个区有什么好的餐厅吗？
**Is there a meeting room** available right now?
现在有空会议室吗？
Asking about Possibility 询问可能
**Is there anything** I can help you with?
有什么我能帮你的吗？
**Are there any good movies** playing this week?
这周有什么好电影上映吗？
**Is there any way** to extend the deadline?
有没有办法延长截止日期？

04 特殊疑问句
疑问词 + is/are there + 地点？
Quantity 数量
How many people **are there** in your WeChat group?
你微信群有多少人？
How many stops **are there** from here to People's Square?
从这里到人民广场有几站？
How much time **is there** before the flight?
赶飞机还有多少时间？
How many vacation days **are there** in a year?
一年有多少天年假？
Content 内容
What**'s there** to do in this town on a Sunday?
周日这个镇上有什么可以玩的？
What **was there** before this mall was built?
这个商场建之前这地方是什么？
What**'s there** in the new policy about remote work?
新政策里关于远程办公有什么规定？
Distance & Degree 距离与程度
How far **is there** from here to the airport?
从这里到机场有多远？
How long **is there** to wait until the next train?
下一班火车要等多久？
How many stations **are there** on this metro line?
这条地铁线有多少站？

05 过去时态
There was/were / There used to be
Past Events 过去事件
**There was a power outage** last night around midnight.
昨晚午夜左右停了一次电。
**There were like 200 people** at the concert!
昨晚音乐会得有二百人吧！
**There was a huge traffic jam** on the highway yesterday.
昨天高速上堵了一大段。
**There was a data breach** at that company last month.
上个月那家公司发生了一起数据泄露。
Past Negation 过去否定
**There wasn't any traffic** this morning — it was so smooth.
今天早上一点都没堵车，太顺了。
**There weren't any seats** left when we arrived.
我们到的时候已经没座位了。
**There wasn't enough time** to finish the presentation.
没有足够时间完成演示。
Past Existence 过去曾有
**There used to be an Internet cafe** on this street.
这条街以前有一家网吧。
**There used to be a lot of rice fields** around here.
这附近以前有很多稻田。
**There used to be a phone booth** on every corner.
以前每个街角都有电话亭。

06 将来和完成时态
There will be / There has/have been
Future Plans 将来安排
**There will be a typhoon** this weekend — stock up on food.
这周末有台风，多储备点食物。
**There will be a company-wide meeting** on Friday afternoon.
周五下午有全公司大会。
**There will be more job opportunities** in the AI industry.
AI行业会有更多工作机会。
**There will be a new metro line** opening next year.
明年有一条新地铁线开通。
Future Negation 将来否定
**There won't be any delivery** during Spring Festival.
春节期间没有快递。
**There won't be any classes** next Monday — it's a holiday.
下周一没有课——是假期。
**There won't be a salary increase** this year.
今年不会涨薪。
Experience & Change 已有经历
**There have been three rounds of layoffs** this year already.
今年已经有三轮裁员了。
**There has been a big data breach** at that company.
那家公司发生了一次大规模数据泄露。
**There hasn't been a single sunny day** this whole week.
这整周没有一天是晴天。

07 数量修饰语
There is/are + (数量词) + 名词
Large Quantity 大量
**There are tons of food stalls** near my office at lunch.
午饭时间我办公室附近有很多小吃摊。
**There are plenty of parking spots** on weekends.
周末有很多停车位。
**There are several new apps** for AI video editing.
有好几个AI视频编辑的新应用。
Small Quantity 少量
**There is a little bit of milk** left — enough for coffee.
还剩一点牛奶——够泡咖啡。
**There are a few typos** in your report, just FYI.
你的报告里有几个拼写错误。
**There are enough leftovers** for tomorrow's lunch.
剩菜够明天午饭了。
**There is limited seating** in the new food court.
新美食广场座位有限。
Negative Quantity 否定数量
**There are few good coffee shops** in this neighborhood.
这个社区没几家好喝的咖啡馆。
**There is little chance** they'll refund the ticket.
他们退票的希望很小。
**There are hardly any parking spots** on weekends.
周末几乎找不到停车位。

08 就近原则
be 动词单复数取决于最靠近的名词
Singular First 单数靠近
**There is a sofa** and four chairs in the living room.
客厅里有一张沙发和四把椅子。
**There is a laptop**, two phones, and a power bank in my bag.
我包里有一台笔记本、两部手机和一个充电宝。
**There is a supermarket** and several restaurants in this complex.
这个综合体有一家超市和几家餐厅。
Plural First 复数靠近
**There are four chairs** and a sofa in the living room.
客厅里有四把椅子和一张沙发。
**There are two phones** and a laptop in my bag.
我包里有两部手机和一台笔记本。
**There are three cafes** and a bookstore on this street.
这条街上有三家咖啡店和一家书店。
Mixed Examples 综合练习
**There is a gym** and several shops in the basement.
地下有一家健身房和几家商店。
**There are several shops** and a gym in the basement.
地下有几家商店和一家健身房。
**There is an apple** and two oranges in the fridge.
冰箱里有一个苹果和两个橙子。
**There are two oranges** and an apple in the fridge.
冰箱里有两个橙子和一个苹果。

09 常用固定搭配
There is no + 名词 / There is no + V-ing
Negative Patterns 否定搭配
**There's no doubt** that she's the best candidate.
毫无疑问她是最佳人选。
**There's no point** in arguing with the customer service bot.
跟客服机器人争论没意义。
**There's no need** to bring cash — everything takes WeChat.
不用带现金——什么都能用微信支付。
**There's no use** complaining about the weather.
抱怨天气没用。
**There's no way** I'm going to eat that.
我绝对不会吃那个的。
Affirmative Patterns 肯定搭配
**There's a chance** the meeting might get cancelled.
会议有可能取消。
**There's nothing good** on TV tonight.
今晚电视上没好*看的。
**There's something weird** about this file — it won't open.
这个文件有点奇怪——打不开。
**There's a rumor** that the company is being acquired.
有传闻说公司要被收购。
**There's a reason** why everyone is quitting.
大家辞职是有原因的。

10 中文"有"的多种英文表达
存在 / 拥有 / 包含 / 具有特色
Existence 存在 — There be
There's a gas station just before the highway entrance.
高速入口前有一个加油站。
There was a loud bang outside my window last night.
昨晚窗户外面有一声巨响。
Ownership 拥有 — have / possess / own
I **have** two brothers — one older, one younger.
我有两个兄弟——一个哥哥一个弟弟。
He **possesses** incredible patience with kids.
他对孩子有着惊人的耐心。
She **owns** a café near the university.
她在大学附近有一家咖啡馆。
Containment 包含 — contain / include / consist of
This box **contains** 12 cans of beer.
这个箱子里有十二罐啤酒。
The price **includes** breakfast, but not dinner.
价格包括早餐，但不包晚餐。
The committee **consists of** 12 members.
委员会由十二名成员组成。
Feature 具有特色 — feature
The new phone **features** a 200MP camera and AI editing.
新手机有2亿像素摄像头和AI编辑。
The park **features** a lake, a running track, and skatepark.
这个公园有湖、步道和滑板场。
