## Story Frame 故事框架

| 步骤 | 框架 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| 以前 | Back then, I used to be ______ . | Back then, I used to be careless with money. |
| 发生了什么 | Then ______ . | Then my rent went up by 800 yuan. |
| 慢慢变化 | Little by little, I became more ______ . | Little by little, I became more careful. |
| 现在 | These days, I'm ______ . | These days, I save 2,000 yuan a month. |
| 回头想想 | Looking back, ______ changed me. | Looking back, that rent hike changed me. |

## Model Story

<!-- bilingual -->

Back then, I used to be really shy, and I never spoke in meetings.
那时候我特别害羞，开会从不发言。

Then my manager asked me to present on a Friday call.
后来经理让我在周五的电话会上做汇报。

At first, my hands shook.
一开始我手都在抖。

Little by little, it got easier, and after that I started volunteering for small talks.
慢慢地就变简单了，之后我开始主动认领小的发言。

These days, I speak my mind at work, and nobody thinks it's strange.
现在我在工作上有话直说，也没人觉得奇怪。

Looking back, that Friday call changed me.
回头看，是那次周五的电话会改变了我。

## Discussion 讨论

| Question | Answer tags | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| What were you like in high school? | Shy / Quiet / Outgoing | 你高中时是什么样的人？ |
| What made you more confident? | My job / A friend / A hard year | 什么让你更自信了？ |
| What is your biggest change this year? | Saving money / Saying no | 今年你最大的变化是什么？ |
| Who do you get along with? | Easygoing / Considerate / Generous | 你跟什么样的人合得来？ |
| Who do you avoid? | Bossy / Cold / Nosy | 你会躲着什么样的人？ |
| What are you still working on? | Being patient / Not overthinking | 你还在改什么？ |
