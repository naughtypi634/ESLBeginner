### evaluation

### phrases

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| It's a **______** bag. | 它是个……的包 | It's a **small black** bag — I take it to the gym. |
| It's made of **______**. | 它是……做的 | My water bottle is **made of** plastic, so it's very light. |
| It comes in **______**. | 它有……颜色 | This bag **comes in** black, brown and beige. |
| It's for **______**. | 它是用来……的 | My desk drawer is **for** my pens and cables. |
| It works well. | 它很好用 | I use this pan every day — **it works well**. |
| It doesn't work. | 它不好使 | My old rice cooker **doesn't work** now. |
| It breaks easily. | 它容易坏 | A 20-yuan umbrella **breaks easily** in the wind. |
| It lasts a long time. | 它很耐用 | **It lasts a long time** — my backpack is five years old. |
| It's worth it. | 值这个价 | 200 yuan for a good umbrella — **worth it**. |
| It's not worth it. | 不值 | 300 yuan for a phone case — **not worth it**. |
| It looks nice but breaks easily. | 中看不中用 | My desk lamp **looks nice but breaks easily**. |

## Discussion 讨论

**Your phone case is two years old. How does it look now?**

> still nice / looks old / cheap-looking / hard to clean

**You want a new water bottle. What do you look at first?**

> color / shape / size / material / price

**A friend buys a new bag. What do you say about it?**

> nice-looking / stylish / good value / too big / not my style

**You need an umbrella. Why do some people pay 200 yuan for one?**

> durable / lasts a long time / well-made / sturdy / looks nice

**Your desk lamp looked nice but stopped working after a month. What do you think?**

> not worth it / a waste of money / looks nice but breaks easily / badly made

**Which things in your home still work well after many years?**

> rice cooker / pan / chair / backpack / shoes

**What matters most in a chair you sit in all day?**

> soft seat / a back / wide seat / light / strong

**Something you bought online looked different in real life. What was wrong?**

> different color / too small / cheap-looking / badly made / doesn't work

**What do you buy again and again because it lasts a long time?**

> shoes / backpack / headphones / pan / umbrella

**Your takeout box — what is it made of, and how strong is it with hot soup?**

> paper / plastic / metal / strong / too weak

**Which is more important for a laptop — light or sturdy?**

> light / easy to carry / sturdy / durable / too heavy
