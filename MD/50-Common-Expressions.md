# 50 Common English Expressions

> 来源：https://basicenglishspeaking.com/common-expressions-english/

## 01 问候  GREETINGS

**正式问候**

- Hello.  你好。
- Good morning.  早上好。
- How are you?  你好吗？
- Nice to meet you.  很高兴认识你。
- It's a pleasure to meet you.  认识你很高兴。

**非正式问候**

- Hi! What's up?  嗨！最近怎么样？
- How's it going?  过得怎么样？
- How have you been?  你最近好吗？
- Long time no see.  好久不见。
- What have you been up to?  你最近在忙什么？

**回应问候**

- I'm fine, thanks. And you?  我挺好的，谢谢，你呢？
- Not bad. You?  还不错，你呢？
- Great, thanks.  很好，谢谢。

## 02 告别  SAYING GOODBYE

**告别**

- Bye! See you!  拜拜！回头见！
- See you tomorrow.  明天见。
- See you later!  待会儿见！
- Talk to you later.  晚点聊。
- See you next time.  下次见。
- I've got to go now.  我得走了。
- Take care!  保重！
- Stay in touch.  保持联系。
- It was nice meeting you.  认识你很高兴。
- I'll miss you.  我会想你的。

## 03 道歉  MAKING APOLOGIES

**道歉**

- Sorry.  对不起。
- I'm so sorry.  真的很抱歉。
- Sorry, it's my fault.  对不起，是我的错。
- Sorry, I'm late.  对不起，我迟到了。
- Sorry for keeping you waiting.  抱歉让你等了。
- Sorry, I didn't mean to.  对不起，我不是故意的。
- Can you forgive me?  你能原谅我吗？
- I apologize for the mistake.  我为这个错误道歉。

**接受道歉**

- That's all right.  没关系。
- Don't worry about it.  别放在心上。
- No problem.  没事。
- It's not your fault.  不是你的错。

## 04 自我介绍与介绍他人  INTRODUCING YOURSELF & OTHERS

**自我介绍**

- Hello, my name is Li Wei.  你好，我叫李伟。
- Hi, I'm Zhang Min.  嗨，我是张敏。
- Nice to meet you. I'm Wang Lin.  很高兴认识你，我是王琳。
- Let me introduce myself. I'm Chen Jie.  让我自我介绍一下，我是陈洁。
- May I introduce myself?  我可以自我介绍一下吗？

**介绍他人**

- This is my friend Tom.  这是我朋友汤姆。
- Have you met Li Na?  你见过李娜吗？
- I'd like you to meet my colleague.  我想让你认识一下我的同事。

**回应**

- Nice to meet you, too.  我也很高兴认识你。
- Glad to meet you.  很高兴认识你。

## 05 感谢  SAYING THANK YOU

**表达感谢**

- Thanks.  谢谢。
- Thank you very much.  非常感谢。
- Thanks a lot.  多谢。
- Thank you so much for your help.  非常感谢你的帮助。
- I really appreciate it.  真的很感激。
- That's so kind of you.  你真是太好了。
- You've made my day.  你让我今天很开心。
- Thanks for the gift.  谢谢你的礼物。
- Thanks for helping me move.  谢谢你帮我搬家。

**回应感谢**

- You're welcome.  不客气。
- My pleasure.  我的荣幸。
- No problem.  没事。
- Don't mention it.  不用客气。
- No big deal.  小事一桩。

## 06 谈论时间  TALKING ABOUT TIME

**问时间**

- What time is it?  现在几点了？
- Do you know what time it is?  你知道现在几点吗？
- It's ten o'clock.  现在十点。
- What time can we meet?  我们几点见面？
- Do you have free time this afternoon?  你今天下午有空吗？

**谈时间**

- The meeting starts at nine.  会议九点开始。
- The supermarket opens at nine.  超市九点开门。
- We have plenty of time.  我们时间很充裕。
- It's time to go.  该走了。
- Time went by so fast.  时间过得真快。
- I didn't think it was so late.  我没想到这么晚了。

## 07 询问语言能力  DO YOU SPEAK ENGLISH?

**问与答**

- Can you speak English?  你会说英语吗？
- I speak a little English.  我会说一点英语。
- I can read English, but I can't speak well.  我能读英语，但说不好。
- How long have you been learning English?  你学英语多久了？
- I want to improve my English speaking.  我想提高英语口语。
- Sorry, my English is not good.  抱歉，我英语不太好。

**请对方帮忙**

- Please speak slowly.  请说慢一点。
- Could you say that again?  能再说一遍吗？
- How do you pronounce this word?  这个词怎么发音？
- What does this word mean?  这个词是什么意思？

## 08 夸奖  GIVING COMPLIMENTS

**夸奖别人**

- Good job!  干得好！
- Well done!  做得真棒！
- That's great!  太棒了！
- Nice work!  干得漂亮！
- You look great today.  你今天看起来很棒。
- I like your haircut.  我喜欢你的新发型。
- This dish is delicious.  这道菜真好吃。
- You have a nice voice.  你声音真好听。

**回应夸奖**

- Thank you.  谢谢。
- How kind of you to say so.  你这么说太客气了。
- I'm glad you like it.  你喜欢我很高兴。
- Thanks for your compliment.  谢谢你的夸奖。

## 09 抱怨  MAKING COMPLAINTS

**提出抱怨**

- I have a complaint to make.  我要投诉。
- I'm not happy with this.  我对这个不满意。
- There's a problem with my order.  我的订单有问题。
- This is not what I asked for.  这不是我要的。
- The food is cold.  食物是凉的。
- The Wi-Fi doesn't work.  Wi-Fi 用不了。
- Sorry to bother you, but...  抱歉打扰你，但是……
- I ordered an hour ago.  我一个小时前下的单。

**回应抱怨**

- I'm so sorry. This won't happen again.  非常抱歉，不会再发生了。
- We'll fix it right away.  我们马上处理。
- Sorry, there's nothing we can do.  抱歉，我们也无能为力。

## 10 喜欢与不喜欢  LIKES & DISLIKES

**表达喜欢**

- I like this song.  我喜欢这首歌。
- I love hotpot.  我爱吃火锅。
- I enjoy cooking.  我喜欢做饭。
- I'm crazy about milk tea.  我超爱奶茶。
- Do you like swimming? Yes, I do.  你喜欢游泳吗？喜欢。

**表达不喜欢**

- I don't like getting up early.  我不喜欢早起。
- I don't like spicy food.  我不喜欢吃辣。
- I can't stand the noise.  我受不了这噪音。
- I hate waiting.  我讨厌等待。
- I'm not a fan of horror movies.  我不太喜欢恐怖片。
## 11 确定与不确定  CERTAINTY & UNCERTAINTY

**询问确定**

- Are you sure?  你确定吗？
- Are you sure about it?  你确定这件事吗？
- How sure are you?  你有几分把握？

**表达确定**

- I'm sure about it.  我很确定。
- I'm absolutely sure.  我完全确定。
- I'm a hundred percent sure.  我百分之百确定。
- I have no doubt about it.  我毫不怀疑。
- Of course.  当然。

**表达不确定**

- I'm not sure about it.  我不太确定。
- I'm not a hundred percent sure.  我不是百分之百确定。
- I doubt it.  我表示怀疑。
- I don't think so.  我不这么认为。
- I don't know yet.  我还不知道。
- It's very unlikely.  可能性很小。

## 12 邀请  MAKING INVITATIONS

**发出邀请**

- Do you want to go to the movies tonight?  今晚想去看电影吗？
- Would you like to have dinner with us?  你愿意和我们一起吃晚饭吗？
- Do you feel like going for a walk?  想出去走走吗？
- How about joining me for lunch?  一起吃午饭怎么样？
- Would you like a cup of coffee?  想喝杯咖啡吗？

**接受邀请**

- I'd love to, thanks.  我很乐意，谢谢。
- Sure. Thank you.  好啊，谢谢。
- That's a great idea.  好主意。
- Thanks for inviting me.  谢谢你的邀请。

**拒绝邀请**

- I can't, sorry. I have to work.  我去不了，抱歉，我得工作。
- Thanks for the invitation, but I'm busy.  谢谢邀请，但我很忙。
- I'd love to, but I have an appointment.  我很想去，但我有个约。
- Sorry, I'm busy tomorrow.  抱歉，我明天有事。

## 13 请求  MAKING REQUESTS

**提出请求**

- Could you please help me?  你能帮帮我吗？
- Can you give me a hand?  你能搭把手吗？
- Could you please say that again?  能再说一遍吗？
- Would you mind opening the window?  你介意开下窗吗？
- Can you tell me what happened?  你能告诉我发生了什么吗？
- Could you take me to the station?  你能送我去车站吗？
- I'd appreciate it if you could help me.  如果你能帮我，我会很感激。

**接受请求**

- Sure, I'd be glad to.  好的，我很乐意。
- No problem.  没问题。
- Of course.  当然。
- Sure, just a moment.  好，稍等一下。

**拒绝请求**

- I'm sorry, I can't.  抱歉，我做不到。
- I'd love to, but I'm busy.  我很想帮你，但我在忙。
- Sorry, I have to go now.  抱歉，我得走了。

## 14 主动帮忙  MAKING OFFERS

**主动提供**

- Can I help you?  需要帮忙吗？
- Can I give you a hand?  要我搭把手吗？
- Let me help you.  让我来帮你。
- Would you like something to drink?  你想喝点什么吗？
- Do you want me to open the door?  要我开门吗？
- I'd be happy to take you to the airport.  我很乐意送你去机场。

**接受帮忙**

- Yes, please.  好的，谢谢。
- Yes, that would be great.  好呀，太好了。
- That's very kind of you.  你真好。

**拒绝帮忙**

- No, thanks.  不用了，谢谢。
- It's OK. I can do it myself.  没事，我自己来。
- Thanks, but I can do it.  谢谢，但我自己可以。

## 15 征求与给予许可  ASKING & GIVING PERMISSION

**征求许可**

- Can I sit here?  我能坐这儿吗？
- Can I ask you a question?  我能问你一个问题吗？
- May I come in?  我可以进来吗？
- Do you mind if I open the window?  你介意我开窗吗？
- Is it okay if I use your phone?  我可以用一下你的手机吗？

**给予许可**

- Sure, go ahead.  当然，去吧。
- No problem.  没问题。
- Yes, you can.  可以。
- Please feel free.  请随意。

**拒绝许可**

- I'm afraid not.  恐怕不行。
- Sorry, that's not possible.  抱歉，不行。
- I'm sorry, but you can't.  对不起，不可以。

## 16 建议与忠告  SUGGESTIONS & ADVICE

**提建议**

- You should get some rest.  你应该休息一下。
- Why don't you try the new café?  为什么不试试那家新咖啡店？
- You'd better bring an umbrella.  你最好带把伞。
- How about going to the park?  去公园怎么样？
- If I were you, I'd call him.  如果我是你，我会给他打电话。
- I suggest you take a nap.  我建议你小睡一会儿。

**接受建议**

- Sounds good.  听起来不错。
- That sounds like a good idea.  听起来是个好主意。
- Thanks, I'll do that.  谢谢，我会照做。
- I think you're right.  我觉得你说得对。

**拒绝建议**

- No, I'd rather not.  不，还是算了。
- I don't think so.  我觉得不行。
- I don't feel like it.  我不想。

## 23 表达看法  GIVING OPINIONS

**表达看法**

- I think we should buy a house.  我觉得我们应该买房。
- In my opinion, this is a good idea.  在我看来，这是个好主意。
- I don't think we should get a new car.  我觉得我们不该买新车。
- Personally, I think you're right.  就我个人而言，你说得对。
- From my point of view, it's too expensive.  在我看来，太贵了。
- As far as I'm concerned, the plan is fine.  就我而言，这个计划没问题。

**同意**

- I couldn't agree more.  我完全同意。
- I completely agree with you.  我完全同意你。

**不同意**

## 24 预约  MAKING AN APPOINTMENT

**约时间**

- Can we meet next Friday?  我们下周五见面行吗？
- Are you free this weekend?  你这周末有空吗？
- When would be a good time to meet you?  什么时候方便见面？
- How about tomorrow afternoon?  明天下午怎么样？
- Is Monday convenient for you?  周一对你方便吗？
- Can I meet you tomorrow afternoon?  我明天下午能见你吗？

**接受预约**

- Sure. I'll come.  好的，我会来。
- Yes, Friday is fine.  可以，周五没问题。
- I'll be there at that time.  我那个时间会到。

**取消或拒绝**

- Sorry, I have another appointment at that time.  抱歉，那个时间我有别的约。
- I'm afraid I can't make it on Sunday.  恐怕周日我来不了。
- Sorry, I don't think I can make it.  抱歉，我可能去不了。

- It's not a big deal.  没什么大不了的。
- Calm down.  冷静点。
- There's nothing to be afraid of.  没什么好怕的。

## 27 承认错误  ADMITTING MISTAKES

- What's your phone number?  你的电话号码是多少？
- Can I add you on WeChat?  我能加你微信吗？
