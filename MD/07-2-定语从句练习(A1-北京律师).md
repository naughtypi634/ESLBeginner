# 定语从句练习 A1（北京律师版）

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习定语从句，了解 who / which / that 的简单用法，学会用定语从句描述人和物，并应用到北京律师的工作与生活场景中。

第一阶梯
基础句式
不修饰，只陈述。
【我的工作】
1. 我是一名律师。
(I am a lawyer.)
2. 我在北京工作。
(I work in Beijing.)
3. 我在一家律所上班。
(I work at a law firm.)
4. 我每天都很忙。
(I am busy every day.)
5. 我有很多客户。
(I have many clients.)
【在律所】
6. 我的办公室不大。
(My office is small.)
7. 桌上有很多文件。
(There are many files on my desk.)
8. 我今天有会议。
(I have a meeting today.)
9. 我下午要写合同。
(I will write a contract this afternoon.)
10. 同事们在打电话。
(My colleagues are on the phone.)
【在法院】
11. 我今天要去法院。
(I am going to court today.)
12. 法院离律所很远。
(The court is far from my firm.)
13. 开庭时间是九点。
(The hearing starts at nine.)
14. 法官很严肃。
(The judge is serious.)
15. 这个案子下周开庭。
(The case goes to court next week.)
【我的客户】
16. 我的客户很着急。
(My client is very worried.)
17. 他今天给我打了电话。
(He called me today.)
18. 我帮他准备材料。
(I help him prepare documents.)
19. 他相信我。
(He trusts me.)
20. 客户今天赢了官司。
(My client won the case today.)

第二阶梯
加形容词
名词前面加一个形容词。
【我的工作】
1. 我有个很忙的工作。
(I have a **busy** job.)
2. 我认识一位有名的律师。
(I know a **famous** lawyer.)
3. 我今天穿了件新西装。
(I wore a **new** suit today.)
4. 我用了新电脑。
(I use my **new** computer.)
5. 我有一位好老板。
(I have a **good** boss.)
【文件与合同】
6. 我有很多重要的文件。
(I have many **important** files.)
7. 我带了份长合同。
(I brought a **long** contract.)
8. 我需要一份干净的复印件。
(I need a **clean** copy.)
9. 我买了台新打印机。
(I bought a **new** printer.)
10. 这是个难的案子。
(This is a **difficult** case.)
【在法院】
11. 今天是个重要的日子。
(Today is an **important** day.)
12. 法院里有间大法庭。
(The court has a **big** courtroom.)
13. 法官问了个难问题。
(The judge asked a **difficult** question.)
14. 我准备了一份详细的报告。
(I prepared a **detailed** report.)
15. 我提交了新证据。
(I submitted **new** evidence.)
【我的客户】
16. 我有个紧张的客户。
(I have a **nervous** client.)
17. 他有个难办的问题。
(He has a **hard** problem.)
18. 我接了个新案子。
(I took a **new** case.)
19. 我收到一封正式的信。
(I got a **formal** letter.)
20. 我认识一位好客户。
(I know a **nice** client.)

第三阶梯
定语从句 — 作主语（who / that + 动词）
把形容词换成句子。who / that 后面直接跟动词。
【人】
1. 我有一位相信我的客户。
(I have a client **who trusts me**.)
2. 我认识一位在法院工作的朋友。
(I know a friend **who works at the court**.)
3. 我认识一位会说英语的同事。
(I know a colleague **who speaks English**.)
4. 我有一位每天都很忙的老板。
(I have a boss **who is busy every day**.)
5. 我认识一位会开车的律师。
(I know a lawyer **who can drive**.)
【东西与工具】
6. 我有一台能扫描文件的打印机。
(I have a printer **that scans documents**.)
7. 我有个能装很多文件的包。
(I have a bag **that holds many files**.)
8. 我有一支好写的笔。
(I have a pen **that writes well**.)
9. 我有一部能录音的手机。
(I have a phone **that records sound**.)
10. 我买了个能锁上的文件柜。
(I bought a cabinet **that locks**.)
【文件与材料】
11. 这是一份需要签名的合同。
(This is a contract **that needs a signature**.)
12. 我有一份需要翻译的文件。
(I have a document **that needs translation**.)
13. 我收到一封通知开庭时间的信。
(I got a letter **that says the hearing time**.)
14. 这是份能帮我们的证据。
(This is evidence **that can help us**.)
15. 我有一份解释案子的报告。
(I have a report **that explains the case**.)
【地点与活动】
16. 我找了一家很有名的律所。
(I found a law firm **that is very famous**.)
17. 我参加了一个教法律的课。
(I took a class **that teaches law**.)
18. 我下了一个查法律的软件。
(I downloaded an app **that checks the law**.)
19. 我常去一家很安静的咖啡店。
(I often go to a café **that is very quiet**.)
20. 我们去了一个坐满人的法庭。
(We went to a courtroom **that was full of people**.)

第四阶梯
定语从句 — 作主语 · 加细节
从句写长一点，加入副词或并列结构。
【客户】
1. 我有一位每天打电话、问很多问题的客户。
(I have a client **who calls every day and asks many questions**.)
2. 我有一位很着急、想快点解决的客户。
(I have a client **who is very worried and wants a quick answer**.)
3. 我认识一位工作认真、从不迟到的律师。
(I know a lawyer **who works hard and is never late**.)
4. 我认识一位开庭前紧张、但表现很好的同事。
(I know a colleague **who is nervous before court but does very well**.)
5. 我有一位很客气、也信任我的客户。
(I have a client **who is very polite and trusts me**.)
【文件与工具】
6. 我有一份很长、要读很久的合同。
(I have a contract **that is very long and takes time to read**.)
7. 我买了一个能装文件、也有轮子的箱子。
(I bought a case **that holds files and has wheels**.)
8. 我有一台能打印、也能扫描的机器。
(I have a machine **that prints and scans**.)
9. 我下了一个能查法律、也能翻译的软件。
(I downloaded an app **that checks the law and translates**.)
10. 我有一支很便宜、也好用的笔。
(I have a pen **that is cheap and works well**.)
【工作与生活】
11. 我住在一个离律所和法院都近的小区。
(I live in a neighborhood **that is close to my firm and the court**.)
12. 我接了一个重要、要准备很久的案子。
(I took a case **that is important and needs a lot of time**.)
13. 我参加了一个讲新法律、也讲案例的培训。
(I joined a training **that explains new laws and real cases**.)
14. 我买了一个保温、也能装咖啡的杯子。
(I bought a cup **that keeps coffee warm**.)
15. 我找到了能证明客户没错的证据。
(I found evidence **that shows my client is right**.)
【法院与出行】
16. 我们去了一个很远、要坐一小时地铁的法院。
(We went to a court **that is far and takes one hour by subway**.)
17. 我找到了一条不堵、也能准时到的路。
(I found a road **that is not busy and gets me there on time**.)
18. 我常去一家安静、适合见客户的咖啡店。
(I often go to a café **that is quiet and good for clients**.)
19. 我买了一件正式、穿着也舒服的西装。
(I bought a suit **that is formal and comfortable**.)
20. 我认识一位有经验、也很有耐心的前辈。
(I know a senior lawyer **who has experience and lots of patience**.)

第五阶梯
定语从句 — 作宾语（that / whom 可省略）
从句修饰宾语。that / whom 可以省略。
【客户与同事】
1. 我昨天见的客户很高兴。
(The client **(that) I met yesterday** is very happy.)
2. 我帮的客户今天赢了官司。
(The client **(whom) I helped** won the case today.)
3. 我认识的律师很有名。
(The lawyer **(whom) I know** is very famous.)
4. 我推荐的同事升职了。
(The colleague **(whom) I recommended** got a promotion.)
5. 我打电话的客户明天来律所。
(The client **(whom) I called** comes to the firm tomorrow.)
【文件与材料】
6. 我写的合同已经发了。
(The contract **(that) I wrote** has been sent.)
7. 我找的文件在桌上。
(The file **(that) I need** is on my desk.)
8. 我准备的报告很长。
(The report **(that) I prepared** is very long.)
9. 我签的协议很麻烦。
(The agreement **(that) I signed** is complicated.)
10. 我改的材料通过了。
(The document **(that) I revised** was accepted.)
【日常与工具】
11. 我用的打印机坏了。
(The printer **(that) I use** is broken.)
12. 我买的包能装下所有文件。
(The bag **(that) I bought** holds all my files.)
13. 我坐的地铁很挤。
(The subway **(that) I take** is very crowded.)
14. 我住的小区离律所很近。
(The neighborhood **(that) I live in** is close to my firm.)
15. 我订的餐厅今天不开门。
(The restaurant **(that) I booked** is closed today.)
【法院与开庭】
16. 我听的庭审很有用。
(The hearing **(that) I listened to** was very useful.)
17. 法官问的问题很难。
(The question **(that) the judge asked** was difficult.)
18. 我提交的证据很重要。
(The evidence **(that) I submitted** is very important.)
19. 我上的法庭在五楼。
(The courtroom **(that) I was in** is on the fifth floor.)
20. 我参加的会议开了两小时。
(The meeting **(that) I attended** lasted two hours.)

第六阶梯
定语从句 — 作宾语 · 加细节
从句写长一点，开头句式变一变。
【客户与案子】
1. 我昨天在律所见的那位客户很着急。
(The client **(that) I met at the firm yesterday** is very worried.)
2. 我帮他准备材料的客户赢了。
(The client **(whom) I helped with the documents** won.)
3. 我上个月接的案子这周开庭。
(The case **(that) I took last month** goes to court this week.)
4. 我研究了很久的问题终于解决了。
(The problem **(that) I studied for a long time** is finally solved.)
5. 我昨天联系的证人今天到了。
(The witness **(whom) I called yesterday** arrived today.)
【文件与办公】
6. 我昨天下午写的合同要修改。
(The contract **(that) I wrote yesterday afternoon** needs changes.)
7. 我放在桌上的文件不见了。
(The file **(that) I put on my desk** is missing.)
8. 我上周准备的材料通过了。
(The materials **(that) I prepared last week** were accepted.)
9. 我打印的判决书少了一页。
(The judgment **(that) I printed** is missing a page.)
10. 我借给同事的书还没还。
(The book **(that) I lent to my colleague** is not back yet.)
【日常与通勤】
11. 我早上坐的地铁今天晚点了。
(The subway **(that) I took this morning** was late today.)
12. 我在楼下买的咖啡凉了。
(The coffee **(that) I bought downstairs** is cold.)
13. 我昨天穿的西装还在干洗店。
(The suit **(that) I wore yesterday** is at the dry cleaner's.)
14. 我订的那家餐厅味道不错。
(The restaurant **(that) I booked** is good.)
15. 我手机里存的录音很有用。
(The recording **(that) I saved on my phone** is very useful.)
【法院与庭审】
16. 我今天上午听的庭审推迟了。
(The hearing **(that) I attended this morning** was postponed.)
17. 法官上周问的问题我还在想。
(I am still thinking about the question **(that) the judge asked last week**.)
18. 我给法院的证据被接受了。
(The evidence **(that) I gave the court** was accepted.)
19. 我复印的身份证件需要盖章。
(The ID copy **(that) I made** needs a stamp.)
20. 我在法院见到的书记员人很好。
(The clerk **(whom) I met at the court** is very nice.)

第七阶梯
关系副词 — WHERE 地点
修饰地点。where = 在那里。
【律所】
1. 我工作的律所在国贸。
(The firm **where I work** is in Guomao.)
2. 我打印的办公室在二楼。
(The office **where I print** is on the second floor.)
3. 我们开会的房间很小。
(The room **where we have meetings** is small.)
4. 我放案卷的柜子在走廊。
(The cabinet **where I keep case files** is in the hallway.)
5. 我午休的休息室有咖啡机。
(The break room **where I rest** has a coffee machine.)
【法院】
6. 我常开庭的法院在朝阳区。
(The court **where I often have hearings** is in Chaoyang District.)
7. 我交材料的窗口在二楼。
(The window **where I submit documents** is on the second floor.)
8. 我等开庭的走廊很安静。
(The hallway **where I wait for hearings** is quiet.)
9. 我上个月立案的法院很远。
(The court **where I filed the case last month** is far away.)
10. 法官休息的房间在走廊尽头。
(The room **where the judge rests** is at the end of the hallway.)
【北京生活】
11. 我换乘的地铁站很大。
(The subway station **where I transfer** is very big.)
12. 我买早餐的店在楼下。
(The shop **where I buy breakfast** is downstairs.)
13. 我加完班吃饭的面馆很便宜。
(The noodle shop **where I eat after work** is cheap.)
14. 我复印材料的店在法院对面。
(The print shop **where I copy documents** is across from the court.)
15. 我中午散步的公园很小。
(The park **where I walk at noon** is small.)
【见客户与办事】
16. 我会见客户的会议室朝南。
(The meeting room **where I meet clients** faces south.)
17. 我们签合同的地方很安静。
(The place **where we signed the contract** is quiet.)
18. 我接待客户的前台在大厅。
(The front desk **where I receive clients** is in the lobby.)
19. 我取快递的驿站就在门口。
(The pickup station **where I get my parcels** is at the gate.)
20. 我常去的咖啡店在法院旁边。
(The café **where I often go** is next to the court.)

第八阶梯
关系副词 — WHEN 时间 & WHY 原因
上 10 句修饰时间，下 10 句修饰原因。
【WHEN · 时间】
1. 我还记得第一次开庭的那天。
(I still remember the day **when I had my first hearing**.)
2. 我还记得第一次接案子的那天。
(I still remember the day **when I took my first case**.)
3. 周五下班的那一刻最开心。
(The moment **when I leave work on Friday** makes me happy.)
4. 早上通勤的那一小时很累。
(The hour **when I commute in the morning** is tiring.)
5. 发工资的那天我很开心。
(Payday is the day **when I am the happiest**.)
6. 我还记得赢第一个案子的那天。
(I still remember the day **when I won my first case**.)
7. 秋天是北京最美的时候。
(Autumn is the season **when Beijing is most beautiful**.)
8. 中午休息的那一小时过得太快。
(The hour **when I rest at noon** passes too fast.)
9. 下班打卡的那一刻最轻松。
(The moment **when I clock out** is the most relaxing.)
10. 我还记得第一次见老板的那天。
(I still remember the day **when I first met my boss**.)
【WHY · 原因】
11. 这就是我喜欢当律师的原因。
(This is the reason **why I like being a lawyer**.)
12. 你知道我早到一小时的原因吗？
(Do you know the reason **why I arrive an hour early**?)
13. 这就是我总做笔记的原因。
(This is the reason **why I always take notes**.)
14. 这就是我坐地铁上班的原因。
(This is the reason **why I take the subway to work**.)
15. 这就是我晚上学英语的原因。
(This is the reason **why I study English at night**.)
16. 你知道客户选我的原因吗？
(Do you know the reason **why clients choose me**?)
17. 这就是我认真读合同的原因。
(This is the reason **why I read contracts carefully**.)
18. 案子复杂是我加班的原因。
(Complicated cases are the reason **why I work overtime**.)
19. 这就是我开会总带手机的原因。
(This is the reason **why I always bring my phone to meetings**.)
20. 这就是我住在律所附近的原因。
(This is the reason **why I live near my firm**.)
