# Short Stories & Narrative · 讲故事

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习讲故事，了解叙述的基本顺序和时态，学会用简单英语讲一个小故事，并应用到分享经历和朋友聚会中。

## 1. Warm-up 热身

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You tell a friend about your morning. What do you say first? | 跟朋友讲早上发生的事，你先说什么？ |
| → I wake up / I drink tea / I go out | 我起床 / 我喝茶 / 我出门 |
| Do you enjoy telling stories about your day? | 你喜欢讲一天的事吗？ |
| → Yes, fun / No, boring / Maybe sometimes | 喜欢，好玩 / 不，无聊 / 偶尔 |

## 2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

### Start a Story 开头

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| First / One day | 首先 / 有一天 | **One day**, I missed the bus. |
| This morning | 今天早上 | **This morning**, I overslept. |
| There is / I see | 有 / 我看到 | **I see** a long line outside. |

### Add More 推进

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Then / Next | 然后 / 接着 | **Then** I scanned the bike code. |
| After / And | 之后 / 而且 | **After** that, I got coffee. |
| So / But | 所以 / 但是 | **But** the app was down. |

### End a Story 收尾

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Finally / In the end | 最后 / 到头来 | **Finally**, I made it on time. |
| That's all / The end | 就这些 / 完 | **That's all** for today. |
| I feel | 我觉得 | **I feel** lucky it worked out. |

### Feelings in a Story 故事里的情绪

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| happy / excited | 开心 / 兴奋 | I was **excited** to get home. |
| tired / hungry | 累 / 饿 | I was **tired** after work. |
| scared / sad | 害怕 / 难过 | I was **scared** of missing the train. |

## 3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

| English | Chinese | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| The story **______** about my morning coffee. | 这个故事是关于我早上咖啡的。 | is / are / am |
| I **______** not tell long stories. | 我不讲长故事。 | do / does / am |
| **______**, I opened the delivery app. | ……，我打开了外卖 App。 | First / Finally / Next |

## 4. Discussion 讨论

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| Do you have a morning story today? | 今天早上有故事吗？ | Late bus / Good breakfast / No story |
| What makes a morning story good? | 什么样的早上故事算好？ | Quick start / Nice food / No rush |
| What is your lunch story today? | 今天午饭有什么故事？ | Order takeout / Eat with friends / Eat alone |
| Is your evening story always happy? | 晚上的故事总是开心的吗？ | Yes, always / Sometimes no / I don't know |
