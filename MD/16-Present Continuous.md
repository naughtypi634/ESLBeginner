# Present Continuous · 现在进行时

## 1. Warm-up 热身

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| You see a friend looking at her phone and smiling. What is she doing? | 朋友看着手机笑，她在干什么？ |
| → Watching a funny clip / Reading a long message / Looking at photos | 看搞笑视频 / 读长消息 / 看照片 |
| Think of a busy street in your city. What are people doing right now? | 想想城里热闹的街，人们现在在干什么？ |
| → Riding shared bikes / Scanning codes / Walking dogs | 骑共享单车 / 扫码 / 遛狗 |

## 2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

### On the Metro 地铁上

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| looking at the phone | 在看手机 | She **is looking at** her phone. |
| listening to music | 在听音乐 | He **is listening to** music. |
| sending a voice message | 在发语音 | They **are sending** voice messages. |
| scanning a QR code | 在扫码 | The man **is scanning** a QR code. |

### In the Office 办公室里

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| typing an email | 在打邮件 | I **am typing** an email. |
| having a meeting | 在开会 | We **are having** a meeting. |
| fixing a printer | 在修打印机 | He **is fixing** the printer. |
| waiting for a call | 在等电话 | She **is waiting for** a call. |

### At a Restaurant 餐厅里

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ordering food | 在点餐 | They **are ordering** food. |
| scanning the menu | 在扫码看菜单 | She **is scanning** the menu. |
| paying the bill | 在买单 | He **is paying** the bill. |
| taking a photo | 在拍照 | People **are taking** photos of the food. |

### At Home 家里

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| watching TV / cooking dinner | 在看电视 / 在做晚饭 | Mom **is cooking** dinner. |
| playing with a pet | 在逗宠物 | I **am playing** with the cat. |
| video calling a friend | 在和朋友视频 | She **is video calling** her friend. |

## 3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

| English | Chinese | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| I am **______** for my takeout order. | 我正在……外卖。 | waiting / wait / waited |
| He is **______** a shared bike right now. | 他正在……共享单车。 | riding / rides / rode |
| Look! It **______** raining outside. | 看！外面正在下雨。 | is / are / am |

## 4. Discussion 讨论

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| The person next to you on the metro is laughing. What are they watching? | 地铁上旁边的人笑了，他们在看什么？ | A funny dog video / A live stream / A Douyin clip |
| Your colleague is frowning at their screen. What are they doing? | 同事对着屏幕皱眉，他在干什么？ | Reading a long email / Fixing a spreadsheet / Waiting for a download |
| You see a line outside a milk tea shop. What are they doing? | 奶茶店门口排着队，人们在干什么？ | Waiting for orders / Showing QR codes / Chatting |
| How do you feel when you are waiting for food delivery? | 等外卖时你什么感觉？ | Getting hungry / Checking the app / Excited |
