Time Clauses · 时间状语从句

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
When do you usually check your phone?你通常什么时候看手机？
→ After waking up / During lunch / Before bed起床后 / 午休 / 睡前
When is the best time for a short break?什么时候适合休息一下？
→ After work / Before dinner / During rain下班后 / 晚饭前 / 下雨时

2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

Starting the Day 起床后

EnglishChineseExample
after I wake up我醒来后I check my phone after I wake up.
before I shower洗澡前I read news before I shower.
while I eat吃饭时I listen to podcasts while I eat.
as soon as I'm ready一准备好I leave as soon as I'm ready.

Using Apps 用 App 时

EnglishChineseExample
when I order food点餐时I open 美团 when I order food.
before I pay付款前I check the total before I pay.
after I scan the code扫码后The menu appears after I scan the code.
while I watch videos看视频时I reply to messages while I watch videos.

Eating Out 在外吃饭

EnglishChineseExample
when I sit down一坐下I scan the menu when I sit down.
before I order点单前I ask for recommendations before I order.
while I wait等餐时I scroll my phone while I wait.
until the bill comes直到账单来We chat until the bill comes.

Going Home 回家路上

EnglishChineseExample
when I leave the office离开办公室时I call my mom when I leave the office.
while I'm on the metro在地铁上时I read while I'm on the metro.
as soon as I unlock the door一开门I turn on the AC as soon as I unlock the door.

3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

EnglishChineseKeywords
I ______ my phone when I get home from work.下班到家我就……手机。charge / charging / charges
When I ______ a delivery, I go to the locker.有快递时，我去快递柜。have / having / has
______ I finish work, I walk home.下班后我走路回家。After / Before / While
