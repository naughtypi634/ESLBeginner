# CONVERSATION STARTER: A1

## UNIT 1: FAMILY AND RELATIONSHIPS

### STORY 01: FAMILY

**Key Vocabulary**

1. family
2. parents
3. sibling
4. close
5. spend time
6. have dinner
7. help
8. busy
9. visit
10. care about

**Story**

My name is Emma. I live in Hangzhou with my husband and our daughter. My parents live in the same city, so we see them every Sunday. We usually have dinner together. My mother makes tomato and egg soup, and my father brings fresh fruit. My daughter likes to sit next to her grandfather and tell him about school.

I have one younger brother. He works in Shenzhen, so we talk on WeChat after work. He is busy during the week, but he visits us during the Spring Festival. We play cards and take photos together. My family is not very big, but we are close. We help each other when life is difficult. I feel happy because my family cares about me, and I care about them too.

**Conversation Questions**

1. Who lives in your home?
2. When do you usually talk with your family?
3. What food does your family like to eat together?
4. Who in your family helps you most?
5. What do you do with your parents?
6. How do you keep in touch with family members in another city?
7. What is one good memory with your family?
8. What family activity would you like to do this month?
9. How can family members help each other when they are busy?
10. What makes a family feel close?

### STORY 02: MARRIAGE

**Key Vocabulary**

1. husband
2. wife
3. partner
4. wedding
5. share
6. plan
7. agree
8. disagree
9. chores
10. together

**Story**

Liam and his wife, Olivia, have been married for six years. They live in a small apartment near a metro [BrE] / subway [AmE] station in Chengdu. On weekdays, Liam leaves home at eight o'clock, and Olivia starts work at nine. They send messages during the day and have dinner together at home when they can.

They do not agree about everything. Liam likes to save money, but Olivia enjoys eating at new restaurants. They talk about their plans and choose a simple budget together. They also share the housework. Liam washes the dishes, and Olivia does the laundry. On Saturday morning, they clean the apartment and buy food for the week.

Their wedding day was special, but ordinary days are important too. They listen to each other, say sorry after a disagreement, and make time for a walk. Liam says marriage is not perfect. It is two people learning to live and plan together.

**Conversation Questions**

1. What makes a good partner?
2. How can two people share housework?
3. What do you like to plan with another person?
4. How do couples solve a small disagreement?
5. What is a good way to spend a quiet evening together?
6. How can a couple save money?
7. What household task do you enjoy or dislike?
8. Why is listening important in a relationship?
9. What can people do when they have different hobbies?
10. What does a happy home look like to you?

### STORY 03: PARENTING

**Key Vocabulary**

1. child
2. parent
3. homework
4. bedtime
5. patient
6. listen
7. encourage
8. rule
9. safe
10. grow up

**Story**

Sophia has a seven-year-old son named Leo. Every weekday, Leo comes home from primary school at five o'clock. He puts his schoolbag by the door, washes his hands, and eats a snack. Then he does his homework at the kitchen table. Sophia sits near him, but she lets him try first.

Leo sometimes feels tired and does not want to read. His mother stays patient. She listens to him and asks, "What is hard today?" Then they read one short page together. Leo feels better when he can choose the next book. His parents have a simple rule: homework comes before games on school nights. After homework, Leo can play a game for thirty minutes.

At bedtime, Sophia and Leo talk about the day. She tells him that mistakes are part of learning. She also teaches him to be kind and safe outside the home. Leo is growing up, so his parents give him more small jobs, such as putting away his toys and feeding the cat.

**Conversation Questions**

1. What can children do to help at home?
2. How can adults help a child with homework?
3. What is a good bedtime for a school child?
4. How should parents respond to a mistake?
5. What rules are useful at home?
6. How can adults teach children to be safe?
7. What books or games do children enjoy in your family?
8. Why is it important to listen to children?
9. What can a child learn from doing small jobs?
10. How can parents and children spend time together after school?

### STORY 04: FRIENDSHIP

**Key Vocabulary**

1. friend
2. classmate
3. colleague
4. trust
5. invite
6. reply
7. share
8. support
9. honest
10. keep in touch

**Story**

Ethan met his best friend, Jack, at university. They were classmates and often studied in the library together. After graduation, Ethan moved to Beijing for work, and Jack stayed in Wuhan. Their cities are far apart, but they keep in touch every week.

On Friday evening, they usually send voice messages. Ethan talks about his new project, and Jack talks about his small coffee shop. Sometimes one friend has a difficult day. The other friend listens and gives simple support. They do not always have the same opinion, but they are honest and respectful.

Last month, Ethan invited Jack to Beijing. Jack took the high-speed train and stayed for three days. They visited a park, tried a new noodle shop, and took a photo near the river. Before Jack went home, they chose a date for their next visit. Good friends do not need to meet every day. They remember each other and make time for each other.

**Conversation Questions**

1. Where did you meet a close friend?
2. How often do you talk with your friends?
3. What do you like to do with a friend?
4. How can friends support each other?
5. What makes a person easy to trust?
6. How do you keep in touch with a friend in another city?
7. What can you say to a friend after a bad day?
8. How can friends stay close when they are busy?
9. What place would you like to visit with a friend?
10. What is one quality of a good friend?

### STORY 05: LOVE AND DATING

**Key Vocabulary**

1. date
2. meet
3. message
4. coffee
5. interest
6. comfortable
7. nervous
8. polite
9. listen
10. relationship

**Story**

Mia met Noah through a friend. They first talked on WeChat about cooking and films. After one week, Noah asked Mia to have coffee with him. They met at a quiet cafe near Mia's office after work. Mia felt a little nervous, but Noah was friendly and polite.

They talked about their jobs, their favorite food, and their plans for the weekend. Noah listened when Mia spoke. He did not look at his phone during the conversation. After coffee, they walked to the metro [BrE] / subway [AmE] station. Mia said she had a good time, and Noah asked if they could meet again.

They now see each other once or twice a week. Sometimes they cook at home. Sometimes they go to a park or watch a film. They are still learning about each other. They do not need to hurry. They talk openly and respect each other's time. For Mia, a good relationship starts with honest words and comfortable conversations.

**Conversation Questions**

1. What is a comfortable place for a first date?
2. What topics are easy to talk about on a first date?
3. Why is listening important when two people meet?
4. What can people do when they feel nervous?
5. How can people show respect on a date?
6. What simple activity can two people enjoy together?
7. What makes a conversation comfortable?
8. How can people learn about each other's interests?
9. Why should people give a new relationship time?
10. What do you look for in a good relationship?
