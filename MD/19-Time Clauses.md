# Time Clauses · 时间状语从句

## 1. Warm-up 热身

| English | Chinese |
|---|---|
| When do you usually check your phone? | 你通常什么时候看手机？ |
| → After waking up / During lunch / Before bed | 起床后 / 午休 / 睡前 |
| When is the best time for a short break? | 什么时候适合休息一下？ |
| → After work / Before dinner / During rain | 下班后 / 晚饭前 / 下雨时 |

## 2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

### Starting the Day 起床后

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| after I wake up | 我醒来后 | I check my phone **after I wake up**. |
| before I shower | 洗澡前 | I read news **before I shower**. |
| while I eat | 吃饭时 | I listen to podcasts **while I eat**. |
| as soon as I'm ready | 一准备好 | I leave **as soon as I'm ready**. |

### Using Apps 用 App 时

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| when I order food | 点餐时 | I open 美团 **when I order food**. |
| before I pay | 付款前 | I check the total **before I pay**. |
| after I scan the code | 扫码后 | The menu appears **after I scan the code**. |
| while I watch videos | 看视频时 | I reply to messages **while I watch videos**. |

### Eating Out 在外吃饭

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| when I sit down | 一坐下 | I scan the menu **when I sit down**. |
| before I order | 点单前 | I ask for recommendations **before I order**. |
| while I wait | 等餐时 | I scroll my phone **while I wait**. |
| until the bill comes | 直到账单来 | We chat **until the bill comes**. |

### Going Home 回家路上

| English | Chinese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| when I leave the office | 离开办公室时 | I call my mom **when I leave the office**. |
| while I'm on the metro | 在地铁上时 | I read **while I'm on the metro**. |
| as soon as I unlock the door | 一开门 | I turn on the AC **as soon as I unlock the door**. |

## 3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

| English | Chinese | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| I **______** my phone when I get home from work. | 下班到家我就……手机。 | charge / charging / charges |
| When I **______** a delivery, I go to the locker. | 有快递时，我去快递柜。 | have / having / has |
| **______** I finish work, I walk home. | 下班后我走路回家。 | After / Before / While |
