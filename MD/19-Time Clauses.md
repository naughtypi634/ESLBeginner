# Time Clauses · 时间状语从句

## 1. 核心结构

### 时间从句：表示某个时间点、同时和先后

**when：当……的时候**

结构：`when + 完整句子`

I put my phone away **when the meeting starts**.

**When I get home**, I feed the cat first.

Call me **when you arrive at the station**.

**while：在……期间**

结构：`while + 完整句子`

I listen to a podcast **while I walk to the station**.

I read messages **while I am on the train**.

She checks the list **while she waits for the delivery**.

**as：在……的时候 / 随着……**

结构：`as + 完整句子`

**As I walk home**, I listen to a voice message.

**As the weather gets colder**, I take the metro more often.

**As the meeting goes on**, everyone becomes more relaxed.

**just as：正当……的时候**

结构：`just as + 完整句子`

**Just as I was leaving**, my manager called me.

**Just as we sat down**, the food arrived.

**Just as I opened the door**, my phone rang.

**before：在……之前**

结构：`before + 完整句子`

I check the address **before I place the order**.

I check the window **before I leave home**.

Read the message twice **before you send it**.

**after：在……之后**

结构：`after + 完整句子`

I call the courier **after the app shows “delivered.”**

**After I finish work**, I usually take the metro home.

We went for coffee **after the meeting ended**.

**as soon as：一……就……**

结构：`as soon as + 完整句子`

I will call you **as soon as I arrive**.

She will reply **as soon as she has a free minute**.

**As soon as the rain stops**, we will leave.

### 时间从句：表示重复、特定次数和紧接发生

**whenever：每当……的时候**

结构：`whenever + 完整句子`

**Whenever I work from home**, I take a short walk at lunch.

Call me **whenever you need help**.

**Whenever the weather is nice**, we eat outside.

**every time：每次……的时候**

结构：`every time + 完整句子`

I listen to the same show **every time I take the metro**.

He takes a taxi **every time it rains heavily**.

**Every time I open this app**, I see a new notice.

**the first time：第一次……的时候**

结构：`the first time + 完整句子`

**The first time I used the app**, I could not find the payment button.

I felt nervous **the first time I spoke in a team meeting**.

**The first time I visited Shanghai**, I used the metro every day.

**the next time：下一次……的时候**

结构：`the next time + 完整句子`

**The next time you order food**, check the delivery address.

I will ask her **the next time I see her**.

**The next time we meet**, let us try the new cafe.

**the last time：上一次……的时候**

结构：`the last time + 完整句子`

**The last time I took this train**, it was very crowded.

I forgot my umbrella **the last time it rained**.

**The last time we had lunch together**, you told me about your new job.

**the moment / the minute：一……就……**

结构：`the moment / the minute + 完整句子`

I changed my clothes **the moment I got home**.

She replied **the minute she saw my message**.

**The moment the door opened**, the children ran outside.

**once：一旦…… / ……之后就……**

结构：`once + 完整句子`

**Once I finish this report**, I can join you for lunch.

The file will open **once you enter the code**.

**Once the meeting starts**, please keep your phone silent.

### 时间从句：表示起点、终点和某个时间点之前

**until / till：一直到……为止**

结构：`until / till + 完整句子`

I stay at my desk **until the report is ready**.

We waited **until the rain stopped**.

Please stay here **till the manager comes back**.

**since / ever since：自从……以来**

结构：`since / ever since + 完整句子`

I have used this app **since I moved to Beijing**.

She has felt more relaxed **since she changed teams**.

I have worked from home **ever since I changed jobs**.

**by the time：到……的时候已经……**

结构：`by the time + 完整句子`

**By the time I get home**, the food will be cold.

**By the time the meeting starts**, I will have the files ready.

**By the time we arrive**, the shop will have closed.

### 时间短语：表示时间范围、持续时间和截止时间

**as long as：在……期间 / 只要……**

结构：`as long as + 完整句子`

You can stay here **as long as you need**.

I can help you **as long as I have time**.

She listened **as long as he was speaking**.

**during：在……期间**

结构：`during + 名词或名词短语`

I checked my phone **during the meeting**.

We talked about work **during lunch**.

It rained heavily **during the night**.

**for：持续……时间**

结构：`for + 一段时间`

I waited **for twenty minutes**.

She worked there **for three years**.

We talked **for an hour**.

**within：在……之内**

结构：`within + 时间段`

Please reply **within three days**.

The delivery should arrive **within an hour**.

You need to finish the form **within ten minutes**.

**by：不晚于……**

结构：`by + 时间点`

Please finish the report **by Friday**.

I need to be home **by nine**.

The payment must arrive **by the end of the day**.

## 2. Time Clauses in Real Life 生活场景

### 早晨出门 Morning Routine

| 时间关系 | 英文表达 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 起床后 | **After I wake up**, I open the curtains and check the weather. | 起床后，我会拉开窗帘并查看天气。 |
| 洗澡前 | I put my phone on charge **before I take a shower**. | 洗澡前，我会给手机充电。 |
| 准备好后立即出门 | I leave **as soon as I am ready**. | 我一准备好就出门。 |
| 出门时 | **When I leave home**, I check that I have my keys. | 出门时，我会确认带了钥匙。 |

### 点餐和付款 Ordering Food

| 时间关系 | 英文表达 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 点餐前 | I check the delivery time **before I order**. | 点餐前，我会查看配送时间。 |
| 扫码后 | **After I scan the code**, the menu opens on my phone. | 我扫码后，菜单会在手机上打开。 |
| 等餐时 | I answer work messages **while I wait for my food**. | 等餐时，我会回复工作消息。 |
| 账单来之前 | We talk about the movie **until the bill comes**. | 账单来之前，我们聊电影。 |

### 工作和会议 Work and Meetings

| 时间关系 | 英文表达 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 会议开始时 | **When the meeting starts**, I put the agenda on the screen. | 会议开始时，我把议程放到屏幕上。 |
| 发邮件前 | I read the message twice **before I send it**. | 发邮件前，我会把消息读两遍。 |
| 客户加入后 | **After the client joins**, we explain the next step. | 客户加入后，我们会说明下一步。 |
| 会议结束后 | I write down the action points **after the call ends**. | 电话会议结束后，我会记下行动要点。 |

### 通勤和回家 Commuting and Home

| 时间关系 | 英文表达 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 坐地铁时 | I usually read **while I am on the metro**. | 坐地铁时，我通常会阅读。 |
| 到站时 | I stand up **when my stop is next**. | 快到站时，我会站起来。 |
| 到家后 | **When I get home**, I change into comfortable clothes. | 到家后，我会换上舒服的衣服。 |
| 雨停后 | We will ride our bikes **after the rain stops**. | 雨停后，我们会骑车。 |

## 3. Speaking Practice 口语练习

### Sentence Starters 句型开头

| English | Chinese | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| **After I ______**, I usually check my work messages. | 我……后，通常会查看工作消息。 | wake up / arrive home / finish dinner |
| I check the address **before I ______**. | 我在……前核对地址。 | place the order / leave home / send the parcel |
| **While I am ______**, I usually listen to something. | 我在……时，通常会听点东西。 | commuting / cooking / walking |
| I will call you **as soon as I ______**. | 我一……就给你打电话。 | arrive / finish work / get home |
| I will stay here **until ______**. | 我会一直待在这里，直到……。 | the bus comes / the rain stops / you arrive |

### Discussion 讨论

| English | Chinese | Answer tags |
|---|---|---|
| What do you usually do after you wake up? | 你起床后通常做什么？ | check phone / drink water / open curtains |
| What do you check before you place an order online? | 你网上下单前会检查什么？ | address / price / delivery time |
| What do you do while you are on the metro? | 你坐地铁时会做什么？ | read / listen to music / reply to messages |
| What will you do as soon as you get home today? | 你今天一到家会做什么？ | change clothes / eat / take a shower |
| What can you do until the rain stops? | 下雨停之前你可以做什么？ | wait in a cafe / read / call a friend |
