Past Simple & Present Perfect · 过去时与现在完成时

课程介绍

本周我们练习一般过去时和现在完成时，了解两种时态的用法，学会用它们谈论经历和变化，并应用到分享故事和日常对话中。

1. Warm-up 热身

EnglishChinese
Have you ever used a 快递 locker?你用过快递柜吗？
→ Yes, I have / No, not yet / Only for packages用过 / 还没 / 只用来收件
Is it easy to learn new apps?学新 App 容易吗？
→ Yes, very easy / Sometimes hard / I ask friends很容易 / 有时难 / 我问朋友

2. Useful Expressions 常用表达

Experience Check 经历

EnglishChineseExample
have tried / have used试过 / 用过I have used 美团 for years.
have been / have seen去过 / 见过I have been to that milk tea shop.
have ordered / have scanned点过 / 扫过She has ordered food online.

Finished Actions 已完成的动作（过去时）

EnglishChineseExample
I ate / I went我吃了 / 我去了I ate noodles for lunch.
I paid / I sent我付了 / 我发了I sent the file yesterday.
I called / I picked我打了电话 / 我取了I picked up the package.

Changes Noticed 变化

EnglishChineseExample
has changed / has moved变了 / 搬了The shop has moved to the corner.
has closed / has opened关了 / 开了A new café has opened downstairs.
has gotten / has become变得The weather has gotten cooler.

Recent Events 最近发生

EnglishChineseExample
just arrived / just finished刚到 / 刚完成I just finished my report.
just paid / just left刚付 / 刚走He just left the office.
just saw / just got刚看到 / 刚拿到I just got the delivery.

3. Sentence Starters 句型开头

EnglishChineseKeywords
I ______ a shared bike this morning.今天早上我……共享单车。rode / have ridden / ride
She ______ a voice message on WeChat.她在微信上……语音。sent / has sent / sends
______ you ever ordered food on 美团?你在美团上点过餐吗？Have / Did / Do
