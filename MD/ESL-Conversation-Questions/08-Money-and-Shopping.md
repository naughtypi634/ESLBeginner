# 08 金钱消费 Money and Shopping

## 01 Saving Money | 存钱

1. Do you save money every month? | 你每个月存钱吗？
2. How much of your income do you save? | 你的收入会存下多少？
3. What are you saving for now? | 你现在存钱是为了什么？
4. Where do you keep your savings? | 你的存款放在哪里？
5. Did your parents teach you to save? | 父母教过你存钱吗？
6. Is it easy to save money in a big city? | 在大城市存钱容易吗？
7. What do you do when you have no money left at the end of the month? | 月底没钱了你会怎么办？
8. What is the best age to start saving? | 什么年龄开始存钱最好？
9. Have you ever saved up for something big? | 你为买大件存过钱吗？
10. What is worth spending money on? | 什么钱值得花？
