# 09 学习成长 Learning and Growth

## 01 Learning English | 学英语

1. How long have you been learning English? | 你学英语多久了？
2. Why are you learning English? | 你为什么学英语？
3. Which is harder, speaking or listening? | 说和听，哪个更难？
4. How do you practice speaking? | 你怎么练口语？
5. Do you watch shows or videos in English? | 你看英语节目或视频吗？
6. How many new words do you learn in a week? | 你一周学几个新词？
7. Do you use an app to learn English? | 你用软件学英语吗？
8. What is the hardest part of English for you? | 英语对你来说最难的是什么？
9. Do you feel shy when you speak English? | 说英语你会害羞吗？
10. What would help you learn faster? | 怎样能让你学得更快？

## 02 Reading | 读书

1. How many books do you read in a year? | 你一年读几本书？
2. Do you read paper books or e-books? | 你读纸质书还是电子书？
3. When do you find time to read? | 你什么时候有空读书？
4. What kind of books do you like? | 你喜欢什么类型的书？
5. What was the last book you read? | 你最近读的一本书是什么？
6. Do you read before bed? | 你睡前看书吗？
7. Where do you buy books? | 你在哪里买书？
8. Do you borrow books from a library? | 你去图书馆借书吗？
9. Do you finish every book you start? | 每本书你都会读完吗？
10. Which book would you recommend to a friend? | 你会推荐朋友读什么书？

## 03 Exams | 考试

1. What exams have you taken? | 你考过什么试？
2. Do you get nervous before an exam? | 考试前你会紧张吗？
3. How do you prepare for a big exam? | 大考前你怎么准备？
4. Have you ever studied all night before an exam? | 你考前通宵复习过吗？
5. What do you do if you fail an exam? | 考试没过你会怎么办？
6. Are exams a good way to test people? | 考试是好的考核方式吗？
7. Do Chinese students take too many exams? | 中国学生考试太多了吗？
8. Which exam would you like to pass next? | 你接下来想过哪门考试？
9. What do you do the night before an exam? | 考前一晚你会做什么？
10. What was your best or worst exam result? | 你考得最好或最差的一次是什么？

## 04 University | 上大学

1. Did you go to university? | 你上过大学吗？
2. What did you study? | 你学的什么专业？
3. How did you choose your major? | 你怎么选的专业？
4. Did you live on campus? | 你住校吗？
5. What was the best part of university life? | 大学生活最好的是什么？
6. How much does university cost in China? | 在中国上大学要多少钱？
7. Would you study abroad if you could? | 如果可以，你会出国留学吗？
8. Do you still use what you learned at university? | 大学学的东西你现在还用得上吗？
9. What advice would you give a new student? | 你会给新生什么建议？
10. Would you go back to school now? | 你现在还会回去读书吗？

## 05 Learning New Skills | 学新技能

1. What new skill do you want to learn? | 你想学什么新技能？
2. What skill did you learn recently? | 你最近学会了什么技能？
3. Do you learn better alone or in a class? | 你自学更好还是上课更好？
4. How much time can you give to learning? | 你能花多少时间学习？
5. Would you pay for a class? | 你会花钱报班吗？
6. Which skill is useful for your work? | 什么技能对工作有用？
7. Is it harder to learn new things as you get older? | 年纪大了学新东西更难吗？
8. Which skill do you wish you had? | 你希望自己会什么技能？
9. Have you ever given up on learning something? | 你有过学一半放弃吗？
10. Which skill should everyone learn? | 什么技能每个人都该学？

## 06 Mistakes and Failure | 犯错失败

1. What was your biggest mistake at work? | 你工作上最大的失误是什么？
2. How do you feel when you make a mistake? | 犯错时你什么感觉？
3. Do you admit your mistakes easily? | 你容易承认错误吗？
4. What did you learn from a failure? | 你从失败中学到了什么？
5. Have you ever failed at something important? | 你在重要的事情上失败过吗？
6. How do you deal with failure? | 你怎么面对失败？
7. Do you worry about making mistakes in English? | 说英语时你怕犯错吗？
8. What do you say when you make a mistake at work? | 工作中出错你会怎么说？
9. Do people in China talk about failure openly? | 在中国，人们会公开谈失败吗？
10. What would you tell someone who just failed? | 对刚失败的人你会说什么？

## 07 Building Habits | 习惯养成

1. What good habit do you have? | 你有什么好习惯？
2. What bad habit do you want to change? | 你想改掉什么坏习惯？
3. How long does it take to build a new habit? | 养成一个新习惯要多久？
4. Have you ever kept a habit for a whole year? | 你有过坚持一年的习惯吗？
5. What habit do you want to start this month? | 这个月你想开始什么习惯？
6. How do you remember to do something every day? | 你怎么提醒自己每天做？
7. Do you use an app to track habits? | 你用软件打卡吗？
8. What makes you give up a new habit? | 什么让你放弃新习惯？
9. Do you have a morning routine? | 你有早晨固定流程吗？
10. Which habit changed your life? | 哪个习惯改变了你的生活？

## 08 Time Management | 时间管理

1. Are you usually busy? | 你平时忙吗？
2. What takes up most of your time? | 你的时间主要花在什么上？
3. Do you make a to-do list? | 你会列待办清单吗？
4. How do you plan your day? | 你怎么安排一天？
5. Do you often feel there is not enough time? | 你常觉得时间不够吗？
6. What do you waste time on? | 你会把时间浪费在什么上？
7. Are you often late for things? | 你常迟到吗？
8. How do you deal with a busy week? | 忙碌的一周你怎么应对？
9. Do you work better in the morning or at night? | 你早上效率高还是晚上？
10. What would you do with one free day? | 多出一天空闲你会做什么？

## 09 Goals and Plans | 目标计划

3. Do you write your goals down? | 你会把目标写下来吗？

5. How do you reach a difficult goal? | 你怎么达成难的目标？
6. Have you ever given up on a goal? | 你有过放弃目标吗？
7. Do you plan five years ahead? | 你会规划五年后吗？
8. Who helps you stay on track? | 谁会督促你？
9. Which small goal could you finish this week? | 这周你能完成什么小目标？
10. What do you want to change about your life? | 你想改变生活的什么？

9. How much time do you give to your hobby each week? | 你每周花多少时间在爱好上？
10. Is a hobby worth the time and money? | 爱好值得花时间和钱吗？
