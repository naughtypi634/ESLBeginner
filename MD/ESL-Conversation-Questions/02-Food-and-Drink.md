# 02 吃喝 Food and Drink

## 01 Hometown Food | 家乡菜

1. What food is your hometown famous for? | 你家乡最有名的食物是什么？
2. What dish do you miss most when you are away? | 离家在外时，你最想念哪道菜？
3. Who cooks the best food in your family? | 你家谁做饭最好吃？
4. What food do you eat only at home? | 有什么食物你只在家里吃？
5. Is your hometown food spicy, sweet, or salty? | 你家乡菜是辣、甜还是咸？
6. Can you find your hometown food in the city where you live now? | 在你现在住的城市，能找到家乡菜吗？
7. What do you cook when you feel homesick? | 想家的时候你会做什么吃？
8. What dish do you always eat at Chinese New Year? | 过年你一定会吃哪道菜？
9. Do your friends from other places like your hometown food? | 外地朋友喜欢你家乡菜吗？
10. What food from your childhood do you still remember? | 小时候吃过的什么食物你现在还记得？

## 02 Coffee and Milk Tea | 咖啡奶茶

1. Do you drink coffee, milk tea, or neither? | 你喝咖啡、奶茶，还是都不喝？
2. How many cups do you have in a week? | 你一周喝几杯？
3. What is your usual order? | 你平时都点什么？
4. How much do you spend on drinks in a month? | 你一个月花多少钱买饮料？
5. Do you make coffee at home or buy it outside? | 你在家做咖啡还是外面买？
6. Do you ever buy milk tea for your coworkers? | 你会给同事带奶茶吗？
7. What do you do when the coffee shop line is long? | 咖啡店排队很长时，你会怎么办？
8. Can you sleep well after coffee in the afternoon? | 下午喝咖啡，你晚上睡得好吗？
9. What is the strangest drink you have tried? | 你喝过最奇怪的饮料是什么？
10. What is your favorite drink for cold days? | 天冷时你最爱的饮品是什么？

10. What is a mild dish for people who cannot eat spicy food? | 不吃辣的人可以点什么菜？
