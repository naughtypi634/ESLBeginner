# 01 日常生活 Everyday Life

## 01 Morning Routines | 早起作息

1. What time do you usually get up on weekdays? | 工作日你通常几点起床？
2. Do you use an alarm clock, or does your phone wake you up? | 你用闹钟还是手机叫醒你？
3. What is the first thing you do after you get up? | 起床后你做的第一件事是什么？
4. Do you eat breakfast at home or buy it on the way? | 早饭你在家吃还是路上买？
5. How long does it take you to get ready in the morning? | 早上洗漱准备要花你多久？
6. Are you a morning person or a night person? | 你是早起型还是夜猫子？
7. What time do you go to bed on weekdays? | 工作日你几点睡觉？
8. Do you check your phone right after you wake up? | 醒来后你会马上看手机吗？
9. What makes you get up late? | 什么情况会让你起晚？
10. What would you do with one extra hour in the morning? | 早上多出一个小时，你会做什么？

4. Do you read the reviews before you order from a new place? | 点新店之前，你会看评价吗？
5. What dish do you order most often? | 你点得最多的一道菜是什么？

7. Do you eat takeout at your desk or at a table? | 你在工位上吃外卖，还是在桌上吃？
8. What do you do when your order arrives cold? | 外卖送到是凉的，你会怎么办？

10. What food would you never order for delivery? | 什么菜你绝对不会点外卖？
