# 03 工作职场 Work and Career

## 01 Working Overtime | 加班

1. How often do you work overtime? | 你多久加一次班？
2. What time do you usually leave the office? | 你通常几点下班？
3. Do you get paid for overtime? | 加班有加班费吗？
4. What do you do when your boss asks you to stay late? | 老板让你加班时，你会怎么做？
5. How do you feel after a long day at work? | 上了一天长班，你什么感觉？
6. Is working overtime common in your city? | 在你所在的城市，加班常见吗？
7. What do you eat when you work late? | 加班时你吃什么？
8. How do you relax after overtime? | 加班后你怎么放松？
9. Would you change jobs to work fewer hours? | 为了少加班，你会换工作吗？
10. What is the latest you have ever stayed at work? | 你最晚加班到几点？

## 02 Job Interviews | 面试

1. When was your last job interview? | 你上次面试是什么时候？
2. How do you prepare for an interview? | 你怎么准备面试？
3. What do you wear to an interview? | 面试你穿什么？
4. What is the hardest interview question? | 最难的面试问题是什么？
5. Do you get nervous before an interview? | 面试前你会紧张吗？
6. Have you ever had an online interview? | 你参加过线上面试吗？
7. What do you say when they ask about your weakness? | 被问到缺点时，你怎么回答？
8. How long do interviews usually last in China? | 在国内面试一般要多久？
9. What questions do you ask at the end? | 最后你会问什么问题？
10. What was your best or worst interview? | 你最好或最糟的一次面试是什么？

## 03 Looking for a Job | 找工作

1. How did you find your current job? | 你现在的工作是怎么找到的？
2. Which app or website do you use to look for jobs? | 你用哪个软件或网站找工作？
3. How many resumes do you send before you get a reply? | 你要投多少份简历才有回音？
4. What do you write in your resume? | 你的简历里写什么？
5. Would you move to another city for a job? | 为了工作，你会搬到别的城市吗？
6. What matters more, salary or interest? | 工资和兴趣，哪个更重要？
7. How long did it take you to find your last job? | 你上次找工作花了多久？
8. Do you ask friends to help you find a job? | 你会托朋友帮忙介绍工作吗？
9. What job did you want when you were a child? | 你小时候想做什么工作？
10. What is your dream job? | 你理想的工作是什么？

## 04 Bosses and Coworkers | 老板同事

1. What makes a good boss? | 什么样的老板是好老板？
2. Do you eat lunch with your coworkers? | 你和同事一起吃午饭吗？
3. How do you get along with your team? | 你和团队相处得怎么样？
4. What do you do when a coworker asks for help? | 同事找你帮忙时，你会怎么做？
5. Have you ever had a difficult boss? | 你遇到过难相处的老板吗？
6. Do you talk about your salary with coworkers? | 你会和同事聊工资吗？
7. What do you do when someone takes credit for your work? | 别人抢你的功劳，你会怎么办？
8. Is your boss easy to talk to? | 你的老板好沟通吗？
9. Do you have a work friend you trust? | 你有信得过的职场朋友吗？
10. What would you tell a new coworker on their first day? | 新同事第一天来，你会跟他说什么？

## 05 Office Life | 办公室日常

1. What time does your workday start? | 你几点上班？
2. Do you have your own desk or share one? | 你有自己的工位还是和别人共用？
3. How many meetings do you have in a week? | 你一周开几次会？
4. What do you keep on your desk? | 你工位上放着什么？
5. Do you drink coffee or tea at work? | 上班时你喝咖啡还是茶？
6. Is your office quiet or noisy? | 你的办公室安静还是吵？
7. How do you take breaks during the day? | 上班时你怎么休息？
8. Does your company have a dress code? | 你们公司有着装要求吗？
9. What do you do when you have nothing to do at work? | 上班没事做时你会干什么？
10. What would make your office a better place? | 怎样能让你的办公室更好？

## 06 Changing Jobs | 跳槽

1. How long have you worked at your current company? | 你在现在的公司工作多久了？
2. Have you ever changed jobs? | 你换过工作吗？
3. Why do people change jobs? | 人们为什么换工作？
4. How many job changes is too many? | 换几次工作算太频繁？
5. What would make you leave your job? | 什么会让你辞职？
6. Is it easy to find a new job now? | 现在找工作容易吗？
7. Do you tell your boss before you leave? | 离职前你会告诉老板吗？
8. Do you keep in touch with coworkers after you leave? | 离职后你还和同事联系吗？
9. Would you take a lower salary for a better boss? | 为了好老板，你会接受更低的工资吗？
10. What is the best age to change careers? | 什么年龄转行最合适？

## 07 Salary and Raises | 工资加薪

1. Are you happy with your salary? | 你对现在的工资满意吗？
2. How often do you get a raise? | 你多久涨一次工资？
3. Do you know how much your friends earn? | 你知道朋友挣多少吗？
4. How do you ask for a raise? | 你会怎么提加薪？
5. What do you spend your salary on first? | 发了工资你先花在哪？
6. Is it polite to talk about money at work? | 在职场谈钱合适吗？
7. What is a good salary in your city? | 在你所在的城市，多少工资算不错？
8. Do you get a bonus at the end of the year? | 你们年底有奖金吗？
9. Would you work more hours for more money? | 为了多挣钱，你愿意多干活吗？
10. How much of your salary do you save? | 你的工资会存下多少？

## 08 Working from Home | 居家办公

1. Do you ever work from home? | 你居家办公吗？
2. Do you like working from home or at the office? | 你喜欢在家办公还是在公司？
3. What is hard about working from home? | 居家办公难在哪？
4. Where do you sit when you work from home? | 居家办公时你坐哪儿？
5. Do you change clothes when you work from home? | 居家办公你会换衣服吗？
6. How do you stay focused at home? | 在家你怎么保持专注？
7. Do you take more breaks at home? | 在家你休息得更多吗？
8. How do you talk to your team when you are at home? | 在家你怎么和团队沟通？
9. Would you work from home every day? | 你愿意每天居家办公吗？
10. What is the best part of working from home? | 居家办公最好的地方是什么？

## 09 Career Plans | 职业规划

1. What do you want to be doing in five years? | 五年后你想在做什么？
2. Do you have a career plan? | 你有职业规划吗？
3. What skill do you want to learn for work? | 为了工作，你想学什么技能？
4. Do you want to be a manager one day? | 你想当管理者吗？
5. What did you want to do when you started working? | 刚工作时你想做什么？
6. Does your job match what you studied in school? | 你的工作和大学专业对口吗？
7. What would you do if money were not a problem? | 如果不缺钱，你会做什么？
8. Who is your role model at work? | 职场上你的榜样是谁？
9. Do you plan to work in another city or country? | 你打算去别的城市或国家工作吗？
10. What is one career goal for this year? | 你今年的职业目标是什么？

## 10 Side Hustles | 副业

1. Do you have a side job? | 你有副业吗？
2. Which side jobs are popular in China now? | 现在中国流行什么副业？
3. How much time do you spend on a side job? | 你在副业上花多少时间？
4. Would you sell things online? | 你会做网店吗？
5. Does your company allow side jobs? | 你们公司允许做副业吗？
6. What is a good side job for a busy person? | 忙的人适合做什么副业？
7. Have you ever made money from a hobby? | 你有靠爱好挣过钱吗？
8. Is a side job worth the lost free time? | 副业值得牺牲空闲时间吗？
9. What would you do if your side job earned more? | 如果副业挣得更多，你会怎样？
10. Which side job would you never try? | 什么副业你绝对不会做？
