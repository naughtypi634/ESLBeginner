# 07 健康身心 Health and Body

## 01 Exercise | 运动

1. How often do you exercise? | 你多久运动一次？
2. What kind of exercise do you like? | 你喜欢什么运动？
3. What time of day do you exercise? | 你一天中什么时候运动？
4. Do you walk a lot every day? | 你每天走路多吗？
5. How do you feel after you exercise? | 运动完你什么感觉？
6. What stops you from exercising more? | 什么让你没法多运动？
7. Do you exercise alone or with others? | 你一个人运动还是和别人一起？
8. Which sport is good for busy people? | 什么运动适合忙的人？
9. Did you play sports as a child? | 你小时候运动吗？
10. What is a simple way to start exercising? | 想开始运动，最简单的办法是什么？
