# 04 居住城市 Home and City

## 01 Renting an Apartment | 租房

1. Do you rent or own your home? | 你的房子是租的还是买的？
2. How did you find your apartment? | 你是怎么找到现在这套房的？
3. How much is the rent in your area? | 你那边的房租是多少？
4. What do you look for in an apartment? | 你租房最看重什么？
5. How long does it take you to get to work from home? | 从家到公司要多久？
6. Have you ever had a problem with a landlord? | 你和房东有过矛盾吗？
7. What is included in your rent? | 你的房租包含什么？
8. Would you rather live alone or share? | 你更愿意独居还是合租？
9. How often does the rent go up? | 房租多久涨一次？
10. What would your dream apartment look like? | 你理想的房子是什么样的？

## 02 Buying a Home | 买房

1. Do you want to buy a home? | 你想买房吗？
2. How old were you when you first thought about buying? | 你第一次考虑买房是几岁？
3. Is it hard to buy a home in your city? | 在你所在的城市，买房难吗？
4. Who helps young people buy a home in China? | 在中国，谁帮年轻人买房？
5. What matters more, the location or the size? | 位置和面积，哪个更重要？
6. How long does it take to pay off a home loan? | 房贷要还多久？
7. Would you buy an old apartment or a new one? | 你会买老房子还是新房？
8. Do you prefer a high floor or a low floor? | 你喜欢高层还是低层？
9. What do you need to check before you buy? | 买房前要检查什么？
10. Is buying a home still a good idea? | 现在买房还是个好选择吗？

## 03 Roommates | 室友

1. Have you ever lived with a roommate? | 你和别人合租过吗？
2. How did you find your roommate? | 你是怎么找到室友的？
3. What rules do roommates need? | 室友之间需要什么规矩？
4. Who cleans the shared spaces? | 公共区域谁打扫？
5. What do you do if a roommate is too noisy? | 室友太吵你会怎么办？
6. Do you share food with your roommate? | 你会和室友分吃的吗？
7. What is the worst thing a roommate can do? | 室友什么行为最让你受不了？
8. Would you live with a stranger? | 你愿意和陌生人合租吗？
9. How do you split the bills? | 你们怎么分摊费用？
10. What makes a good roommate? | 什么样的室友算好室友？

## 04 Moving | 搬家

1. How many times have you moved? | 你搬过几次家？
2. When was your last move? | 你上次搬家是什么时候？
3. Do you hire movers or ask friends? | 你请搬家公司还是找朋友帮忙？
4. What is the hardest part of moving? | 搬家最难的是什么？
5. How long does it take you to pack? | 打包要多久？
6. What do you throw away when you move? | 搬家时你会扔掉什么？
7. How do you choose a new place? | 你怎么挑新住处？
8. Do you feel sad when you leave an old home? | 离开旧家你会难过吗？
9. How long does it take to feel at home in a new place? | 在新家要多久才住得惯？
10. What would you never leave behind? | 有什么东西你搬家绝对不会扔？

## 05 Neighbors | 邻居

1. Do you know your neighbors? | 你认识你的邻居吗？
2. How often do you talk to them? | 你多久和他们说一次话？
3. What makes a good neighbor? | 什么样的邻居算好邻居？
4. Have you ever had a noisy neighbor? | 你遇到过吵闹的邻居吗？
5. What do you do when a neighbor makes too much noise? | 邻居太吵你会怎么办？
6. Do neighbors help each other where you live? | 在你那里，邻居之间互相帮忙吗？
7. Have you ever borrowed something from a neighbor? | 你向邻居借过东西吗？
8. Do you give neighbors food during holidays? | 过节你会给邻居送吃的吗？
9. What would you do if a neighbor asked for a favor? | 邻居找你帮忙你会怎么做？
10. Would you move because of a neighbor? | 你会因为邻居而搬家吗？

## 06 Subways and Buses | 地铁公交

1. How do you usually get around your city? | 你在城市里通常怎么出行？
2. How long is your commute? | 你通勤要多久？
3. Do you take the subway or the bus more? | 你坐地铁多还是公交多？
4. What do you do on the subway? | 你在地铁上做什么？
5. Is the subway crowded at rush hour? | 高峰期的地铁挤吗？
6. How much do you spend on transport in a month? | 你一个月交通费多少？
7. Have you ever missed your stop? | 你坐过站吗？
8. Do you give your seat to older people? | 你会给老人让座吗？
9. What is the best way to travel in your city? | 在你那里，最好的出行方式是什么？
10. What would make public transport better? | 怎样能让公共交通更好？

## 07 Ride-Hailing | 打车

1. How often do you take a taxi or a ride-hailing car? | 你多久打一次车？
2. Which app do you use? | 你用哪个软件打车？
3. Do you talk to the driver? | 你会和司机聊天吗？
4. What do you do when the driver takes a long route? | 司机绕路你会怎么办？
5. How long do you usually wait for a car? | 你一般等车要多久？
6. Is it easy to get a car when it rains? | 下雨天好打车吗？
7. Have you ever left something in a car? | 你把东西落在车上过吗？
8. Do you take the front seat or the back seat? | 你坐副驾还是后排？
9. How much is a taxi compared with the subway? | 打车比坐地铁贵多少？
10. What was your best or worst ride? | 你最好或最糟的一次打车经历是什么？

## 08 City or Hometown | 大城市与老家

1. Do you live in your hometown now? | 你现在住在老家吗？
2. Why did you leave your hometown, or why did you stay? | 你为什么离开老家，或者为什么留下？
3. What do you like about big cities? | 大城市有什么好？
4. What do you miss about small towns? | 小地方有什么让你怀念？
5. Is life cheaper in your hometown? | 老家生活更便宜吗？
6. Where do your parents want you to live? | 你父母希望你住在哪里？
7. Would you move back home one day? | 你以后会回老家吗？
8. What is hard about living far from family? | 离家远最难的是什么？
9. Do young people in China prefer big cities now? | 现在中国的年轻人更喜欢大城市吗？
10. Where would you live if you could choose anywhere? | 如果随便选，你想住在哪里？

## 09 Neighborhood | 小区社区

1. What is your neighborhood like? | 你住的小区是什么样的？
2. What do you like about it? | 你喜欢它什么？
3. What would you change about it? | 你想改变它什么？
4. Is it easy to buy food near your home? | 你家附近买菜方便吗？
5. Is there a park or a gym nearby? | 附近有公园或健身房吗？
6. How do people in your neighborhood get packages? | 你们小区的快递怎么取？
7. Do you know the people at the front gate? | 你认识小区门口的人吗？
8. Is your neighborhood quiet at night? | 你们小区晚上安静吗？
9. What do children do in your neighborhood? | 小区里的孩子玩什么？
10. Would you recommend your neighborhood to a friend? | 你会推荐朋友住你的小区吗？

## 10 Decorating | 家装收拾

1. How would you describe your home style? | 你会怎么形容你家的风格？
2. Did you decorate your home yourself? | 你家是你自己装修的吗？
3. How much does it cost to decorate in China? | 在中国装修要花多少钱？
4. What color are the walls in your home? | 你家的墙是什么颜色？
5. What is your favorite thing in your home? | 家里你最喜欢哪样东西？
6. Do you like to buy things for your home? | 你喜欢给家里买东西吗？
7. How do you keep your home tidy? | 你怎么让家里保持整洁？
8. Would you rather have a big sofa or a big table? | 你更想要大沙发还是大桌子？
9. What plant or decoration do you have at home? | 你家养什么植物或摆什么装饰？
10. What would you change at home if you had more money? | 如果钱更多，你想改动家里的什么？
