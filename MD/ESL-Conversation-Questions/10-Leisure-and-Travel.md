# 10 休闲出行 Leisure and Travel

## 01 Travel | 旅游

1. How often do you travel? | 你多久旅游一次？
2. Where did you go on your last trip? | 你上次去哪里旅游了？
3. Do you travel alone or with others? | 你一个人旅游还是和别人一起？
4. How do you plan a trip? | 你怎么规划旅行？
5. Do you prefer the mountains or the beach? | 你更喜欢山里还是海边？
6. How much do you spend on a trip? | 一次旅行你花多少钱？
7. Do you take a lot of photos when you travel? | 旅游时你会拍很多照片吗？
8. What is the best place you have visited in China? | 你在国内去过最好的地方是哪里？
9. Would you travel abroad? Where? | 你会出国旅游吗？想去哪里？
10. What do you always take with you? | 你旅行一定会带什么？

## 02 Vacations | 假期

1. How many vacation days do you get? | 你有几天年假？
2. How do you use your vacation days? | 你怎么用年假？
3. Do you rest or travel on vacation? | 假期你会休息还是旅游？
4. What is a long vacation like in China? | 中国的长假是什么样的？
5. Do you like staying home on vacation? | 假期你喜欢待在家吗？
6. How do you feel when a vacation ends? | 假期结束你什么感觉？
7. Do you check work messages on vacation? | 假期你会看工作消息吗？
8. Where would you go with a month off? | 有一个月假你想去哪？
9. Do you travel during holidays or at other times? | 你节假日出行还是平时出行？
10. What is your perfect vacation? | 你理想的假期是什么样的？

## 03 Movies | 电影

1. How often do you watch movies? | 你多久看一次电影？
2. Do you go to the movie theater? | 你会去电影院吗？
3. What was the last movie you saw? | 你最近看的一部电影是什么？
4. What kind of movies do you like? | 你喜欢什么类型的电影？
5. Who do you watch movies with? | 你和谁一起看电影？
6. Do you watch movies in English? | 你看英语电影吗？
7. Do you read reviews before you watch? | 看之前你会看影评吗？
8. Have you ever walked out of a movie? | 你有过看电影中途离场吗？
9. Which movie makes you cry? | 什么电影会让你哭？
10. Which movie would you watch again? | 什么电影你会再看一遍？

## 05 TV Shows | 追剧

2. How many episodes do you watch at a time? | 你一次看几集？
3. Do you watch Chinese or foreign shows? | 你看国产剧还是外国剧？
4. How late do you stay up watching? | 追剧你会看到几点？
5. Do you watch shows while you eat? | 吃饭时你会看剧吗？

9. Have you ever watched a whole season in one day? | 你有过一天看完一季吗？
10. Which show would you recommend? | 你会推荐什么剧？

3. Is it hard to buy tickets? | 票好买吗？
4. Would you travel to another city for a concert? | 你会为了演唱会去外地吗？
5. Have you been to a museum recently? | 你最近去过博物馆吗？
6. Which exhibition would you like to see? | 你想看什么展览？
7. Do you go to these events alone or with friends? | 你一个人去还是和朋友一起？
8. What do you do when the show is sold out? | 票卖完了你会怎么办？
9. Are concerts better live or on video? | 演唱会是现场好还是看视频好？
10. What was the best event you have been to? | 你去过最好的活动是什么？
