# Citywalk · Places, Prepositions & Directions

## 课程介绍

本周我们练习城市漫步场景，掌握 26 个核心城市地点词，了解 in/on/at、next to/between/opposite 等位置介词和 into/out of、across/along/through 等方向介词的用法，学会用 There be 句型说清附近有什么、用问路指路的句子说清怎么走，并应用到约见面、找店铺、骑车通勤与周末逛街等日常场景中。

## 1. Prepositions of Place 位置介词

| 介词 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **in** 空间里面 | My power bank is **in** my backpack. | 充电宝在背包里。 |
| **on** 表面、屏幕、地图 | The meeting point is **on** the map. | 集合点在地图上。 |
| **at** 具体点位、门口 | Let's meet **at** Exit B of the subway station. | 我们在地铁站 B 口见。 |
| **in front of / behind** | The taxi stand is **in front of** the mall; the parking lot is **behind** it. | 出租车候车点在商场前面，停车场在后面。 |
| **next to / beside** | The ATM is **next to** the pharmacy door. | 取款机在药店门口旁边。 |
| **between / among** | The bank is **between** the bakery and the movie theater. | 银行在面包店和电影院之间。 |
| **opposite / across from** | The gym is **opposite** the park; the bus stop is **across from** my building. | 健身房在公园对面；公交站就在我楼下对面。 |
| **under / over** | We waited **under** the pedestrian bridge. | 我们在人行天桥下等。 |
| **above / below** | The cafe is **above** the bookstore; the supermarket is one floor **below**. | 咖啡店在书店上面；超市在下面一层。 |
| **near / far from** | My place is **near** the subway station but **far from** the office. | 我家离地铁站近，但离公司远。 |

## 3. There be 句型

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| There is + 单数 | There is a supermarket **near my home**. | 我家附近有一家超市。 |
| There are + 复数 | There are two subway stations **near the office**. | 公司附近有两个地铁站。 |
| 否定 | There isn't a bank **on this street**. | 这条街上没有银行。 |
| 疑问 | **Is there** a movie theater **near here**? | 这附近有电影院吗？ |

## 4. Prepositions of Movement 方向介词

| 介词 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **into / out of** | She walked **into** the pharmacy and came **out of** it with a cold drink. | 她走进药店，拿着一瓶冷饮出来。 |
| **on / off** | Get **on** Line 2 at People's Square and get **off** at Nanjing Road. | 在人民广场上 2 号线，南京东路下。 |
| **up / down** | Walk **up** this street, then **down** the stairs. | 沿这条街往上走，然后下楼梯。 |

| 介词 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| **across / along** | We walked **across** the square and then **along** the river. | 我们横穿广场，然后沿江走。 |
| **over / under** | The bike lane goes **over** the pedestrian bridge; cars go **under** it. | 自行车道走天桥上面，汽车走下面。 |
| **towards / away from** | The delivery rider is riding **towards** our building. | 外卖骑手正朝我们楼骑过来。 |
| **through** | Walk **through** the underpass and take Exit 3. | 走地下通道，从 3 号口出来。 |
| **past / around** | Walk **past** the pharmacy, then **around** the fountain. | 走过药店，然后绕过喷泉。 |
| **across** | Walk **across** the road at the crosswalk. | 走斑马线过马路。 |

## 5. Directions 问路与指路

| 场合 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| 问附近有没有 | Excuse me, **is there** a subway station **near here**? | 请问，这附近有地铁站吗？ |
| 问怎么走 | Excuse me, **how do I get to** the City Museum? | 请问，去市博物馆怎么走？ |
| 问时间、出口 | **How long does it take on foot**? **Which exit** should I take? | 走路要多久？我该走哪个出口？ |
| 直走 | **Go straight** along this street for about 300 meters. | 沿这条街直走约三百米。 |
| 转弯 | **Turn left** at the second traffic light. | 在第二个红绿灯左转。 |
| 经过地标 | Walk **past** the pharmacy; it's **right after** the bakery. | 走过药店，就在面包店再过去一点。 |
| 过马路、穿通道 | **Cross** the road **at** the crosswalk, then go **through** the underpass. | 走斑马线过马路，然后走地下通道。 |
| 在左右手边 | It's **on your left**, **opposite** the post office. | 在你左手边，邮局对面。 |
| 拐个弯、距离 | It's **just around the corner** — **you can't miss it**. About **a five-minute walk**. | 拐个弯就是——不会找不到的。走路大约五分钟。 |
| 在楼上 | Go **up** to the fifth floor; the gym is **next to** the elevator. | 上五楼，健身房在电梯旁边。 |
| 建议 | You can also **ride a shared bike** — it's faster. | 也可以骑共享单车，更快。 |


## 6. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| What is your favorite place to walk in your city? Why? | 你最喜欢在城里的哪个地方散步？为什么？ | riverside / old street / park |
| What is between your home and your office? | 你家和公司之间有什么？ | bank / bakery / crosswalk |
| Which is better for a citywalk: subway or shared bike? | 城市漫步坐地铁还是骑共享单车更好？ | faster / cheaper / bike lane |
| Tell us about a time you got lost. | 说说你迷路的一次经历。 | wrong exit / past / asked |

### 造句练习

| 提示 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| two gyms / this neighborhood | There are two gyms in this neighborhood. | 这个片区有两家健身房。 |
| convenience store / on the corner | There is a convenience store on the corner. | 路口有一家便利店。 |
| any crosswalks / on this street? | Are there any crosswalks on this street? | 这条街上有斑马线吗？ |
| bike lane / along the river | There is a bike lane along the river. | 沿江有一条自行车道。 |
| no parking / in front of the mall | There is no parking in front of the mall. | 商场前面不能停车。 |
