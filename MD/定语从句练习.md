# 定语从句练习

定语从句渐进练习（一）
第一阶梯
基础句式
不修饰，只陈述。
1. 我换了新手机。
(I got a new phone.)
2. 外卖还没到。
(The delivery hasn't arrived yet.)
3. 这个视频很火。
(This video is really popular.)
4. 他是我同事。
(He is my coworker.)
5. 这杯咖啡凉了。
(This coffee is cold.)
6. 我养了一只猫。
(I adopted a cat.)
7. 我买了辆电动车。
(I bought an e-bike.)
8. 我去健身房了。
(I went to the gym.)
9. 她发了篇小红书。
(She posted a Xiaohongshu note.)
10. 我在听播客。
(I'm listening to a podcast.)
第二阶梯
加形容词
名词前面加一个形容词。
1. 我换了台很轻的手机。
(I got a very light phone.)
2. 我点了份特辣的外卖。
(I ordered a super spicy delivery.)
3. 她发了条搞笑的视频。
(She posted a funny video.)
4. 我有个靠谱的同事。
(I have a reliable coworker.)
5. 我买了杯冰的咖啡。
(I ordered an iced coffee.)
6. 他捡了只流浪的猫。
(He rescued a stray cat.)
7. 我租了辆共享电动车。
(I rented a shared e-bike.)
8. 我去了家24小时的健身房。
(I went to a 24-hour gym.)
9. 她写了篇爆款的小红书。
(She wrote a viral Xiaohongshu post.)
10. 我订阅了个冷门的播客。
(I subscribed to a niche podcast.)

定语从句渐进练习（二）
第三阶梯
定语从句 — 作主语（who / that + 动词）
把形容词换成句子。who / that 后面直接跟动词。
1. 我换了台能卫星通话的手机。
(I got a phone that supports satellite calls.)
2. 我点了份满减后才15块的外卖。
(I ordered a delivery that cost only 15 yuan after discounts.)
3. 她发了条一夜百万播放的视频。
(She posted a video that got a million views overnight.)
4. 我有个天天带饭不点外卖的同事。
(I have a coworker who brings lunch and never orders delivery.)
5. 我买了杯用券后才9块9的咖啡。
(I ordered a coffee that was only 9.9 yuan with a coupon.)
6. 他捡了只特别粘人的猫。
(He rescued a cat that is super clingy.)
7. 我租了辆满电能跑80公里的电动车。
(I rented an e-bike that goes 80 km on a full charge.)
8. 我去了家教练不推销课的健身房。
(I went to a gym that has no pushy trainers.)
9. 她写了篇三天涨粉两万的小红书。
(She wrote a Xiaohongshu post that gained 20k followers in three days.)
10. 我订阅了个每期都请大佬的播客。
(I subscribed to a podcast that invites industry leaders every episode.)
第四阶梯
定语从句 — 作主语 · 加细节
从句写长一点，加入副词或并列结构。
1. 我换了台拍视频特别稳、夜景也清楚的手机。
(I got a phone that shoots steady video and works well at night.)
2. 我点了份用券满减后连配送费都免了的外卖。
(I ordered a delivery that ended up free after all the coupons and promos.)
3. 她发了条火到连我爸妈都刷到了的视频。
(She posted a video that even my parents saw on their feed.)
4. 我有个自己带饭还老劝我也别点外卖的同事。
(I have a coworker who packs lunch and always tells me to stop ordering delivery.)
5. 我买了杯味道跟某幸一模一样但便宜一半的咖啡。
(I ordered a coffee that tastes exactly like Luckin's but costs half as much.)
6. 他捡了只粘人粘到连上厕所都要跟着的猫。
(He rescued a cat that follows him everywhere, even to the bathroom.)
7. 我租了辆扫码就能骑、还车不用找固定桩的电动车。
(I rented an e-bike that you unlock with a QR code and can park anywhere.)
8. 我去了家24小时营业、半夜撸铁也没人管的健身房。
(I went to a gym that's open 24/7 and nobody cares if you lift at midnight.)
9. 她写了篇看完让人立刻想下单的小红书。
(She wrote a Xiaohongshu post that makes you want to buy it immediately.)
10. 我订阅了个每集20分钟、正好通勤听完的播客。
(I subscribed to a podcast whose episodes are 20 minutes, perfect for my commute.)

定语从句渐进练习（三）
第五阶梯
定语从句 — 作宾语（that / whom 可省略）
从句修饰宾语。that / whom 后面跟的是人。
1. 我刚换的那台手机拍照巨好。
(The phone (that) I just got takes amazing photos.)
2. 你点的外卖到了，在门口。
(The delivery (that) you ordered is here at the door.)
3. 她昨天发的那条视频我看了五遍。
(I watched the video (that) she posted yesterday five times.)
4. 我跟你说的那个同事今天又带饭了。
(The coworker (whom) I told you about brought lunch again.)
5. 你给我带的那杯咖啡太好喝了。
(The coffee (that) you brought me was so good.)
6. 他捡的那只猫现在胖了一圈。
(The cat (that) he rescued has gained so much weight.)
7. 我刚租的那辆电动车没电了。
(The e-bike (that) I just rented ran out of battery.)
8. 我常去的那家健身房今天关门。
(The gym (that) I usually go to is closed today.)
9. 她写的那篇小红书被官方推荐了。
(The Xiaohongshu post (that) she wrote got featured.)
10. 你推荐的那个播客我听了，很棒。
(The podcast (that) you recommended was great.)
第六阶梯
定语从句 — 作宾语 · 加细节
从句写长一点，开头句式变一变。
1. 我上个月在拼多多百亿补贴买的那台手机又降了200。
(The phone (that) I bought last month on Pinduoduo already dropped another 200 yuan.)
2. 你半小时前点的那份外卖还在门口，都凉透了。
(The delivery (that) you ordered half an hour ago is still outside, completely cold.)
3. 她上周随手拍的那条视频居然被官方推荐了。
(The video (that) she casually filmed last week somehow got recommended by the platform.)
4. 我跟你提过那个天天带饭还考了营养师证的同事今天辞职了。
(The coworker (whom) I mentioned who packs lunch and got a nutritionist license quit today.)
5. 你上次安利我的那款咖啡我已经回购三次了。
(I've already reordered the coffee (that) you recommended to me three times.)
6. 他从楼下捡的那只猫现在胖得跟球一样。
(The cat (that) he found downstairs is now round as a ball.)
7. 我扫码租的那辆电动车骑到一半没电了。
(The e-bike (that) I rented with a QR code died halfway through my ride.)
8. 我办了三年的那家健身房上个月跑路了。
(The gym (that) I had a three-year membership at suddenly shut down last month.)
9. 她花了一周写的那篇小红书只有14个赞。
(The Xiaohongshu post (that) she spent a week on got only 14 likes.)
10. 你上次说听了就停不下来的那个播客我入坑了。
(I got hooked on the podcast (that) you said was totally addictive.)

定语从句渐进练习（四）
第七阶梯
关系副词 — WHERE 地点
修饰地点。where = 在那里。
1. 这是我每天买咖啡的那家店。
(This is the shop where I buy coffee every day.)
2. 我换乘的那个地铁站终于装空调了。
(The subway station where I transfer finally installed AC.)
3. 我周末常去的那个共享办公室涨价了。
(The coworking space where I go on weekends raised prices.)
4. 大妈们跳广场舞的那个公园装了隔音墙。
(The park where aunties dance at night got soundproof walls.)
5. 我妈买菜的那家菜市场要拆了。
(The wet market where my mom shops is closing down.)
6. 那家可以坐着看一整天书的书店搬走了。
(The bookstore where you could sit and read all day moved away.)
7. 我们团建去的那家火锅店巨好吃。
(The hotpot place where we had our team dinner is amazing.)
8. 我考研时天天泡的那个图书馆现在要预约了。
(The library where I studied for my exams now needs a reservation.)
9. 我每次停电动车都找不到位的那个地铁口终于画了停车区。
(The subway exit where I could never park my e-bike finally has a parking zone.)
10. 我们晚上常去吹风的那个天台被封了。
(The rooftop where we used to chill at night has been locked up.)
第八阶梯
关系副词 — WHEN 时间 & WHY 原因
上 5 句修饰时间，下 5 句修饰原因。
1. 我还记得第一次用AI写周报的那天。
(I still remember the day when I first used AI to write my report.)
2. 地铁停运我走路回家的那个晚上太难忘了。
(The night when the subway stopped and I walked home was unforgettable.)
3. 发工资的那天是我每个月最开心的时候。
(Payday is the day when I am happiest every month.)
4. 周五下班走出办公室的那一刻最爽。
(The moment when I walk out of the office on Friday feels the best.)
5. 我还记得共享单车刚出现的那一年。
(I still remember the year when shared bikes first appeared.)
6. 这就是我不用美团只用饿了么的原因。
(This is the reason why I use Ele.me instead of Meituan.)
7. 你知道他从来不点外卖的原因吗？
(Do you know the reason why he never orders delivery?)
8. 房租太贵是我搬去郊区的唯一原因。
(High rent is the only reason why I moved to the suburbs.)
9. 这就是我一直不删小红书的原因。
(This is the reason why I never delete Xiaohongshu.)
10. 没人知道他突然离职的真正原因。
(Nobody knows the real reason why he suddenly quit.)
