# 美国签证面试问答（B1/B2 旅游 & F-1 学生）—— 修正版（A1 水平 + 过签策略）

> **使用说明**：所有回答已简化为 A1 初级英语，并刻意强化“回国约束力”（工作、家庭、财产）。请熟记回答要点，面试时自然、自信地说出。

---

## B1/B2 旅游签证

| 问题 | 推荐回答（A1 水平） | 策略要点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What's the purpose of your trip?** | I go to the US for tourism. I want to see New York and Los Angeles. After my trip, I will go back to China. | 明确旅游目的，并立即强调“会回来”。 |
| **How long will you stay?** | I will stay for 14 days. I have only 2 weeks of holiday from my job. Then I must go back to work. | 用“假期只有两周”强调必须回国上班。 |
| **Which cities will you visit?** | I will visit New York and Los Angeles. I plan to see the Statue of Liberty and Hollywood. | 提到具体景点，让行程显得真实。 |
| **When will you leave China?** | I will leave in October. My flight is on October 10th. | 简单给出日期。 |
| **Do you have an itinerary?** | Yes, here is my travel plan. I have hotel bookings and flight tickets. | 主动出示材料，展示准备充分。 |
| **What do you do for work?** | I have a full-time job in Beijing. I work for a company. I need to come back to work after my trip. | **工作是最强的回国理由**，必须强调。 |
| **How long have you worked there?** | I have worked there for 8 years. My job is stable. I cannot leave my job. | “稳定”+“不能离开”强化约束力。 |
| **What's your monthly income?** | My monthly income is 15,000 RMB. I save money from my job for this trip. | 说明有稳定收入，并自己存钱旅游。 |
| **Do you own a house in China?** | Yes, I own a house in Beijing. My family lives there. I live with them. | 房子+家人，双重约束。 |
| **Are you married?** | I am married. My wife/husband and children live in China. They are waiting for me. | 家人留在国内，必须回来。 |
| **Who will pay for your trip?** | I will pay for everything. I saved money from my job. I do not need help. | 独立支付，显示经济能力。 |
| **How much money do you have for this trip?** | I have about 50,000 RMB in my bank account. This is enough for 14 days. | 给出具体数字，更有说服力。 |
| **Have you been abroad before?** | Yes, I visited Japan. I came back to China after that trip. I always come back. | 强调之前出国也回来了，建立良好记录。 |
| **Do you have relatives in the US?** | No, I don't have any relatives there. I only go for sightseeing. | 明确无亲友，降低移民嫌疑。 |
| **Have you ever been denied a US visa before?** *(新增)* | No, this is my first time applying for a US visa. | 如实回答，第一次申请更有利。 |
| **Do you know anyone in the US?** *(新增)* | No, I don't know anyone there. I only go for tourism. | 与上一条一致，强化无社会关系。 |

## F-1 学生签证

| 问题 | 推荐回答（A1 水平） | 策略要点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Why do you want to study in the US?** | US has good schools for Computer Science. I will learn new skills. Then I will go back to China to find a good job. | 把“学成回国找工作”作为核心动机。 |
| **Why this university?** | This university has good courses for my major. Its program fits my study goals. | 简单说明选校理由，不要贬低中国大学。 |
| **What will you major in?** | My major is Computer Science. I like this subject. I studied it in China too. | 与国内学习衔接，显示连贯性。 |
| **Where is your school located?** | It is in Boston, Massachusetts. | 简单回答地点。 |
| **How long is your program?** | My program is 2 years. I will finish in 2 years and then go back to China. | 强调时长并再次强调回国。 |
| **Why not study in China?** | I want international experience. This US program has better resources for my field. But I will come back to China for work. | 不贬低国内，只说想体验国际教育，但最终回国。 |
| **Who is your sponsor?** | My parents pay for my study. They have stable jobs and good income. They support me to study and come back. | 父母有稳定收入，且支持你回国。 |
| **What do your parents do?** | My father is a businessman. My mother works in a company. They both have good jobs. | 简单说明父母职业，强调稳定。 |
| **Do you have enough money for your study?** | Yes, my family has enough money for tuition and living costs. We are financially prepared. | 明确有足够资金。 |
| **What will you do after graduation?** | I will go back to China. My family is there. I want to work near my family. I also have job connections in China. | 家庭+工作关系，双重回国理由。 |
| **Why will you come back to China?** | My parents live in China. They need me. I can find a good job in China because I have a US degree. | 用“父母需要我”这个最强情感纽带。 |
| **What is your education background?** | I graduated from XX University in China. I have a bachelor's degree. | 简单说明学历。 |
| **Have you taken TOEFL or IELTS?** | Yes, my IELTS score is 6.0. | 如实报分数。 |
| **Have you ever been denied a US visa before?** *(新增)* | No, I have never applied for a US visa before. | 如实回答。 |
| **Do you have a study plan?** *(新增)* | Yes. I will study for 2 years. I will take classes, do projects, and get my degree. Then I graduate and go back to China. | 清晰的学习计划，以回国为终点。 |
| **Why this major?** *(新增)* | I like Computer Science. I want to learn more and work in this field in China. China has many IT jobs. | 把专业和中国就业市场联系起来。 |
| **What will you do if you cannot get a visa?** *(新增)* | I will find a job in China. I have many options at home. I don't need to go to the US. | 表明不依赖美国，有退路——最强“无移民倾向”证据。 |

## 🔑 通用面试小贴士（A1 水平）

1. **回答要简短** — 每个回答 1~2 句话，不要长篇大论。
2. **最后一句总是“我回中国”** — 所有回答末尾都要暗示或明示回国。
3. **准备好材料** — 把表格中提到的文件（工作证明、房产证、存款证明、录取通知书等）放在手边，随时可以出示。
4. **眼神交流，微笑** — 自信比完美的英语更重要。
5. **如果没听懂，礼貌地请签证官重复** — 可以说：*“Pardon? Could you please repeat that?”* 或 *“Could you speak slower?”*
