Present Perfect · Experiences 现在完成时（经历）

1. Form 结构

句型例句中文对照
肯定：have / has + 过去分词I have tried the noodle place downstairs.我试过楼下那家面馆。
第三人称单数用 hasMy roommate has been to that gym twice.我室友去过那家健身房两次。
否定：haven't / hasn'tI haven't tried camping yet.我还没试过露营。
疑问：Have you ever…?Have you ever ordered groceries online?你在网上买过菜吗？
简答Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. / Not yet.有。/ 没有。/ 还没。
次数I have taken the high-speed train four times.我坐过四次高铁。

3. ever / never / already / yet

副词位置例句中文对照
ever疑问句，过去分词前Have you ever tried hot pot alone?你一个人吃过火锅吗？
never过去分词前I have never sung in front of people.我从没在人前唱过歌。
alreadyhave / has 之后I have already tried that milk tea shop.那家奶茶店我已经试过了。
yet否定句、疑问句句末I haven't tried rock climbing yet.我还没试过攀岩。
before句末I have been to Beijing before.我以前去过北京。
twice / a few times句末I have tried camping twice.露营我试过两次。

4. Sentence Patterns 常用句型

句型例句中文对照
Have you ever + 过去分词?Have you ever ordered takeout after midnight?你半夜点过外卖吗？
I've been to + 地点I have been to Dali twice.我去过大理两次。
I've never + 过去分词I have never lived alone.我从没一个人住过。
I've already + 过去分词I have already finished today's report.今天的报告我写完了。
I haven't + 过去分词 + yetI haven't booked the train ticket yet.我还没订高铁票。
It's the first time I've + 过去分词It's the first time I have spoken English at work.这是我第一次在工作中说英语。
I've been meaning to + 动词原形I have been meaning to try that gym.我一直想试那家健身房。
How many times have you + 过去分词?How many times have you moved since college?毕业后你搬过几次家？

5. Present Perfect or Past Simple 该用哪个时态

用法现在完成时一般过去时
说经历，不点时间I have been to Chengdu.—
点明时间—I went to Chengdu in 2024.
问经历Have you ever flown first class?—
问某次具体经过—When did you fly first class?
常见信号词ever / never / already / yet / beforeyesterday / last week / in 2024 / ago
结果留到现在I have lost my key.I lost my key yesterday.

常见错误

常见错误正确说法说明
I have went to Shanghai.I have been to Shanghai.用过去分词 been
I have seen him yesterday.I saw him yesterday.有具体时间用过去时
Have you ever gone to Chengdu?Have you ever been to Chengdu?问"去过"用 been to
I am in Shanghai for three years.I have been in Shanghai for three years.持续到现在用现在完成时
