# Setting Up a Meeting 约会议

## 1. 提议时间

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Are you free on + 时间? | **Are you free on** Tuesday afternoon? | 你周二下午有空吗？ |
| Does + 时间 + work for you? | **Does** 3 p.m. **work for you**? | 下午三点你方便吗？ |
| How about + 时间? | **How about** Thursday at 10? | 周四十点怎么样？ |
| Can we do it after lunch? | **Can we do it after lunch** — 1:30? | 午饭后行吗？一点半。 |
| I'll send an invite. | **I'll send an invite** for 3 p.m. | 我发个日历邀请，下午三点。 |
| What time works best for you? | **What time works best for you?** | 你什么时间最方便？ |

## 2. 回答行不行

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| That works for me. | **That works for me** — see you at 3. | 我可以，三点见。 |
| Sounds good. | **Sounds good** — I'll put it in my calendar. | 行，我记到日历里。 |
| Sorry, I'm booked then. | **Sorry, I'm booked then** — how about 4? | 抱歉那个点有安排，四点行吗？ |
| I have a conflict. | **I have a conflict** at 3 — can we move it? | 三点我有个冲突，能改吗？ |
| Let me check my calendar. | **Let me check my calendar** and confirm. | 我看下日历再确认。 |
| Morning is better for me. | **Morning is better for me** — I'm still on jet lag. | 我上午更好，还在倒时差。 |

## 3. 改时间和取消

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can we push it to + 时间? | **Can we push it to** Friday? | 能推到周五吗？ |
| Something came up. | **Something came up** — can we reschedule? | 临时有点事，能改期吗？ |
| I need to move our call. | **I need to move our call** to next week. | 我们的电话会得改到下周。 |
| Sorry for the short notice. | **Sorry for the short notice** — half an hour. | 抱歉通知得急，就提前半小时。 |
| Let's cancel this one. | **Let's cancel this one** and use email instead. | 这次取消吧，用邮件沟通。 |
| Can we make it 30 minutes? | **Can we make it 30 minutes** instead of an hour? | 能不能改成半小时？ |

## 4. 时差和跨时区

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Your 3 p.m. is my 9 a.m. | **Your 3 p.m. is my 9 a.m.** — I'm in Berlin. | 你的下午三点是我的早上九点，我在柏林。 |
| I'm on Beijing time. | **I'm on Beijing time** — GMT+8. | 我在北京时间，GMT+8。 |
| That's 11 p.m. here. | **That's 11 p.m. here** — a bit late for me. | 这边是晚上十一点，对我有点晚。 |
| Can we find a middle ground? | **Can we find a middle ground** — 7 a.m. your time? | 我们找个折中时间？你那边早上七点？ |
| I don't mind early mornings. | **I don't mind early mornings** — 7 is fine. | 早起我没问题，七点可以。 |
| Your Friday is my Saturday. | **Your Friday is my Saturday** — I'm in Sydney. | 你的周五是我的周六，我在悉尼。 |

## 5. 会前确认

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Just confirming our 3 p.m. | **Just confirming our 3 p.m.** — still good? | 确认一下三点，还行吗？ |
| I've sent the invite. | **I've sent the invite** — check your spam. | 邀请我发了，看下垃圾邮件。 |
| Who else is joining? | **Who else is joining** — just us two? | 还有谁参加？就我们俩？ |
| Can you bring the numbers? | **Can you bring the numbers** for October? | 你能带上十月的数字吗？ |
| The link is in the invite. | **The link is in the invite** — we use Teams. | 链接在邀请里，我们用 Teams。 |
| I'll dial in from the office. | **I'll dial in from the office** — see you then. | 我从办公室拨进来，到时见。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Are you convenient on Tuesday? | Are you free on Tuesday? | 问人有空用 free。 |
| I have a meeting at that time. | I'm in a meeting then. | 用 be in a meeting 更自然。 |
| Let's make a meeting tomorrow. | Let's set up a meeting for tomorrow. | 约会议用 set up。 |
| What time is good to you? | What time works for you? | 固定说法 works for you。 |
| Can we meet at next Monday? | Can we meet next Monday? | next Monday 前不加 at。 |
| I will attend the meeting. | I'll be there. | 口语里 I'll be there 更自然。 |
