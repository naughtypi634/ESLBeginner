# Company Structure and Titles 公司架构与称呼

## 1. 说部门和层级

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm in the + 部门 + department | **I'm in the** marketing **department**. | 我在市场部。 |
| I report to the + 职位 | **I report to the** regional director. | 我向区域总监汇报。 |
| She's my manager. | **She's my manager** — I go to her for approvals. | 她是我主管，审批找她。 |
| He's two levels above me. | **He's two levels above me** — I rarely see him. | 他比我高两级，很少见到。 |
| I'm on the + 级别 | **I'm on the** junior side — second year. | 我算初级的，第二年。 |
| We sit under + 部门 | **We sit under** the operations group. | 我们归运营口。 |
| The team is split into two. | **The team is split into two** — mine does exports. | 团队分两块，我这边做出口。 |

## 4. 怎么称呼对方

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| People use first names here. | **People use first names here** — just call me Wei. | 我们这儿都叫名字，叫我伟就行。 |
| Do you go by Mr. Wang or Wei? | **Do you go by Mr. Wang or Wei?** | 我该叫你王先生还是伟？ |
| Our director goes by Anna. | **Our director goes by Anna** — no titles. | 我们总监就叫 Anna，不带职衔。 |
| Should I use your title? | **Should I use your title** in the email? | 邮件里要写你的职衔吗？ |
| In China we often say "Wang zong". | **In China we often say** "Wang zong" — like Mr. Wang. | 我们国内常说"王总"，相当于王先生。 |
| Sorry, I wasn't sure how to address you. | **Sorry, I wasn't sure how to address you.** | 抱歉，我不确定该怎么称呼你。 |

## 5. 说清谁管什么

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns this? | **Who owns this** — the app team or us? | 这块归谁？App 组还是我们？ |
| That's Anna's call. | **That's Anna's call** — she signs off. | 那个 Anna 说了算，她签批。 |
| It goes through three people. | **It goes through three people** before it's approved. | 要过三个人才批得下来。 |
| I don't own that process. | **I don't own that process** — talk to finance. | 那个流程不是我负责，你找财务。 |
| Who is the decision maker? | **Who is the decision maker** on this? | 这个谁拍板？ |
| It's above my level. | **It's above my level** — I can't approve it. | 这超出我的权限，我批不了。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I am in market department. | I'm in the marketing department. | 部门名前加 the。 |
| My leader is Mr. Wang. | My manager is Mr. Wang. | 主管用 manager，不用 leader。 |
| I belong to sales department. | I'm in the sales department. | belong to 偏中式，用 be in。 |
| He is my superior. | He's my manager. | superior 太生硬。 |
| My boss is higher than me. | My boss is above me in the structure. | higher 不合适，用 above。 |
| I am the assistant of the manager. | I'm the manager's assistant. | 用所有格更自然。 |
| I am under Mr. Wang. | I report to Mr. Wang. | 汇报用 report to。 |
