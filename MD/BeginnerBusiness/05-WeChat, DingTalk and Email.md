# WeChat, DingTalk and Email 工作微信、钉钉与邮件

## 1. 发文件和信息

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'll send it on WeChat. | **I'll send it on WeChat** — check in a second. | 我微信发你，稍等看下。 |
| Let me share the file. | **Let me share the file** in the group. | 我把文件发群里。 |
| Check the group chat. | **Check the group chat** — I posted the schedule. | 看下群，我把日程发上去了。 |
| I'll send you the link. | **I'll send you the link** to the meeting room. | 我把会议链接发你。 |
| Give me one minute. | **Give me one minute** — the file is uploading. | 等我一下，文件在传。 |
| Did you get my message? | **Did you get my message?** I sent it last night. | 我消息你收到了吗？昨晚发的。 |

## 2. 群里说话和 @ 人

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'll @ you when the draft is ready. | **I'll @ you** when the draft is ready. | 稿子好了我在群里 @ 你。 |
| Can you put that in the group? | **Can you put that in the group** so everyone sees it? | 你发群里吧，大家都看得到。 |
| Let's move this to a group. | **Let's move this to a group** — it's easier. | 我们拉个群吧，方便些。 |
| I'll reply in the thread. | **I'll reply in the thread** — less noise. | 我在楼中楼回，别刷屏。 |
| Sorry, I missed it. | **Sorry, I missed it** — the group was busy. | 不好意思漏了，群里消息太多。 |
| Send it to me directly. | **Send it to me directly** — no need to copy the group. | 直接发我就行，不用发群里。 |

## 3. 邮件的开头和结尾

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Hi Wei, quick one — | **Hi Wei, quick one** — do you have the invoice? | Wei 你好，简单问一下，发票你有吗？ |
| Hope you're doing well. | **Hope you're doing well** — quick update below. | 你好，下面是个简短的更新。 |
| Just a quick note to… | **Just a quick note to** confirm Friday's call. | 简单说一下，确认周五的电话会。 |
| Thanks for the quick reply. | **Thanks for the quick reply** — that helps a lot. | 谢谢你这么快回复，帮大忙了。 |
| Let me know if you need anything. | **Let me know if you need anything.** | 有需要跟我说。 |
| Best, | **Best,** Wei | 结尾写 Best 或 Thanks 就行。 |

## 4. 邮件正文常用句

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I've attached + 文件 | **I've attached** the price list as a PDF. | 我把报价单做成 PDF 附上了。 |
| Please take a look when you can. | **Please take a look when you can** — no rush. | 你有空看一下，不急。 |
| Could you confirm by + 时间? | **Could you confirm by** Thursday? | 你能周四前确认吗？ |
| Just to confirm what we agreed. | **Just to confirm what we agreed** on the call. | 确认一下我们电话里说好的。 |
| Sorry for the late reply. | **Sorry for the late reply** — I was out yesterday. | 抱歉回晚了，我昨天不在。 |
| I'll get back to you by + 时间. | **I'll get back to you by** Friday. | 我周五前给你答复。 |

## 5. 抄送、转发、回复

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'll cc you. | **I'll cc you** so you have it on record. | 我抄送你一份，留个底。 |
| Let me loop in Anna. | **Let me loop in Anna** — she owns the budget. | 我把 Anna 拉进来，预算归她。 |
| Can you forward that to me? | **Can you forward that to me?** I can't find it. | 你能转我一下吗？我找不到。 |
| Please reply all. | **Please reply all** so the team sees it. | 请回复全部，让团队都看到。 |
| I'm moving you to bcc. | **I'm moving you to bcc** — just so you know. | 我把你放到密送，让你知道就行。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Please kindly find the attached file. | I've attached the file. | 现代邮件不用 kindly find attached。 |
| I will reply you tomorrow. | I'll get back to you tomorrow. | 回复某人用 get back to you。 |
| Please give me your reply ASAP. | Could you get back to me by Friday? | 给个具体时间比 ASAP 好。 |
| I am writing to inform you of the delay. | Just to let you know — we're delayed. | 别用 I am writing to inform you。 |
| Dear Manager Wang. | Hi Mr. Wang, / Hi Wei, | Manager 不是称呼，用 Mr. 或名字。 |
| Thanks for your cooperation. | Thanks for your help. | 中式套话，换成具体的感谢。 |
| Awaiting your reply. | Let me know what you think. | 更自然的收尾。 |
