# Delivery, MOQ and Payment 交期、起订量与付款

## 1. 交期

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What's your lead time? | **What's your lead time** for 2,000 units? | 2000 台交期多久？ |
| It takes about + 时间. | **It takes about** 30 days after the deposit. | 收到定金后大约 30 天。 |
| We can ship by + 时间. | **We can ship by** the end of November. | 我们 11 月底前能发货。 |
| Production starts after the deposit. | **Production starts after the deposit** clears. | 定金到账后开始生产。 |
| Can you do it faster? | **Can you do it faster** — we need it by the 20th. | 能快一点吗？我们 20 号要。 |
| We'll do our best. | **We'll do our best** — I can't promise. | 我们尽量，不敢保证。 |

## 2. 起订量

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What's your MOQ? | **What's your MOQ** for this model? | 这个型号的起订量是多少？ |
| Our MOQ is + 数量. | **Our MOQ is** 500 pieces per color. | 我们的起订量是每色 500 个。 |
| Can you accept a smaller order? | **Can you accept a smaller order** — just 200? | 能接小单吗？就 200 个。 |
| We can do a trial order. | **We can do a trial order** at 300. | 300 个我们可以做试单。 |
| Below that, the unit cost goes up. | **Below that, the unit cost goes up.** | 低于这个数，单件成本就上去了。 |
| Mix colors if you like. | **Mix colors if you like** — same total. | 总数够的话可以混色。 |

## 3. 付款方式

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What are your payment terms? | **What are your payment terms?** | 你们的付款条件是什么？ |
| 30% deposit, 70% before shipping. | **30% deposit, 70% before shipping.** | 30% 定金，70% 发货前付。 |
| Can we pay after delivery? | **Can we pay after delivery** — 30 days? | 能货到后付吗？30 天。 |
| Do you accept a letter of credit? | **Do you accept a letter of credit?** | 你们接受信用证吗？ |
| We usually work with T/T. | **We usually work with T/T.** | 我们一般用电汇。 |
| The first order is deposit only. | **The first order is deposit only** — our policy. | 第一单只做定金，这是我们的规定。 |

## 4. 催货和延期

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| When will it be ready? | **When will it be ready** — any news? | 什么时候能好？有消息吗？ |
| We're two weeks behind. | **We're two weeks behind** — I'm sorry. | 我们晚了两周，抱歉。 |
| Can you split the shipment? | **Can you split the shipment** — half now, half later? | 能分批吗？先一半。 |
| The customer is pushing us. | **The customer is pushing us** for a date. | 客户在催我们要个日期。 |
| We'll air-freight the first batch. | **We'll air-freight the first batch** at our cost. | 第一批我们空运，费用我们出。 |
| I'll confirm the date tomorrow. | **I'll confirm the date tomorrow** morning. | 明天早上确认日期给你。 |

## 5. 包装和物流

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How should we pack it? | **How should we pack it** — pallets or cartons? | 怎么包装？托盘还是纸箱？ |
| We need our logo on the box. | **We need our logo on the box.** | 箱子上要印我们的标。 |
| Sea freight takes about a month. | **Sea freight takes about a month** to Hamburg. | 海运到汉堡大约一个月。 |
| Air freight is much more expensive. | **Air freight is much more expensive** right now. | 现在空运贵很多。 |
| Do you handle the customs papers? | **Do you handle the customs papers?** | 报关文件你们弄吗？ |
| I'll send the packing list. | **I'll send the packing list** before shipping. | 发货前我把装箱单发你。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| The delivery time is 30 days. | The lead time is 30 days. | 交期常用 lead time。 |
| We will delivery on Monday. | We'll deliver on Monday. | delivery 是名词，动词用 deliver。 |
| Please pay 30% deposit first. | We ask for a 30% deposit. | 别用命令式要求付款。 |
| The goods will be send tomorrow. | The goods will be sent tomorrow. | 被动要 be + 过去分词。 |
| How many is your MOQ? | What's your MOQ? | 问 MOQ 用 What's。 |
| We need the goods urgently. | We need them by the 20th. | 给具体日期比 urgently 有用。 |
