# Job Interview 面试

## 1. 开场和自我介绍

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Thanks for having me. | **Thanks for having me** — I'm Wei. | 谢谢你们安排面试，我是伟。 |
| I've been in + 领域 + for + 时间. | **I've been in** logistics **for** five years. | 我在物流行业五年了。 |
| I currently work at + 公司. | **I currently work at** a trading company in Ningbo. | 我现在在宁波一家贸易公司。 |
| I'm looking for + 目标. | **I'm looking for** a role with more scope. | 我想找个职责范围更大的岗位。 |
| I saw the posting on + 渠道. | **I saw the posting on** your website. | 我在你们官网看到这个职位。 |
| A former colleague recommended it. | **A former colleague recommended it** — she works here. | 以前同事推荐的，她在这儿。 |

## 2. 常见问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself. | **Tell me about yourself.** | 请自我介绍一下。 |
| Why do you want this job? | **Why do you want this job?** | 你为什么想要这份工作？ |
| Why are you leaving your current role? | **Why are you leaving** your current role? | 你为什么离开现在的岗位？ |
| What are you good at? | **What are you good at** — what's your strength? | 你擅长什么？优势是什么？ |
| Where do you see yourself in five years? | **Where do you see yourself in five years?** | 你五年后想做到什么位置？ |
| What do you know about us? | **What do you know about us?** | 你对我们了解多少？ |

## 3. 说经验

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I worked on + 项目. | **I worked on** the Vietnam supplier project. | 我做过越南供应商那个项目。 |
| I have + 时间 + of experience. | **I have** three years **of experience** in quality control. | 我有三年质检方面的经验。 |
| My last job was + 职位. | **My last job was** a junior buyer. | 我上一份工作是初级采购。 |
| I handled + 事情. | **I handled** the monthly orders for 12 stores. | 我负责 12 家门店的月度订单。 |
| For example, … | **For example**, I cut our delivery time by a week. | 比如，我把交期缩短了一周。 |
| I learned a lot there. | **I learned a lot there** about negotiation. | 我在那儿学了不少谈判的东西。 |

## 4. 说优点和缺点

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm good at + 动名词. | **I'm good at** keeping things organized. | 我擅长把事理顺。 |
| People say I'm + 形容词. | **People say I'm** easy to work with. | 别人说跟我合作挺舒服。 |
| My strength is + 名词. | **My strength is** following through. | 我的优势是跟到底。 |
| I'm working on + 事情. | **I'm working on** my English for meetings. | 我在练开会用的英语。 |
| I used to + 问题. | **I used to** take on too much at once. | 我以前一次接太多活。 |
| I've gotten better at + 事情. | **I've gotten better at** saying no. | 我现在更懂得拒绝了。 |

## 5. 问公司问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What does a typical day look like? | **What does a typical day look like** in this role? | 这个岗位典型的一天什么样？ |
| Who would I report to? | **Who would I report to?** | 我向谁汇报？ |
| How is the team set up? | **How is the team set up?** | 团队是怎么分工的？ |
| What are the biggest challenges right now? | **What are the biggest challenges** right now? | 目前最大的挑战是什么？ |
| What's the next step? | **What's the next step** in the process? | 接下来是什么流程？ |

## 6. 谈薪资和到岗

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What's the salary range? | **What's the salary range** for this role? | 这个岗位的薪资范围是多少？ |
| I'm looking for + 金额. | **I'm looking for** around 18,000 a month. | 我希望月薪在 18000 左右。 |
| I'm flexible on the details. | **I'm flexible on the details.** | 细节上可以商量。 |
| I can start in + 时间. | **I can start in** a month — after my notice period. | 我一个月后能到岗，要交接。 |
| What benefits do you offer? | **What benefits do you offer** — insurance, bonus? | 你们有什么福利？保险、奖金？ |
| Is there a bonus? | **Is there a bonus** at the end of the year? | 年底有奖金吗？ |

## 7. 结束时

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Thanks for your time. | **Thanks for your time** — this was helpful. | 谢谢你的时间，聊得很有收获。 |
| I'm very interested in this role. | **I'm very interested in this role.** | 我对这个岗位很感兴趣。 |
| I look forward to hearing from you. | **I look forward to hearing from you.** | 期待你们的消息。 |
| When can I expect to hear back? | **When can I expect to hear back?** | 大概什么时候能收到消息？ |
| You can reach me on WeChat or email. | **You can reach me on WeChat or email.** | 微信或邮件都能找到我。 |

## 8. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I am graduated from Fudan University. | I graduated from Fudan University. | graduate 是动词，不加 am。 |
| I have 5 years experience. | I have five years of experience. | 年份后面加 of。 |
| My English is not good, but I will study hard. | I'm improving my English for meetings. | 别主动强调缺点。 |
| I want to know your salary. | What's the salary range for this role? | 问薪资用 range 更得体。 |
| I have no question. | I do have one question. | 说没问题会显得不上心。 |
| I am very interested with this job. | I'm very interested in this job. | interested 后接 in。 |
| Please consider me. | I'd be glad to move forward. | please consider me 偏中式。 |

## 9. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Have you interviewed in English before? | 你用英语面试过吗？ |
| What question do you find hardest? | 哪个问题你觉得最难答？ |
| What do you usually ask the interviewer? | 你一般会问面试官什么？ |
| Is it OK to talk about salary in the first interview? | 一面就谈薪资合适吗？ |
| What makes you choose one offer over another? | 什么会让你选一个 offer 而不选另一个？ |
