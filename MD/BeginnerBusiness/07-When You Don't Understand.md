# When You Don't Understand 没听懂的时候

## 1. 请对方重复

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sorry, could you say that again? | **Sorry, could you say that again?** I missed it. | 抱歉，你能再说一遍吗？我没听清。 |
| Sorry, I didn't catch that. | **Sorry, I didn't catch that** — the last part. | 抱歉没听清，最后那部分。 |
| One more time, please. | **One more time, please** — a bit slower. | 麻烦再说一遍，慢一点。 |
| Could you repeat the number? | **Could you repeat the number** — 40 or 14? | 数字再说一遍？是 40 还是 14？ |
| Sorry, what was that? | **Sorry, what was that?** The line was bad. | 抱歉你说什么？信号不好。 |
| Can you spell that for me? | **Can you spell that for me?** The name, I mean. | 能拼一下吗？我说的是名字。 |

## 2. 请对方换个说法

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Could you say it in another way? | **Could you say it in another way?** | 你能换个说法吗？ |
| What does + 词 + mean? | **What does** MOQ **mean**? | MOQ 是什么意思？ |
| Sorry, I don't know that word. | **Sorry, I don't know that word** — "lead time"? | 抱歉这个词我不认识，lead time？ |
| Can you put it in the chat? | **Can you put it in the chat?** Easier for me. | 你能打在下面对话框吗？我好看些。 |
| Can you send me a message? | **Can you send me a message** with the details? | 你能把细节发条消息给我吗？ |
| Could you write it down? | **Could you write it down** — I'll read it. | 你能写下来吗？我看着读。 |

## 3. 确认自己听对了

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you mean + 意思? | **Do you mean** the price went up? | 你的意思是涨价了？ |
| So you're saying + 意思. | **So you're saying** we ship on Monday. | 所以你的意思是我们周一出货。 |
| Let me make sure I got that. | **Let me make sure I got that** — 500 units, right? | 我确认一下听对了没，500 台对吗？ |
| If I understand you correctly… | **If I understand you correctly**, we pay in 30 days. | 如果我没理解错，是 30 天付款。 |
| Is that right? | **Is that right** — or did I mix it up? | 对吗？还是我搞混了。 |
| Just to be clear… | **Just to be clear** — you want it by Friday? | 确认一下，你要周五前？ |

## 4. 争取时间

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me think about it. | **Let me think about it** and get back to you. | 我想一想，再回复你。 |
| Can I check and get back to you? | **Can I check and get back to you** this afternoon? | 我查一下，下午回复你好吗？ |
| I need to ask my manager. | **I need to ask my manager** first. | 我得先问一下我主管。 |
| That's a good question. | **That's a good question** — let me look it up. | 这个问题问得好，我查一下。 |
| Give me a second to find it. | **Give me a second to find it** in the file. | 等我一下，我在文件里找找。 |
| I'll come back to you on that. | **I'll come back to you on that** by tomorrow. | 那个事我明天之前答复你。 |

## 5. 不懂就问

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sorry, one more question. | **Sorry, one more question** — who signs it? | 不好意思再问一个，谁签字？ |
| I'm still learning the terms. | **I'm still learning the terms** — bear with me. | 这些术语我还在学，多包涵。 |
| Could you explain that part again? | **Could you explain that part again?** | 那部分你能再解释一下吗？ |
| Am I saying that right? | **Am I saying that right** — "procurement"? | 我念对了吗？procurement？ |
| Thanks for explaining. | **Thanks for explaining** — that's clear now. | 谢谢解释，现在清楚了。 |
| Sorry to keep asking. | **Sorry to keep asking** — it's important. | 不好意思一直问，这事挺重要。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| What's your meaning? | What do you mean? | 问意思用 What do you mean。 |
| How to say this in English? | How do you say this in English? | 问句要有 do you。 |
| Pardon? | Sorry, could you say that again? | pardon 在美式里显生硬。 |
| I didn't listen clearly. | I didn't catch that. | 听清用 catch。 |
| Please repeat again. | Could you say that again? | repeat 本身已含 again。 |
| Sorry, I can't understand your English. | Sorry, I didn't catch that. | 别说听不懂对方的口语。 |
