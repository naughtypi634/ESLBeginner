# Business Trips 出差

## 1. 出差前

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm going on a business trip. | **I'm going on a business trip** to Munich. | 我要去慕尼黑出差。 |
| I need to book a flight. | **I need to book a flight** for Monday morning. | 我要订周一早上的机票。 |
| Do I need a visa for + 国家? | **Do I need a visa for** Germany? | 去德国要签证吗？ |
| Can you book the hotel for me? | **Can you book the hotel for me** — near the office? | 能帮我订酒店吗？离办公室近点。 |
| I'll send you my itinerary. | **I'll send you my itinerary** once it's set. | 行程定了我发你。 |
| I'm away from + 时间. | **I'm away from** Tuesday to Friday. | 我周二到周五不在。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I will go to a business trip. | I'm going on a business trip. | 出差是 go on a business trip。 |
| I live in the hotel this week. | I'm staying at a hotel this week. | 住酒店用 stay at。 |
| Please wait me at the airport. | Please wait for me at the airport. | wait 后要加 for。 |
| I arrived to Munich yesterday. | I arrived in Munich yesterday. | 大城市用 arrive in。 |
| The flight was late for two hours. | The flight was delayed by two hours. | 晚点用 delayed by。 |
| I will back on Friday. | I'll be back on Friday. | 要加 be 动词。 |
