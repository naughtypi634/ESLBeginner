# Asking for Help and Saying No 求助与说"不"

## 1. 请人帮忙

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Could you help me with + 事情? | **Could you help me with** the customs form? | 你能帮我弄下报关单吗？ |
| Would you mind + 动名词? | **Would you mind** checking these numbers? | 你方便帮我看下这些数字吗？ |
| Can I borrow + 东西? | **Can I borrow** your charger for ten minutes? | 借你充电器用十分钟行吗？ |
| Do you have time to look at this? | **Do you have time to look at this** today? | 你今天有空看下这个吗？ |
| I'm stuck on + 事情. | **I'm stuck on** the tax part — can you help? | 我卡在税务这块了，能帮下吗？ |
| Could you show me how? | **Could you show me how** to export it? | 你能教我一下怎么导出吗？ |

## 2. 请人教你做

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you walk me through it? | **Can you walk me through it** once? | 你能带我走一遍吗？ |
| Where do I find + 东西? | **Where do I find** the expense form? | 报销单在哪儿找？ |
| Which system do you use? | **Which system do you use** for invoices? | 发票你们用哪个系统？ |
| Is there a guide for this? | **Is there a guide for this** somewhere? | 这个有说明文档吗？ |
| Who should I ask about this? | **Who should I ask about this** — IT or finance? | 这个该问谁？IT 还是财务？ |

## 3. 答应了

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sure, no problem. | **Sure, no problem** — send it over. | 行，没问题，发我吧。 |
| Happy to help. | **Happy to help** — give me ten minutes. | 乐意帮忙，给我十分钟。 |
| I'll take a look this afternoon. | **I'll take a look this afternoon.** | 我下午看一下。 |
| Leave it with me. | **Leave it with me** — I'll sort it out. | 交给我吧，我来弄。 |
| I can do that. | **I can do that** — anything else? | 这个我可以，还有别的吗？ |

## 4. 忙不过来时怎么拒绝

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd love to, but I'm swamped. | **I'd love to, but I'm swamped** this week. | 我很想帮，但这周实在忙。 |
| Can it wait until Monday? | **Can it wait until Monday?** I'm on a deadline. | 能等到周一吗？我在赶期限。 |
| I'm not the right person. | **I'm not the right person** — try the design team. | 这个不是我负责的，你找设计组。 |
| Sorry, I can't take this on. | **Sorry, I can't take this on** right now. | 抱歉我现在接不了这个。 |
| I don't have the bandwidth. | **I don't have the bandwidth** this month. | 我这个月腾不出精力。 |
| Let me finish this first. | **Let me finish this first**, then I can help. | 让我先把这个做完，再来帮你。 |

## 5. 不硬拒，先拖一下

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me check with my manager. | **Let me check with my manager** and come back. | 我问下我主管再答复你。 |
| Can I get back to you tomorrow? | **Can I get back to you tomorrow** on this? | 这个我明天答复你行吗？ |
| Let me see what I can do. | **Let me see what I can do** — no promises. | 我看看能怎么办，不敢保证。 |
| I'll need to check the schedule. | **I'll need to check the schedule** first. | 我得先看下排期。 |
| That might be possible. | **That might be possible** — let me ask. | 也许可以，我问一下。 |
| I'll let you know by 5. | **I'll let you know by 5** either way. | 五点前我不管行不行都告诉你。 |

## 6. 请人给宽限

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you give me until Friday? | **Can you give me until Friday?** | 能给我到周五吗？ |
| Could we push the deadline? | **Could we push the deadline** by two days? | 截止期能推两天吗？ |
| I need a bit more time. | **I need a bit more time** to do it right. | 我需要多点时间才能做好。 |
| Can we drop one item? | **Can we drop one item** to make the date? | 砍掉一项能赶上时间吗？ |
| I can do half by tomorrow. | **I can do half by tomorrow** — is that OK? | 我明天能交一半，行吗？ |

## 7. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Please help me to do this. | Could you help me with this? | 请求用 Could you，help 后接 with。 |
| I want you to check it. | Could you check it when you have time? | I want you to 太硬。 |
| Can you borrow me your pen? | Can you lend me your pen? | 借出用 lend，借入用 borrow。 |
| I have no time to help you. | I'm afraid I don't have time today. | 直接说 no time 太生硬。 |
| Sorry, I can't. | I'd love to, but I'm on a deadline. | 拒绝时给个理由。 |
| Please do it for me quickly. | Could you do it when you get a chance? | quickly 命令感太强。 |
