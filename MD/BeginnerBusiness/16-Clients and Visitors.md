# Clients and Visitors 接待客户

## 1. 接机和见面

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome to Shanghai. | **Welcome to Shanghai** — how was your flight? | 欢迎来上海，飞机顺利吗？ |
| Let me help with your bag. | **Let me help with your bag** — the car is outside. | 我帮你拿包，车在外面。 |
| Did you find us OK? | **Did you find us OK** with the address? | 地址好找吗？ |
| It's about 40 minutes. | **It's about 40 minutes** to your hotel. | 到你酒店大约 40 分钟。 |
| Is this your first time here? | **Is this your first time here** in Suzhou? | 你是第一次来苏州吗？ |
| I've got the schedule printed. | **I've got the schedule printed** — here's your copy. | 日程我打印好了，这份给你。 |

## 2. 到公司和会议室

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me show you around. | **Let me show you around** before the meeting. | 开会前我带你转转。 |
| This way, please. | **This way, please** — the meeting room is on 6. | 这边请，会议室在六楼。 |
| Can I get you anything? | **Can I get you anything** — coffee, tea, water? | 要喝点什么？咖啡、茶还是水？ |
| The restroom is down the hall. | **The restroom is down the hall** on the left. | 洗手间在走廊尽头左边。 |
| Please help yourself. | **Please help yourself** to the snacks. | 点心随便拿。 |
| We have Wi-Fi — here's the password. | **We have Wi-Fi** — here's the password. | 有 Wi-Fi，这是密码。 |

## 3. 介绍团队

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me introduce our team. | **Let me introduce our team** — this is Anna. | 我介绍一下我们团队，这是 Anna。 |
| Anna leads the design side. | **Anna leads the design side.** | Anna 负责设计这块。 |

| You'll be working with him mostly. | **You'll be working with him** mostly. | 你主要跟他打交道。 |

## 4. 参观和介绍产品

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me walk you through + 内容. | **Let me walk you through** the production line. | 我带你走一遍产线。 |
| Watch your step here. | **Watch your step here** — the floor is wet. | 这儿小心脚下，地面是湿的。 |
| Please put this on first. | **Please put this on first** — safety rules. | 请先把这个穿上，安全规定。 |
| This is our newest model. | **This is our newest model** — launched in March. | 这是我们最新的型号，三月上市的。 |
| Feel free to take photos. | **Feel free to take photos** — except in this room. | 随便拍照，就这间不行。 |

| I'll email the summary tonight. | **I'll email the summary tonight.** | 我今晚把纪要发邮件给你。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Please sit, please sit. | Please take a seat. | 别重复 please sit。 |
| You are so polite. | That's very kind of you. | 别评价对方的礼貌。 |
| Please enjoy your meal slowly. | Please take your time. | slowly 有催促感。 |
| I will pick up you at the airport. | I'll pick you up at the airport. | pick up 代词放中间。 |
| Welcome you to our company. | Welcome to our company. | welcome 后面不加 you。 |
| Do you want to drink something? | Would you like something to drink? | Would you like 更礼貌。 |
