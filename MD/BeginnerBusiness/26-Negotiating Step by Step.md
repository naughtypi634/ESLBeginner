# Negotiating Step by Step 谈判与让步

## 1. 开场和摸底线

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What are you looking for? | **What are you looking for** in this deal? | 这个合作你们主要想要什么？ |
| What's your target price? | **What's your target price** — give me a number. | 你们的目标价是多少？给个数。 |
| Where's the gap? | **Where's the gap** between us? | 我们差在哪儿？ |
| What matters most to you? | **What matters most to you** — price or timing? | 你们最看重什么？价格还是时间？ |
| Let's list what we agree on. | **Let's list what we agree on** first. | 先把一致的部分列出来。 |
| We're not far apart. | **We're not far apart** — maybe 5%. | 我们差得不多，大概 5%。 |

## 2. 先给再要

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| If you do + 数量, we can + 做法. | **If you do** 5,000 units, **we can** hold the price. | 你们做到 5000 台，我们就能保价。 |
| We can move on price if… | **We can move on price** if you commit to a year. | 你们签一年的话价格可以让。 |
| What can you offer on your side? | **What can you offer on your side?** | 你们这边能让什么？ |
| We could be flexible on timing. | **We could be flexible on timing** if the price works. | 价格合适的话时间可以灵活。 |
| Let's trade something. | **Let's trade something** — not just give. | 我们换条件，不是单方面让。 |
| That works for both of us. | **That works for both of us.** | 这样双方都合适。 |

## 3. 让步的阶梯

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| That's my limit. | **That's my limit** — I can't go further. | 这是我的底线，不能再让了。 |
| We can go to + 数字. | **We can go to** 11 dollars, not 10. | 我们最多到 11 美元，10 不行。 |
| I can give you one of the two. | **I can give you one of the two** — not both. | 两个里我只能给一个，不能都给。 |
| Let's split the difference. | **Let's split the difference** — 5% each way. | 差价各让一半，各 5%。 |
| That's already a stretch for us. | **That's already a stretch for us.** | 这已经是我们的极限了。 |
| I need something back. | **I need something back** to sell this internally. | 我得拿到点东西才好回去交代。 |

## 4. 争取时间

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me take this back to my team. | **Let me take this back to my team.** | 这个我带回去跟团队商量。 |
| Can we come back to this tomorrow? | **Can we come back to this tomorrow?** | 这个明天再谈行吗？ |
| I need to check the numbers. | **I need to check the numbers** before I agree. | 我得先核算一下才能答应。 |
| Let's not decide today. | **Let's not decide today** — it's a big one. | 今天先不定，这事太大。 |
| I'll come back with a number. | **I'll come back with a number** by Thursday. | 我周四给个数字。 |

## 5. 谈不下去时

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's park this for now. | **Let's park this for now** and talk about the rest. | 这条先放着，谈其他的。 |
| We may not agree on this today. | **We may not agree on this today.** | 这条今天可能谈不拢。 |
| Let's put it in writing. | **Let's put it in writing** so nothing gets lost. | 写下来，免得漏掉。 |
| I'll talk to my manager. | **I'll talk to my manager** and call you back. | 我问下主管再打给你。 |
| Do we need a break? | **Do we need a break?** Ten minutes. | 要不要休息十分钟？ |
| Let's keep the rest of the deal. | **Let's keep the rest of the deal** and note this one. | 其他部分照旧，这条单独记。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I want you to reduce the price. | Could you do anything on the price? | 谈判别用命令式。 |
| You must give me a discount. | Is there room for a discount? | must 太硬。 |
| This is my last price, take it or leave it. | That's the best we can do. | 别用威胁式说法。 |
| I agree, but I want more. | I'm happy with that, with one change. | 别自相矛盾。 |
| Give me your bottom line. | What's the best you can do? | bottom line 太冲。 |
| You are not sincere. | I feel we're not there yet. | 别评价对方的诚意。 |
| OK, we accept everything. | Let me check one point first. | 别一口全答应。 |
