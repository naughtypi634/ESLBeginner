# Describing Your Company 介绍公司

## 1. 公司做什么

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We make + 产品 | **We make** lithium batteries for e-bikes. | 我们做电动自行车的锂电池。 |
| We sell + 产品 | **We sell** kitchen tools online. | 我们在网上卖厨房用品。 |
| We're a + 公司类型 | **We're a** logistics company — mainly sea freight. | 我们是物流公司，主要做海运。 |
| We do + 业务 | **We do** packaging design for food brands. | 我们给食品品牌做包装设计。 |
| Our main product is + 产品 | **Our main product is** the A2 air purifier. | 我们主打产品是 A2 空气净化器。 |
| We work with + 客户 | **We work with** supermarkets in Europe. | 我们的客户是欧洲的超市。 |
| We also have + 业务 | **We also have** a small repair service. | 我们还有一小块维修业务。 |

## 2. 公司在哪儿

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We're based in + 城市 | **We're based in** Ningbo, near the port. | 我们在宁波，离港口很近。 |
| Our head office is in + 城市 | **Our head office is in** Beijing. | 我们的总部在北京。 |
| Our factory is in + 城市 | **Our factory is in** Dongguan, two hours away. | 我们工厂在东莞，两小时车程。 |
| We have a branch in + 城市 | **We have a branch in** Ho Chi Minh City. | 我们在胡志明市有分公司。 |
| We have offices in + 数字 + cities | **We have offices in** six cities in China. | 我们在国内六个城市有办公室。 |
| The warehouse is near the port. | **The warehouse is near the port** — that saves time. | 仓库就在港口边上，省时间。 |

## 3. 规模和人数

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We have about + 数字 + employees | **We have about** 400 employees. | 我们大约有 400 名员工。 |
| Our team is + 大小 | **Our team is** small — just eight of us. | 我们团队很小，就八个人。 |
| We started in + 年份 | **We started in** 2011, in a garage. | 我们是 2011 年在一个车库里起步的。 |
| We've grown a lot since + 时间 | **We've grown a lot since** 2020. | 2020 年以来我们发展很快。 |
| We're one of the biggest + 名词 | **We're one of the biggest** suppliers in Zhejiang. | 我们是浙江最大的供应商之一。 |
| We ship to + 数量 + countries | **We ship to** over 30 countries. | 我们发货到 30 多个国家。 |

## 4. 你在公司里的位置

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm in the + 部门 + team | **I'm in the** sales team, based in Shanghai. | 我在销售组，常驻上海。 |
| I look after + 客户/地区 | **I look after** our Southeast Asia clients. | 我负责东南亚的客户。 |
| My department has + 数字 + people | **My department has** 12 people. | 我们部门有 12 个人。 |
| I've been with the company + 时间 | **I've been with the company** for four years. | 我在公司四年了。 |
| I started as a + 职位 | **I started as a** junior engineer here. | 我是从初级工程师做起的。 |
| I moved over from + 部门 | **I moved over from** the quality team last year. | 我去年从质量组调过来的。 |

## 5. 三句话介绍公司

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We are + 做什么 | **We are** a shoe maker in Fujian. | 我们是福建的一家制鞋企业。 |
| We do + 特色 | **We do** custom orders, small runs too. | 我们做定制，小批量也接。 |
| We're known for + 特色 | **We're known for** fast delivery. | 我们以交期快出名。 |
| Our customers are mostly + 客户 | **Our customers are mostly** in North America. | 我们的客户主要在北美。 |
| We mainly serve + 客户 | **We mainly serve** hotel and restaurant chains. | 我们主要服务酒店和连锁餐厅。 |
| Right now we're working on + 事情 | **Right now we're working on** a new factory line. | 我们现在在上一台新的产线。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Our company is located in Beijing city. | We're based in Beijing. | 城市后不加 city。 |
| Our company has 400 staffs. | We have 400 employees. | staff 不加 s，人多用 employees。 |
| We are a company which make shoes. | We're a shoe company. | 简单说 shoe company 就够。 |
| Our mainly product is bags. | Our main product is bags. | 修饰名词用 main，不用 mainly。 |
| We are doing business in Europe. | We sell in Europe. | doing business 太笼统。 |
| The headquarter is in Shenzhen. | Our head office is in Shenzhen. | 总部用 head office 或 HQ。 |
