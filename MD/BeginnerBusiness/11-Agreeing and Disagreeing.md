# Agreeing and Disagreeing 同意与委婉反对

## 1. 同意

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I agree. | **I agree** — let's do it that way. | 我同意，就这么办。 |
| That works for me. | **That works for me** — no problem. | 我没问题。 |
| Sounds good to me. | **Sounds good to me** — let's try it. | 我觉得行，试试吧。 |
| You're right. | **You're right** — I hadn't thought of that. | 你说得对，我没往那想。 |
| That makes sense. | **That makes sense** — the numbers back it up. | 有道理，数据也支持。 |
| I'm with you on that. | **I'm with you on that** — same conclusion here. | 这点我跟你一边，我结论也一样。 |

## 2. 部分同意

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I agree up to a point. | **I agree up to a point** — the timing worries me. | 我部分同意，但时间上我担心。 |
| You're right about the price. | **You're right about the price**, not the volume. | 价格你说得对，数量上不对。 |
| I see your point, but… | **I see your point, but** we tried that before. | 我明白你的意思，但我们试过。 |
| That's true, but… | **That's true, but** it costs more. | 是这样，但成本更高。 |
| I like the idea. | **I like the idea** — I'm just not sure about the cost. | 想法我喜欢，就是成本没底。 |
| Partly. | **Partly** — the first half, yes. | 一半吧，前半段我同意。 |

## 3. 委婉反对

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm not sure about that. | **I'm not sure about that** — can we see the data? | 这个我不太确定，能看下数据吗？ |
| I see it a bit differently. | **I see it a bit differently** — from the factory side. | 我看法有点不同，从工厂角度。 |
| I'd push back on that. | **I'd push back on that** — 30 days is tight. | 这点我有异议，30 天太紧。 |
| I don't think that will work. | **I don't think that will work** with our lead time. | 按我们的交期，我觉得不行。 |
| Can I offer another option? | **Can I offer another option?** | 我能提另一个方案吗？ |
| I'm afraid I disagree. | **I'm afraid I disagree** — the risk is too high. | 恐怕我不同意，风险太大。 |

## 4. 更强硬的反对

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| That won't work for us. | **That won't work for us** — we'd lose money. | 这个对我们不行，会亏。 |
| We can't accept that. | **We can't accept that** — it's below cost. | 这个我们不能接受，低于成本了。 |
| I have to be honest with you. | **I have to be honest with you** — this is a dealbreaker. | 我说实话，这条没法谈。 |
| That's a hard no from us. | **That's a hard no from us** on exclusivity. | 独家这条我们坚决不行。 |
| Let me be direct. | **Let me be direct** — that price is impossible. | 我直说，这个价格做不到。 |

## 5. 提另一个方案

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How about we + 做法? | **How about we** split it into two shipments? | 我们分两批发怎么样？ |
| What if we + 做法? | **What if we** start with 500 units? | 我们先做 500 台呢？ |
| Another option is to… | **Another option is to** use a local supplier. | 另一个办法是用本地供应商。 |
| We could meet in the middle. | **We could meet in the middle** at 3%. | 我们各让一步，3% 行不行。 |
| Why don't we try it for a month? | **Why don't we try it for a month** and review? | 先试一个月再看怎么样？ |
| Let's take it offline. | **Let's take it offline** and come back Friday. | 我们私下聊，周五再谈。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I very agree with you. | I completely agree. | very 不能修饰 agree。 |
| I am agree. | I agree. | agree 是动词，不加 am。 |
| I disagree with you strongly. | I'm afraid I disagree. | 太直，先软化再说。 |
| Your idea is wrong. | I see it differently. | 别否定人，说看法不同。 |
| I can't agree more. | I couldn't agree more. | 固定说法用 couldn't。 |
| I have different opinion. | I have a different view. | opinion 前加 a，view 更委婉。 |

## 7. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Are you comfortable disagreeing with people? | 你习惯跟人意见不同吗？ |
| How do you disagree without upsetting people? | 怎么反对又不伤人？ |
| Do people in your team say what they think? | 你们组的人会说真话吗？ |
| What do you do when the boss disagrees with you? | 老板跟你意见不同你怎么办？ |
| Is it better to agree or to argue in a meeting? | 会上顺着说好还是争一争好？ |
