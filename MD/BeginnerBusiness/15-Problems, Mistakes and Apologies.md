# Problems, Mistakes and Apologies 出错、道歉与投诉

## 1. 承认错误

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| My mistake. | **My mistake** — I'll fix it now. | 是我的错，我现在改。 |
| Sorry, that's on me. | **Sorry, that's on me** — I sent the wrong file. | 抱歉是我的问题，我发错文件了。 |
| I got it wrong. | **I got it wrong** — the figure is 40, not 14. | 我弄错了，数字是 40 不是 14。 |
| I should have checked. | **I should have checked** before sending it. | 我发之前应该核一下。 |
| That was my call. | **That was my call** — I made the decision. | 那是我拍的板，决定是我做的。 |
| I take full responsibility. | **I take full responsibility** for the delay. | 延期的事我负全责。 |

## 2. 道歉的轻重

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| My bad. | **My bad** — I'll resend it. | 我的锅，我重发。 |
| Sorry about that. | **Sorry about that** — it won't happen again. | 抱歉，不会再发生了。 |
| I'm really sorry. | **I'm really sorry** — that cost you time. | 非常抱歉，耽误你时间了。 |
| I apologize for + 事情. | **I apologize for** the confusion. | 造成的混乱我很抱歉。 |
| Please accept my apologies. | **Please accept my apologies** on behalf of the team. | 我代表团队向你道歉。 |
| I'm sorry to hear that. | **I'm sorry to hear that** — how can I help? | 听到这个我很遗憾，我能帮什么？ |

## 3. 说怎么补救

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'll fix it today. | **I'll fix it today** and send you the new one. | 我今天就改好，把新的发你。 |
| Here's what I'll do. | **Here's what I'll do** — reship on Monday. | 我这么办，周一重发。 |
| We'll cover the cost. | **We'll cover the cost** of the return. | 退货运费我们出。 |
| I'll keep you updated. | **I'll keep you updated** until it's done. | 到做完之前我每天跟你同步。 |
| Can I send a replacement? | **Can I send a replacement** this week? | 我这周补发一个行吗？ |
| It won't happen again. | **It won't happen again** — we added a check. | 不会再发生了，我们加了道检查。 |

## 4. 回消息慢了

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sorry for the late reply. | **Sorry for the late reply** — I was out yesterday. | 抱歉回晚了，我昨天不在。 |
| Sorry I missed this. | **Sorry I missed this** — it got buried. | 抱歉漏看了，消息太多压下去了。 |
| I only saw it now. | **I only saw it now** — I was in meetings. | 我刚看到，一直在开会。 |
| Thanks for your patience. | **Thanks for your patience** — here it is. | 谢谢你等着，这个给你。 |
| Apologies for the delay. | **Apologies for the delay** on the quote. | 报价晚了，抱歉。 |

## 5. 提问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| There's a problem with + 事情. | **There's a problem with** the shipment. | 这批货有问题。 |
| We have an issue. | **We have an issue** with the packing. | 包装上出了个问题。 |
| The quality isn't right. | **The quality isn't right** — 20 pieces are cracked. | 质量不对，有 20 个是裂的。 |
| This isn't what we ordered. | **This isn't what we ordered** — wrong color. | 这不是我们订的，颜色不对。 |
| Can you look into it? | **Can you look into it** today? | 你今天能查一下吗？ |
| I need to flag something. | **I need to flag something** about the invoice. | 发票这边我得提个问题。 |

## 6. 投诉要说法

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| This is the second time. | **This is the second time** this month. | 这个月第二次了。 |
| That's not good enough. | **That's not good enough** — we lost a client. | 这样不行，我们丢了个客户。 |
| I'd like an explanation. | **I'd like an explanation** by Friday. | 我希望周五前有个解释。 |
| We need to sort this out. | **We need to sort this out** properly. | 这事得好好解决。 |
| Let's fix the process. | **Let's fix the process**, not just this order. | 我们改流程，不只是这一单。 |

## 7. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I am sorry for my late. | Sorry I'm late. | late 是形容词，不能说 my late。 |
| This is your mistake. | Let's see what went wrong. | 别指责对方，说事。 |
| I very sorry. | I'm very sorry. | 要加 be 动词。 |
| Sorry to trouble you. | Sorry for the confusion. | trouble you 偏中式。 |
| It's not my fault. | Let me check what happened on my side. | 先查再定责。 |
| Please forgive me. | I apologize for the delay. | forgive 太重，用 apologize。 |
