# Phone and Video Calls 电话与视频会议

## 1. 打和接工作电话

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| This is Wei speaking. | **This is Wei speaking** — is Anna there? | 我是伟，Anna 在吗？ |
| Is now a good time? | **Is now a good time?** I can call back. | 现在方便吗？我可以晚点打。 |
| I'm calling about + 事情 | **I'm calling about** the shipment on order 4471. | 我打电话是想问 4471 那单的发货。 |
| Can I call you back in ten? | **Can I call you back in ten** — I'm walking in. | 我十分钟后回你，正进门。 |
| Could you hold for a second? | **Could you hold for a second?** Another call. | 稍等一下好吗？有另一个电话。 |
| She's not at her desk. | **She's not at her desk** — can I take a message? | 她不在工位，要留言吗？ |
| Sorry, wrong number. | **Sorry, wrong number** — my mistake. | 抱歉打错了。 |

## 2. 线上会议开场

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can everyone hear me? | **Can everyone hear me?** Say something. | 大家能听到我吗？说句话。 |
| You're on mute. | **You're on mute** — we can't hear you. | 你静音了，听不到。 |
| Let me share my screen. | **Let me share my screen** — can you see it? | 我共享一下屏幕，能看到吗？ |
| I'll turn on my camera. | **I'll turn on my camera** — one second. | 我开一下摄像头，等一下。 |
| Can you see my slides? | **Can you see my slides** or just the desktop? | 你能看到我的幻灯片吗？还是只有桌面？ |
| Let's give people two minutes. | **Let's give people two minutes** to join. | 再等两分钟，让人进来。 |

## 3. 技术问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| My camera isn't working. | **My camera isn't working** — I'll type instead. | 我摄像头不好使，我打字吧。 |
| The audio is cutting out. | **The audio is cutting out** — can you hear me now? | 声音断断续续的，现在能听到吗？ |
| I lost connection. | **I lost connection** for about a minute. | 我掉线了大概一分钟。 |
| Let me switch to my phone. | **Let me switch to my phone** — the laptop mic is bad. | 我换手机连，笔记本麦克不行。 |
| Can you re-share your screen? | **Can you re-share your screen?** It stopped. | 你能重新共享一下屏幕吗？停了。 |
| Let's try again in five. | **Let's try again in five** — everyone rejoin. | 五分钟后重试，大家重新进。 |

## 4. 听不清和信号差

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sorry, you're breaking up. | **Sorry, you're breaking up** — say that again? | 抱歉你那边断断续续，再说一遍？ |
| Could you speak up a little? | **Could you speak up a little** — I'm in a taxi. | 你能大点声吗？我在出租车上。 |
| Could you say that more slowly? | **Could you say that more slowly** — my English isn't great. | 你能说慢一点吗？我英语一般。 |
| The subway has no signal. | **The subway has no signal** — I'll message you. | 地铁里没信号，我发消息给你。 |
| Let's move to the chat. | **Let's move to the chat** — it keeps dropping. | 我们换文字聊吧，老断。 |
| It's noisy on my end. | **It's noisy on my end** — I'm at the factory. | 我这边太吵，我在工厂。 |

## 5. 结束通话和会议

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'll let you get back to work. | **I'll let you get back to work.** Thanks for the call. | 不耽误你了，谢谢来电。 |
| Let's wrap up here. | **Let's wrap up here** — we're out of time. | 我们就到这吧，时间到了。 |
| I'll send a recap. | **I'll send a recap** right after this. | 会后我马上把纪要发出来。 |
| Thanks, everyone. | **Thanks, everyone** — good discussion. | 谢谢大家，讨论得不错。 |
| Talk soon. | **Talk soon** — bye for now. | 回头聊，先这样。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Please hold on a moment, I will check. | Let me check — one second. | 别用 please hold on a moment。 |
| My English is poor, please forgive me. | Sorry, could you say that again? | 别自我贬低，直接请对方重复。 |
| I can't hear you clearly. | You're breaking up. | 断断续续用 breaking up。 |
| Can you open your camera? | Can you turn on your camera? | 开摄像头用 turn on。 |
| Please listen to me carefully. | Can you hear me OK? | 别命令对方，先确认能不能听见。 |
| I will call to you later. | I'll call you later. | call 后面直接接人。 |
