# Business Meals 商务饭局

## 1. 邀请和订位

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me take you to dinner. | **Let me take you to dinner** tonight. | 今晚我请你吃饭。 |
| Do you have any plans tonight? | **Do you have any plans tonight?** | 你今晚有安排吗？ |
| I've booked a table for 7. | **I've booked a table for 7** near the hotel. | 我订了七点的桌，就在酒店附近。 |
| Is Chinese food OK? | **Is Chinese food OK**, or would you prefer Western? | 中餐可以吗？还是你想吃西餐？ |
| How many people are coming? | **How many people are coming** — should I book for six? | 来几个人？我订六人桌？ |
| I'll pick you up at 6:30. | **I'll pick you up at 6:30** in the lobby. | 六点半我在大堂接你。 |

## 2. 点菜

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you eat spicy food? | **Do you eat spicy food?** This place is known for it. | 你吃辣吗？这家以辣出名。 |
| Anything you don't eat? | **Anything you don't eat?** Any allergies? | 有什么不吃的吗？有过敏吗？ |
| What would you recommend? | **What would you recommend** here? | 这儿你推荐什么？ |
| Let's get a few dishes to share. | **Let's get a few dishes to share.** | 我们点几个菜一起吃。 |
| This is a local specialty. | **This is a local specialty** — river fish. | 这是本地特色，河鱼。 |
| I'll order for us. | **I'll order for us** if that's OK. | 我来点，可以吗？ |

## 3. 敬酒和碰杯

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me propose a toast. | **Let me propose a toast** to the new project. | 我提议为新项目干一杯。 |
| Cheers! | **Cheers!** Thanks for coming all this way. | 干杯！谢谢你们跑这么远。 |
| I'll drink to that. | **I'll drink to that.** | 这个我喝。 |
| I don't drink, but I'll join. | **I don't drink, but I'll join** with tea. | 我不喝酒，以茶代酒。 |
| Just a small one for me. | **Just a small one for me** — I'm driving. | 我就一小杯，我开车。 |
| One more round? | **One more round?** Or should we call it? | 再来一轮？还是就到这？ |

## 4. 桌上聊天

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Is this your first time in China? | **Is this your first time in China?** | 你第一次来中国吗？ |
| How do you like the food? | **How do you like the food** so far? | 这些菜还合口味吗？ |
| Try this one — it's mild. | **Try this one** — it's mild. | 尝尝这个，不辣。 |
| In China we usually share dishes. | **In China we usually share dishes.** | 我们这边一般是合餐。 |
| How's the hotel? | **How's the hotel?** Is the location OK? | 酒店怎么样？位置方便吗？ |
| Do you have time to see the city? | **Do you have time to see the city** tomorrow? | 你明天有空逛逛吗？ |

## 5. 买单和收尾

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let me get this. | **Let me get this** — you're our guest. | 我来买单，你是客人。 |
| It's on me. | **It's on me** tonight. | 今晚我请。 |
| Let's split it. | **Let's split it** — easier that way. | 我们 AA 吧，这样简单。 |
| Can we have the check, please? | **Can we have the check, please?** | 买单。 |
| Can I get a receipt? | **Can I get a receipt** for the company? | 能开发票吗？公司报销用。 |
| Should we call it a night? | **Should we call it a night?** Big day tomorrow. | 今天到这儿吧？明天还一天。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I respect you, I finish. | Let me propose a toast. | 别说 I respect you。 |
| Please eat more, don't be polite. | Please help yourself. | 劝菜别直译。 |
| Do you want to eat something? | Would you like something to eat? | Would you like 更礼貌。 |
| I am full, I can't eat. | I'm full, thanks. | 加 thanks 更自然。 |
| Please sit here, this is the best seat. | Please take this seat. | 别解释"最好的位置"。 |
| I will pay the bill for you. | Let me get this. | 买单用 let me get this。 |
