# Speaking Up in a Meeting 会上发言

## 1. 插话

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can I jump in? | **Can I jump in** for one second? | 我能插一句吗？就一下。 |
| Sorry, can I say something? | **Sorry, can I say something** about the timeline? | 抱歉，关于时间表我能说两句吗？ |
| Just quickly — | **Just quickly** — the client already agreed. | 快说一下，客户已经同意了。 |
| Can I add one thing? | **Can I add one thing** before we move on? | 往下走之前我能补一句吗？ |
| Sorry to interrupt. | **Sorry to interrupt** — the numbers don't match. | 抱歉打断一下，数字对不上。 |
| Can I come back to that? | **Can I come back to that** in a minute? | 这个我能等会儿再说吗？ |

## 2. 问问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Quick question. | **Quick question** — who signs off on this? | 问个问题，这个谁签批？ |
| Can I ask + 问题? | **Can I ask** why we're changing it now? | 我能问下为什么现在改吗？ |
| What do you mean by + 词? | **What do you mean by** "final version"? | 你说的 final version 是指什么？ |
| Could you go back a slide? | **Could you go back a slide** — the table. | 能翻回上一页吗？那个表。 |
| How does that work in practice? | **How does that work in practice?** | 实际操作上怎么弄？ |
| Can you send that after? | **Can you send that after** the meeting? | 会后你能发我一下吗？ |

## 3. 补充和附和

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Just to add to that… | **Just to add to that** — we tried it in 2024. | 补充一句，我们 2024 年试过。 |
| Same here. | **Same here** — our factory has the same issue. | 我们也是，我们工厂同样的问题。 |
| I agree with Anna. | **I agree with Anna** — the price is the problem. | 我同意 Anna，问题在价格。 |
| That's a good point. | **That's a good point** about the lead time. | 交期这点说得好。 |
| Building on what Lei said… | **Building on what Lei said**, we could split it. | 接着磊说的，我们可以拆开做。 |
| Exactly. | **Exactly** — that's what I meant. | 对，我就是这个意思。 |

## 4. 不确定和不知道

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm not sure, but… | **I'm not sure, but** I think it's 20%. | 我不太确定，我觉得是 20%。 |
| I'd have to check. | **I'd have to check** — I don't want to guess. | 我得查一下，不想瞎说。 |
| I don't have that in front of me. | **I don't have that in front of me** — one minute. | 我手边没这个数据，等我一下。 |
| Let me get back to you. | **Let me get back to you** on the exact number. | 具体数字我回头答复你。 |
| I might be wrong here. | **I might be wrong here** — correct me if so. | 我可能说得不对，不对你纠正我。 |
| Don't quote me on this. | **Don't quote me on this** — it's not official yet. | 这个别当准，还没正式定。 |

## 5. 让别人发言

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What do you think, Lei? | **What do you think, Lei?** You know the client best. | 磊你怎么看？你最了解客户。 |
| Anna, how about you? | **Anna, how about you** — you're closer to it. | Anna 你呢？你更了解情况。 |
| Anyone else want to add? | **Anyone else want to add** something? | 还有谁要补充吗？ |
| Let's hear from the factory side. | **Let's hear from the factory side.** | 听一下工厂那边的意见。 |
| I don't want to put you on the spot. | **I don't want to put you on the spot** — just your view. | 不是非要你表态，就想听你的看法。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I want to say two points. | I have two things to add. | point 太书面，口语用 things。 |
| Please let me speak. | Can I jump in here? | 别用命令式让别人让你说。 |
| My opinion is that… | I think… | 口语里 I think 更自然。 |
| I have a question to ask you. | Can I ask you something? | 中式，直接用 Can I ask。 |
| I can't agree your idea. | I don't agree with your idea. | agree 后面要加 with。 |
| Sorry, my English is not good. | Sorry, let me say that again. | 别每次道歉，重说就好。 |
