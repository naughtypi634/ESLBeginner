# Price and Discount 谈价格与折扣

## 1. 问价和报价

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What's your price for + 数量? | **What's your price for** 1,000 units? | 1000 台你们什么价？ |
| Can you quote us for + 数量? | **Can you quote us for** 5,000 pieces? | 能报 5000 个的价吗？ |
| Our price is + 金额 + per unit. | **Our price is** 12 dollars **per unit**. | 我们的价格是每台 12 美元。 |
| That's FOB Shanghai. | **That's FOB Shanghai** — shipping is extra. | 这是上海离岸价，运费另算。 |
| The price depends on the quantity. | **The price depends on the quantity.** | 价格要看数量。 |
| This quote is valid for 30 days. | **This quote is valid for 30 days.** | 这个报价 30 天有效。 |

## 2. 说贵

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| That's a bit high for us. | **That's a bit high for us** — can we talk? | 对我们来说有点高，能谈谈吗？ |
| Your price is above our budget. | **Your price is above our budget** this year. | 你们的价格超出我们今年预算。 |
| We got a lower quote elsewhere. | **We got a lower quote elsewhere** — 10 dollars. | 别家报得更低，10 美元。 |
| It's not as competitive as we hoped. | **It's not as competitive as we hoped.** | 没有我们期望的那么有竞争力。 |
| Can you do better on the price? | **Can you do better on the price?** | 价格上还能再让点吗？ |
| The exchange rate is hurting us. | **The exchange rate is hurting us** this quarter. | 这季度汇率对我们不利。 |

## 3. 要折扣

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can you give us a discount? | **Can you give us a discount** for 5,000? | 5000 台能给个折扣吗？ |
| What's your best price? | **What's your best price** for a yearly order? | 年度订单你们最低什么价？ |
| How about 5% off? | **How about 5% off** if we pay in advance? | 预付的话打 95 折怎么样？ |
| Can you match that price? | **Can you match that price** — 10 dollars? | 你们能做到那个价吗？10 美元。 |
| What if we order more? | **What if we order more** — would that help? | 我们多订点呢？有用吗？ |
| Is there any room on the price? | **Is there any room on the price?** | 价格还有空间吗？ |

## 4. 给折扣或者守住价格

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We can do 3%. | **We can do 3%** if you order 5,000. | 订 5000 的话我们能给 3%。 |
| Our price is already tight. | **Our price is already tight** — margins are thin. | 我们的价格已经很紧了，利润很薄。 |
| I can't go below that. | **I can't go below that** without losing money. | 再低就亏了，我不能降。 |
| That's our best offer. | **That's our best offer** — I'm being straight. | 这是我们的底价，我跟你说实话。 |
| The material cost went up 8%. | **The material cost went up 8%** since spring. | 材料成本春天以来涨了 8%。 |
| We could do it if you commit. | **We could do it** if you commit to a year. | 你们签一年的话可以做。 |

## 5. 谈条件交换

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We can move on price. | **We can move on price** if you move on delivery. | 交期上你让一步，价格我让一步。 |
| If you order more, we can lower it. | **If you order more**, we can lower it. | 你们多订，我们就能降。 |
| Let's meet in the middle. | **Let's meet in the middle** — 11 dollars. | 我们各让一步，11 美元。 |
| I'll ask my manager. | **I'll ask my manager** and come back today. | 我问下主管，今天回复你。 |
| Can you give me something to work with? | **Can you give me something to work with?** | 你给个能谈的数吧。 |
| Let's put it in writing. | **Let's put it in writing** so we're clear. | 我们写下来，免得有歧义。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Your price is too expensive. | Your price is too high. | 价格高用 high，不用 expensive。 |
| Can you discount me? | Can you give me a discount? | discount 是名词，要加 give。 |
| I want a cheaper price. | Can you do a better price? | cheaper price 偏中式。 |
| The price is very cheap. | The price is very low. | 价格低用 low，东西便宜用 cheap。 |
| Give me your lowest price. | What's your best price? | 别用命令式要底价。 |
| We can't reduce any more. | That's our best offer. | 更委婉，也更常见。 |
