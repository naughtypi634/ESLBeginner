# Introducing Yourself at Work 职场自我介绍

## 1. 说自己的名字和职位

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm + 名字 | **I'm Wei** — I just joined the marketing team. | 我叫伟，刚进市场部。 |
| I'm on the + 团队 | **I'm on the** supply chain team, on the third floor. | 我在供应链组，在三楼。 |
| I work in + 部门 | **I work in** finance — I do the monthly reports. | 我在财务，做每月的报表。 |
| I work for + 公司 | **I work for** a German car parts company. | 我在一家德国汽车零件公司上班。 |
| I'm based in + 城市 | **I'm based in** Shenzhen, but I travel a lot. | 我常驻深圳，但经常出差。 |
| I've been here for + 时间 | **I've been here for** two years this June. | 到今年六月我就满两年了。 |
| This is my + 第几天 | **This is my** third day — still lost in this building. | 我才来第三天，楼里还老迷路。 |

## 2. 问对方是做什么的

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What do you do? | **What do you do** at the Shanghai office? | 你在上海办公室做什么？ |
| Which team are you on? | **Which team are you on** — app or hardware? | 你在哪个组？App 还是硬件？ |
| How long have you been here? | **How long have you been here?** Two years? | 你来多久了？两年？ |
| Who do you report to? | **Who do you report to** — Anna or David? | 你向谁汇报？Anna 还是 David？ |
| Where are you based? | **Where are you based** — Shanghai or Suzhou? | 你常驻哪里？上海还是苏州？ |

## 3. 第一次见面

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Nice to meet you. | **Nice to meet you** — I've seen your emails. | 很高兴认识你，你的邮件我看过不少。 |
| Good to put a face to the name. | **Good to put a face to the name** — finally. | 邮件来回几个月，终于见到本人了。 |
| How do you say your name? | **How do you say your name** — Lay or Lee? | 你名字怎么念？Lay 还是 Lee？ |
| I'm bad with names. | **I'm bad with names** — remind me? | 我记名字很差，再说一遍？ |
| We've met before, right? | **We've met before, right?** At the trade fair? | 我们见过吧？四月的展会上？ |

## 4. 加微信、钉钉和换名片

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's connect on WeChat. | **Let's connect on WeChat** — I'll send my QR code. | 加个微信吧，我把二维码发你。 |
| Can I scan your code? | **Can I scan your code?** My phone's right here. | 我扫你吧？手机就在手上。 |
| I'll add you on DingTalk. | **I'll add you on DingTalk** — that's what we use. | 我在钉钉上加你，我们上班都用。 |

| I'll add you to the group. | **I'll add you to the group** so you get the updates. | 我把你拉进群，通知你都能收到。 |

## 5. 介绍别人认识

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| This is Lei — he does design. | **This is Lei** — he handles all our design. | 这是磊，设计都归他管。 |
| Have you two met? | **Have you two met?** Lei, this is Anna from Berlin. | 你们俩认识吗？这是柏林的 Anna。 |
| Lei, meet Anna. | **Lei, meet Anna** — she's our buyer in Berlin. | 磊，这是 Anna，我们柏林的采购。 |
| She's new here too. | **She's new here too** — she started two weeks ago. | 她也是新来的，两周前入职。 |

## 6. 怎么称呼对方

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Please call me Wei. | **Please call me Wei** — everyone here does. | 叫我伟就行，这儿都这么叫。 |
| Do you go by Anna or Ms. Keller? | **Do you go by Anna or Ms. Keller?** | 我该叫你 Anna 还是 Keller 女士？ |
| You can just use my first name. | **You can just use my first name** — no Mr. | 直接叫我名字，不用加先生。 |
| Sorry, I didn't catch your title. | **Sorry, I didn't catch your title.** | 没听清你的职位，你是产品经理吗？ |

## 7. 第一次见面怎么收尾

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Anyway, good to finally meet you. | **Anyway, good to finally meet you.** | 总之很高兴见到你，先忙你的。 |
| I'll ping you on WeChat. | **I'll ping you on WeChat** about lunch. | 明天午饭的事我微信喊你。 |
| Let's grab coffee sometime. | **Let's grab coffee sometime** — floor 5? | 改天一起喝咖啡，五楼那台不错。 |
| Come find me if you need anything. | **Come find me if you need anything.** | 有事来找我，我坐窗边。 |

## 8. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I am a staff in this company. | I work at this company. | 说一个人别用 staff，用 employee。 |
| I am from marketing department. | I'm in the marketing department. | 部门前加 the，from 换成 in。 |
| I joined the company for two years. | I've been with the company two years. | 持续到现在用 have been。 |
| Please to meet you. | Nice to meet you. | 固定说法，没有 please to。 |
| This is my colleague, he is called Lei. | This is my colleague Lei. | 介绍人直接接名字。 |
| I am working here since 2024. | I've worked here since 2024. | since 配现在完成时。 |
