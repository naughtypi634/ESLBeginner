# Factory and Showroom Visits 参观工厂与展厅

## 1. 带着客户走

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| This way, please. | **This way, please** — the line starts here. | 这边请，产线从这儿开始。 |
| Watch your step. | **Watch your step** — there's a cable on the floor. | 小心脚下，地上有根线。 |
| Please put these on. | **Please put these on** — hair nets and shoe covers. | 请把这些戴上，发网和鞋套。 |
| No photos in this area. | **No photos in this area** — client rules. | 这个区域不能拍照，客户的规定。 |
| We'll stop here for questions. | **We'll stop here for questions** before we go on. | 我们在这儿停一下，有问题先问。 |
| It takes about 20 minutes. | **It takes about 20 minutes** to walk the whole line. | 走完整条线大概 20 分钟。 |

## 2. 介绍产线和产能

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| This line runs + 数量 + a day. | **This line runs** 2,000 units **a day**. | 这条线一天产 2000 台。 |
| We have + 数量 + lines. | **We have** four **lines** in this building. | 这栋楼里我们有四条线。 |
| Our capacity is + 数量. | **Our capacity is** 60,000 a month. | 我们的产能是每月六万台。 |
| This machine does + 事情. | **This machine does** the welding automatically. | 这台机器自动焊接。 |
| We check every unit here. | **We check every unit here** before packing. | 每台都在这里检查完才包装。 |
| Lead time is about + 时间. | **Lead time is about** 30 days after deposit. | 定金后交期约 30 天。 |

## 3. 客户常问的问题

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How many workers do you have? | **How many workers do you have** on this line? | 这条线有多少工人？ |
| Can you do custom packaging? | **Can you do custom packaging?** We need our logo. | 能做定制包装吗？要印我们的标。 |
| What's your monthly output? | **What's your monthly output?** | 你们月产量多少？ |
| Do you make everything in-house? | **Do you make everything in-house?** | 所有工序都是你们自己做吗？ |
| What's your defect rate? | **What's your defect rate?** | 你们的次品率是多少？ |
| Can we see the QC room? | **Can we see the QC room** as well? | 能也看看质检室吗？ |

## 4. 答不上来的时候

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| That's a good question. | **That's a good question** — let me ask the engineer. | 这个问题问得好，我问下工程师。 |
| I'll confirm the exact number. | **I'll confirm the exact number** by tomorrow. | 准确数字我明天确认给你。 |
| Let me get the manager. | **Let me get the manager** — she runs this line. | 我把主管叫来，这条线她管。 |
| I don't want to give you a wrong number. | **I don't want to give you a wrong number.** | 我不想给你个错数字。 |
| We can test that for you. | **We can test that for you** — give us a week. | 我们可以给你做个测试，给一周。 |
| I'll put it in writing. | **I'll put it in writing** after the visit. | 参观完我书面发你。 |

## 5. 样品和打样

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Here are some samples. | **Here are some samples** — take them with you. | 这些是样品，你带走。 |
| We can send samples by Friday. | **We can send samples by Friday.** | 样品我们周五前能寄出。 |
| Sample cost is + 金额. | **Sample cost is** 200 yuan, refunded on order. | 样品费 200 元，下单后退。 |
| It takes a week to make. | **It takes a week to make** a new sample. | 做一个新样品要一周。 |
| Do you want any changes? | **Do you want any changes** to the sample? | 样品要改哪里吗？ |
| We'll courier it to Berlin. | **We'll courier it to Berlin** — three days. | 我们用快递寄到柏林，三天到。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Please mind your head. | Mind your head. | 固定说法不加 please。 |
| Here is our produce line. | Here's our production line. | 产线是 production line。 |
| We have 200 workers in this factory. | We have 200 workers at this factory. | 用 at this factory 更自然。 |
| The machine is produced in Germany. | The machine is made in Germany. | 产地用 made in。 |
| Our quality is very high level. | Our quality is high. | 别用 very high level。 |
| Please follow me to here. | Please follow me. | to here 是多余的。 |
