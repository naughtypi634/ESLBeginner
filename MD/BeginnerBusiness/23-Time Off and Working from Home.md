# Time Off and Working from Home 请假、调休与居家办公

## 1. 请年假

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to take some time off. | **I'd like to take some time off** next month. | 我想下个月请几天假。 |
| I'm taking + 时间 + off. | **I'm taking** three days **off** in October. | 我十月请三天假。 |
| Can I book annual leave for + 时间? | **Can I book annual leave** for the 12th to 14th? | 12 到 14 号我能请年假吗？ |
| I'll be away for a week. | **I'll be away for a week** — is that OK? | 我要休一周，行吗？ |
| I've handed over my work. | **I've handed over my work** to Lei. | 我的活已经交给磊了。 |
| Who's covering while I'm away? | **Who's covering while I'm away?** | 我不在的时候谁顶？ |

## 2. 请病假

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm not feeling well today. | **I'm not feeling well today** — I'll work from home. | 我今天不太舒服，在家办公。 |
| I need to take a sick day. | **I need to take a sick day** — I have a fever. | 我得请一天病假，我发烧了。 |
| I'll send a doctor's note. | **I'll send a doctor's note** this afternoon. | 我下午把病假条发过来。 |
| I've lost my voice. | **I've lost my voice** — can we do this by text? | 我嗓子哑了，能打字聊吗？ |
| I'll be offline this morning. | **I'll be offline this morning** — at the clinic. | 我上午不在线，在医院。 |
| I should be back tomorrow. | **I should be back tomorrow** if I feel better. | 好点的话我明天就回来。 |

## 4. 调休和排班

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'll take a comp day. | **I'll take a comp day** for the weekend work. | 周末加班我想调休一天。 |

## 5. 居家办公和弹性工时

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Can I work from home tomorrow? | **Can I work from home tomorrow?** | 我明天能居家办公吗？ |
| I'm on a flexible schedule. | **I'm on a flexible schedule** — I start at 10. | 我是弹性工时，十点上班。 |
| I'll be online but quiet. | **I'll be online but quiet** — deep work today. | 我在线但不太说话，今天要专心。 |
| My internet is down. | **My internet is down** — using my phone. | 我家网断了，用手机热点。 |
| I'll set an out-of-office. | **I'll set an out-of-office** before I leave. | 我走之前设个自动回复。 |
| I can't take calls at home. | **I can't take calls at home** — kids are off school. | 我在家接不了电话，孩子放假。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I want to ask for leave tomorrow. | I'd like to take tomorrow off. | 请假说 take…off。 |
| I will take leave for 3 days. | I'm taking three days off. | 说天数更自然。 |
| I am sick today, so I won't come. | I'm not feeling well, so I'll work from home. | 更委婉，也给个方案。 |
| Can I absent tomorrow? | Can I take tomorrow off? | absent 是形容词，不能这么用。 |
| I will rest at home. | I'll be working from home. | rest at home 听起来像纯休息。 |
| Please approve my leave. | Would you be able to approve my leave? | 加 would you 更礼貌。 |
