# Follow-Ups and Reminders 催进度与跟进

## 1. 跟进

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Just following up on + 事情. | **Just following up on** the invoice I sent Monday. | 跟进一下我周一发的发票。 |
| Any update on + 事情? | **Any update on** the sample order? | 样品单有进展吗？ |
| Did you get a chance to look? | **Did you get a chance to look** at the file? | 那个文件你看了吗？ |
| Just checking in. | **Just checking in** — no rush. | 就是问一下，不急。 |
| Where are we on + 事情? | **Where are we on** the contract? | 合同这边到哪一步了？ |
| Circling back on this. | **Circling back on this** — any news? | 再问一下这个，有消息吗？ |

## 2. 催但不冒犯

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Sorry to chase you. | **Sorry to chase you** — the client is asking. | 不好意思催你，客户在问。 |
| I need it by Thursday. | **I need it by Thursday** — the deadline is tight. | 我周四必须要，期限很紧。 |
| Could you send it today? | **Could you send it today** if possible? | 方便的话今天能发我吗？ |
| This is now urgent. | **This is now urgent** — the ship leaves Friday. | 现在这个很急，船周五走。 |
| Can I put a deadline on this? | **Can I put a deadline on this** — Friday? | 这个定个期限行吗？周五。 |
| Second reminder on this. | **Second reminder on this** — see my email below. | 这是第二次提醒，见下面的邮件。 |

## 3. 提醒别人 deadline

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Just a reminder — | **Just a reminder** — the report is due Friday. | 提醒一下，报告周五到期。 |
| Heads-up: | **Heads-up:** the meeting moved to 2 p.m. | 提个醒，会议改到下午两点了。 |
| Don't forget + 事情. | **Don't forget** to send the file tonight. | 别忘了今晚把文件发出来。 |
| The deadline is tomorrow. | **The deadline is tomorrow** at noon. | 截止是明天中午。 |
| You're on for + 事情. | **You're on for** the slides, right? | 幻灯片是你做，对吧？ |

## 4. 收到并确认

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Got it. | **Got it** — I'll take care of it. | 收到，我来处理。 |
| Will do. | **Will do** — by end of day. | 好的，下班前搞定。 |
| Noted. | **Noted** — I'll add it to the list. | 记下了，我加进清单。 |
| On it. | **On it** — give me an hour. | 在弄了，给我一小时。 |
| Thanks for the reminder. | **Thanks for the reminder** — I almost forgot. | 谢谢提醒，我差点忘了。 |
| Let me confirm after I check. | **Let me confirm after I check** the stock. | 我查完库存再确认。 |

## 5. 会后的跟进

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Here's a quick recap. | **Here's a quick recap** of what we agreed. | 简单回顾一下我们说好的。 |
| Action items are below. | **Action items are below** — check your name. | 待办事项在下面，看下你的名字。 |
| I'll own item one. | **I'll own item one** — Lei takes item two. | 第一项我来，磊负责第二项。 |
| Let's review on Friday. | **Let's review on Friday** at the same time. | 我们周五同一时间过一遍。 |
| Anything I missed? | **Anything I missed?** Reply by tomorrow. | 有遗漏吗？明天前回复。 |
| I've put it in the group. | **I've put it in the group** — pin it if useful. | 我发群里了，有用就置顶。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I am waiting for your reply. | Could you let me know by Friday? | 催回复给出具体时间。 |
| Please reply me as soon as possible. | Could you get back to me today? | reply 后面不加人。 |
| I have sent you the file yesterday. | I sent you the file yesterday. | 有过去时间用一般过去时。 |
| Please remember to do it. | Just a reminder to do it. | remember 有命令感。 |
| You didn't reply my email. | I haven't heard back on my email. | 更委婉，reply 不加人。 |
| Do it before Friday. | Could you have it done by Friday? | 加 could you 更礼貌。 |
