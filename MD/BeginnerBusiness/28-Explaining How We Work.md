# Explaining How We Work 解释我们的工作方式

## 1. 回答"你们怎么协作"

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| We mostly use WeChat groups. | **We mostly use WeChat groups** — email is for outside. | 我们主要用微信群，邮件对外用。 |
| Everything goes through my manager. | **Everything goes through my manager** first. | 什么都先过我主管。 |
| We decide fast if it's small. | **We decide fast if it's small** — one call. | 小事决定得快，一个电话的事。 |
| Big decisions take longer. | **Big decisions take longer** — three approvals. | 大事慢，要走三道审批。 |
| We work closely with the factory. | **We work closely with the factory** — same town. | 我们跟工厂走得很近，在同一个镇。 |
| My boss has the final say. | **My boss has the final say.** | 最后是我老板拍板。 |

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Our culture is very special and deep. | Let me show you how we usually do it. | 别用 culture 做万能解释。 |
| You can't understand Chinese culture. | It takes a while to get used to. | 别说对方理解不了。 |
| In China we always work overtime. | Many teams work late in peak season. | 避免 always。 |
| Our way is better. | Both ways have pros and cons. | 别评判优劣。 |
| Sorry, China is different. | It works differently here. | 用陈述代替道歉。 |
| It's tradition, you won't get it. | It's a habit here — happy to explain. | 别用 tradition 挡问题。 |
