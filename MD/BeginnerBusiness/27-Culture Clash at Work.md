# Culture Clash at Work 跨文化误会

## 1. 直说还是委婉

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| In China we usually hint. | **In China we usually hint** — we don't say no directly. | 我们国内一般会暗示，不直接说不行。 |
| They're often more direct. | **They're often more direct** — don't take it personally. | 他们说话更直接，别往心里去。 |
| "Not ideal" usually means no. | **"Not ideal" usually means no.** | 说"不太理想"通常就是不行。 |
| They expect you to say no. | **They expect you to say no** if you disagree. | 你不同意就直接说，他们希望你这样。 |
| It's not rude, it's just direct. | **It's not rude, it's just direct.** | 这不是没礼貌，只是直接。 |
| I'll be honest with you. | **I'll be honest with you** — I don't think this works. | 我说实话，我觉得这样不行。 |

## 2. 开会方式的差异

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| People jump in without waiting. | **People jump in without waiting** — that's normal. | 这儿的人不等别人说完就插话，很正常。 |
| Silence can mean disagreement. | **Silence can mean disagreement** in some meetings. | 有些会上，没人说话其实就是不同意。 |
| First names, even with the boss. | **First names, even with the boss** — that's normal. | 直呼老板名字，在他们那儿很正常。 |
| They expect you to give an opinion. | **They expect you to give an opinion** — say something. | 他们期待你发表意见，说点什么。 |
| Small talk comes before business. | **Small talk comes before business** — five minutes. | 他们先寒暄再谈正事，大概五分钟。 |
| Deadlines are firm there. | **Deadlines are firm there** — a day late is late. | 他们那边截止期很硬，晚一天就是晚。 |

## 4. 饭桌、礼物和小事

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Don't bring up salary or politics. | **Don't bring up salary or politics** at dinner. | 饭桌上别聊工资和政治。 |
| Keep gifts small and useful. | **Keep gifts small and useful** — tea is fine. | 礼物小而实用就行，茶叶就不错。 |

## 5. 遇到不舒服时怎么说

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| That's not how we usually do it. | **That's not how we usually do it** here. | 我们这边一般不这么做。 |
| Can I explain how it works here? | **Can I explain how it works here?** | 我解释一下我们这边的做法行吗？ |
| I think there's a misunderstanding. | **I think there's a misunderstanding** about the date. | 日期上我想有个误会。 |
| I don't want to be rude. | **I don't want to be rude** — just checking. | 我不是要冒犯，就是确认一下。 |
| Let's talk about it offline. | **Let's talk about it offline** — not in the meeting. | 这个私下聊，别在会上说。 |
| I'll take that as feedback. | **I'll take that as feedback** — thanks. | 我当建议收下了，谢谢。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| You are too direct, it is impolite. | That's more direct than we're used to. | 别直接说对方没礼貌。 |
| Foreigners don't understand China. | It works differently here — let me explain. | 别说"外国人不懂中国"。 |
| Sorry, our culture is different. | In China we usually do it this way. | 用陈述代替道歉。 |
| I can't drink, it's our culture. | I don't drink, but I'll join with tea. | culture 不是挡酒的理由。 |
| You should learn Chinese culture. | Let me tell you how we do it here. | 别要求对方去学。 |
| Chinese people always work overtime. | Many teams work late in peak season. | 避免以偏概全。 |

## 7. Discussion 讨论

| Question | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| Have you worked with people from other countries? | 你跟外国人共事过吗？ |
| What surprised you most about foreign colleagues? | 外国同事最让你意外的是什么？ |
| Is direct feedback rude in your view? | 你觉得直接给反馈是没礼貌吗？ |
| What's hard to explain about Chinese work culture? | 中国职场的哪些习惯不好跟老外解释？ |
| What do foreigners usually get wrong about us? | 老外通常误会我们什么？ |
