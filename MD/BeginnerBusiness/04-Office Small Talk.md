# Office Small Talk 办公室寒暄

## 1. 电梯里、茶水间怎么开场

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Morning! How's it going? | **Morning! How's it going?** Busy day? | 早，怎么样？今天忙吗？ |
| How was your weekend? | **How was your weekend?** Did you go anywhere? | 周末过得怎么样？出去了吗？ |
| Long time no see! | **Long time no see!** Have you been traveling? | 好久不见，你出差了？ |
| Did you have a good break? | **Did you have a good break?** You look rested. | 假期过得好吗？看着精神不错。 |
| Are you in line for coffee? | **Are you in line for coffee?** I'll wait with you. | 你也来买咖啡？我跟你一起排。 |
| Nice weather today, huh? | **Nice weather today, huh?** Finally some sun. | 今天天气不错啊，终于出太阳了。 |
| Is the elevator always this slow? | **Is the elevator always this slow?** I've waited 4 minutes. | 电梯一直这么慢吗？我等四分钟了。 |

## 3. 聊通勤和住哪儿

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| How long is your commute? | **How long is your commute?** Mine's 50 minutes. | 你通勤多久？我要 50 分钟。 |
| I take Line 2 the whole way. | **I take Line 2 the whole way** — no transfer. | 我一趟 2 号线到底，不用换乘。 |
| I bike to work when it's nice. | **I bike to work when it's nice** — 20 minutes. | 天气好我就骑车上班，二十分钟。 |
| Do you live near the office? | **Do you live near the office?** Rents there are crazy. | 你住公司附近吗？那边房租太贵。 |
| I moved closer last year. | **I moved closer last year** — worth every yuan. | 我去年搬近了，多花的钱很值。 |
| I leave at 7 to beat the rush. | **I leave at 7 to beat the rush** — otherwise it's packed. | 我七点出门躲高峰，不然挤死。 |

## 5. 回应和追问

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| Really? Which show? | **Really? Which show?** I need something new. | 真的？哪部剧？我正缺新剧看。 |
| That sounds nice. | **That sounds nice** — I've wanted to try that. | 听着不错，我一直想试试。 |
| How was it? | **How was it?** Worth the trip? | 怎么样？值得跑一趟吗？ |
| Oh no, what happened? | **Oh no, what happened?** Was it serious? | 啊，怎么了？严重吗？ |
| Same here. | **Same here** — I did nothing all weekend. | 我也一样，整个周末啥也没干。 |
| Good for you! | **Good for you!** That's a nice break. | 挺好的！这样能歇歇。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| How about your weekend? | How was your weekend? | 问周末过得如何用 How was。 |
| I very like the weather today. | I really like the weather today. | 很喜欢用 really like。 |
| The weather is so good, right? | Nice weather today, huh? | 口语里常用 huh? 收尾。 |
| I am boring. | I'm bored. | 人无聊用 bored，事无聊用 boring。 |
| Long time no see, how are you recently? | Long time no see — how have you been? | 用 how have you been 更自然。 |
| I go to home by subway. | I take the subway home. | go home 不加 to。 |
