# Your Job and Your Team 我的工作与团队

## 1. 说每天做什么

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I handle + 事情 | **I handle** all the customer emails. | 客户的邮件都归我处理。 |
| I'm in charge of + 事情 | **I'm in charge of** the Suzhou warehouse. | 苏州仓归我负责。 |
| I take care of + 事情 | **I take care of** the invoices and payments. | 发票和付款是我在弄。 |
| I'm working on + 事情 | **I'm working on** the Q4 budget this week. | 这周我在做四季度的预算。 |
| My main job is + 动名词 | **My main job is** checking quality. | 我主要的工作是查质量。 |
| I do + 事情 + for + 对象 | **I do** the design **for** the app team. | 我给 App 组做设计。 |
| It's mostly + 事情 | **It's mostly** data entry and filing. | 主要是录数据和归档。 |
| I cover for + 人 | **I cover for** Anna when she's on leave. | Anna 休假时我顶她的活。 |

## 2. 说团队和汇报关系

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I report to + 人 | **I report to** Anna, the marketing director. | 我向市场总监 Anna 汇报。 |
| There are six of us. | **There are six of us** on the design team. | 设计组我们一共六个人。 |
| I work with + 人/团队 | **I work with** the Dongguan factory every day. | 我每天跟东莞的工厂对接。 |
| My teammate handles that. | **My teammate handles that** — I'll ask her. | 那是我同事在管，我问她。 |
| We split the work. | **We split the work** — I do apps, he does hardware. | 我们分工，我做 App，他做硬件。 |
| He's on my team. | **He's on my team** — same floor, same meetings. | 他跟我一个组，同层同会。 |

## 3. 说忙和闲

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm swamped. | **I'm swamped** — three reports due Friday. | 我忙炸了，周五要交三份报告。 |
| I'm on a deadline. | **I'm on a deadline** — can we talk at 4? | 我在赶截止期，四点聊行吗？ |
| Things are quiet this week. | **Things are quiet this week** — everyone's away. | 这周挺清闲，大家都休假了。 |
| I have a lot on my plate. | **I have a lot on my plate** right now. | 我手上现在事情很多。 |
| It's been crazy lately. | **It's been crazy lately** — peak season. | 最近忙疯了，正是旺季。 |
| I'm off this Friday. | **I'm off this Friday** — using my comp day. | 我周五休息，用调休。 |
| I'll be out all morning. | **I'll be out all morning** — back after lunch. | 我一上午不在，午饭后回来。 |

## 4. 在哪儿上班、怎么上班

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| I work from home on Fridays. | **I work from home on Fridays** — no commute. | 我周五居家办公，不用通勤。 |
| I'm in the office three days a week. | **I'm in the office three days a week.** | 我一周到公司三天。 |
| I'm on the early shift. | **I'm on the early shift** — 8 to 5. | 我上早班，八点到五点。 |
| I'm on a business trip. | **I'm on a business trip** in Guangzhou. | 我在广州出差。 |

## 5. 问同事在忙什么

| 说法 | 例句 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|---|
| What are you working on? | **What are you working on** this week? | 你这周在忙什么？ |
| How's the project going? | **How's the project going?** Still on track? | 项目怎么样了？还顺利吗？ |
| Are you free for a quick chat? | **Are you free for a quick chat?** Five minutes. | 有空聊两句吗？五分钟。 |
| Do you have a minute? | **Do you have a minute?** It's about the order. | 你有空吗？是订单的事。 |
| Can I pick your brain? | **Can I pick your brain** about the pricing? | 能请教你个事吗？关于报价。 |
| I'll come by your desk. | **I'll come by your desk** after the stand-up. | 站会开完我去你工位。 |

## 6. 常见错误

| 常见错误 | 正确说法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| My work is very busy. | I'm very busy at work. | 忙的是人，不是工作。 |
| I do a job in the finance team. | I work in the finance team. | 说在哪工作用 work in。 |
| I am responsible of the report. | I'm responsible for the report. | responsible 后面用 for。 |
| I am working in this company 3 years. | I've worked here for three years. | 持续到现在用现在完成时。 |
| I have many works to do. | I have a lot of work to do. | work 不可数，没有 works。 |
| I am in charge of the marketing. | I'm in charge of marketing. | 部门名称前不加 the。 |
| I am busy on my work. | I'm busy with work. | busy 后面用 with。 |
