===== PAGE 1 =====
Travel English Phrases 
At the Airport
Excuse me, how do I… ? 
• Check in. “how do I get to the check-in counter?” 
Where is the… ? 
• Information desk/center.
• Boarding Gate. 
• Restroom. Also called a bathroom, washroom, men’s room, lady’s room.
• Charging station.
• Restaurant./ Coffee shop/ Café/ Cafeteria.
• Convenience store.
• Supermarket.
• Tax Refund.
• Duty Free shop.
How do I get to… ? 
What time is my flight? 
How much luggage am I allowed to carry on? 
Are meals included?

What time is breakfast served?
What time is check-in / check-out?

• Fridge/ mini-bar.
• Wi-Fi. Can you help me with Wi-Fi?/ Wi-Fi doesn’t work./ Wi-Fi isn’t working.
• Air Conditioner.
What floor am I on?

• Towels.
• Toilet paper.
• Bedsheets.
• Toothbrush/ Shampoo/ Bodywash/ 
Conditioner
• Free Bottled water

• Grocery store/ Supermarket.
• Hospital/ Pharmacy/ Drug store.
• Cinema/ Park/ Shopping Mall
• Bank/ ATM/ Money Change.
• Restaurant.
